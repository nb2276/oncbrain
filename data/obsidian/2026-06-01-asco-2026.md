# 2026-06-01 — ASCO Annual Meeting 2026

**DeLLphi-304 post hoc: 2L tarlatamab CNS PFS NE vs 7.2mo (HR 0.54), 6.5 vs 4.2mo in brain-met subset (HR 0.40).**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Thoracic carried the day on a CNS question radiation oncologists own: tarlatamab delayed intracranial progression in 2L SCLC, but with no RT exposure data the SRS/WBRT deferral question stays open. Lower GI adds a ctDNA surveillance caution, 41% sensitivity for local regrowth is too low to relax nonoperative imaging.

## 🌀 GI Lower

Watch-and-wait surveillance after chemoRT: ctDNA misses most local regrowths, so endoscopy and MRI keep carrying the salvage window.

### ctDNA and Local Regrowth/Distant Mets in Nonoperative Rectal Cancer

> ctDNA sensitivity for local regrowth only 41% (12/29), specificity 94%; distant mets sensitivity 74%, specificity 97%.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The number that gates watch-and-wait practice is 41% sensitivity for local regrowth (12/29 samples): a negative ctDNA cannot license lengthening MRI or endoscopy intervals in an organ-preservation protocol. Specificity 94% means a positive result is worth acting on, and ctDNA+ at regrowth tracked ypT3-4 in 6/8 vs 3/14 (p=0.01).

![ctDNA and Local Regrowth/Distant Mets in Nonoperative Rectal Cancer](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HJvLPL_XgAATN04.jpg)

| Outcome | Sensitivity | Specificity | Accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|
| For local regrowth | 12 / 29 (41%) | 480 / 509 (94%) | 492 / 538 (91%) |
| For distant metastasis | 31 / 42 (74%) | 611 / 627 (97%) | 642 / 669 (96%) |

- 🔍 INTERCEPT program, N=110, 2020-2024, MSS stage I-III rectal adenoca, cCR/nCR after NAT then NOM
- 🔍 Tumor-informed ctDNA (Signatera Exome), serial draws; per-sample analysis of 669 samples, events within ±90d of draw
- 📊 Per-sample ctDNA accuracy: local regrowth vs distant metastasis
- 📊 Per-sample test performance (669 samples)
- 📐 Ever-positive longitudinal ctDNA → worse 2yr regrowth-free and MFS (log rank p=0.0002 and p<.0001)
- 📐 First post-NAT ctDNA (within 180d) positive → worse regrowth-free (p=0.0006) and MFS (p<.0001)
- 📊 ctDNA+ at regrowth tracked deeper invasion at salvage surgery: ypT3-4 in 6/8 (75%) vs 3/14 (21%), p=0.01
- 🔍 Event rates: 23/110 (21%) local regrowth, 12/110 (11%) distant mets; median f/u 25mo (IQR 18-37)
- ⚠️ First post-NAT ctDNA analysis rests on n=12 evaluable pts per the source text; treat that curve as anecdotal
- ⚠️ Retrospective single-institution cohort; no comparison against MRI or endoscopy performance, so the incremental value over standard NOM surveillance is untested
- ⚠️ Per-sample framing inflates n (669 draws from 110 pts) and does not adjust for within-patient correlation

## 🫁 Thoracic / Lung

Post hoc intracranial read on a systemic agent, framed against the SRS/WBRT timing decision it does not yet resolve.

### DeLLphi-304

> Post hoc CNS analysis: median CNS PFS NE vs 7.2mo, HR 0.54 (0.39-0.75) favoring 2L tarlatamab in SCLC.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> For an RT reader the actionable number is the brain-met subset: CNS PFS 6.5 vs 4.2mo, HR 0.40 (0.24, 0.66) by mRANO-BM BICR, with CNS CR 14.9% vs 5.4%. But >70% had prior CNS-directed treatment and no RT exposure or intracranial-failure pattern is reported, so this informs surveillance interval rather than deferring SRS.

- 🔍 Post hoc intracranial analysis of DeLLphi-304, 2L SCLC, tarlatamab vs chemotherapy
- 📊 ITT CNS PFS by investigator RECIST: n=254 vs 255, median NE (13.7, NE) vs 7.2mo (5.6, NE)
- 📐 ITT CNS PFS HR 0.54 (95% CI 0.39, 0.75); slide states 46% lower risk of CNS progression or death
- 🔍 Brain-met subset analysis used mRANO-BM by BICR, n=67 vs 56, with ≥1 post-baseline scan
- 📊 Brain-met subset CNS PFS 6.5mo (4.3, 13.7) vs 4.2mo (2.9, 5.5), HR 0.40 (0.24, 0.66)
- 📊 CNS tumor shrinkage ≥30% in 9/16 (56.3%) vs 5/13 (38.5%) among pts with lesions ≥10mm
- ⚠️ >70% of pts had received prior CNS-directed treatment, so response categories were CR / non-CR-non-PD / PD, not ORR
- ⚠️ Post hoc, two different assessment frameworks (RECIST investigator for ITT, mRANO-BM BICR for the subset); brain-met HR from an unstratified Cox model
- ⚠️ Median follow-up only 11.4mo (tarlatamab) and 11.5mo (chemo); ITT median CNS PFS not reached, so the ITT magnitude is unstable
- ⚠️ No intracranial-only vs extracranial failure breakdown and no radiotherapy exposure data in source, so the SRS/WBRT deferral question is not answered
