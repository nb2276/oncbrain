# 2026-07-14

**TREASURE: consolidative TRT after chemo-IO in ES-SCLC halted for excess fatal SAEs, mOS 6.7 vs 13.4mo (HR 1.55) and SAEs 61.3% vs 18.2%.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Thoracic carried the day, and it is a negative safety signal, not an efficacy one: adding 30 Gy/10 fx to atezolizumab maintenance tripled SAEs and killed 19.4% of pts, with no dosimetric or timing explanation and near-universal post-TRT lymphopenia. The control arm outperformed IMpower133, so the deficit sits with TRT plus ongoing IO, not a weak comparator.

## 🫁 Thoracic / Lung

Consolidative TRT layered onto continuing checkpoint blockade in ES-SCLC is the question, and the SMC answered it before the trial finished accruing.

### Consolidative Thoracic RT + Atezolizumab Maintenance (ES-SCLC) · [[NCT04462276]]

> mOS 6.7 vs 13.4mo (HR 1.55, P=.34) and SAEs 61.3% vs 18.2% with consolidative TRT; trial halted early.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The PFS/OS dissociation is the RT read: PFS was identical (2.4 vs 2.6 mo, HR 0.92), so 30 Gy/10 fx bought no systemic disease control while OS fell, pointing at toxicity rather than progression. Lymphopenia was TRT-specific and persistent, and timing (concurrent vs sequential) did not rescue it.

- 📊 Efficacy and safety, arm A (atezo + TRT) vs arm B (atezo alone), ITT

  | Endpoint | Atezo + TRT | Atezo only | Effect size |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Median OS | 6.7 mo (5.1-9.0) | 13.4 mo (10.7-17.5) | HR 1.55 (0.90-2.69), P = .34 |
  | Median PFS | 2.4 mo (1.3-3.9) | 2.6 mo (1.2-3.9) | HR 0.92 (0.54-1.55), P = .85 |
  | 1-y OS | 30.3% (15.9-46.1) | 56.6% (37.9-71.6) | n/a |
  | 2-y OS | 24.2% (11.4-39.6) | 15.7% (5.7-30.2) | n/a |
  | SAEs | 19 pts (61.3%) | 6 pts (18.2%) | P < .001 |
  | Fatal AEs | 6 pts (19.4%) | 1 pt (3.0%) | P = .04 |

- 🔍 TREASURE: multicenter open-label randomised phase 2, 20 sites Germany/Austria, N=68 of 104 planned
- 🔍 Eligibility: ES-SCLC with ≥ SD after induction carboplatin-etoposide-atezolizumab
- 📐 TRT 30 Gy in 10 fx, target volume from postinduction primary + involved nodal volume
- ⚠️ Recruitment halted by the SMC for excess fatal SAEs in arm A; power lost, analyses descriptive
- ⚠️ Doses and volumes were within typical ranges and below OAR thresholds, so this is not a plan-quality failure
- ⚠️ What did NOT explain the excess toxicity in arm A
  - Baseline ECOG, Charlson index, PFTs, thoracic tumor burden, tumor location
  - Clinical or dosimetric parameters (eTables 8 and 9)
  - Concurrent vs sequential TRT timing (eFigure 4, eTable 11)
- 📊 TRT associated with AE risk over time (HR 2.47, 95% CI 1.15-5.32, P = .01); atezolizumab exposure was not (HR 0.57, 0.30-1.08, P = .06)
- 📊 Lymphopenia after TRT: cycle 2 ALC <0.001/μL in 22/24 (91.7%) arm A vs 3/22 (13.6%) arm B, P < .001; leukocytes and neutrophils unaffected
- 📐 Fatal-AE pts in arm A had worse baseline DLCO SB (45.3 [13.7] vs 56.7 [11.4], P = .04)
- 🔗 Arm B mOS 13.4mo and 1-y OS 56.6% exceed the IMpower133 atezolizumab arm (12.3mo, 51.7%); arm A fell below it
- 🔗 Directionally consistent with PACIFIC-2 and CheckMate-73L, where simultaneous thoracic chemoRT + IO in NSCLC also produced more fatal infections

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Consolidative Thoracic Radiotherapy With Atezolizumab Maintenance in Extensive-Stage Small Cell Lung Cancer** — Bozorgmehr, Farastuk; Chung, Inn; Behnisch, Rouven et al.. *JAMA Oncology* (2026-07). [doi:10.1001/jamaoncol.2026.2330](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaoncol.2026.2330)
  > Importance Chemoimmunotherapy followed by immunotherapy maintenance is the standard first-line treatment for extensive-stage small cell lung cancer (ES-SCLC), yet data regarding efficacy and safety of consolidative thoracic radiotherapy (TRT) are lacking. Objective To determine whether combining consolidative TRT with immunotherapy maintenance in ES-SCLC is safe and improves patients’ overall survival (OS) and progression-free survival (PFS). Design, Settings, and Participants This was a multicenter open-label phase 2 randomized clinical trial recruiting patients from September 2020 to August 2022 in Germany and Austria, with the last follow-up visit in September 2024. Eligible patients had ES-SCLC with at least stable disease after induction chemoimmunotherapy (carboplatin−etoposide−atezolizumab). Although the database was locked in April 2025, a post hoc survival update was performed in April 2026. Data were analyzed from April 2025 to April 2026. Interventions Randomized (1:1) to either atezolizumab maintenance combined with consolidative TRT (30 Gy in 10 fractions) (arm A) or atezolizumab maintenance alone (arm B). Main Outcome and Measure OS (time from randomization to death due to any cause). Results Of 96 patients assessed for eligibility, 68 patients were randomized; recruitment was prematurely terminated due to safety concerns. Median OS in the combination arm was numerically shorter compared to atezolizumab only (6.7 months [95% CI, 5.1-9.0] vs 13.4 months [95% CI, 10.7-17.5]; HR, 1.55 [95% CI, 0.90-2.69]; P = .34), while median PFS was similar (2.4 months [95% CI, 1.3-3.9] vs 2.6 months [95% CI, 1.2-3.9]; HR, 0.92 [95% CI, 0.54-1.55]; P = .85). TRT plus atezolizumab was accompanied by higher frequency of severe adverse events (SAEs) (19 patients [61.3%] vs 6 patients [18.2%]; P &amp;amp;lt; .001) and fatal outcomes (6 patients [19.4%] vs 1 patient [3.0%]; P = .04). Safety analysis revealed predominance of infection and respiratory disorder−related SAEs, possibly facilitated by a depletion of lymphocytes observed specifically in patients after TRT, and by a lower single breath diffuse capacity of the lungs for carbon monoxide in patients with fatal AEs in arm A. No further risk factors were identified, given that baseline characteristics were well balanced between both arms and between patients with and without SAEs, including fatal SAEs. Conclusions and Relevance In this randomized clinical trial, TRT combined with immunotherapy increased toxic effects in unselected patients with ES-SCLC without survival benefit, presumably due to radiation-induced lymphocyte depletion facilitating infections. Cautious patient selection and further investigation to identify those who may benefit without undue risk are required. Trial Registration ClinicalTrials.gov Identifier: [[NCT04462276]]
