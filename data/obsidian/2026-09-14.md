## 🫁 Thoracic / Lung

Studies from before MRI surveillance favored PCI (fewer brain mets, improved OS); MAVERICK asks whether that still holds now that MRI brain surveillance is routine.

### SWOG S1827/MAVERICK

> Randomized phase III, LS or ES SCLC: PCI 25 Gy/10 fx + MRI vs MRI surveillance alone; no CFFS or OS results in source tweets.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The RT question is PCI omission, not RT omission: the surveillance arm still gets RT at brain mets (SRS, WBRT or HA-WBRT at discretion), so CFFS pits upfront 25 Gy/10 fx PCI against deferred salvage. HA-PCI was also discretionary, diluting any hippocampal-sparing read. No CFFS or OS result in source tweets.

- 📊 No CFFS or OS results in source tweets; slides show design, eligibility and hypothesis only
- 🔍 Randomized phase III: PCI + MRI brain surveillance vs MRI brain surveillance alone (no PCI)
- 🔍 Stratified by LS vs ES stage, immunotherapy (y/n), PS 0-1 vs 2
- 🔍 Eligibility
  - LS or ES SCLC, ≥18y, PS 0-2
  - LS: completed platinum chemo plus definitive thoracic RT or surgical resection
  - ES: completed platinum chemo
  - Immunotherapy (concurrent and/or adjuvant) allowed in LS and ES
  - Response to upfront therapy, no progression (CT or PET/CT within 42 days)
  - No prior brain mets; no brain mets on MRI within 28 days of enrollment
  - Enrollment within 16 weeks of Day 1 of last chemo cycle
- 🔍 PCI 25 Gy/10 fx; hippocampal-avoidance PCI allowed at physician discretion
- 🔍 1° EP: cognitive failure free survival (time to cognitive decline or death)
- 🔍 Brain MRI and cognitive testing at 3, 6, 9, 12, 18, 24 mo
- ⚠️ Discretionary HA-PCI mixes techniques within the PCI arm, blurring the cognitive comparison
- ⚠️ Salvage SRS, WBRT or HA-WBRT at brain mets left to physician discretion; deferred-RT cognitive cost varies
- 🔗 Per tweet: pre-MRI-surveillance studies showed fewer brain mets and improved OS with PCI
