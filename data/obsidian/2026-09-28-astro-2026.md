# 2026-09-28 — ASTRO Annual Meeting 2026

**SWOG S1827/MAVERICK: MRI surveillance alone beat MRI + PCI on CFFS, HR 0.60 (90% CI 0.46-0.78), in LS- and ES-SCLC; final OS pending.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Thoracic, CNS and prostate RT decisions led. In SCLC without brain mets after 1L therapy, MAVERICK favors MRI surveillance over PCI on a cognition-driven composite; OS immature. ROADS: Cs-131 tile brachy at resection cut surgical bed recurrence vs post-op SRT, HR 0.06. PACE-A at 8 yrs: pad use 8% SBRT vs 48% RP; only 13 BCF events.

## 🌰 Prostate

SBRT randomised against RP in GG1-2 disease: urinary function data strong, oncologic comparison underpowered.

### PACE-A

> ≥1 urinary pad 14/29 (48%) RP vs 3/36 (8%) SBRT; BCF HR 0.48 (95% CI 0.16-1.46), mFU 8 yrs, GG1-2 localised PCa.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The continence gap is the RT read: ≥1 pad in 14/29 (48%) after RP vs 3/36 (8%) after 36.25 Gy/5fx, with bowel toxicity 0 (0%) on SBRT. With mFU 8 yrs and 7 deaths none from PCa, this supports SBRT as the lower-urinary-morbidity option when counselling surgical candidates, though efficacy parity rests on 13 events.

## 🧠 CNS

Brachy at resection challenges delayed post-op cavity SRT for resected brain mets; endpoint amendment and open-label review warrant scrutiny.

### ROADS · [[NCT04365374]]

> Cs-131 tile brachytherapy at resection cut surgical bed recurrence vs post-op SRT: time-to-SBR HR 0.06 (0.01-0.46), SB-RFS HR 0.48, in newly diagnosed resected brain mets.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The surgical bed effect held in the per-protocol population (time-to-SBR HR 0.06), so it is not just an artifact of the 17.8% of SRT pts who never got cavity RT. Control was modern, mostly fractionated 27-30 Gy SRT. This moves the cavity-RT decision from a delayed post-op SRT course toward intra-op Cs-131 implant.

- 🔍 Randomized, open-label, non-inferiority phase 3; 32 US centers; 230 randomized 1:1 pre-operatively
- 🔍 mITT N=204 (103 R+TBRT, 101 R+SRT); median f/u 12.9 mo
- 🔍 Eligible: one surgical brain metastasis 2.0-7.0 cm; non-resected mets got SRT post-op
- 💊 RT delivery by arm
  - TBRT: collagen tile with four Cs-131 seeds, implanted at surgery after intra-op path confirmation
  - Cavity SRT: 1 fx (17-20 Gy), 3 fx (27 Gy) or 5 fx (30 Gy), planned 21±7 days post-op
  - Multi-fraction SRT in 92.2% of R+SRT; median surgery to SRT 27.0 days
- 📊 Efficacy, mITT (R+TBRT vs R+SRT)
- 📊 SBR events: 1 in R+TBRT vs 12 in R+SRT
- 📊 Any AE 79.0% vs 80.7%; ≥G3 TRAEs 20.0% vs 21.7%
- 📊 No difference in QoL (FACT-Br), KPS, Barthel ADL or neurocognition
- ⚠️ Co-primary endpoints amended May 2025: SB-RFS originally censored death in error; outcome data reportedly unreviewed at amendment
- ⚠️ 18 R+SRT pts never received SRT (17.8% non-completion); PP SB-RFS HR 0.57 (0.35-0.93), time-to-SBR HR 0.06 held
- ⚠️ OS benefit lost in PP: HR 0.73 (0.44-1.20), p=0.2156; mechanism unexplained, authors call for validation
- ⚠️ Open-label; seed artifacts on MRI may have unblinded central SBR review
- 🔗 Control arm performed as expected: 15.4% 12-mo SBR vs 84-87% freedom-from-SBR in cited fractionated SRT series

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Surgery and tile-based radiation therapy versus surgery and stereotactic radiation for newly diagnosed brain metastases (ROADS): a randomized, open-label, phase 3 trial** — Weinberg, Jeffrey S.; Imber, Brandon S.; DiNapoli, Vincent et al.. *Journal of Clinical Oncology* (2026-09). [doi:10.1200/jco-26-01894](https://doi.org/10.1200/jco-26-01894)
  > PURPOSE Post-operative stereotactic radiation (SRT) is the standard-of-care for resected brain metastases. Implantation of cesium-131 collagen tiles (tile-based radiation therapy, TBRT) initiates focal radiation immediately after resection, potentially offering therapeutic and logistical advantages. ROADS: a randomized, open-label, non-inferiority, phase 3 trial ( [[NCT04365374]] ) compared the safety and efficacy of resection with TBRT (R+TBRT) to resection with SRT (R+SRT) for patients with a newly diagnosed brain metastasis indicated for surgical resection. PATIENTS AND METHODS Across 32 United States centers, patients were pre-operatively randomized 1:1 to resection R+TBRT or R+SRT. Any non-resected brain metastases received SRT post-operatively. Co-primary outcomes were time-to-surgical bed recurrence (SBR) and surgical bed recurrence-free survival (SB-RFS). Outcomes were analyzed using Cox proportional hazards models with stratification factors as covariates. Multiplicity was controlled by hierarchical testing. Analyses used the pre-specified modified intent-to-treat (mITT) population (patients who underwent surgery, had pathologic confirmation of brain metastasis, and had follow-up information). RESULTS From April 2021 through August 2025, 230 patients were randomized (115 per arm); 204 of whom (103 R+TBRT, 101 R+SRT) comprised the mITT population. Median follow-up was 12.9 months. Median time-to-SBR was not reached (R+TBRT) versus 17.4 months (R+SRT) (hazard ratio [HR]: 0.06; 95% confidence interval [CI]: 0.01–0.46; p=0.0070). SB-RFS was improved with R+TBRT, with a median of not reached versus 10.9 months (R+SRT) (HR: 0.48; 95% CI: 0.30–0.76; p=0.0021). Overall adverse events did not appear to differ: 83 patients (79.0%, 95% CI: 70.0–86.4) (R+TBRT) versus 67 patients (80.7%, 95% CI:70.6–88.6) (R+SRT). CONCLUSION For patients with newly diagnosed brain metastases requiring resection, R+TBRT significantly improved SBR and SB-RFS, demonstrating both non-inferiority and superiority.

> [!question] Open questions
> - Does the OS benefit replicate in an independent trial?
> - TBRT vs pre-operative SRT for resectable brain metastases
> - Late radiation necrosis after Cs-131 tiles with longer follow-up

## 🫁 Thoracic / Lung

PCI omission with MRI surveillance now has phase III randomised support in both LS- and ES-SCLC.

### SWOG S1827/MAVERICK

> MRI surveillance alone beat MRI + PCI for cognitive failure-free survival, HR 0.60 (90% CI 0.46-0.78), in LS- and ES-SCLC without brain mets after first-line therapy.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The benefit held with HA-PCI in 77% of the PCI arm and in the HA-PCI-only subgroup, so hippocampal sparing does not rescue PCI. Stage did not modify the effect (LS HR 0.59, ES HR 0.65, interaction p=0.79), which moves the LS-SCLC PCI default toward omission with MRI surveillance, pending final OS.

- 🔍 Randomized phase III, total accrual 304; PCI + MRI n=152 vs MRI alone n=151
- 🔍 Eligible: SCLC (limited OR extensive), no prior brain mets, no brain mets on MRI after 1st-line therapy
- 🔍 Stratified by LS vs ES stage, immunotherapy (y/n), PS 0-1 vs 2
- 🔍 RT and surveillance parameters
  - PCI 25 Gy / 10 fx
  - HA-PCI allowed at physician discretion; 77% of PCI pts received HA-PCI
  - MRI + cognitive testing at 3, 6, 9, 12, 18, 24 mo
  - RT recommended at brain mets (WBRT and SRS allowed)
- 📐 1° EP CFFS: HR 0.60, 90% CI 0.46-0.78, one-sided p=0.0005, favoring MRI alone
- 📊 CFFS benefit by stage (MRI alone vs MRI + PCI)

  | Subgroup | CFFS HR |
  |---|---|
  | LS-SCLC | HR 0.59 |
  | ES-SCLC | HR 0.65 |
  | Interaction | p=0.79 |

- 📊 HA-PCI-only subgroup showed similar CFFS benefit with MRI alone; HR not reported in source
- 📊 Preliminary OS after 128 events: no significant difference; final OS at 190 events
- 📊 Brain mets decreased with PCI, but no significant PFS difference; brain mets magnitude not reported in source
- 🔍 68% LS-SCLC; 41% received immunotherapy with upfront treatment
- ⚠️ CFFS is a composite of death + cognitive failure; with OS not different, the signal is cognitive
- ⚠️ 90% CI with one-sided p; OS still immature (128 of 190 planned events)
- ⚠️ ADRIATIC (consolidation durvalumab for LS-SCLC) published mid-enrollment; backbone heterogeneous
