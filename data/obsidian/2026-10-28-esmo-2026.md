# 2026-10-28 — ESMO Congress 2026

**SMART-KEY: cisplatin-free induction → IMRT + pembrolizumab gives 2y LFS 65.1% in stage III–IVB glottic/supraglottic SCC, matching RTOG 9111's 64.9% cross-trial; single-arm, N=43.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Head and neck carried the day: larynx preservation without cisplatin (SMART-KEY), on-CRT ctDNA clearance tracking EFS in HPV- LA HNSCC (KEYNOTE-412), and neoadjuvant NivoDP MPR 60.9% in node-positive high-grade SGC, all single-arm or retrospective. Four LA HNSCC/SGC reads, all IO-layered around CRT or surgery; none isolates RT's contribution, and RT dose/volumes are missing from two of the four (SMART-KEY, NivoDP).

### GORTEC 2024-03 IDEAL · [[NCT06959108]]

> Interim safety: G3 TEAEs in 6 vs 2 of 10 pts per arm, no G4; preliminary post-induction ORR 71% (first 15 evaluable) before 70 Gy IMRT-cisplatin.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The RT question is whether three cycles of EGFR-ADC induction compromise delivery of 70 Gy IMRT with concurrent cisplatin, and the source gives no CRT-phase compliance or toxicity data yet. The 12-wk post-CRT CR primary endpoint will be the first read on whether induction adds to definitive CRT response.

- 🔍 Randomized (1:1) phase II, LA-HNSCC: larynx, hypopharynx, oral cavity, oropharynx (p16- and p16+)
- 💊 Arm A: induction pucotenlimab 200 mg + becotatug vedotin 2.3 mg/kg Q3W ×3 → CRT → adjuvant pucotenlimab Q3W ×8
- 💊 Arm B: induction becotatug vedotin (EGFR-ADC) alone → CRT, no adjuvant IO
- 🔍 CRT backbone: IMRT 70 Gy + concurrent cisplatin, identical in both arms
- 🔍 Primary endpoint: CR at 12 wk post-CRT; each arm tested independently (one-stage Fleming, H0=30%), 106 pts planned
- 🔍 49 randomized Oct 2025 to Apr 2026; 31 oropharynx, 19 of them p16+
- 📊 First interim safety analysis (after cycle 1, first 20 pts)

  | Measure | Arm A (IO + ADC) | Arm B (ADC alone) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Pts evaluated | 10 | 10 |
  | Pts with G3 TEAEs | 6 | 2 |
  | G4 AEs | None | None |
  | Deaths | None | 1 (post-gastrostomy, deemed unrelated) |

- 📊 G3 TEAEs by arm (11 events in 8 pts)
  - Arm A: ALT increase + constipation (1), myalgia + ALT increase + hypertension (1), neutropenia (1), diabetes (1), diarrhea (1), hyponatremia (1)
  - Arm B: peripheral arterial ischemia (1), fatigue (1)
- 📊 ORR (RECIST 1.1) after induction 71% in first 15 evaluable pts; not split by arm, immature
- ⚠️ Primary endpoint (12-wk post-CRT CR) not yet reported; no CRT compliance or RT-phase toxicity data in source
- ⚠️ Safety read is cycle 1 only, 10 pts per arm; non-comparative design cannot isolate the PD-1 contribution

### Neoadjuvant NivoDP in High-grade Salivary Gland Carcinoma · [[NCT05727410]]

> MPR 60.9% and pCR 32.6% in 46 operated pts with node-positive high-grade SGC, above the MPR ≥50% threshold; 24-mo DFS 76.0%.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Adjuvant RT was risk-adapted after surgery, yet the abstract gives no dose, volume, or how a pCR (32.6% of operated pts) changed the RT plan. That leaves the de-escalation question open: whether pCR after NivoDP can justify reduced or omitted adjuvant RT in node-positive SGC is untested here.

- 🔍 Phase II, single-center, single-arm; 55 enrolled, 50 analyzed
- 💊 Nivolumab 360 mg + docetaxel 60 mg/m2 + cisplatin 60 mg/m2 q3w x3 → surgery → risk-adapted adjuvant RT
- 🔍 Treatment-naïve resectable high-grade SGC, clinically node-positive; stage III/IVA/IVB 34.0%/64.0%/2.0%
- 🔍 Salivary duct carcinoma 74.0% of cohort; 88.0% male
- 📊 ORR 52.0%; 46 pts (92.0%) reached surgery, R0 93.5%
- ⚠️ 4 not operated: 1 withdrawal, 2 progression, 1 G3 drug-induced pneumonitis
- 📊 Pathologic response by histology (best responders)

  | Histology | MPR | pCR |
  |---|---|---|
  | Salivary duct carcinoma | 67.6% | 29.4% |
  | Adenocarcinoma | 75.0% | 75.0% |
  | Poorly differentiated carcinoma | 66.7% | 66.7% |

- 📊 12-/24-mo DFS 89.0%/76.0%; 24-mo OS 91.2%; median f/u 16.2 mo from surgery
- 📐 MPR vs non-MPR DFS: NR vs 24.2 mo, HR 0.19 (95% CI 0.05-0.71), p=0.013
- ⚠️ MPR-vs-non-MPR DFS is a responder comparison, prognostic not proof of treatment benefit
- ⚠️ Adjuvant RT dose, volumes and risk-adaptation criteria not reported in source

### KEYNOTE-412 ctDNA analysis · [[NCT03040999]]

> In HPV- LA HNSCC, ctDNA clearance at C3D1 linked to better EFS in both arms, OS only with pembro (P<0.001); HPV+ prognostic signal absent.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The ctDNA draw sits at C3D1, after 5 of 6-7 wk of CRT, so clearance measures mostly the response to radiation and not a pretreatment selector. In HPV- disease clearance tracked EFS with or without pembro (P = 0.001, P = 0.011), which points toward on-treatment adaptive escalation. No effect sizes are reported, so it cannot yet set a threshold.

- 🔍 Retrospective ctDNA analysis of phase 3 KEYNOTE-412: pembro or pbo + concurrent CRT, newly diagnosed unresected LA HNSCC
- 🔍 473 of 804 pts evaluable at both timepoints (pembro n = 233; pbo n = 240)
- 🔍 Tumor-informed Natera Signatera 16-plex assay; HPV status by p16
- 🔍 On-treatment draw at C3D1, in pts who completed 5 of 6-7 wk of CRT
- 📊 BL ctDNA positivity 95.6% HPV+ vs 95.3% HPV-
- 📊 BL ctDNA level correlated with BL target lesion size (Spearman ρ = 0.49 HPV-, 0.34 HPV+)
- 📊 ctDNA clearance rate at C3D1, by arm and HPV status

  | HPV status | Pembro + CRT | Pbo + CRT |
  |---|---|---|
  | HPV- | 72.6% | 63.6% |
  | HPV+ | 76.8% | 62.7% |

- 📐 C3D1 ctDNA clearance vs outcome (P values; no HRs reported in source)

  | Subgroup · endpoint | Pembro + CRT | Pbo + CRT |
  |---|---|---|
  | HPV- · EFS | P = 0.001 | P = 0.011 |
  | HPV- · OS | P < 0.001 | P = 0.075 |
  | HPV+ · EFS | no association | P = 0.028 |
  | HPV+ · OS | no association | P = 0.013 |

- 📊 Higher BL ctDNA → worse OS (pembro arm) and worse EFS + OS (pbo arm) in HPV- pts (P < 0.05); no association in HPV+
- ⚠️ BL ctDNA prognostic value in HPV- was dependent on tumor size and stage, independent of ECOG PS
- ⚠️ No HRs, medians or absolute EFS/OS by ctDNA status in source; P values only
- ⚠️ Retrospective; evaluable subset only, and the C3D1 draw requires near-completed CRT (survivorship selection)

### SMART-KEY (LACOG 0720) · [[NCT04943445]]

> 2y laryngectomy-free survival 65.1% (80% CI 54.9-73.5) with cisplatin-free induction + IMRT/pembrolizumab, numerically similar to RTOG 9111's 64.9%.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The concurrent phase was IMRT with pembrolizumab alone, and the 2y failure pattern is locoregional (18.8%) far more than distant (7.4%), so the open RT question is whether IO can substitute for cisplatin radiosensitization in-field. RT dose and volumes are not reported in source, which limits transfer to a given larynx-preservation protocol.

- 🔍 Single-arm, multicenter phase 2; N=43, 9 centers, enrolled Feb 2022 to Aug 2023
- 🔍 Newly diagnosed stage III–IVB glottic/supraglottic SCC; 62.8% glottic, 83.7% former/current smokers
- 💊 Regimen (no cisplatin, no concurrent chemo)
  - Induction: carboplatin AUC6 + paclitaxel 175 mg/m2 + pembrolizumab 200 mg, three 21-day cycles
  - Concurrent: IMRT + pembrolizumab alone
  - Consolidation: fourteen 21-day cycles of pembrolizumab
- ⚠️ RT dose, fractionation and target volumes not reported in source
- 📊 2y outcomes vs historical controls (as tabulated by the investigators)

- 📊 2y cumulative incidence: locoregional recurrence 18.8%, distant recurrence 7.4%
- 📊 G≥3 TRAEs 41.9%, one grade 5 event
- 📊 Immune-mediated G≥3 TRAEs 16.3%

### RADIANT

> RT foundation model on 119,782 cases: Dice 83.52% GTV / 81.53% CTV; external toxicity AUCs 0.78-0.85, LC/LRC C-index 0.76-0.86.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The toxicity heads carry the RT-specific read: radiation pneumonitis AUC 0.78-0.81 and rectal proctitis 0.84-0.85, from a model that sees the 3D dose map rather than DVH summaries. If that holds after calibration, it bears on dose-constraint and OAR-sparing decisions per patient. Auto-contouring at Dice 83.52% GTV still needs per-site review before it replaces physician delineation.

- 🔍 3D Swin Transformer encoder, masked-autoencoder pretraining on 119,782 RT cases from 14 centres
- 🔍 Inputs: paired planning CT, RT structure sets, 3D dose distributions
- 🔍 Encoder refined with hierarchical GTV/CTV segmentation plus dose generation
- 📊 Hold-out set: 22,058 cases across the 10 most prevalent tumour sites
- 📊 Hold-out overall Dice: 83.52% GTV, 81.53% CTV

### APCCC 2026: High-risk Localised, Locally Advanced, and Biochemically Recurrent PC

> Consensus (≥75%) reached on 44 of 134 questions (32.8%); fractionation for high-risk localised PC reached no consensus despite 88% favoring some hypofractionation.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Fractionation drew 88% for some form of hypofractionation but no consensus on which form, and 44 panellists abstained or declared themselves unqualified. Moderate hypofractionation (2.5–3.4 Gy/fx) is framed as the established option. SBRT for high-risk disease rests on non-randomised SHARP pooling, with 5-yr G2 GU toxicity of 23%, so it stays a trial question.

- 🔍 Modified Delphi: 144 international experts drafted 313 questions; 134 covered high-risk localised / locally advanced PC
- 🔍 133 voting panellists, web-based survey before the conference
- 📐 Consensus defined a priori as 75% agreement; strong consensus 90%
- 📊 Seven questions reached strong consensus
- 📊 Key panel votes, high-risk localised / locally advanced PC

  | Question | Setting | Panel vote |
  |---|---|---|
  | Q1–Q2 | High-risk cN0 M0 (conventional or next-gen imaging) | RT + long-term ADT ± ARPI 62–68%; surgery as multimodal 32–38% |
  | Q3–Q4 | Very high-risk cN0 M0 | RT + long-term ADT + abiraterone 2 yr 65–71%; very few for enzalutamide |
  | Q5 | Neoadjuvant systemic therapy before RT | Selected or most pts, combined 83%+ (87% combined) |
  | Q6 | Perioperative ADT + ARPI before RP | Consensus against |
  | Q7 | Stop ADT after 2 yr with abiraterone | Most or selected pts, combined 83% |
  | Q19 | Fractionation, high-risk localised / locally advanced | No consensus; combined 88% some hypofractionation; 44 abstained / unqualified |

- 💊 ENZARAD: 2 yr enzalutamide added to RT + 2 yr ADT, MFS HR 0.88 (95% CI 0.67–1.15; p = 0.34)
- 🔗 ENZARAD lower risk than STAMPEDE: cN1 11% vs 39%, cT3–T4 47% vs 94%
- 🔗 DIL focal boost RCT (>500 pts): 77 Gy/35 fx whole gland ± boost up to 95 Gy → better bPFS, lower local failure
- 🔗 SHARP pooled SBRT (440 high-risk pts): 5-yr BCR 22%, DM 9.2%, G2 GU 23%, G2 GI 10%
- ⚠️ HYPO-RT-PC only 11% high-risk; ultrahypofractionation evidence for high-risk is scarce per authors
- ⚠️ PROTEUS presented after APCCC 2026, so the Q6 vote against perioperative ADT + ARPI predates its data
- ⚠️ Votes are opinion; authors state no formal literature review or meta-analysis
