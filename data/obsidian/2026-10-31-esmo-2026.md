# 2026-10-31 — ESMO Congress 2026

**SARON: consolidation RT added to 1L SACT in synchronous oligometastatic AGA-negative NSCLC, mOS 31.0 vs 28.0mo (HR 0.99, p=0.95) despite mPFS HR 0.64.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Two randomised RT-plus-systemic trials missed their primary endpoints. SARON consolidation RT cut 12-mo local failure (13.0% vs 39.5%) with no OS gain. KEYNOTE-975 showed no EFS gain from concurrent pembro plus dCRT in CPS ≥10 (HR 0.97). Thoracic and upper GI carried the news. MATISSE 2 (CSCC, 71% clinical response) and [212Pb]VMT-α-NET (no DLTs, n=67) are early signals.

## 📋 Other

### [212Pb]VMT-α-NET · [[NCT05636618]]

> No DLTs or G4/5 AEs across 67 pts; previously reported ORR 43.4% in an evaluable cohort 2 subset (n=23/46) at 5 mCi.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The safety read is the transferable part: across 67 pts there was no serious renal event and no clinically significant treatment-related myelosuppression, the two toxicities that usually limit how much SSTR2 radioligand can be given. That keeps the 6 mCi level and Cohort 4 escalation open, though the abstract does not say whether pts had prior 177Lu-DOTATATE.

- 🔍 Dose-escalation phase 1/2a, N=67 dosed as of 03Apr2026 ([[NCT05636618]])
- 🔍 Enrollment by dose cohort
  - Cohort 1 (2.5 mCi): 2 pts
  - Cohort 2 (5 mCi): 46 pts
  - Cohort 3 (6 mCi): 19 pts
  - Now enrolling Cohort 4
- 🔍 Eligibility: unresectable/metastatic SSTR2+ NET, radiologic progression after ≥1 prior systemic line
- 💊 Up to 4 administrations of the alpha emitter at the assigned dose level; DLT window 42 days
- 📊 Safety across all 67 pts: none observed of
  - DLTs or Grade 4/5 AEs
  - Treatment-related discontinuations
  - Serious renal events or dysphagia
  - Clinically significant treatment-related myelosuppression
- 📊 ORR 43.4% in cohort 2 came from a prior read of 23/46 pts with sufficient f/u (60.1 wks)
- 📊 Cohort 3: 2 pts reached PR after 1 and 2 cycles
- 📊 Cohort 1: both pts still SD after 2 years of follow-up
- ⚠️ Efficacy set of 56 pts announced; updated ORR not stated in the abstract
- ⚠️ Investigator-assessed RECIST 1.1, no central review stated
- 🔗 Same SSTR2 target as approved beta RLT (177Lu-DOTATATE); no head-to-head data, and prior-RLT exposure not reported

The therapy is well tolerated with no observed dose-limiting toxicities or serious treatment-related adverse events. This study is ongoing and enrolling into Cohort 4.

## 🌞 Skin / Melanoma

Neoadjuvant NIVO/IPI with surgery omitted in CSCC responders: high early response, durability not yet known at 8 mo median f/u.

### MATISSE 2 · [[NCT06823479]]

> 71% clinical response (22/31) and 48% CCR after ultra-short neoadjuvant NIVO/IPI in CSCC facing extensive surgery; SOC omitted in responders, median f/u 8 mo.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The SOC being omitted in MATISSE 2 includes surgery and, per MATISSE, adjuvant RT; 22/31 (71%) skipped both at week 5. Only 9/31 (29%) went on to SOC, so for RT the near-term question is response durability in the wait-and-monitoring arm, which 8 mo median f/u cannot yet answer.

- 🔍 Single-arm cohort, planned N=41 CSCC pts with indication for extensive or disfiguring curative surgery; 31 included so far
- 💊 NIVO weeks 0 and 2, IPI week 0 only; no maintenance immunotherapy
- 🔍 Response assessed week 5; non-responders proceed to SOC week 6, responders enter strict wait-and-monitoring
- 📊 Week-5 outcomes (n=31)
  - Clinical response, SOC withheld: 22/31 (71%)
  - CCR during wait-and-monitoring: 15/31 (48%)
  - Partial clinical response: 7/31 (23%)
  - Received SOC: 9/31 (29%), 1 with complete pathological response
- ⚠️ Immune-related toxicity G3-4 19%; no surgical delay attributed to irAEs
- 🔗 Builds on MATISSE ([[NCT04620200]]): 80% partial or major path response after 2 NIVO/IPI courses, 9 pts with CCR without SOC
- ⚠️ Median f/u 8 mo is short for durability of CCR in a wait-and-monitoring strategy
- ⚠️ Interim report: 31 of 41 planned pts; durability in CCR and deepening in PCR pts not yet known
- ⚠️ Phase not stated in source; no randomized comparator vs surgery ± adjuvant RT

Van Dijk. *ESMO Congress 2026* (2026-10). [article](https://cslide.ctimeetingtech.com/esmo2026/attendee/confcal/presentation/list)
  > ESMO Congress 2026 abstract 2486RO · Rapid oral: Melanoma and other skin tumours · 2026-10-25 · presented by Stan W. Van Dijk (Amsterdam, Netherlands) · [[NCT06823479]]
  > Background: The MATISSE trial ([[NCT04620200]]) showed that 80% of patients with a cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma (CSCC) and an indication for extensive curative surgery with or without adjuvant radiotherapy as standard of care (SOC) reached a partial or major pathological response following two courses of neoadjuvant nivolumab and ipilimumab (NIVO/IPI). In addition, 9 patients reached a clinical complete remission (CCR) after only two courses of NIVO/IPI, without SOC. This raised the question whether organ preservation and cure can be reached via only ultra-short neoadjuvant NIVO/IPI, without SOC and without maintenance immunotherapy.
  > Methods: In the MATISSE 2 trial ([[NCT06823479]]), 41 CSCC patients with an indication for extensive or disfiguring curative surgery will be treated with neoadjuvant NIVO (weeks 0 & 2) and IPI (only week 0). Response evaluation is performed in week 5. In case of a non-response, patients undergo SOC in week 6. There was no delay in surgery due to immune-related toxicity.

So far, 48% of patients reached a CCR following only ultra-short neoadjuvant NIVO/IPI, without SOC and without maintenance immunotherapy. Longer follow-up is required to investigate further deepening of the response in PCR patients and the durability of the response in CCR patients.

## 🫁 Thoracic / Lung

Consolidation RT in synchronous oligometastatic NSCLC: PFS and local control gain without OS benefit, on a mixed chemo/IO backbone.

### SARON · [[NCT02417662]]

> Consolidation RT added to 1L SACT: mOS 31.0 vs 28.0mo (HR 0.99, p=0.95) despite mPFS 13.9 vs 8.9mo (HR 0.64) in synchronous oligometastatic AGA-negative NSCLC.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Pattern of failure is the RT read: 12-mo local failure fell 39.5% to 13.0%, but new distant mets ran 36.2% vs 32.3%. Comprehensive RT controls what it treats without changing systemic seeding, so consolidation in AGA-negative oligometastatic NSCLC is a PFS and local-control decision, not a survival claim.

- 📊 SACT+RT vs SACT alone, median f/u 37.4mo

  | Endpoint | SACT+RT | SACT | Effect |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | mOS (primary) | 31.0 mo | 28.0 mo | HR 0.99 (95% CI 0.63-1.55), p=0.95 |
  | mPFS | 13.9 mo | 8.9 mo | HR 0.64 (95% CI 0.43-0.95), p=0.03 |
  | 12-mo cumulative incidence, local failure | 13.0% | 39.5% | p<0.01 |
  | 12-mo cumulative incidence, new distant mets | 36.2% | 32.3% | p=0.28 |
  | G≥3 AE rate | 28.6% | 28.6% | n/a |

- 🔍 Randomised (1:1) phase II, N=140, 22 UK centres, accrual Oct 2016 to Jul 2023
- 🔍 1-5 metastases in ≤3 organs, AGA-negative; randomised after initial SACT with CR/PR/SD
- 🔍 RT delivered: 62/70 (88.6%) in experimental arm
  - 51 conventional radical RT, 11 SABR
  - 54 received SABR to ≥1 OM site
  - Radical RT to lung primary ± locoregional nodes
  - Dose/fractionation not reported in source
- 🔍 62.9% had 1 OM site; 52.9% N2/3; 6.4% brain mets
- 💊 SACT: 40.0% chemo, 15.0% IO, 45.0% chemo-IO; control arm added IO Jan 2020
- 📊 6 pts had G3 events possibly RT-related; QLQ-C30/LC13 QoL similar between arms
- 🔗 vs Gomez LCT phase II: OS 41.2 vs 17.0mo there, but n=49 and pre-IO era
- ⚠️ OS-powered with N=140 in phase II: a modest OS effect cannot be excluded (CI to 0.63)
- ⚠️ Mixed systemic backbone across eras; only 60% received any IO
- ⚠️ Post-progression salvage RT in control arm not reported in source; may dilute OS

- 📄 **Primary results of SARON: A randomised-controlled trial of consolidation radiotherapy for synchronous oligometastatic non-small cell lung cancer** — Fiona McDonald. *ESMO Congress 2026* (2026-10). [article](https://cslide.ctimeetingtech.com/esmo2026/attendee/confcal/presentation/list)
  > ESMO Congress 2026 abstract 2613O · Proffered paper 1: NSCLC, metastatic · 2026-10-25 · presented by Fiona McDonald (London, United Kingdom) · [[NCT02417662]]
  > Background: Standard first-line systemic anti-cancer therapy (SACT) for advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) without actionable genomic alteration (AGA-negative) is chemotherapy and/or immunotherapy. We investigated whether patients with synchronous oligometastatic (OM) AGA-negative NSCLC benefit from the addition of consolidation radiotherapy (RT).
  > Methods: SARON, a multicentre phase II trial, randomised (1:1) patients with NSCLC, 1–5 metastases in ≤3 organs, to SACT with or without radical RT. The control arm changed to include immunotherapy in January 2020. The experimental arm received radical RT to the lung primary ± locoregional lymph nodes, and stereotactic ablative RT (SABR) to OM sites. Stratification included SACT, histology, N-stage, number of metastases, brain metastases and recruiting site. Primary and major secondary endpoints were overall (OS) and progression-free survival (PFS).
  > Results: 140 patients were randomised across 22 UK centres (October 2016 to July 2023). Median age was 68 years (range 45–85), 59.3% male, 58.6% adenocarcinoma, 52.9% N-stage 2/3, 62.9% 1x OM site (23.6% 2x, 9.3% 3x, 3.6% 4x, 0.7% 5x OM sites), and 6.4% brain metastases. SACT was 40.0% chemotherapy, 15.0% immunotherapy and 45.0% combination. Median SACT cycles received in both arms was 4, and 62/70 (88.6%) received RT (51 conventional and 11 SABR to primary, 54 SABR to ≥1 OM site). After 37.4 months median follow-up, median OS was 31.0 versus 28.0 months (HR=0.99, 95%CI 0.63–1.55, p=0.95) and median PFS 13.9 versus 8.9 months (HR=0.64, 95%CI 0.43–0.95, p=0.03) in the SACT+RT and SACT arms, respectively. The cumulative incidence of local failure within 12 months was 13.0% versus 39.5% (p<0.01), compared to 36.2% versus 32.3% (p=0.28) for new distant metastasis, respectively. The grade ≥3 adverse event rate was 28.6% in both arms; 6 patients had a grade 3 event possibly related to RT. EORTC QLQ-C30 and LC13 quality of life measures were similar in both arms.
  > Conclusions: Patients with synchronous OM AGA-negative NSCLC receiving first-line SACT+RT achieved better local disease control and longer PFS than SACT alone with acceptable side-effects, however, this did not translate into survival benefit.
  > Clinical trial identification: [[NCT02417662]].

> [!question] Open questions
> - Does consolidation RT add OS on a uniform chemo-IO backbone?
> - Can ctDNA or systemic response select pts who gain OS from consolidation?
> - How much did control-arm salvage RT compress the OS difference?

## 🍽️ GI Upper

Concurrent pembro with dCRT fails in unresectable esophageal/GEJ cancer; dCRT backbone unchanged.

### KEYNOTE-975 · [[NCT04210115]]

> EFS HR 0.97 (95% CI 0.77-1.23), median 15.2 vs 14.7 mo in PD-L1 CPS ≥10: no EFS gain adding concurrent pembro to dCRT in unresectable esophageal/GEJ cancer.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The trial failed in the enriched PD-L1 CPS ≥10 group, where IO was most likely to help, and the curves sat on top of each other (EFS HR 0.97, OS HR 1.00) at 47.1 mo median f/u. Grade 5 AEs ran 13% vs 10%, so concurrent pembro with 50-60 Gy dCRT adds toxicity without benefit. This leaves dCRT alone as the backbone.

- 📊 Dual-primary endpoints in PD-L1 CPS ≥10 at IA2 (pembro + dCRT vs pbo + dCRT)

  | Endpoint (CPS ≥10) | Pembro + dCRT | Pbo + dCRT | HR (95% CI), P |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Median EFS (investigator) | 15.2 mo | 14.7 mo | 0.97 (0.77-1.23), P=0.3994; boundary 0.0193 |
  | Median OS | 32.2 mo | 35.0 mo | 1.00 (0.77-1.30), nominal P=0.5003 |

- 📐 Hierarchical testing: EFS in CPS ≥10 failed, so CPS ≥1, all-pt EFS and OS went untested
- 🔍 Study stopped after IA2; median f/u 47.1 mo (range 21.7-70.2), data cut-off Jan 21, 2026
- 💊 Pembro 200 mg Q3W ×8 cycles, then 400 mg Q6W ×5 cycles, concurrent with dCRT
- 🔍 dCRT backbone by site choice
  - 5-FU + cisplatin with RT 50 Gy
  - 5-FU + cisplatin with RT 60 Gy
  - FOLFOX with RT 50 Gy
  - Fractionation and target volume not reported in source
- 📊 Safety (pembro + dCRT vs pbo + dCRT)

  | AE | Pembro + dCRT | Pbo + dCRT |
  |---|---|---|
  | Grade ≥3 | 100% | 100% |
  | Grade 5 | 44 (13%) | 37 (10%) |
  | Discontinuation for any AE | 117 (34%) | 97 (27%) |

- ⚠️ RT dose (50 vs 60 Gy) and chemo backbone were by site choice; no outcome split by dose in source
- ⚠️ ESCC and EAC/GEJ pooled; histology-specific results not reported in source
- ⚠️ No local control or patterns-of-failure data in source
- 🔗 Concurrent IO from CRT start, not PACIFIC-style consolidation after CRT; timing question untested here

- 📄 **Pembrolizumab or placebo plus concurrent definitive chemoradiotherapy in esophageal and gastroesophageal junction (GEJ) cancer: The phase 3 randomized KEYNOTE-975 study** — Manish A. Shah. *ESMO Congress 2026* (2026-10). [article](https://cslide.ctimeetingtech.com/esmo2026/attendee/confcal/presentation/list)
  > ESMO Congress 2026 abstract 2875O · Proffered paper 1: Gastrointestinal tumour, upper digestive · 2026-10-23 · presented by Manish A. Shah (New York, United States of America) · [[NCT04210115]]
  > Background: The role of immunotherapy in participants (pts) with locally advanced, esophageal cancer (EC) treated with definitive chemoradiotherapy (dCRT) is not established. KEYNOTE-975 ([[NCT04210115]]) is an international phase 3, randomized, double-blind, placebo (pbo) controlled multicenter study that examines dCRT ± pembro in pts with previously untreated locally advanced unresectable esophageal and GEJ cancer. We report results from the second interim analysis (IA2).
  > Methods: Eligible pts with histologically confirmed cTX N+ M0 or cT2-T4a NX M0 esophageal squamous cell cancer (ESCC) or adenocarcinoma (EAC) or GEJ cancer, or cTX N+ M1 cervical or upper thoracic EC with supraclavicular lymph node metastases only, ECOG PS 0-1, and who were ineligible for curative surgery but suitable for dCRT, were randomized 1:1 to 200 mg pembro or pbo Q3W for 8 cycles then 400 mg pembro or pbo Q6W for 5 cycles + site choice of dCRT (5-fluorouracil + cisplatin [FP] with radiotherapy [RT] 50 Gy or 60 Gy, or FOLFOX with RT 50 Gy). Dual-primary endpoints were event-free survival (EFS) by investigator and OS in pts with PD-L1 combined positive score (CPS) ≥10, CPS ≥1, and all pts. Data cut-off was Jan 21, 2026.
  > Results: A total of 703 pts were randomized (349 pembro + dCRT; 354 pbo + dCRT). Median follow-up was 47.1 mo (range, 21.7-70.2) in all pts. At IA2, in pts with PD-L1 CPS ≥10, EFS was not improved with pembro + dCRT vs pbo + dCRT (median 15.2 vs 14.7 mo; HR 0.97; 95% CI, 0.77-1.23 [P=0.3994; P-value boundary for significance 0.0193). In pts with PD-L1 CPS ≥10, median OS was 32.2 mo vs 35.0 mo (HR 1.00; 95% CI, 0.77-1.30 [nominal P-value 0.5003]). As EFS was not positive in PD-L1 CPS ≥10, EFS in PD-L1 CPS ≥1 and all pts, and OS were not tested for statistical significance, and the study was stopped. Grade ≥3 AEs occurred in 100% of pts in each arm, with grade 5 AEs in 44 (13%) vs 37 (10%) pts. Discontinuation due to any AE occurred in 117 (34%) vs 97 (27%) pts.
  > Conclusions: Pembrolizumab + dCRT vs pbo + dCRT did not significantly improve EFS in untreated unresectable esophageal or GEJ cancer with PD-L1 CPS ≥10. No new safety signals were observed.
  > Clinical trial identification: [[NCT04210115]].
