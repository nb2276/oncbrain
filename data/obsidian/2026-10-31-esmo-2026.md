# 2026-10-31 — ESMO Congress 2026

**KEYNOTE-975: pembro + dCRT EFS 15.2 vs 14.7 mo, HR 0.97 (0.77-1.23) in CPS ≥10 locally advanced unresectable esophageal/GEJ cancer; trial stopped, no IO add-on to dCRT.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Two RT readouts, no OS gain. KEYNOTE-975 adds no EFS with pembro to dCRT in CPS ≥10, plus more grade 5 AEs; SARON consolidation RT in synchronous OM AGA-negative NSCLC cut 12-mo local failure 13.0% vs 39.5% and improved PFS (HR 0.64), OS HR 0.99. MATISSE 2 interim: 71% skip surgery ± adjuvant RT after NIVO/IPI in CSCC.

## 🌞 Skin / Melanoma

Ultra-short neoadjuvant IO in CSCC raises surgery and adjuvant RT omission, on 8-mo f/u only.

### MATISSE 2 · [[NCT06823479]]

> 71% (22/31) clinical response at week 5 with SOC withheld, 48% CCR, after ultra-short neoadjuvant NIVO/IPI in CSCC; median f/u 8 mo.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The SOC being withheld here is surgery with or without adjuvant RT, so 22/31 (71%) responders skipped both, with 7/31 (23%) still only partial responders under surveillance. Partial responders carry the local-failure risk that would land on salvage surgery or RT, and 8 mo median f/u cannot yet show how many convert or progress.

- 🔍 CSCC with indication for extensive or disfiguring curative surgery; 41 planned, 31 included so far
- 💊 NIVO weeks 0 & 2 + IPI week 0 only; no maintenance IO
- 🔍 Response evaluated week 5; non-responders proceed to SOC week 6
- 🔍 Clinical responders skip SOC (surgery ± adjuvant RT) → strict wait-and-monitoring
- 📊 Response distribution (n=31)

  | Outcome | n/N | % |
  |---|---|---|
  | Clinical response, SOC withheld | 22/31 | 71% |
  | CCR | 15/31 | 48% |
  | PCR | 7/31 | 23% |
  | Received SOC | 9/31 | 29% |

- 📊 1 of 9 SOC pts had complete pathological response in surgical specimen
- 📊 Grade 3-4 irAEs 19%; no surgical delay from immune toxicity
- 🔗 Builds on MATISSE ([[NCT04620200]]): 80% partial/major pathological response after 2 courses NIVO/IPI, 9 CCRs
- ⚠️ Median f/u 8 mo; durability of CCR without surgery or RT unknown
- ⚠️ CCR is a clinical, not pathological, endpoint; no biopsy confirmation reported
- ⚠️ Interim report, 31 of 41 planned pts; no comparator arm

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Towards organ preservation and cure via immunotherapy in cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma patients, normally undergoing extensive curative surgery and radiotherapy (MATISSE 2)** — Stan W. Van Dijk. *ESMO Congress 2026* (2026-10). [article](https://cslide.ctimeetingtech.com/esmo2026/attendee/confcal/presentation/list)
  > ESMO Congress 2026 abstract 2486RO · Rapid oral: Melanoma and other skin tumours · 2026-10-25 · presented by Stan W. Van Dijk (Amsterdam, Netherlands) · [[NCT06823479]]
  > Background: The MATISSE trial ([[NCT04620200]]) showed that 80% of patients with a cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma (CSCC) and an indication for extensive curative surgery with or without adjuvant radiotherapy as standard of care (SOC) reached a partial or major pathological response following two courses of neoadjuvant nivolumab and ipilimumab (NIVO/IPI). In addition, 9 patients reached a clinical complete remission (CCR) after only two courses of NIVO/IPI, without SOC. This raised the question whether organ preservation and cure can be reached via only ultra-short neoadjuvant NIVO/IPI, without SOC and without maintenance immunotherapy.
  > Methods: In the MATISSE 2 trial ([[NCT06823479]]), 41 CSCC patients with an indication for extensive or disfiguring curative surgery will be treated with neoadjuvant NIVO (weeks 0 & 2) and IPI (only week 0). Response evaluation is performed in week 5. In case of a non-response, patients undergo SOC in week 6. In case of a clinical response following NIVO/IPI, we will withhold SOC and patients enter a strict wait-and-monitoring programme.
  > Results: So far, thirty-one patients have been included (median follow-up of 8 months). Overall, 22/31 (71%) patients showed a clinical response in week 5 and did not undergo SOC per protocol, of whom 15/31 (48%) achieved a CCR and 7/31 (23%) achieved a partial clinical response (PCR) during their wait-and-monitoring program. In total 9/31 (29%) patients received SOC, of whom one patient showed a complete pathological response in the surgical specimen. Immune-related toxicity (19% grade 3-4) was well manageable. There was no delay in surgery due to immune-related toxicity.
  > Conclusions: Overall, 71% of patients reached a clinical response and did not undergo surgery. So far, 48% of patients reached a CCR following only ultra-short neoadjuvant NIVO/IPI, without SOC and without maintenance immunotherapy. Longer follow-up is required to investigate further deepening of the response in PCR patients and the durability of the response in CCR patients.
  > Clinical trial identification: [[NCT06823479]] EU trial nr 2024-516152-17-00.

> [!question] Open questions
> - Durability of CCR without surgery, RT, or maintenance IO
> - Do partial clinical responders deepen or recur locally?
> - Salvage success after local failure under wait-and-monitoring

### [212Pb]VMT-α-NET · [[NCT05636618]]

> No DLTs, G4/5 AEs, or serious renal events across 67 pts; ORR 43.4% in a cohort 2 subset (n=23/46), SSTR2+ progressive NETs.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The dosimetry-limiting organs read clean so far: no serious renal events and no clinically significant treatment-related myelosuppression across 67 pts through the 6 mCi level, with escalation continuing into Cohort 4. The response figure is the 43.4% from 23 cohort 2 pts; the 56-pt efficacy set is not reported in source.

- 🔍 Phase 1/2a dose escalation, alpha-emitting SSTR2-targeted radiopharmaceutical
- 🔍 Unresectable/metastatic NETs, radiologic progression after ≥1 prior systemic line, SSTR2+ on FDA-approved SSTR PET
- 💊 Up to 4 administrations at assigned dose level; DLT window 42 days
- 🔍 Enrollment by dose cohort (data cut 03Apr2026)

  | Cohort | Dose | Treated (n) | Efficacy set (n) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Cohort 1 | 2.5 mCi | 2 | 2 |
  | Cohort 2 | 5 mCi | 46 | 46 |
  | Cohort 3 | 6 mCi | 19 | 8 |

- 📊 Safety, all 67 treated pts: none observed of
  - DLTs
  - Grade 4 or 5 AEs
  - Treatment-related discontinuations
  - Serious renal events
  - Dysphagia
  - Clinically significant treatment-related myelosuppression
- 📊 Cohort 2 ORR 43.4% (RECIST v1.1, investigator-assessed), previously reported subset n=23/46, f/u 60.1 wks
- 📊 Cohort 3: 2 PRs after 1 and 2 cycles; both Cohort 1 pts SD at 2 yrs
- ⚠️ Full 56-pt efficacy set results not given in abstract; ORR comes from the earlier subset only
- ⚠️ Investigator-assessed response, no central review stated; no PFS or DoR reported in source
- 🔗 Context: 177Lu-DOTATATE (NETTER-1) is the beta-emitter SSTR2 benchmark; no head-to-head data here

## 🫁 Thoracic / Lung

Consolidation RT in synchronous OM NSCLC buys local control and PFS, but new distant mets still drive OS.

### SARON · [[NCT02417662]]

> Consolidation RT added to 1L SACT: mOS 31.0 vs 28.0 mo, HR 0.99 (p=0.95); mPFS 13.9 vs 8.9 mo, HR 0.64 in synchronous OM AGA-negative NSCLC.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The RT effect is confined to treated sites: 12-mo local failure 13.0% vs 39.5%, while new distant metastasis was 36.2% vs 32.3% (p=0.28). Consolidation RT to primary plus SABR to all OM sites buys PFS (HR 0.64) at no added G≥3 toxicity or QoL cost, so the consent conversation shifts from survival to durable site control.

- 📊 Endpoint OS not met: HR 0.99 (95% CI 0.63–1.55), p=0.95
- 📊 PFS improved: HR 0.64 (95% CI 0.43–0.95), p=0.03
- 📊 Outcomes by arm, 37.4 mo median f/u

  | Endpoint | SACT+RT | SACT alone | Effect |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | mOS | 31.0 mo | 28.0 mo | HR 0.99 (0.63–1.55), p=0.95 |
  | mPFS | 13.9 mo | 8.9 mo | HR 0.64 (0.43–0.95), p=0.03 |
  | 12-mo cumulative local failure | 13.0% | 39.5% | p<0.01 |
  | 12-mo new distant mets | 36.2% | 32.3% | p=0.28 |
  | G≥3 AE | 28.6% | 28.6% | n/a |

- 🔍 Randomised (1:1) multicentre phase II, N=140, 22 UK centres, Oct 2016 to Jul 2023
- 🔍 1–5 synchronous mets in ≤3 organs; randomised after ~6–8 wk SACT with CR/PR/SD
- ☢️ RT delivery: 62/70 (88.6%) of the RT arm received RT
  - 51 conventional radical RT, 11 SABR
  - 54 SABR to ≥1 OM site
  - Primary ± locoregional nodes in field; dose/fractionation not reported in source
- 💊 SACT: 40.0% chemo, 15.0% IO, 45.0% chemo-IO; control arm added IO from Jan 2020
- ⚠️ RT controlled treated sites but did not reduce new distant mets; systemic relapse drove OS
- ⚠️ Mixed-era systemic backbone; cannot isolate RT effect on a uniform chemo-IO population
- ⚠️ Phase II size for an OS primary; post-progression salvage RT and subsequent therapy not reported in source
- 📊 6 pts had G3 events possibly RT-related; EORTC QLQ-C30/LC13 QoL similar between arms
- 🔗 Gomez phase II (49 pts, pre-IO era) reported mOS 41.2 vs 17.0 mo; SARON does not reproduce the OS signal

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Primary results of SARON: A randomised-controlled trial of consolidation radiotherapy for synchronous oligometastatic non-small cell lung cancer** — Fiona McDonald. *ESMO Congress 2026* (2026-10). [article](https://cslide.ctimeetingtech.com/esmo2026/attendee/confcal/presentation/list)
  > ESMO Congress 2026 abstract 2613O · Proffered paper 1: NSCLC, metastatic · 2026-10-25 · presented by Fiona McDonald (London, United Kingdom) · [[NCT02417662]]
  > Background: Standard first-line systemic anti-cancer therapy (SACT) for advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) without actionable genomic alteration (AGA-negative) is chemotherapy and/or immunotherapy. We investigated whether patients with synchronous oligometastatic (OM) AGA-negative NSCLC benefit from the addition of consolidation radiotherapy (RT).
  > Methods: SARON, a multicentre phase II trial, randomised (1:1) patients with NSCLC, 1–5 metastases in ≤3 organs, to SACT with or without radical RT. The control arm changed to include immunotherapy in January 2020. The experimental arm received radical RT to the lung primary ± locoregional lymph nodes, and stereotactic ablative RT (SABR) to OM sites. Stratification included SACT, histology, N-stage, number of metastases, brain metastases and recruiting site. Primary and major secondary endpoints were overall (OS) and progression-free survival (PFS).
  > Results: 140 patients were randomised across 22 UK centres (October 2016 to July 2023). Median age was 68 years (range 45–85), 59.3% male, 58.6% adenocarcinoma, 52.9% N-stage 2/3, 62.9% 1x OM site (23.6% 2x, 9.3% 3x, 3.6% 4x, 0.7% 5x OM sites), and 6.4% brain metastases. SACT was 40.0% chemotherapy, 15.0% immunotherapy and 45.0% combination. Median SACT cycles received in both arms was 4, and 62/70 (88.6%) received RT (51 conventional and 11 SABR to primary, 54 SABR to ≥1 OM site). After 37.4 months median follow-up, median OS was 31.0 versus 28.0 months (HR=0.99, 95%CI 0.63–1.55, p=0.95) and median PFS 13.9 versus 8.9 months (HR=0.64, 95%CI 0.43–0.95, p=0.03) in the SACT+RT and SACT arms, respectively. The cumulative incidence of local failure within 12 months was 13.0% versus 39.5% (p<0.01), compared to 36.2% versus 32.3% (p=0.28) for new distant metastasis, respectively. The grade ≥3 adverse event rate was 28.6% in both arms; 6 patients had a grade 3 event possibly related to RT. EORTC QLQ-C30 and LC13 quality of life measures were similar in both arms.
  > Conclusions: Patients with synchronous OM AGA-negative NSCLC receiving first-line SACT+RT achieved better local disease control and longer PFS than SACT alone with acceptable side-effects, however, this did not translate into survival benefit.
  > Clinical trial identification: [[NCT02417662]].

## 🍽️ GI Upper

Phase 3 null for concurrent/maintenance pembro with dCRT; RT dose by arm not reported in source.

### KEYNOTE-975 · [[NCT04210115]]

> EFS 15.2 vs 14.7 mo, HR 0.97 (0.77-1.23) in PD-L1 CPS ≥10; adding pembro to dCRT failed, and the trial was stopped.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The toxicity signal is the RT-relevant read: grade 5 AEs 13% vs 10% and AE discontinuation 34% vs 27% with pembro, with EFS HR 0.97 and OS HR 1.00 in the enriched CPS ≥10 group. For dCRT at 50 or 60 Gy, concurrent PD-1 blockade adds harm without local or systemic control gain.

- 🔍 Phase 3, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multicenter; N=703 (349 pembro + dCRT vs 354 pbo + dCRT)
- 🔍 Untreated locally advanced unresectable ESCC, EAC or GEJ cancer; ineligible for curative surgery, ECOG PS 0-1
- 🔍 Eligible stage groups
  - cTX N+ M0 or cT2-T4a NX M0
  - cTX N+ M1 cervical/upper thoracic EC with supraclavicular LN mets only
- 💊 Regimen (randomized 1:1)
  - Pembro 200 mg or pbo Q3W x8 cycles, then 400 mg or pbo Q6W x5 cycles
  - dCRT by site choice: FP + RT 50 Gy or 60 Gy, or FOLFOX + RT 50 Gy
- 📊 Results at IA2 (data cut-off Jan 21, 2026; median f/u 47.1 mo)

  | Endpoint | Pembro + dCRT | Pbo + dCRT | Effect |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Median EFS, CPS ≥10 | 15.2 mo | 14.7 mo | HR 0.97 (95% CI 0.77-1.23), P=0.3994 |
  | Median OS, CPS ≥10 | 32.2 mo | 35.0 mo | HR 1.00 (95% CI 0.77-1.30), nominal P=0.5003 |
  | Grade ≥3 AEs | 100% | 100% | n/a |
  | Grade 5 AEs | 44 (13%) | 37 (10%) | n/a |
  | Discontinuation for AE | 117 (34%) | 97 (27%) | n/a |

- 📐 EFS significance boundary at IA2 was P=0.0193; observed P=0.3994
- ⚠️ EFS in CPS ≥1 and all pts, and OS, never formally tested (hierarchical stop)
- ⚠️ More grade 5 AEs and AE discontinuations with pembro, with no efficacy offset
- ⚠️ RT dose (50 vs 60 Gy) and fractionation not reported by arm; outcomes by histology not in source

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Pembrolizumab or placebo plus concurrent definitive chemoradiotherapy in esophageal and gastroesophageal junction (GEJ) cancer: The phase 3 randomized KEYNOTE-975 study** — Manish A. Shah. *ESMO Congress 2026* (2026-10). [article](https://cslide.ctimeetingtech.com/esmo2026/attendee/confcal/presentation/list)
  > ESMO Congress 2026 abstract 2875O · Proffered paper 1: Gastrointestinal tumour, upper digestive · 2026-10-23 · presented by Manish A. Shah (New York, United States of America) · [[NCT04210115]]
  > Background: The role of immunotherapy in participants (pts) with locally advanced, esophageal cancer (EC) treated with definitive chemoradiotherapy (dCRT) is not established. KEYNOTE-975 ([[NCT04210115]]) is an international phase 3, randomized, double-blind, placebo (pbo) controlled multicenter study that examines dCRT ± pembro in pts with previously untreated locally advanced unresectable esophageal and GEJ cancer. We report results from the second interim analysis (IA2).
  > Methods: Eligible pts with histologically confirmed cTX N+ M0 or cT2-T4a NX M0 esophageal squamous cell cancer (ESCC) or adenocarcinoma (EAC) or GEJ cancer, or cTX N+ M1 cervical or upper thoracic EC with supraclavicular lymph node metastases only, ECOG PS 0-1, and who were ineligible for curative surgery but suitable for dCRT, were randomized 1:1 to 200 mg pembro or pbo Q3W for 8 cycles then 400 mg pembro or pbo Q6W for 5 cycles + site choice of dCRT (5-fluorouracil + cisplatin [FP] with radiotherapy [RT] 50 Gy or 60 Gy, or FOLFOX with RT 50 Gy). Dual-primary endpoints were event-free survival (EFS) by investigator and OS in pts with PD-L1 combined positive score (CPS) ≥10, CPS ≥1, and all pts. Data cut-off was Jan 21, 2026.
  > Results: A total of 703 pts were randomized (349 pembro + dCRT; 354 pbo + dCRT). Median follow-up was 47.1 mo (range, 21.7-70.2) in all pts. At IA2, in pts with PD-L1 CPS ≥10, EFS was not improved with pembro + dCRT vs pbo + dCRT (median 15.2 vs 14.7 mo; HR 0.97; 95% CI, 0.77-1.23 [P=0.3994; P-value boundary for significance 0.0193). In pts with PD-L1 CPS ≥10, median OS was 32.2 mo vs 35.0 mo (HR 1.00; 95% CI, 0.77-1.30 [nominal P-value 0.5003]). As EFS was not positive in PD-L1 CPS ≥10, EFS in PD-L1 CPS ≥1 and all pts, and OS were not tested for statistical significance, and the study was stopped. Grade ≥3 AEs occurred in 100% of pts in each arm, with grade 5 AEs in 44 (13%) vs 37 (10%) pts. Discontinuation due to any AE occurred in 117 (34%) vs 97 (27%) pts.
  > Conclusions: Pembrolizumab + dCRT vs pbo + dCRT did not significantly improve EFS in untreated unresectable esophageal or GEJ cancer with PD-L1 CPS ≥10. No new safety signals were observed.
  > Clinical trial identification: [[NCT04210115]].

> [!question] Open questions
> - Does histology (ESCC vs EAC) modify any IO effect with dCRT?
> - Would IO given after dCRT, not concurrently, change the result?
> - What drove the excess grade 5 events with pembro?
