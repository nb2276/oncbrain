# 2026-05-30 — ASCO Annual Meeting 2026

**Neo-CRAG: preop 45Gy/25fx added to periop XELOX lifts 3yr DFS 55.6% vs 42.4% (HR 0.750) and halves LRR, 9.4% vs 18.3%.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> RT owned the day's positive randomised signals: Neo-CRAG is the first phase 3 to make preop chemoRT pay in node-heavy gastric/EGJ where CRITICS and TOPGEAR were null, and ROADS puts surgical-bed recurrence at 1% with GammaTile vs 12% post-op SRS. Prostate carried the biomarker news (TALAPRO-3, ENZAMET-Decipher), all systemic.

## 💧 Bladder

Trimodality is being pressured from both sides: RAD-IO adds durvalumab to 55Gy/20fr chemoRT with RT delivery intact, while EV-309 puts chemoradiation in the control arm against an EV-only bladder-sparing pathway.

### RAD-IO

> 12-mo DFS 40/50 (80%, 95% CI 0.67-0.89) with durvalumab added to 5FU/MMC chemoRT, clearing the pre-set GO bar (≥75%).

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The RT is unchanged from standard UK practice (55Gy/20fr bladder, 46Gy/20fr nodes when N+), so nothing here alters target volume or dose. What is new is that durvalumab was delivered neoadjuvant, synchronous AND adjuvant around that backbone, and only 61% completed it, which is the number gating any future randomised trial design.

- 🔍 ASCO 2026 GU education session (Brendan J. Guercio, MD) on managing MIBC after pathologic complete response, not a new trial report
- 📊 pCR (pT0N0) after neoadjuvant chemo is a prognostic marker: SWOG 8710 reported 85% 5-yr OS if pT0
- 📐 Meta-analysis pooled RR 0.19 for RFS in the pCR group (per slide 1)
- 🔍 Standard of care is framed by cisplatin eligibility, with immunotherapy-based sandwich regimens reshaping practice
- 🔍 Perioperative regimens by cisplatin eligibility (as presented)
  - Cisplatin-ineligible: KEYNOTE-905 periop EVP, 3 cycles EVP → cystectomy → 6 cycles EV + 14 cycles pembro
  - Cisplatin-ineligible: VOLGA preoperative EV + periop durvalumab/tremelimumab, EFS improvement by press release only
  - Cisplatin-eligible: NIAGARA pre-op gem/cis + periop durvalumab, 24-mo OS 82.2% vs 75.2%
  - Cisplatin-eligible: VESPER established ddMVAC over gem/cis, 5-yr OS 66% vs 57%, greater toxicity
  - Cisplatin-eligible: KEYNOTE-B15 periop EVP improved OS vs gem/cis, under FDA review
- 📊 KEYNOTE-905 slide reports median OS 41.7 mo and hazard ratio 0.50, p<0.001 (OCR partial, CI not legible)
- 📊 Post-pCR practice today: resume durvalumab for another 8 months after cystectomy on the NIAGARA regimen; resume EVP after cystectomy on periop EVP
- 📊 After cisplatin-based chemo alone, surveillance after pCR is standard
- ⚠️ Not all pts in the perioperative trials completed adjuvant therapy, sometimes due to toxicity, so the trials do not cleanly test the full planned course
- ⚠️ No trial has randomized post-pCR continuation vs stopping; current practice is extrapolated from intention-to-treat perioperative designs
- ⚠️ Bladder preservation / trimodality therapy is not addressed in these slides; the entire framing is cystectomy-based

## 🌰 Prostate

A biomarker-heavy day (Decipher, 22-gene GC, HRR) where RT+ADT is the assumed backbone and only the systemic intensification varies; A-DREAM tests stopping ADT+ARPI outright.

### A-DREAM

> 41% treatment-free with eugonadal T at 18mo after stopping ADT+ARPI in responding mHSPC (80% CI 33.1-48.9%, one-sided p 0.0249).

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The RT-relevant detail is baseline burden: 51.3% had prostate RT and 29.5% had RT to metastatic sites, so the cohort that tolerated interruption was heavily locally treated, and 4 pts (5.1%) took RT as their non-protocol next step off ADT. Metastasis-directed RT is not tested here, but this is the population where a de-intensification question meets it.

- 🔍 Phase 2 single-arm, Alliance, N=78 eligible, enrolled 07/2022-03/2024; planned N=75
- 🔍 Entry gate: mHSPC on ADT+ARPI, PSA <0.2 (stable/falling) after 18-24mo ADT with ≥12mo ARPI
- 📊 1° EP treatment-free for 18mo WITH eugonadal T (≥150 ng/dL): 32/78 (41.0%)
- 📐 80% CI 33.1-48.9%, one-sided p 0.0249
- 📊 Disposition components at 26.9mo median f/u
  - T recovery (≥150) by 18mo: 52 (66.7%)
  - Treatment-free for 18mo: 45 (57.7%)
  - Both (1° EP): 32 (41.0%)
  - Median time to eugonadal T: 9.0mo (95% CI 8.4-12.4)
- 📊 Where pts went after interruption
  - Resumed ADT+initial ARPI per protocol criteria: 29 (37.2%)
  - Continuing on interruption: 30 (38.5%)
  - Resumed before meeting criteria: 5 (6.4%)
  - Started non-protocol treatment: 7 (9.0%), incl RT 4 (5.1%)
- 📊 rPFS 15/78 events, median NE; OS 4/78 events, median NE
- 🔍 Resumption triggers were protocolized: PSA ≥5 ng/mL, PCWG3 radiographic change, or PrCa-related sx
- ⚠️ Single-arm with no continuous-treatment comparator, so the OS/rPFS curves cannot speak to the safety of interruption
- ⚠️ Enriched population: only deep responders (PSA <0.2 after 18-24mo) entered, so 41% does not generalize to unselected mHSPC
- ⚠️ 4 of 29 pts who resumed per protocol later progressed (2 confirmed rPD, 1 PDu, 1 by PET) and came off the original ARPI
- 🔗 RT was used in this cohort at baseline (51.3% prostate RT, 29.5% RT to metastatic sites) and as non-protocol salvage in 4 pts, but no RT question was randomized

### Precision Oncology: New Biomarkers in GU Practice (Panel)

> Discussant session on biomarker readiness in prostate cancer; discordance between Decipher and clinical risk emerged in 24% of events.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The transferable point for an RT reader is the training-context caveat: the Decipher-type classifiers cited were developed in cohorts that did not use ARPIs, and only 427 of 3816 pts had tissue. ### ARACOG (AFT-47)

> Enzalutamide MCCD decline -36.1 vs -15.8 for darolutamide at 24wks (P=0.009) in randomized phase 2, N=111.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The comparison is between each pt's own worst-hit domain, and those differed by arm (PALFAM for daro, SWM for enza), so the read is how far the worst domain falls, not which one. Crossover before 24wks was scored at crossover inside the randomized arm, which attenuates rather than widens the gap. Moves ARSI selection when cognitive burden matters, not efficacy.

Dose was conventional 45Gy/25fx preoperatively, and G3+ postop complications stayed flat (9.0% vs 7.6%), so the deliverability objection to preop RT before D2 loses force.

| Endpoint | CRT | CT | Effect size |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3yr DFS | 55.6% (50.1-61.1) | 42.4% (36.9-47.9) | HR 0.750 (0.607-0.928), P=0.008 |
| Median DFS | 52.7 mo | 24.4 mo | n/a |
| 5yr OS | 50.1% | 44.2% | HR 0.781 (0.628-0.970), P=0.025 |
| Median OS | 67.5 mo | 37.6 mo | n/a |

- 🔍 Ph3 RCT, N=620 (310/arm), 13 Chinese centers, enrolled 2013-2022; 225 (36.3%) EGJ primaries
- 🔍 High-risk LAGC only: cT3N2-3M0, cT4aN+M0, cT4bNanyM0 gastric/EGJ (Siewert II/III) adenoCA
- 🔍 RT was 45 Gy/25 fx concurrent after C1 of preop chemo, with reduced-dose oxaliplatin 100 mg/m² + capecitabine 825 mg/m²
- 📊 LRR after R0 resection 20/213 (9.4%) CRT vs 38/208 (18.3%) CT, the locoregional signal behind the DFS curve
- 📊 Pathologic response after D2 gastrectomy (448 resected)
- 📐 5yr OS 50.1% vs 44.2%, HR 0.781 (0.628-0.970), P=0.025
- 📊 Toxicity, no excess surgical morbidity from RT
  - G3+ heme: 44/302 (14.6%) CRT vs 31/301 (10.3%) CT
  - G3+ postop complications: 20/223 (9.0%) vs 17/225 (7.6%)
- ⚠️ Backbone is XELOX, not FLOT; control mOS 37.6mo sits below modern FLOT expectations, so the RT delta may shrink against a current standard
- ⚠️ mOS gap (67.5 vs 37.6mo) is far wider than the OS HR 0.781 implies; single-country, all-D2 population limits transfer
- 🔗 vs CRITICS and TOPGEAR, both null for adding RT to periop chemo; Neo-CRAG differs by restricting to node-heavy cT3N2+ disease and by giving RT preoperatively with D2 in every pt

## 🤝 Supportive / QoL

SPIN scores who responds to celiac plexus SRS, derived and validated in the same 90 pts.

### SPIN Score (Celiac Plexus Radiosurgery) · [[NCT03323489]]

> Post-hoc predictors of pain response after celiac SRS: response 32% / 53% / 89% by 0 / 1 / 2 SPIN points.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The actionable RT read is timing, not technique: neurotoxic chemo exposure carried OR 5.1 (p=0.009) against response, so the referral decision moves earlier in the disease course, before oxaliplatin or taxane neuropathy. Celiac SRS is NCCN-listed and single-fraction, so the gate is who you irradiate and when, not dose.

![SPIN Score (Celiac Plexus Radiosurgery)](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HJlU8XZXcAY6Hm7.jpg)

| SPIN score | n | Pain response |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 31 | 32% |
| 1 | 40 | 53% |
| 2 | 19 | 89% |

- 📊 SPIN = baseline Pain >6 (+1) + No prior Neurotoxic chemo (+1), range 0-2
- 🔍 Post-hoc analysis of 90 evaluable pts from the phase 2 celiac SRS trial ([[NCT03323489]])
- 📊 Parent trial pain response 53% (95% CI 42-64%), Lancet Oncol 2024;25:1070-79
- 📐 1° response definition: ≥2-point reduction in average BPI-SF pain, baseline → 3 wks
- 📊 Univariate → multivariate predictor fate

  | Predictor | Univariate | Multivariate |
  |---|---|---|
  | Neurotoxic chemo exposure | OR 5.33 (2.13-13.4), p<0.001 | OR 5.1, p=0.009 |
  | Baseline pain intensity | OR 1.73, p=0.003 | OR 1.8, p=0.003 |
  | Age | OR 1.06, p=0.014 | lost significance |
  | Therapy line | OR 0.65, p=0.04 | lost significance |

- 📊 Pain-threshold dose-response (response rate by baseline pain cutpoint)
  - >5: 61.5%
  - >6: 65.8%, chosen as the optimal discrimination point
  - >7: 83.3%
  - >8: 85.7%
- 📐 Apparent AUC 0.716; optimism-corrected AUC 0.714 after 500-iteration bootstrap
- ⚠️ Post-hoc on a single-arm phase 2; the score was derived and validated in the same 90 pts
- ⚠️ Subgroup n's are small: 2-point group n=19, so the 89% rests on ~17 responders
- ⚠️ No external validation; authors state it is required before clinical use
