# 2026-11-12

**ASTRO brain mets guideline: SRS strong for ECOG 0-2 with up to 4 intact mets, conditional for 5-10; HA-WBRT plus memantine strong when WBRT used.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Guideline and consensus day, no new efficacy readouts; CNS and oligo-mets carried it. ASTRO grades SRS strong for up to 4 brain mets, conditional for 5-10; ESTRO conditionally prefers 2x12Gy for painful vertebral mets. Common gap: dose/fractionation and re-RT rest on retrospective data or opinion, and the pelvic SABR re-RT Delphi split on cumulative OAR constraints.

## 🧠 CNS

ASTRO extends SRS to 5-10 intact mets on conditional, low-QoE grounds, anchored by JLGK0901.

### ASTRO Clinical Practice Guideline: Radiation Therapy for Brain Metastases

> SRS strongly recommended for ECOG 0-2 with up to 4 intact mets, conditional for 5-10; HA-WBRT plus memantine strong when WBRT is used.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The size-indexed dosing is the transferable piece: single-fraction 2000-2400 cGy under 2 cm, multifraction (2700 cGy/3 or 3000 cGy/5) at 3-4 cm, with BED10 ≥5000 cGy tied to better local control. After resection, cavity SRS carries nodular meningeal risk up to 28% at 1 yr, the reason preop SRS is being tested.

- 🔍 ASTRO task force guideline, 4 key questions; AHRQ systematic review, 97 studies, literature through Sept 2020
- 🔍 KQ1 intact brain mets: recommendation grid (Table 3)

  | Scenario | Recommendation | Strength | QoE |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | ECOG 0-2, up to 4 intact mets | SRS | Strong | High |
  | ECOG 0-2, 5-10 intact mets | SRS | Conditional | Low |
  | <2 cm | Single-fraction SRS 2000-2400 cGy | Strong | Moderate |
  | ≥2 to <3 cm | SF 1800 cGy or multifraction (2700 cGy/3 fx, 3000 cGy/5 fx) | Conditional | Low |
  | ≥3 to 4 cm | Multifraction SRS (2700 cGy/3 fx, 3000 cGy/5 fx) | Conditional | Not attributable in source extract |
  | >4 cm | Surgery; if not feasible, multifraction over SF SRS | Conditional | Not attributable in source extract |
  | Symptomatic, CNS-active systemic candidate | Upfront local therapy | Strong | Not attributable in source extract |
  | Asymptomatic, CNS-active systemic eligible | MDT decision on deferring local therapy | Conditional | Not attributable in source extract |

- 📊 Other strong recommendations (abstract)
  - Resected mets: SRS to improve local control
  - Favorable prognosis receiving WBRT: hippocampal avoidance and memantine
  - Poor prognosis: early palliative care for symptoms and caregiver support
- 🔍 Favorable prognosis, ineligible for surgery/SRS: WBRT (eg, 3000 cGy in 10 fx) recommended as primary treatment
- 📊 Postop cavity SRS vs observation: surgical bed control 72% vs 43% at 12 mo
- ⚠️ Nodular meningeal disease after cavity SRS reported up to 28% at 1 yr; preop SRS under investigation
- 🔗 JLGK0901 (prospective observational) underpins 5-10 mets: post-SRS survival noninferior vs 2-4 mets
- ⚠️ 11-15 mets not covered: supporting retrospective series had only 10 such pts
- 🔗 RTOG 90-05 MTDs 2400/1800/1500 cGy for ≤2, 2.1-3, 3.1-4 cm anchor single-fraction dosing
- ⚠️ No prospective comparative data on SRS dose/fractionation; BED10 ≥5000 cGy link is retrospective
- ⚠️ No randomized data on deferring local therapy for CNS-active systemics; up to 40% progress early without response

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Radiation Therapy for Brain Metastases: An ASTRO Clinical Practice Guideline** — Gondi, Vinai; Bauman, Glenn; Bradfield, Lisa et al.. *Practical Radiation Oncology* (2022-07). [doi:10.1016/j.prro.2022.02.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.prro.2022.02.003)
  - 📎 [[papers/cns/2022-gondi-radiation-therapy-brain-metastases-astro-clinical.pdf|ASTRO Clinical Practice Guideline: Radiation Therapy for Brain Metastases (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions
> - SRS vs HA-WBRT plus memantine for 5-15 brain mets
> - Safety of deferring local therapy with CNS-active systemic therapy
> - Preop vs postop SRS for nodular meningeal disease

## 🎯 Oligometastatic / Mets

Two spine SBRT documents: ESTRO grades selection and dosing, and an international panel carries mobile-spine compartment CTV logic to the sacrum.

### International consensus on target volume delineation for sacral metastases and spinal SBRT

> Kappa 0.73 for GTV and 0.59 for CTV across 9 experts and 10 cases; consensus compartment-based sacral CTV built on a new eight-segment classification.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Compartmental, not margin-based: entire GTV compartment plus the adjacent marrow compartment at risk, with the ala ossification line usable to cap the CTV only when the GTV spares the ala, and any S1 ala involvement starting the CTV above the S1 VB. Thecal sac contoured to inferior S5 stands in for the cauda equina.

- 🔍 Eight radiation oncologists + one neurosurgeon, 9 international centres, independently contoured 10 sacral SBRT cases
- 🔍 Panel: >7850 spine SBRT cases collectively, median 13 yr spine SBRT experience (range 7-18)
- 📐 Agreement scored with STAPLE; consensus contours generated at 80% confidence level
- 📊 Inter-expert contour agreement (STAPLE)

  | Metric | GTV | CTV |
  |---|---|---|
  | Kappa (range) | 0.73 (0.59-0.83) | 0.59 (0.41-0.70) |
  | Mean sensitivity (range) | 0.70 (0.54-0.87) | 0.55 (0.44-0.64) |
  | Agreement level | Substantial | Moderate |

- 📊 Most CTV variability in Case 2 (kappa 0.41) and Case 4 (kappa 0.48), ala lesions encroaching VB/posterior elements
- 📊 Modified STAPLE CTV accepted by consensus in 9 of 10 cases; Case 5 predominant only, 6/9 (67%)
- 🔍 Experts' own CTV approach
  - 6/9: anatomic compartment approach
  - 2: limited 3 to 5 mm margin into adjacent bone
  - 1: no GTV-to-CTV margin
- 🔍 Sacral classification system (SCS), eight segments
  - 1 vertebral body
  - 2 left anterior ala · 3 left posterior ala
  - 4 left lamina · 5 spinous process · 6 right lamina
  - 7 right posterior ala · 8 right anterior ala
- 🔍 Key CTV and OAR rules
  - Entire GTV compartment plus adjacent marrow compartment at risk
  - Superior/inferior extent set by vertebral levels
  - Any S1 ala involvement: CTV starts at superior S1 ala
  - Ala ossification line may cap CTV only when GTV spares the ala
  - Thecal sac contoured to inferior S5 as cauda equina surrogate
- 🔗 Extends compartmental logic of mobile-spine SBRT consensus (Cox et al., Redmond et al.), which excluded the sacrum
- 🔗 Cited sacral SBRT series: cumulative LC 86.5% at 1 yr, 78.7% at 2 yr
- ⚠️ No sacrum-specific pattern-of-failure data underpin the CTV
- ⚠️ Three experts preferred isotropic 0-5 mm margin over nerve root, bowel, insufficiency fracture concerns
- ⚠️ Bone-confined disease only; not epidural/paraspinal extension, re-irradiation, or postop

**Sources:**
- 📄 **International consensus recommendations for target volume delineation specific to sacral metastases and spinal stereotactic body radiation therapy (SBRT)** — Dunne, Emma M.; Sahgal, Arjun; Lo, Simon S. et al.. *Radiotherapy and Oncology* (2020-04). [doi:10.1016/j.radonc.2019.11.026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.radonc.2019.11.026)
  - 📎 [[papers/oligo-mets/2020-dunne-international-consensus-recommendations-target-volume.pdf|International consensus on target volume delineation for sacral metastases and spinal SBRT (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions
> - Does compartment-based sacral CTV improve local control vs limited margins?
> - Sacral plexus and nerve root dose tolerance with SBRT
> - Insufficiency fracture risk with larger ala-inclusive CTVs

### ESTRO Clinical Practice Guideline: SBRT for Spine Metastases

> 22 recommendations, 5 statements (≥75% consensus): SBRT above 1x18Gy equivalent (BED10=50Gy10), 2x12Gy conditionally preferred for painful vertebral metastases; mostly moderate/expert-opinion evidence.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The guideline splits single fraction by dose: 1x16 or 1x18Gy is not recommended over 1x8Gy for pain, while single fraction >20Gy carries a VCF penalty fractionated SBRT avoids. That leaves 2x12Gy as the conditionally preferred palliative schedule, with the floor for durable LC set above 1x18Gy equivalent (BED10=50Gy10).

- 🔍 Built on a PRISMA systematic review, Jan 2005 to Sep 2021, with RTOG 0631 added April 2023
- 🔍 Voting panel: 10 rad oncs, 1 spinal neurosurgeon, 3 physicists, 1 RTT (2 residents non-voting)
- 🔍 Scope excludes re-irradiation and symptomatic metastatic spinal cord compression
- 📊 Randomized SBRT vs CRT pain trials the panel weighed

  | Trial | SBRT | CRT | N | Pain result (SBRT vs CRT) |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Sprave, randomized phase II | 24Gy/1fx | 30Gy/10fx | 55 | Response 6mo 73.7% vs 35%, p=0.015 (3mo p=0.13) |
  | CCTG SC.24, phase II/III | 24Gy/2fx | 20Gy/5fx | 114 vs 115 | Complete response 3mo 35% vs 14%, p=0.0002 |
  | NRG/RTOG 0631, phase 3 | 16 or 18Gy/1fx | 8Gy/1fx | 339 (2:1) | Response 3mo 41.3% vs 60.5%, favoring CRT |
  | ROBOMET, phase III (abstract only) | 20Gy/1fx | 8Gy/1fx | 126 | CR 1mo 37% vs 25% ns (ITT); 3mo per-protocol 54% vs 31%, p=0.048 |
  | TwiCs randomized trial | 18Gy/1, 30Gy/3, 35Gy/5 | 8Gy/1, 20Gy/5, 30Gy/10 | 110 | No benefit at 3mo; not spine-specific, underpowered |

- 🔍 Selection for SBRT over CRT in painful vertebral metastases (KQ1)
  - Not frankly unstable (SINS greater than 12 excluded)
  - No or minimal epidural disease (Bilsky 0-1)
  - Up to 3 contiguous vertebral segments in the treatment volume
  - Prolonged life expectancy with durable local control intended
  - CRT preferred: short life expectancy, can't tolerate MRI/immobilization, uncontrolled pain on oral analgesia
- 💊 Dose and fractionation (KQ2)
  - Prescription above the equivalent of 1x18Gy (BED10=50Gy10)
  - De novo high-dose schedules: 1x20Gy, 1x24Gy, 2x12Gy, 3x10Gy, 5x7Gy
  - Expected LC 80-90% at 1-2 years with these schedules
  - 2x12Gy conditionally recommended as preferred palliative SBRT schedule
  - 1x16 or 1x18Gy not recommended over 1x8Gy when pain/QoL is the primary goal
- 📊 SC.24 Sunnybrook sub-cohort (137 pts): 2yr local failure 14.8% vs 35.6%, p<0.001, SBRT vs CRT
- 📊 Same sub-cohort: re-RT 2.2% vs 15.8% at 1 yr (p=0.002); all G3 fractures (n=5) in SBRT arm
- 📊 Randomized 24Gy/1fx vs 27Gy/3fx in 117 oligometastatic pts (56% spinal): better LC with single fraction
- 📐 Graded recommendations and statements with unambiguous grading (KQ1, KQ4)

  | KQ | Item | Strength | Evidence |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | Baseline + post-SBRT pain scoring (BPI, VAS or NRS) | Strong | High |
  | 1 | Consider SBRT in selected painful vertebral metastases | Conditional | Moderate |
  | 4 | Pre-SBRT SINS assessment | Strong | High |
  | 4 | Surgical stabilization review if SINS 7-12, especially 13-18 | Strong | Expert opinion |
  | 4 | Pre-SBRT Bilsky grading | Strong | Moderate |
  | 4 | No SBRT in symptomatic MSCC | Strong | High |
  | 4 | Myelopathy mitigation: MRI/CT myelo cord, PRV, cord over coverage | Strong | High |
  | 4 | GTV coverage over nerve root sparing | Strong | High |
  | 4 | No routine prophylactic steroids for pain flare | Conditional | Moderate |
  | 4 | Statement: fractionated SBRT, no VCF excess vs CRT | Statement | High |
  | 4 | Statement: single fraction >20Gy, more VCF vs CRT and fractionated SBRT | Statement | High |

- 📐 HyTEC cord Dmax (thecal sac as PRV) keeping myelopathy risk under 5%

  | Fractions | Cord Dmax |
  |---|---|
  | 1 | 12.4-14.0Gy |
  | 2 | 17.0-19.3Gy |
  | 3 | 20.3-23.1Gy |
  | 4 | 23.0-26.2Gy |
  | 5 | 25.3-28.8Gy |

- ⚠️ Toxicity benchmarks cited
  - VCF 6-14% in two large multicenter analyses; single fraction >20-24Gy raises it
  - Myelopathy 0.4% in pooled de novo series of >1,000 pts
  - Pain flare 43% vs 33%, SBRT vs CRT (meta-analysis of RCTs)
  - Plexopathy: 14 events in 13 of 447 pts after 18-26Gy single fraction
- 🔍 Technique requirements (KQ3)
  - Sim CT slice ≤1.5mm, co-registered to T1 and T2 MRI
  - GTV/CTV to PTV margin ≤3mm, minimum 1mm
  - Dose grid <1.5mm; type C algorithm, else type B
  - End-to-end tolerance under 2mm; IMRT, VMAT, tomotherapy or robotic delivery
  - Rigid mask above cervicothoracic junction (e.g. T4 and above)
  - 6DoF conditionally recommended, verification imaging after pitch/roll corrections
- ⚠️ LC expectation inconsistent: narrative cites 80-95% at 1-2 yrs, the recommendation states 80-90%
- ⚠️ High-grade epidural disease left open; only 2% of SC.24 SBRT pts had Bilsky 2-3
- ⚠️ No medical oncologist voted, yet the panel issues an IO/targeted-therapy toxicity recommendation

**Sources:**
- 📄 **ESTRO clinical practice guideline: Stereotactic body radiotherapy for spine metastases** — Guckenberger, M; Andratschke, N; Belka, C et al.. *Radiotherapy and Oncology* (2024-01). [doi:10.1016/j.radonc.2023.109966](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.radonc.2023.109966)
  - 📎 [[papers/oligo-mets/2024-guckenberger-estro-clinical-practice-guideline-stereotactic-body.pdf|ESTRO Clinical Practice Guideline: SBRT for Spine Metastases (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions
> - Optimal management of high-grade epidural disease (Bilsky 2-3)
> - Spine SBRT toxicity with concurrent immunotherapy or targeted therapy
> - Single-fraction >20Gy LC gain vs vertebral fracture cost

## 📊 Cross-cutting

Pelvic SABR re-RT Delphi agreed on schedules and IMRT with daily verification, but not on interval (43%) or cumulative OAR limits.

### International Delphi consensus for pelvic SABR re-irradiation

> 66% of statements reached consensus among 23 international radiation oncologists; no agreement on a 12-month interval (43%) or cumulative OAR constraints.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The agreed schedules (30–37.5 Gy/5–6 fx or 21–27 Gy/3 fx, alternate days), IMRT and daily volumetric or fiducial verification are the technique layer you can borrow. Dosimetry stayed unresolved: cumulative OAR constraints, PTV Dmax ≤140% (73%) and a 12-month interval (43%). The recovery assumption in each plan is still an institutional call.

- 🔍 3-round international Delphi; 44 practice statements generated after Round 1
- 📊 Round 2: 29 statements reached consensus; 15 re-presented in Round 3
- 🔍 Panel composition
  - 23 radiation oncologists in Round 1 (56% of 41 invited); 21 in Round 2, 22 in Round 3
  - Countries: Italy 6, USA 5, UK 4, Canada 2, Switzerland 2, France 1, South Korea 1
  - Sub-specialty: GU 19 (83%), lower GI 11 (48%), gyn 8 (35%)
  - Median 10 cases/yr per participant (range 10–100)
- 📊 Where the panel agreed and where it split (from text + consensus summary)

  | Topic | Status |
  |---|---|
  | Definition: overlap with prior pelvic dose risking excess OAR dose/toxicity | Consensus |
  | Alternative to surgical exenteration after MDT discussion | Consensus |
  | With extra-pelvic oligometastases controllable by MDT | Consensus |
  | Not usually appropriate with direct luminal OAR invasion | Consensus (90%) |
  | May not be appropriate with luminal/critical OAR contact | No consensus (50%) |
  | Minimum 12 months since prior pelvic RT | No consensus (43%) |
  | Caps: ≤3 lesions, ≤6 cm per lesion | No consensus |
  | 30–37.5 Gy/5–6 fx or 21–27 Gy/3 fx, alternate days | Agreement |
  | IMRT + daily volumetric or fiducial verification | Agreement |
  | PTV point Dmax ≤140% of prescription | No consensus (73%) |
  | Cumulative OAR constraints | No consensus |

- 📊 PET-CT recommended by 19 (83%); 3 (13%) thought MRI unnecessary for nodal staging
- 🔗 Optimal cumulative constraints apply AAPM TG-101 5-fraction de novo SABR limits cumulatively
- 🔗 As with a prior thoracic re-RT expert study, no consensus on a quantified dose-overlap threshold
- ⚠️ Organisers selected the experts; participants were offered authorship
- ⚠️ GU-dominant panel (83%): gyn and rectal statements rest on fewer voices
- ⚠️ Agreement threshold for consensus not stated in source excerpt

**Sources:**
- 📄 **An international Delphi consensus for pelvic stereotactic ablative radiotherapy re-irradiation** — Slevin, Finbar; Aitken, Katharine; Alongi, Filippo et al.. *Radiotherapy and Oncology* (2021-11). [doi:10.1016/j.radonc.2021.09.010](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.radonc.2021.09.010)
  - 📎 [[papers/oligo-mets/2021-slevin-international-delphi-consensus-pelvic-stereotactic.pdf|International Delphi consensus for pelvic SABR re-irradiation (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions
> - Minimum safe interval between pelvic RT courses
> - Validated cumulative OAR constraints with recovery assumptions
> - SABR re-irradiation vs exenteration outcomes

## 🌞 Skin / Melanoma

GEC-ESTRO review accepts >5 mm prescription depth with thicker bolus in selected large NMSC; retrospective evidence only.

### GEC-ESTRO HNS critical review: skin superficial brachytherapy with flaps and customized moulds

> Thicker bolus permits >5 mm prescription depth: at 5 mm depth, 10 vs 5 mm bolus cuts surface Dmax 148% vs 187%; no dose-PROM data.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> At 5 mm depth on a 6×6 cm CTV, thickening bolus from 5 to 10 mm drops surface Dmax from 187% to 148% while widening R100-R50 from 16.0 to 18.7 mm. A percentage Dmax cap also ignores fractionation: at 150% Dmax, EQD2 (a/b 3 Gy) spans 121.5 to 189 Gy across ACROP schedules, so any skin constraint has to be read per schedule.

- 📊 WG position: >5 mm prescription depth acceptable in selected large NMSC when interstitial BT or EBRT not viable
- 🔍 PRISMA review, PubMed to 31 May 2019, HDR Ir-192 superficial BT with flaps or moulds
- ⚠️ Evidence base is retrospective series, case reports and surveys; no clinical trial data
- 📊 Bolus thickness at fixed 5 mm prescription depth, 6×6 cm CTV (TG-43 plan, Oncentra)

  | Bolus thickness | Surface Dmax | R100 - R50 |
  |---|---|---|
  | 3 mm | 228% | 14.3 mm |
  | 5 mm | 187% | 16.0 mm |
  | 10 mm | 148% | 18.7 mm |

- 🔍 Guideline limits vs published practice

  | Parameter | Guideline (ACROP / ABS / GEB) | Published practice |
  |---|---|---|
  | Prescription depth | Max 5 mm | Up to 20 mm (scalp SCC); 15 mm (cheek SCC); 9-10 mm in several series |
  | Surface Dmax | 125% flaps, 140% moulds (ABS, GEB) | 125%-150% (16-centre survey); up to 200% (Kuncman) |
  | Bolus thickness | Ideally 5 mm from skin | 2.5-15 mm (6 Canadian centres); 10, 15 or 20 mm (Somanchi) |

- 🔗 Jaberi (Co-60): at 10 mm depth, mould 5 → 20 mm cuts surface dose 250% → 150%
- ⚠️ Thicker bolus flattens gradient: dose spreads further beyond the CTV
- ⚠️ No published link between 125%-140% Dmax, 5 mm depth or 5 mm bolus and PROMs
- 📐 At 150% Dmax, EQD2 (a/b 3 Gy) spans 121.5 to 189 Gy across ACROP schedules (55% difference)
- ⚠️ Dosimetry uncertainty in Dmax itself
  - All included studies used TG-43, which ignores missing scatter
  - MC deviation below 3% at 5 mm depth (Granero)
  - Up to 5% with Freiburg flaps from air between spheres (Vijande)
  - No reviewed paper defined how Dmax was calculated (bins, sample points)

[doi:10.1016/j.radonc.2022.08.022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.radonc.2022.08.022)
