# 2026-11-12

**ASTRO brain mets guideline: SRS strongly recommended for up to 4 intact mets (ECOG 0-2), conditionally for 5-10; size-tiered SRS dose grid and HA-WBRT plus memantine codified.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> A guideline-and-consensus day, no new outcome data. ASTRO (CNS) and ESTRO (spine) codify SRS/SBRT dose by lesion size and BED10, with 2x12Gy conditionally preferred for painful spine mets. Sacral SBRT contouring and pelvic SABR re-RT consensus both left gaps: CTV kappa 0.59, and no agreement on cumulative OAR limits or re-RT interval.

## 🎯 Oligometastatic / Mets

Spine SBRT gets an ESTRO dose and technique framework (above 1x18Gy equivalent, PTV margin ≤3mm) plus the first sacrum-specific contouring consensus.

### International consensus: target volume delineation for sacral metastases and spinal SBRT

> CTV kappa 0.59 vs GTV 0.73 across 9 experts contouring 10 sacral metastasis cases; compartment-based CTV rules and an eight-segment sacral map proposed.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The rule that changes contours is the S1 ala exception: any GTV in the S1 ala starts the CTV above the S1 VB, and an involved ala's ossification line is not a barrier. Disagreement concentrated at ala lesions abutting posterior elements (CTV kappa 0.41, 0.48), so that boundary stays physician judgment.

- 🔍 9 experts (8 RO, 1 neurosurgeon) from 9 international centres contoured GTV, CTV and neural elements on 10 sacral metastasis cases
- 🔍 Panel experience: >7850 spine SBRT cases, median 13 yrs (range 7-18) treating spine SBRT
- 🔍 Agreement by STAPLE at an 80% confidence level, kappa corrected for chance agreement
- 📊 GTV kappa 0.73 (range 0.59-0.83), substantial agreement
- 📊 CTV kappa 0.59 (range 0.41-0.70), moderate agreement
- ⚠️ Lowest CTV agreement: Case 2 (kappa = 0.41) and Case 4 (kappa = 0.48), ala disease encroaching on VB and posterior elements
- 🔍 How experts built the CTV

  | Approach | Experts | Extent |
  |---|---|---|
  | Anatomic compartment | 6/9 | Whole GTV compartment plus adjacent compartment(s), or at least 3 to 5 mm into adjacent bone |
  | Limited margin | 2 | 3 to 5 mm into adjacent bone |
  | No margin | 1 | CTV = GTV |

- 📊 Modified STAPLE CTV accepted by consensus in 9 of 10 cases; Case 5 predominant only (6 of 9, 67%)
- 🔍 Key CTV rules stated in text
  - Include the whole involved compartment plus the adjacent marrow space at risk
  - GTV in any part of the S1 ala: CTV starts at the superior S1 ala
  - Uninvolved ala included prophylactically: visible ossification line may limit CTV size
  - Involved ala: ossification line is not a barrier, do not trim CTV to it
  - Thecal sac contoured to inferior S5 VB as cauda equina surrogate
- 🔍 New sacral classification system: 8 segments (VB, left/right anterior and posterior ala, left/right lamina, spinous process)
- ⚠️ Bone-confined disease only; epidural or paraspinal extension, re-irradiation and postoperative sacrum not addressed
- ⚠️ No sacrum-specific pattern-of-failure data behind the rules; no external validation
- 🔗 Extends the Cox et al. and Redmond et al. mobile-spine contouring guidelines, which did not cover the sacrum

**Sources:**

[doi:10.1016/j.radonc.2019.11.026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.radonc.2019.11.026)
  - 📎 [[papers/oligo-mets/2020-dunne-international-consensus-recommendations-target-volume.pdf|International consensus: target volume delineation for sacral metastases and spinal SBRT (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions
> - Sacrum-specific pattern of failure after SBRT
> - Sacral plexus and nerve root dose tolerance

### ESTRO Clinical Practice Guideline: SBRT for Spine Metastases

> 22 recommendations, 5 statements: spine SBRT dose above 1x18Gy (BED10=50Gy10) for LC; 2x12Gy conditionally preferred for painful mets; PTV margin ≤3mm.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The dose floor is the actionable line: above 1x18Gy (BED10=50Gy10), with 2x12Gy preferred for painful mets, because RTOG 0631's 16 or 18Gy single fraction lost to 8Gy on 3mo pain response (41.3% vs 60.5%). Single fractions >20Gy carry high-level VCF evidence, so 24Gy x1 for oligomets trades LC against fracture.

- 🔍 ESTRO guideline built on a PRISMA systematic review, Jan 2005-Sep 2021 (RTOG 0631 added Apr 2023)
- 🔍 22 recommendations + 5 statements; consensus threshold ≥75% panel agreement
- 📐 Dose and fractionation recommendations
  - Prescription above 1x18Gy equivalent (BED10=50Gy10) for durable LC
  - De novo high-dose schedules: 1x20Gy, 1x24Gy, 2x12Gy, 3x10Gy, 5x7Gy; expected LC 80-90% at 1-2y
  - 2x12Gy conditionally recommended as preferred palliative SBRT schedule
  - 16 or 18Gy x1 not recommended over 1x8Gy when pain relief or QoL is the goal
- 🔍 Patient selection for SBRT
  - Not frankly unstable (SINS >12 excluded)
  - Bilsky 0-1, up to 3 contiguous vertebral segments in the volume
  - Prolonged life expectancy with durable control intended
  - Surgical stabilization assessment if SINS 7-12, especially 13-18
  - SBRT not recommended in symptomatic MSCC
- 🔍 Technique recommendations
  - Sim CT slice ≤1.5mm co-registered to T1 and T2 MRI
  - GTV/CTV to PTV margin ≤3mm, minimum PTV margin 1mm
  - Intensity-modulated delivery; online IGRT before each fraction
  - Cord PRV prioritized over target coverage
  - GTV coverage prioritized over nerve root sparing
- 📊 Randomized SBRT vs CRT pain trials cited

  | Trial | SBRT | CRT | Pain result |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Sprave, rand phase II (n=55) | 24Gy/1fx | 30Gy/10fx | Response 6mo 73.7% vs 35%, p=0.015 |
  | CCTG SC.24 phase II/III | 24Gy/2fx (n=114) | 20Gy/5fx (n=115) | CR 3mo 35% vs 14%, p=0.0002 |
  | RTOG 0631 phase 3 (n=339, 2:1) | 16 or 18Gy/1fx | 8Gy/1fx | Response 3mo 41.3% vs 60.5%, favoring CRT |
  | ROBOMET phase III, abstract (n=126) | 20Gy/1fx | 8Gy/1fx | CR 1mo 37% vs 25%, ns (ITT) |

- 🔗 SC.24 Sunnybrook subcohort (137 pts): 2y local failure 14.8% vs 35.6%, SBRT vs CRT, p<0.001
- 📊 Vertebral fracture statements (KQ4)

  | Statement | Level of evidence |
  |---|---|
  | Fractionated SBRT: no increased VCF risk vs CRT | High |
  | Single-fraction >20Gy: increased VCF vs CRT and vs fractionated SBRT | High |

- 📊 Toxicity benchmarks cited
  - VCF 6-14% in two large multicenter analyses
  - Myelopathy 0.4% in pooled analysis of >1,000 de novo pts
  - HyTEC cord Dmax for <5% risk: 12.4-14.0Gy in 1fx up to 25.3-28.8Gy in 5fx
  - Pain flare 43% vs 33%, SBRT vs CRT; routine prophylactic steroids not recommended
- ⚠️ Panel: 10 radonc, 1 spinal neurosurgeon, 3 physicists, 1 RTT; no medical oncologist listed
- ⚠️ Re-irradiation and symptomatic MSCC excluded from scope

**Sources:**
- 📄 **ESTRO clinical practice guideline: Stereotactic body radiotherapy for spine metastases** — Guckenberger, M; Andratschke, N; Belka, C et al.. *Radiotherapy and Oncology* (2024-01). [doi:10.1016/j.radonc.2023.109966](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.radonc.2023.109966)
  - 📎 [[papers/oligo-mets/2024-guckenberger-estro-clinical-practice-guideline-stereotactic-body.pdf|ESTRO Clinical Practice Guideline: SBRT for Spine Metastases (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions
> - Does 2x12Gy transfer beyond high-volume SC.24-type centers?
> - Spine SBRT safety with concurrent targeted therapy or immunotherapy
> - Role of separation surgery before SBRT in high-grade epidural disease

## 📊 Cross-cutting

### International Delphi consensus: pelvic SABR re-irradiation

> 66% of statements reached consensus among 21-23 international radiation oncologists; interval since prior RT, lesion number/size and cumulative OAR limits stayed unresolved.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The transferable piece is the agreed technique: 30-37.5 Gy in 5-6 fx or 21-27 Gy in 3 fx on alternate days, IMRT, daily volumetric verification. Luminal OAR contact split the panel at 50% while direct invasion was a 90% exclusion, so contact cases remain judgment calls without a consensus fallback.

- 🔍 3-round Delphi, 23 radiation oncologists in Round 1 (56% of 41 invited); 21 and 22 in Rounds 2-3
- 🔍 Panel weighted to GU: 19 (83%) GU, 11 (48%) lower GI, 8 (35%) gyn subspecialty interest
- 🔍 44 statements after Round 1; 29 reached consensus in Round 2, 15 re-presented in Round 3
- 📊 Panel agreement on selected statements (values from the paper's text)

  | Statement | Agreement | Consensus |
  |---|---|---|
  | SABR re-RT not usually appropriate with direct luminal OAR invasion | 90% | Yes |
  | May not be appropriate with luminal OAR contact (no invasion) | 50% | No |
  | Minimum 12 mo since prior pelvic RT | 43% | No |
  | PTV point max dose not to exceed 140% | 73% | No |

- 📊 Patient-selection statements that reached consensus
  - Alternative to surgical exenteration after MDT discussion
  - May be considered with extra-pelvic oligometastatic disease controllable by MDT
  - Feasibility weighs lesion number, target size, location and OAR proximity
  - Minimum WHO PS 2 (or equivalent)
  - Expected/transient prior acute RT toxicity does not in itself preclude re-RT
- 📐 Agreed fractionation: 30-37.5 Gy in 5-6 fx or 21-27 Gy in 3 fx, alternate days
- 📐 Delivery agreement: IMRT (or similar high-conformity) plus daily online volumetric or fiducial verification
- ⚠️ Proposed cumulative OAR constraints (AAPM TG-101 5-fx limits used cumulatively) did not reach consensus
- ⚠️ Agreement measures expert opinion; no control or toxicity outcomes were generated by this study

**Sources:**
- 📄 **An international Delphi consensus for pelvic stereotactic ablative radiotherapy re-irradiation** — Slevin, Finbar; Aitken, Katharine; Alongi, Filippo et al.. *Radiotherapy and Oncology* (2021-11). [doi:10.1016/j.radonc.2021.09.010](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.radonc.2021.09.010)
  - 📎 [[papers/oligo-mets/2021-slevin-international-delphi-consensus-pelvic-stereotactic.pdf|International Delphi consensus: pelvic SABR re-irradiation (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions
> - Minimum safe interval between pelvic RT courses
> - Cumulative OAR EQD2 limits allowing for tissue recovery
> - Maximum lesion number and size for pelvic SABR re-irradiation

## 🧠 CNS

### ASTRO Clinical Practice Guideline: RT for Brain Metastases

> SRS strongly recommended for up to 4 intact brain mets (ECOG 0-2), conditionally for 5-10; size-tiered SRS dose grid and HA-WBRT plus memantine codified.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The dose grid is the RT payload: SF 2000-2400 cGy for <2 cm (Strong), SF 1800 cGy or 2700/3 or 3000/5 for ≥2 to <3 cm, multifraction for ≥3 to 4 cm. No randomized fractionation data exist, so thresholds rest on RTOG 90-05 MTDs and a BED10 ≥5000 cGy retrospective association.

- 🔍 ASTRO task force guideline, 4 KQs: intact and resected brain mets from nonhematologic solid tumors
- 🔍 Built on AHRQ systematic review, 97 studies; literature end extended to Sept 2020
- 📊 KQ1 intact brain mets: recommendation by scenario

  | Scenario | Recommendation | Strength |
  |---|---|---|
  | ECOG 0-2, up to 4 intact mets | SRS | Strong |
  | ECOG 0-2, 5-10 intact mets | SRS | Conditional |
  | 11 to 15 mets | Not extended (only 10 pts in supporting series) | No recommendation |
  | Symptomatic, candidate for local + CNS-active systemic tx | Upfront local therapy | Strong |
  | Asymptomatic, eligible for CNS-active systemic tx | MDT decision on safe deferral of local therapy | Conditional |

- 📊 KQ1 SRS dose-fractionation by intact met diameter

  | Diameter | SRS regimen | Strength |
  |---|---|---|
  | <2 cm | SF 2000-2400 cGy; MF 2700 cGy/3 fx or 3000 cGy/5 fx if V12 Gy >10 cm3 | Strong |
  | ≥2 to <3 cm | SF 1800 cGy or MF 2700 cGy/3 fx or 3000 cGy/5 fx | Conditional |
  | ≥3 to 4 cm | MF preferred; SF up to 1500 cGy if chosen | Conditional |
  | >4 cm | Surgery; if not feasible, MF over SF | Conditional |
  | >6 cm | SRS discouraged (implementation remark) | Not graded |

- 📐 QoE: High for SRS in up to 4 mets; Low for 5-10 mets
- 📊 Other KQs (resected, WBRT, palliative)
  - Resected mets: SRS strongly recommended to improve local control
  - Favorable prognosis, ineligible for surgery/SRS: WBRT (eg 3000 cGy in 10 fx) recommended
  - Favorable prognosis receiving WBRT: hippocampal avoidance + memantine strongly recommended
  - HA not appropriate for mets near hippocampi or leptomeningeal disease
  - Poor prognosis: early palliative care strongly recommended
  - Mass effect and/or >4 cm: neurosurgery discussion conditionally recommended
- 🔗 Size grid descends from RTOG 90-05 MTDs: 2400, 1800, 1500 cGy for ≤2, 2.1-3, 3.1-4 cm
- 📐 BED10 ≥5000 cGy associated with improved LC in a multi-institutional retrospective analysis
- 🔗 5-10 met rec rests on JLGK0901: noninferior post-SRS survival vs 2-4 mets, cumulative volume ≤15 cm3
- ⚠️ No randomized or prospective controlled data on SRS dose or fractionation
- ⚠️ [[NCT01592968]] (SRS vs WBRT, 5-15 intact mets) final report unpublished at guideline development
- 🔍 SRS alone requires MRI every 2-3 mo for 1-2 yr, then every 4-6 mo

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Radiation Therapy for Brain Metastases: An ASTRO Clinical Practice Guideline** — Gondi, Vinai; Bauman, Glenn; Bradfield, Lisa et al.. *Practical Radiation Oncology* (2022-07). [doi:10.1016/j.prro.2022.02.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.prro.2022.02.003)
  - 📎 [[papers/cns/2022-gondi-radiation-therapy-brain-metastases-astro-clinical.pdf|ASTRO Clinical Practice Guideline: RT for Brain Metastases (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions
> - SRS vs WBRT outcomes for 5-15 intact brain mets
> - SRS vs HA-WBRT plus memantine for neurocognition
> - Safe deferral of local therapy with CNS-active systemic therapy

## 🌞 Skin / Melanoma

### GEC-ESTRO HNS critical review: skin superficial brachytherapy with flaps and customized moulds

> Prescription >5 mm deep judged acceptable for selected large NMSC using tailored bolus; at 5 mm depth, 5→10 mm bolus cut skin Dmax 187%→148%.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The planning lever is bolus thickness, not the depth ceiling: at 5 mm depth on a 6×6 cm² CTV, going from 5 to 10 mm bolus cut skin Dmax 187% to 148% while R100-R50 widened 16.0 to 18.7 mm. With EQD2 at a 150% Dmax spanning 121.5 to 189 Gy across ACROP schedules, a flat percentage cap is a weak surface constraint.

- 🔍 PRISMA systematic review, PubMed through 31 May 2019; HDR Ir-192 superficial BT for NMSC
- 🔍 Evidence base: retrospective single-centre series, case studies, surveys; no clinical trial data available
- 🧭 Current guidelines vs HNS WG position

  | Parameter | ACROP / ABS / GEB guidance | HNS WG position |
  |---|---|---|
  | Max prescription depth | 5 mm | >5 mm acceptable in selected large tumours if interstitial BT or EBRT not viable |
  | Bolus thickness | Catheters ideally 5 mm from skin (ACROP, ABS) | Boli of different thicknesses acceptable to optimize plan |
  | Skin surface Dmax | 125% flaps, 140% custom moulds (ABS, GEB) | No PROM-based value can be recommended |

- 📊 Bolus thickness at 5 mm prescription depth, 6×6 cm² CTV, TG-43 TPS (Fig 3)

  | Bolus | Skin Dmax | R100-R50 |
  |---|---|---|
  | 3 mm | 228% | 14.3 mm |
  | 5 mm | 187% | 16.0 mm |
  | 10 mm | 148% | 18.7 mm |

- 📊 Trade-off: thicker bolus lowers surface Dmax but widens dose spread beyond CTV
- 🔗 Jaberi (Co-60): at 10 mm prescription, mould 5→20 mm cut surface dose 250%→150%
- 🔗 Published practice beyond the 5 mm depth limit
  - Scalp SCC prescribed to 20 mm (Semrau)
  - Exophytic necrotic cheek SCC to 15 mm
  - Tumours up to 10 mm (Kalaghchi, Lancellotta, Chan)
  - Up to 9 mm (Jumeau)
  - ABS survey: 2/16 centres to 7 and 8 mm
- 📐 Dmax as % ignores fractionation: EQD2 at 150% Dmax spans 121.5 to 189 Gy across ACROP schedules (α/β 3)
- ⚠️ No published series links Dmax, prescription depth, or bolus thickness to toxicity or cosmesis
- ⚠️ Deep-prescription series never report that subcohort's share or separate outcomes
- ⚠️ All series used TG-43; MC deviations up to 5% with Freiburg flaps (Vijande)
- ⚠️ No reviewed paper defines how Dmax was computed; value depends on DVH bins and sample points
