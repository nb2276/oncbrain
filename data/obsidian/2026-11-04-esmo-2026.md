# 2026-11-04 — ESMO Congress 2026

**SANO: 5yr OS 50% vs 54% (HR 1.17, 0.77-1.77) for active surveillance vs surgery after cCR to nCRT in oesophageal cancer; distant mets 46% vs 29%.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Organ preservation dominates. SANO's mature 5yr data keep surveillance after nCRT cCR viable but flag a distant mets excess. In dMMR/MSI-H LARC, dostarlimab alone gives INV cCR12 68% vs ICR 47%, interim and single-arm. PE-PE: adding pembrolizumab to MDT (73% SABR) in omRCC improves mRFS 25.4 vs 11.0 mo (HR 0.47).

## 🍽️ GI Upper

Mature 5yr follow-up of active surveillance after nCRT cCR, 43% spared oesophagectomy.

### SANO

> 5yr OS 50% vs 54% (HR 1.17, 0.77-1.77) for active surveillance vs surgery after cCR to nCRT; distant mets 46% vs 29%.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The distant mets signal is the read: 46% vs 29% at 5yr under surveillance, but OS still 50% vs 54%. Response assessment after nCRT (biopsy, EUS, PET-CT) now gates the surgery decision. Whether deferred surgery lets occult disease seed, or just exposes disease surgery would only have delayed, is not settled here.

- 🔍 Multicentre stepped-wedge cluster-randomised non-inferiority trial, 2017-2021, N=309
- 🔍 Locally advanced oesophageal cancer with cCR after nCRT: no tumour on endoscopic biopsies, EUS, or PET-CT
- 🔍 198 active surveillance vs 111 standard surgery; median f/u 76 mo (IQR 67-86)
- 📊 5yr outcomes, active surveillance vs standard surgery

  | Endpoint | Active surveillance | Standard surgery | Effect |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 5yr OS | 50% (95% CI 34-57) | 54% (95% CI 46-64) | HR 1.17 (95% CI 0.77-1.77) |
  | DFS | not reported per arm | not reported per arm | HR 1.39 (95% CI 0.94-2.06) |
  | 5yr cumulative incidence distant mets | 46% (95% CI 39-53) | 29% (95% CI 21-37) | P<.01 |

- 📊 OS similar across histologic subtypes; subtype HRs not reported in source
- 📊 85/198 (43%) on surveillance spared non-beneficial oesophagectomy
- 🔗 Extends the 2yr SANO report: OS non-inferiority at 2yr, better QoL with surveillance
- ⚠️ Distant mets excess under surveillance unexplained; OS CIs wide enough to hide a real deficit
- ⚠️ nCRT regimen, dose, and fractionation not stated in source
- ⚠️ Stepped-wedge cluster design: arm by hospital and timing, not individual randomisation; unequal arms (198 vs 111)

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Active surveillance versus standard surgery after neoadjuvant chemoradiotherapy for oesophageal cancer: Long-term results of a cluster-randomised controlled trial (SANO)** — Sanjiv S. Gangaram-Panday. *ESMO Congress 2026* (2026-10). [article](https://cslide.ctimeetingtech.com/esmo2026/attendee/confcal/presentation/list)
  > ESMO Congress 2026 abstract LBA78 · Proffered paper 1: Gastrointestinal tumour, upper digestive · 2026-10-23 · presented by Sanjiv S. Gangaram-Panday (Rotterdam, Netherlands)
  > Background: The Surgery As Needed for Oesophageal cancer (SANO)-trial demonstrated that active surveillance is non-inferior to standard surgery in terms of two-year overall survival in patients with a complete clinical response after neoadjuvant chemoradiotherapy (nCRT). Active surveillance resulted in improved quality of life. Here, we report 5-year outcomes.
  > Methods: From 2017 to 2021, a multicentre stepped-wedge cluster-randomised non-inferiority trial was conducted. Patients with locally advanced oesophageal cancer who achieved complete clinical response (defined as no tumour detected on endoscopic biopsies, -ultrasound, or PET-CT) underwent active surveillance or standard surgery. Cox regression was used to compare 5-year overall and disease-free survival, and the cumulative incidence of distant metastases was estimated using competing risk analysis.
  > Results: A total of 309 patients were included, 198 underwent active surveillance and 111 underwent standard surgery. Median follow-up was 76 months (IQR 67–86). Five-year overall survival was not statistically significantly different between active surveillance and standard surgery (50%, 95%CI 34-57 vs. 54%, 95%CI 46-64; HR 1.17, 95%CI 0.77-1.77) with similar results across histologic subtypes. Disease-free survival was not statistically significantly different (HR 1.39, 95% CI 0.94-2.06). The cumulative incidence of distant metastases at five years was significantly higher in the active surveillance group compared to the standard surgery group (46%, 95%CI 39-53 vs. 29%, 95%CI 21-37; P<.01). Overall, 85 of 198 patients (43%) were spared non-beneficial oesophagectomy, including those with sustained complete clinical response at last follow-up or distant metastases detected within six months after nCRT.
  > Conclusions: Overall survival at 5 years is not statistically significantly different between active surveillance and standard surgery in patients with oesophageal cancer and a complete clinical response after nCRT, despite a higher incidence of distant metastases in the active surveillance group. These findings support active surveillance as a feasible treatment strategy in these patients.
  > Clinical trial identification: The Netherlands Trial Register (NTR-6803).

## 🌀 GI Lower

Checkpoint monotherapy as an RT-free organ-preservation route in dMMR/MSI-H LARC.

### AZUR-1 · [[NCT05723562]]

> cCR12 68% (INV, n=144) and cCR12+pCR 76% with dostarlimab alone in dMMR/MSI-H LARC; ICR cCR12 47% (54/116).

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The ICR read is the number to anchor on: 47% (54/116) cCR12 vs 68% by investigator, with 38 pts still in cCR pending confirmation. Even the lower figure sits above the 21–41% TNT cCR range cited, which supports deferring chemoRT in dMMR/MSI-H LARC and reserving RT for non-responders.

- 🔍 Global single-arm phase II registrational trial, N=154 enrolled, [[NCT05723562]] (ESMO 2026 LBA87)
- 💊 Dostarlimab 500 mg IV q3w × 9 cycles, monotherapy; no chemo or RT in the initial regimen
- 🔍 Data cutoff 20 April 2026; median f/u 26.3 mo (range 2–36)
- 📊 Sustained cCR at 12 mo (cCR12) by assessor

  | Assessor | Evaluable | cCR12 |
  |---|---|---|
  | Investigator (INV) | 144 (≥12 mo f/u) | 68% |
  | Independent central review (ICR) | 116 | 47% (54/116) |

- 📊 Surgery after dostarlimab alone in 15 pts: pCR 12 (80%); composite cCR12 (INV) + pCR 76%
- 📊 98% of pts with cCR stayed in cCR ≥12 mo; no recurrences to date after cCR or pCR
- 📊 93% in surveillance with no progression after dostarlimab ± SOC; 71% with dostarlimab alone
- 📊 88% completed all 9 cycles; G≥3 immune-mediated AEs in 4 pts
- ⚠️ ICR cCR12 47% vs INV 68%: sponsor attributes the gap to later ICR confirmation; 38 pts still pending cCR12 by ICR
- ⚠️ Interim analysis, single-arm, no randomized TNT comparator; long-term organ-preservation durability not yet known
- 🔗 Context: TNT yields cCR 21–41% per abstract background; prior small dostarlimab study showed durable cCRs

- 📄 **Clinical complete response with dostarlimab monotherapy in dMMR/MSI-H locally advanced rectal cancer (LARC): First results from the global registrational AZUR-1 trial** — Andrea Cercek. *ESMO Congress 2026* (2026-10). [article](https://cslide.ctimeetingtech.com/esmo2026/attendee/confcal/presentation/list)
  > ESMO Congress 2026 abstract LBA87 · Proffered paper: Gastrointestinal tumours, lower digestive · 2026-10-25 · presented by Andrea Cercek (New York, United States of America, NY) · [[NCT05723562]]
  > Background: Multimodal treatment with chemotherapy, radiation and surgery is a standard of care (SOC) for LARC associated with significant toxicity/morbidity. After TNT, the range of clinical complete response (cCR) is 21–41%. dMMR/MSI-H tumours are exceptionally responsive to PD-1 blockade: in a small study, neoadjuvant dostarlimab treatment led to durable cCRs and eliminated the need for SOC in most patients (pts) with LARC. AZUR-1 ([[NCT05723562]]) is a global single-arm trial and the first registrational study evaluating the clinical benefit of dostarlimab monotherapy in pts with dMMR/MSI-H LARC.
  > Methods: Dostarlimab 500 mg was administered IV every 3 weeks for 9 cycles. Key endpoints include rate of sustained cCR for 12 months (cCR12) following end of treatment (EOT) with dostarlimab alone by investigator (INV) and independent central review (ICR), and composite rate of cCR12 (INV) + pathological CR (pCR).
  > Results: At data cutoff (DCO, 20 April 2026), 154 pts were enrolled and median follow-up was 26.3 months (range 2–36). Of 144 pts with ≥12 months follow-up, 68% achieved cCR12 (INV). Of 15 pts who underwent surgery after dostarlimab alone, 12 (80%) had pCR, resulting in a cCR12+pCR rate of 76%. Fewer pts were cCR12-evaluable by ICR, primarily due to cCR being confirmed later by ICR after initial EOT evaluation. Of pts evaluable by ICR, 47% (54/116) achieved cCR12, with 38 pts in cCR and being followed for cCR12 at DCO. 98% of pts who achieved cCR remained in cCR for ≥12 months by INV or ICR, with no recurrences to date for pts who achieved cCR or pCR. Overall, 93% of patients are in surveillance with no evidence of disease progression after treatment (dostarlimab ± SOC) and 71% achieved this outcome with dostarlimab alone. The safety profile was acceptable: 88% of pts completed all 9 cycles of dostarlimab and Grade ≥3 immune-mediated AEs occurred in 4 pts.
  > Conclusions: In this AZUR-1 interim analysis, 68% of pts achieved cCR12 and 76% achieved cCR12+pCR with dostarlimab alone with no disease recurrences to date. These durable responses indicate the potential to eliminate the need for chemotherapy, radiation and surgery, and redefine the curative-intent treatment paradigm for pts with dMMR/MSI-H LARC.

## 🫘 Kidney

Randomized phase II data on systemic IO added to SABR-dominant MDT in oligometastatic RCC.

### PE-PE · [[NCT05578664]]

> mRFS 25.4 vs 11.0 mo, HR 0.47 (0.24-0.92), p=0.024, adding 1 yr perioperative pembrolizumab to MDT (73% SABR) in omRCC.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> SABR was the MDT in 73% vs 72% of pts, so this is largely a SABR-plus-pembrolizumab result. Local relapse, the read that separates SABR in-field control from new-site seeding, is a listed secondary but unreported in the abstract. It reopens the SABR-alone-with-surveillance decision in omRCC.

- 🔍 Randomized, open-label, multicenter phase II; 2:1; N=85 (56 P+MDT vs 29 MDT alone)
- 🔍 omRCC after prior Nx, ≤3 metastases within 5 years of Nx or prior metastasectomy
- 💊 Pembrolizumab 400 mg q6w × 9 cycles (1 yr); first dose given before MDT
- 📊 Outcomes and MDT delivery by arm (median f/u 17.5 mo)

  | Endpoint | P+MDT (n=56) | MDT alone (n=29) | Effect |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Median RFS | 25.4 mo | 11.0 mo | HR 0.47 (95% CI 0.24-0.92), p=0.024 |
  | RFS events | 19 | 16 | n/a |
  | Deaths | 1 | 1 | n/a |
  | Any AE | 80% | 21% | n/a |
  | G3 AE | 7% | 3% | n/a |
  | SABR as MDT | 73% | 72% | n/a |
  | Surgery as MDT | 23% | 28% | n/a |

- 💊 Pembrolizumab exposure and toxicity
  - Median 6.6 cycles delivered of 9 planned
  - Discontinuation: PD in 11 pts, AE in 4 pts
  - P-related AEs any grade 59%
  - Hypothyroidism 23%, fatigue 18%, diarrhea 16%
  - 2 pts G3; no G4 or G5
- ⚠️ Local relapse is a listed secondary endpoint but not reported in the abstract; SABR in-field control unknown
- ⚠️ Only 29 controls and 16 control events; upper CI bound 0.92 sits near the null
- ⚠️ Two deaths total; OS uninformative at this follow-up
- ⚠️ RFS outcomes not split by SABR vs surgery in source
- 🔗 vs KEYNOTE-564: carries the adjuvant pembrolizumab rationale into SABR-ablated, later-recurring oligometastatic disease

**Sources:**
- 📄 **PErioperative PEmbrolizumab (P) plus metastasis-directed therapy (MDT) versus MDT alone in patients (pts) with oligometastatic renal cell carcinoma (omRCC): The randomized phase II PE-PE trial** — Roberto Iacovelli. *ESMO Congress 2026* (2026-10). [article](https://cslide.ctimeetingtech.com/esmo2026/attendee/confcal/presentation/list)
  > ESMO Congress 2026 abstract LBA88 · Proffered paper 2: Genitourinary tumours, renal and urothelial · 2026-10-25 · presented by Roberto Iacovelli (Rome, Italy) · [[NCT05578664]]
  > Background: Adjuvant P is the standard of care for pts with intermediate-high/high risk of relapse after nephrectomy (Nx), as well as for those with M1NED within 1y of previous Nx. Although omRCC pts may receive anti-PD-1-based combos, the optimal management of those eligible for MDT remains uncertain.
  > Methods: The PE-PE trial ([[NCT05578664]]) is a randomized, open-label, multicenter phase II study evaluating the efficacy of P in delaying tumor progression for omRCC pts candidates for MDT, including surgery or SABR to all metastatic sites. Eligible pts had undergone prior Nx and presented with ≤3 metastases within 5 years of Nx or previous metastasectomy. Pts were randomized 2:1 to receive P 400 mg every 6 weeks for 9 cycles (1 year) plus MDT (P+MDT) or MDT alone. The first dose of P was administered before MDT. The primary endpoint was relapse-free survival (RFS), while others included distant RFS, local relapse, OS, and safety.
  > Results: 85 pts were randomized between March 2023 and February 2026: 56 to the P+MDT arm and 29 to the MDT-alone arm. Baseline characteristics were well balanced between treatment groups. SABR was selected in 73% vs. 72% of pts, while surgery was performed in 23% vs. 28%, respectively. The median number of P cycles administered was 6.6. The most frequent reasons for P discontinuation were disease progression (11 pts) and adverse events (AE, 4 pts). After a median follow-up of 17.5 mos, 35 RFS events were observed (19 vs.16), with two deaths (one in each arm). Median RFS was 25.4 mos in the P+MDT arm vs. 11.0 mos in the MDT-alone arm (HR 0.47; 95% CI, 0.24-0.92; p=0.024). Regarding safety, 80% vs. 21% of pts experienced at least one AE, while G3 AEs occurred in 7% vs. 3% of pts in the P+MDT and MDT-alone arms, respectively. P-related AEs of any G occurred in 59%, the most common being hypothyroidism (23%), fatigue (18%), and diarrhea (16%). Two pts experienced G3 toxicity without G4 or 5.
  > Conclusions: The PE-PE trial supports the use of 1 year of perioperative P in pts with omRCC eligible for MDT, demonstrating a statistically significant and clinically meaningful improvement in RFS with a manageable and consistent safety profile.
  > Clinical trial identification: [[NCT05578664]].
