## 🎀 Breast

Surgical de-escalation of the axilla continues to lean on nodal RT rather than replace it.

### SENOMAC · [[NCT02240472]]

> 5yr RFS 89.7% vs 88.7% omitting completion ALND, HR 0.89 (0.66-1.19), noninferior in 1-2 SLN macromets.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The axilla here was irradiated, not left alone: 89.9% vs 88.4% received nodal target volumes, so this validates SLNB plus regional nodal RT, not surgical de-escalation without RT. Untreated non-SLN disease was present in 34.5%, meaning the RT field is carrying real burden. Nodal level doses and volumes are not yet reported.

- 🔍 Phase 3 noninferiority RCT, 67 hospitals in 5 countries, N=2766 enrolled Jan 2015-Dec 2021; per-protocol 2540
- 🔍 Eligible: cN0 T1-T3, one or two sentinel-node MACROmetastases (>2mm); micromets excluded (IBCSG 23-01 settled that)
- 🔍 Extended eligibility vs prior trials: mastectomy allowed (36.7% / 35.7%), T3 tumors (5.5% / 6.1%), extracapsular extension allowed
- 📐 Noninferiority margin: upper CI bound for recurrence/death HR had to be below 1.44; observed 1.19, P<0.001
- 📊 Primary endpoint was OS (changed from BCSS in 2020); this report is the prespecified secondary RFS. 5yr OS 92.9% vs 92.0%, BCSS 97.1% vs 96.6%
- 📊 Recurrence and death by arm (per-protocol, entire follow-up)

  | Event | SLNB only (N=1335) | cALND (N=1205) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Local recurrence | 12 (0.9) | 10 (0.8) |
  | Regional recurrence | 6 (0.4) | 6 (0.5) |
  | Distant recurrence | 44 (3.3) | 53 (4.4) |
  | Death | 62 (4.6) | 69 (5.7) |
  | Recurrence or death as first event | 95 (7.1) | 96 (8.0) |

- ☢️ 89.9% (1192/1326) SLNB-only and 88.4% (1058/1197) dissection pts got RT INCLUDING nodal target volumes. This is an RT-treated axilla, not an untreated one
- ☢️ RT QA: 1154 plans reviewed; eCRF matched plan in 99.3% for breast/chest wall and 96.6% for nodal target volumes. Detailed nodal level doses/volumes not yet reported
- ⚠️ Protocol stipulated NO specific RT target volumes or doses; national guidelines governed. Protocol-prohibited RT was therefore not even assessable
- 🔗 Authors state the result is comparable to AMAROS and OTOASOR (axilla treated with RT), NOT to Z0011, SINODAR-ONE or POSNOC
- ⚠️ Nonsentinel-node disease was NOT rare: 403/1167 (34.5%) had additional non-SLN mets at dissection; 31.3% with 1 macromet, 51.3% with 2. pN2 in 9.9% and pN3 in 3.0% of the dissection arm
- ⚠️ Median f/u 46.8 mo (range 1.5-94.5) with predominantly luminal disease, which recurs late; enrollment fell short of target
- ⚠️ ER+/HER2+ subgroup HR 0.26 (0.07-0.96) favoring SLNB only is biologically implausible as a real effect; 3 vs 10 events, one of 13 subgroups
- ⚠️ No clinical lymphedema measurement; arm morbidity rests on questionnaires, 3yr data for the whole population not yet available

**Sources:**
