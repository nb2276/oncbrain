### SENOMAC · [[NCT02240472]]

> 5-yr RFS 89.7% vs 88.7%, HR 0.89 (0.66-1.19), noninferior omitting completion ALND after 1-2 SN macromets.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Nodal RT is the hidden co-intervention: 89.9% and 88.4% of the two arms got regional nodal irradiation, so this is axillary de-escalation ON TOP OF nodal RT, closer to AMAROS/OTOASOR than to Z0011. Target volumes and doses to specific nodal levels are not reported (detailed RT review ongoing), so the field that made omission safe is undefined.

- 🔍 Phase 3 noninferiority, 67 hospitals in 5 countries, N=2766 enrolled 2015-2021; per-protocol 2540
- 🔍 Eligibility widened vs prior trials: cN0 T1-T3, mastectomy allowed, extracapsular extension allowed, 1-2 SN macromets (>2mm)
- 📐 Noninferiority margin: upper CI bound for recurrence-or-death HR had to be <1.44; observed 1.19, P<0.001
- 📊 Nodal RT in ~89% of both arms (1192/1326 SLNB-only, 1058/1197 dissection); whole-breast RT mandatory after BCS
- ⚠️ Protocol stipulated NO specific nodal target volumes or doses, so this is 'nodal RT as per national guidelines', not a defined field
- 📐 RT quality control: 1154 plans reviewed; eCRF matched plan in 99.3% for breast/chest wall, 96.6% for nodal target volumes
- 📊 pN upstaging in the dissection arm: pN2 in 9.9% and pN3 in 3.0%, vs pN2 0.5% and pN3 0% in the SLNB-only arm
- 📊 Non-SN metastases found in 403/1167 (34.5%) dissected pts; 31.3% with one SN macromet, 51.3% with two
- 🔗 vs Z0011 (BCS + whole-breast tangents only) and AMAROS (axillary RT replaced ALND): SENOMAC excluded micromets, allowed T3 and ECE, and enrolled >1/3 mastectomy pts
- ⚠️ Median f/u 46.8 mo (range 1.5-94.5) in a mostly luminal population that recurs late; 5-yr read is early
- ⚠️ Primary endpoint is OS (switched from BCSS in 2020) and is NOT reported here; this paper reports the secondary RFS endpoint
- 📊 Recurrence + death by type (per-protocol, SLNB-only vs ALND)

  | Event | SLNB only (N=1335) | ALND (N=1205) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Local recurrence | 12 (0.9) | 10 (0.8) |
  | Regional recurrence | 6 (0.4) | 6 (0.5) |
  | Distant recurrence | 44 (3.3) | 53 (4.4) |
  | Death | 62 (4.6) | 69 (5.7) |
  | Recurrence or death as first event | 95 (7.1) | 96 (8.0) |

- 📊 Subgroup HRs for recurrence or death (SLNB only vs ALND), all crossing 1.00 except ER+/HER2+

  | Subgroup | Events SLNB only | Events ALND | HR (95% CI) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | T3 | 5/73 | 10/74 | 0.47 (0.16-1.39) |
  | Mastectomy | 41/490 | 45/430 | 0.79 (0.52-1.21) |
  | Breast-conserving | 48/845 | 46/775 | 0.98 (0.65-1.47) |
  | Extracapsular extension | 34/461 | 31/409 | 0.94 (0.58-1.54) |
  | 2 macrometastases | 13/192 | 19/197 | 0.79 (0.39-1.59) |
  | ER-positive, HER2-positive | 3/84 | 10/88 | 0.26 (0.07-0.96) |

- ⚠️ The ER+/HER2+ subgroup 'favoring' omission (HR 0.26, 13 events total) is a fragile subgroup finding, not a signal to act on
- ⚠️ Only 10 male pts enrolled over 7 yrs, so the stated aim of covering men was not met and no sex subgroup was possible
