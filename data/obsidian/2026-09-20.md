### Gamma Knife vs LINAC-based radiosurgery for vestibular schwannoma (retrospective review)

> Local control 98.8%; no difference in new vertigo, gait disturbance or facial palsy with GKRS (n=36) vs LINAC (n=49) SRS for vestibular schwannoma.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The 98.8% local control is freedom from further VS-directed intervention; volumetric enlargement still occurred in 6/36 GKRS and 9/49 LINAC pts. The LINAC arm pools margin-free 12 Gy to the 80-99% IDL with post-May 2017 frameless 12 Gy plus a 5 mm PTV margin, era split unreported, so it cannot say which LINAC approach matches GK.

- 🔍 Retrospective, multi-site single-institution chart review, 2008-2017; N=85 (36 GKRS, 49 LINAC)
- 🔍 Platform chosen by provider preference; GKRS and LINAC sat at different centers
- 🔍 Single-fraction SRS technique by platform
  - GKRS: 12.5 Gy to 50% IDL, stereotactic head frame
  - LINAC pre-May 2017: 12 Gy to 80-99% IDL, no CTV/PTV margin, dynamic conformal arc or IMRS
  - LINAC post-May 2017: frameless mask, 12 Gy to 100% IDL, 5 mm PTV margin, VMAT
  - Split of LINAC pts between the two eras not reported
- 📊 New post-SRS symptoms in pts free of that symptom at baseline

  | New symptom | GKRS | LINAC | p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Tinnitus | 1/13 (7.7%) | 1/19 (5.5%) | 0.0007 (MVA) |
  | Vertigo | 1/28 (3.6%) | 2/31 (6.5%) | 0.785 |
  | Gait disturbance | 4/18 (22.3%) | 4/26 (15.4%) | 1.000 |
  | Facial palsy (HB ≥3) | 0/34 (0.0%) | 2/48 (4.2%) | 0.509 |

- ⚠️ Tinnitus p=0.0007 (MVA) rests on 1 new event per arm; not a usable signal
- 📊 Tumor response by platform (direction of size change p=0.199)

  | Tumor outcome | GKRS (n=36) | LINAC (n=49) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Decreased or stable | 30/36 | 40/49 (81.6%) |
  | Increased size | 6/36 (16.7%) | 9/49 (18.4%) |
  | Salvage required | None | 1/49 (2.0%) |

- 📐 Median f/u 27 mo; mean 24 mo GKRS vs 36 mo LINAC
- ⚠️ Baseline imbalance by platform
  - Extracanalicular-only: 25.0% GKRS vs 0.0% LINAC
  - Brainstem mass effect: 19.4% vs 4.1%
  - Mean GTV 1.1 vs 0.6 cc, p=0.072
- ⚠️ Hearing not reported, though cochlear dose was collected
