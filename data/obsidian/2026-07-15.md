# 2026-07-15

**STAMPEDE, high-risk non-metastatic prostate: 2yr abiraterone ± enzalutamide + ADT → MFS HR 0.53 (0.44-0.64), OS HR 0.60 (0.48-0.73); enzalutamide adds nothing.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Prostate carries the day: pooled STAMPEDE IPD in high-risk non-metastatic disease shows 6-yr OS 86% vs 77% adding 2yr abiraterone to ADT. MFS benefit held whether RT was planned (HR 0.54) or not (HR 0.51); 85% planned RT. Abi+enza arm: grade 3+ AEs 58% vs 32%, with no extra MFS benefit.

## 🌰 Prostate

RT-backbone population: 85% planned prostate RT (74 Gy/37 fx or hypofractionated equivalent), mandated for N0.

### STAMPEDE abiraterone ± enzalutamide in high-risk non-metastatic prostate cancer (meta-analysis) · [[NCT00268476]]

> MFS HR 0.53 (0.44-0.64) and OS HR 0.60 (0.48-0.73) adding 2yr abiraterone ± enzalutamide to ADT in high-risk non-metastatic prostate cancer.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The MFS benefit held whether or not RT was planned: HR 0.54 (0.44-0.67) with RT vs 0.51 (0.34-0.76) without, interaction p=0.67. So abiraterone adds to prostate RT and does not stand in for it. The RT was 74 Gy/37 fx to prostate + SVs, and the source does not describe pelvic nodal fields for the 39% who were N+.

- 📊 1° EP MFS: 6-yr 82% vs 69%, HR 0.53 (95% CI 0.44-0.64), p<0.0001
- 📊 OS: 6-yr 86% vs 77%, HR 0.60 (95% CI 0.48-0.73), p<0.0001
- 📊 Efficacy, combination therapy vs ADT alone (pooled IPD)

  | Endpoint | Combination | Control | HR (95% CI) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | MFS (1°) | 6-yr 82% | 6-yr 69% | 0.53 (0.44-0.64), p<0.0001 |
  | OS | 6-yr 86% | 6-yr 77% | 0.60 (0.48-0.73), p<0.0001 |
  | Prostate cancer-specific survival | median not reached | median not reached | 0.49 (0.37-0.65), p<0.0001 |
  | Biochemical failure-free survival | median not reached | median 86 mo | 0.39 (0.33-0.47), p<0.0001 |
  | PFS | median not reached | median not reached | 0.44 (0.36-0.54), p<0.0001 |

- 💊 Adding enzalutamide gave no extra MFS benefit: interaction HR 1.02 (0.70-1.50), p=0.91
- 📐 MFS HR by prespecified subgroup

  | Subgroup | HR (95% CI) | p interaction |
  |---|---|---|
  | RT planned: yes | 0.54 (0.44-0.67) | 0.67 |
  | RT planned: no | 0.51 (0.34-0.76) | 0.67 |
  | N0 | 0.60 (0.46-0.78) | 0.22 |
  | N+ | 0.49 (0.38-0.64) | 0.22 |
  | WHO PS 0 | 0.47 (0.38-0.58) | 0.00656 |
  | WHO PS 1-2 | 0.86 (0.58-1.28) | 0.00656 |
  | NSAID/aspirin: no | 0.62 (0.51-0.77) | 0.0052 |
  | NSAID/aspirin: yes | 0.32 (0.21-0.48) | 0.0052 |

- 🔍 RT per local guidelines: 74 Gy/37 fx to prostate + SVs or hypofractionated equivalent
- 🔍 RT mandated for N0, encouraged for N+; 1684 (85%) planned RT
- ⚠️ Grade 3+ AEs in first 24 mo

  | AE | Abi trial: combo | Abi trial: control | Abi+enza trial: combo | Abi+enza trial: control |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Any grade 3+ | 37% | 29% | 58% | 32% |
  | Hypertension | 5% | 1% | 14% | 2% |
  | ALT increase | 6% | <1% | 13% | 1% |

- ⚠️ Grade 5 AEs: 0 control, 3 abiraterone, 4 abiraterone + enzalutamide
- ⚠️ Primary endpoint switched from OS to MFS, prespecified before inspecting efficacy by arm
- ⚠️ Open-label; controls could get 2nd-gen hormonal agents for nmCRPC, which would bias OS toward null
