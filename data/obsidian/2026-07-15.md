# 2026-07-15

**STAMPEDE meta-analysis: 2yr abiraterone + ADT/RT in high-risk M0 cuts metastasis risk near half (MFS HR 0.53, OS HR 0.60); enza adds toxicity only.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Prostate carries the day, and it lands squarely on the RT clinic: 85% of the 1974 pts had RT planned (74Gy/37fx or hypofx equivalent), so this is an intensification question layered on definitive local therapy, not a systemic-only one. 6yr MFS 82% vs 69%, OS 86% vs 77%. Enza on top: interaction HR 1.02, G3+ 58% vs 37%.

## 🌰 Prostate

High-risk M0 disease treated with definitive RT + ADT: the open question is which agent to add, and at what toxicity cost.

### STAMPEDE (abiraterone ± enzalutamide, high-risk non-metastatic) · [[NCT00268476]]

> MFS HR 0.53 (0.44-0.64) and OS HR 0.60 adding 2yr abiraterone to ADT+RT in high-risk M0; enzalutamide adds nothing.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> This is an RT-population trial: 85% were planned for 74Gy/37fx or hypofractionated equivalent, and benefit was identical whether RT was planned (HR 0.54, 0.44-0.67) or not (0.51, 0.34-0.76). So the 2yr abiraterone layers onto 3yr ADT plus definitive RT without an RT-related interaction, and N+ pts (39% of cohort) did at least as well (HR 0.49) as N0.

- 🔍 Two open-label phase 3 STAMPEDE trials, non-overlapping controls, IPD meta-analysis; N=1974, 113 UK/Swiss sites, median f/u 72mo
- 🔍 RT mandated for N0, encouraged for N+: 74Gy/37fx to prostate + SV or hypofractionated equivalent
- 📊 RT planned in 1684 (85%) of 1974; 99% of newly diagnosed N0, 71% of N+, 7% of previously treated
- 📐 6yr MFS 82% (79-85) combination vs 69% (66-72) control; 6yr OS 86% vs 77%
- 📊 Secondary endpoints, combination vs control (all p<0.0001)
- 📊 MFS by prespecified stratification subgroup (forest, Fig 3)
- 📐 Enza adds nothing: interaction HR 1.02 (0.70-1.50), p=0.91; between-trial heterogeneity I² p=0.90
- ⚠️ Enza costs toxicity without efficacy (G3+ within 24mo, combination vs control)
  - Abi trial: 169/451 (37%) vs 130/455 (29%)
  - Abi+enza trial: 298/513 (58%) vs 172/533 (32%)
  - Hypertension: 23 (5%) abi vs 73 (14%) abi+enza
  - ALT rise: 25 (6%) abi vs 69 (13%) abi+enza
  - Grade 5: 3 in abi group, 4 in abi+enza group, none in controls
- ⚠️ Open label; toxicity reported only during first 2yrs of treatment; subsequent-therapy data called unreliable by authors
- ⚠️ Relapsing-after-local-therapy pts under-represented; authors flag benefit uncertain in that group
- ⚠️ PS 1-2 and NSAID/aspirin heterogeneity (p=0.0052) has no biological explanation; authors say it should not change management
