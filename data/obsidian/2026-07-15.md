# 2026-07-15

**STAMPEDE: 2yr abiraterone on top of ADT+RT cuts metastasis risk nearly in half (MFS HR 0·53), 6-yr MFS 82% vs 69%; enzalutamide adds toxicity, not benefit.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Prostate carried the day. In high-risk non-metastatic disease treated with ADT + 74 Gy/37 fx (85% planned for RT), 2yr abiraterone improves MFS HR 0·53 and OS HR 0·60, with no interaction by planned RT (p=0·67) or nodal status (p=0·22). Adding enzalutamide doubles G3+ toxicity (58% vs 37%) for no efficacy gain.

## 🌰 Prostate

Intensifying the systemic arm around definitive RT, with the enzalutamide question answered in the negative.

### STAMPEDE (abiraterone ± enzalutamide, high-risk non-metastatic) · [[NCT00268476]]

> MFS HR 0·53 (0·44–0·64), 6-yr MFS 82% vs 69%, adding 2yr abiraterone to ADT+RT; enzalutamide adds nothing.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The RT-relevant read is that 85% of pts were planned for radiotherapy (74 Gy/37 fx to prostate and SV, or hypofractionated equivalent), so this benefit sits on top of definitive local treatment, not instead of it. MFS gain held whether RT was planned or not (HR 0·54 vs 0·51, p-interaction 0·67), which moves the systemic intensification decision at the RT consult.

- 🔍 Two phase 3 trials in the STAMPEDE platform, 113 UK/Swiss sites, N=1974 non-metastatic, no overlapping controls, IPD meta-analysed
- 🔍 RT mandated for N0, encouraged for N+: 74 Gy/37 fx to prostate + SV or hypofractionated equivalent
- 🔍 1684 (85%) of 1974 planned for local RT; 99% of node-negative, 71% of node-positive newly diagnosed pts
- 📊 6-yr OS 86% combination vs 77% control; OS HR 0·60 (0·48–0·73), p<0·0001
- 📊 Secondary endpoints, combination vs ADT alone (HR, 95% CI)

  | Endpoint | HR | 95% CI | p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Overall survival | 0·60 | 0·48–0·73 | <0·0001 |
  | Prostate cancer-specific survival | 0·49 | 0·37–0·65 | <0·0001 |
  | Biochemical failure-free survival | 0·39 | 0·33–0·47 | <0·0001 |
  | Progression-free survival | 0·44 | 0·36–0·54 | <0·0001 |

- 📐 MFS by planned RT: RT yes HR 0·54 (0·44–0·67), RT no HR 0·51 (0·34–0·76), p-interaction 0·67
- 📐 MFS by nodal status: N0 HR 0·60 (0·46–0·78), N+ HR 0·49 (0·38–0·64), p-interaction 0·22
- 💊 Adding enzalutamide gave no extra efficacy: interaction HR 1·02 (0·70–1·50), p=0·91, I² p=0·90
- ⚠️ Enzalutamide added toxicity without benefit (abi trial vs abi+enza trial, G3+)
  - Any G3+ over 24 mo: 169/451 (37%) vs 298/513 (58%)
  - Hypertension: 23 (5%) vs 73 (14%)
  - Fatigue: 10 (2%) vs 49 (10%)
  - Aminotransferase rise: 25 (5%) vs 69 (13%)
- ⚠️ Open label, and control pts could access 2nd-gen hormones in nmCRPC trials or, since 2019, as SOC
- ⚠️ Toxicity reported only for the first 24 months of treatment; late effects not captured
- ⚠️ MFS heterogeneity by WHO PS (p=0·00656) and NSAID/aspirin use (p=0·0052); authors call the latter biologically unexplained
