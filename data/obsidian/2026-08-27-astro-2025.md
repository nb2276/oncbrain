# 2026-08-27 — ASTRO Annual Meeting 2025

**MARCAP IPD meta-analysis (10,853 pts, 11.4-yr f/u): ADT added to RT improves MFS HR 0.83 and OS HR 0.86; dose escalation moves bRFS only.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Prostate carried the day, and the message is where to spend effort: ADT intensification, not Gy. Relative ADT benefit holds across NCCN risk (absolute benefit larger in high risk, NNT 8.4 vs 18.0 for 10-yr distant mets), and is invariant to RT dose (≥74 Gy HR 0.83, <74 Gy HR 0.83). Adjuvant prolongation to 18-36mo pays; neoadjuvant prolongation does not (MFS HR 0.95).

## 🌰 Prostate

Conference presentation of an IPD meta-analysis reframing the dose-vs-ADT question for definitive RT: the bRFS gains from escalation don't translate to MFS or OS.

### MARCAP Consortium

> IPD meta-analysis, 10,853 pts: ADT added to RT improved MFS HR 0.83 and OS HR 0.86; dose escalation improved bRFS only.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The dose-invariance result is the load-bearing one: ADT's benefit was identical at ≥74 Gy and <74 Gy (both HR 0.83), so escalating dose does not buy out the ADT decision. Adjuvant prolongation carries the benefit (MFS HR 0.84), neoadjuvant prolongation does not (HR 0.95), which moves sequencing, not just duration.

- 🔍 IPD meta-analysis, 12 eligible trials, N=10,853, median f/u 11.4 yrs (IQR 9.0-15.0)
- 📊 ADT addition: MFS HR 0.83 (0.77-0.89), NNT 11; OS HR 0.86 (0.80-0.92), NNT 12.5
- 📊 Adjuvant ADT prolongation (4-6mo → 18-36mo): MFS HR 0.84 (0.78-0.91), NNT 12.5; OS HR 0.85 (0.78-0.94), NNT 16.7
- 📊 Neoadjuvant ADT prolongation (3-4mo → 6-9mo): MFS HR 0.95 (0.83-1.09), OS HR 0.95 (0.82-1.10). No benefit
- 📊 ADT absolute benefit by NCCN risk (NNT, 10-yr distant metastasis)

  | Intervention | Intermediate risk | High risk |
  |---|---|---|
  | ADT addition | 18.0 | 8.4 |
  | Adjuvant ADT prolongation | 16.1 | 10.4 |

- 🔍 Relative benefit of ADT similar irrespective of NCCN risk group; absolute benefit greater in high risk
- 📐 ADT benefit invariant to RT dose: ≥74 Gy HR 0.83, <74 Gy HR 0.83
- 🔗 HEAT network meta-analysis (13 trials, 11,862 pts): dose escalation to 79.2 Gy alone or with ADT improved biochemical RFS but not MFS or OS
- 🔗 High-dose RT + long-term ADT named the best strategy in HEAT's six-permutation network
- ⚠️ The bRFS-to-MFS disconnect is risk-group dependent: 5-yr biochemical benefit 21% in low risk (PROG 05/09) where few pts metastasize, only 3% in high risk (GETUG-18)
- ⚠️ Conference presentation of published + accepted-but-unpublished analyses (DHARMA duration data not yet public); no new randomised data here
