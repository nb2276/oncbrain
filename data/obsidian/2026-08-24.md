# 2026-08-24

**STAR-TREC: 12-mo TME-free survival 78.5% vs 60.6% with SCRT, HR 1.90 (1.29-2.81) — long-course chemoRT is the organ-preservation backbone in mrT1-T3bN0 rectal.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Lower GI carried the day: 50 Gy/25 fx + capecitabine beat 25 Gy/5 fx for organ preservation at 12 months (cCR 64% vs 36%), at the cost of more early grade ≥3 SAEs (6% vs 1%). Caveat: the 30-mo primary endpoint is still unreported, and 22% of TME specimens were node-positive despite node-negative baseline MRI.

## 🌀 GI Lower

Fractionation, not the decision to preserve, is what moved: long-course chemoRT doubled complete response over short-course in the watch-and-wait pathway.

### STAR-TREC

> 12-mo TME-free survival 78.5% with LCCRT vs 60.6% with SCRT, HR 1.90 (1.29-2.81), in mrT1-T3bN0 rectal cancer.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> For an RT reader the actionable number is the response gradient: cCR 64% with 50 Gy/25 fx plus capecitabine vs 36% with 25 Gy/5 fx, which is what drives the 78.5% vs 60.6% TME-free survival. Acute SAE grade ≥3 within 4 weeks of RT ran higher with LCCRT (6% vs 1%), so the trade is upfront toxicity for organ preservation.

- 📊 12-mo TME-free survival 78.5% (95% CI 72.4-85.1) LCCRT-OP vs 60.6% (53.6-68.4) SCRT-OP
- 📐 HR 1.90 (95% CI 1.29-2.81); posterior probability of LCCRT-OP superiority >99.5%
- 🔍 Open-label phase 2/3, 37 sites in five European countries, 503 enrolled; 409 in mITT (163 LCCRT-OP, 168 SCRT-OP, 78 primary TME)
- 🔍 RT dose: LCCRT-OP 50 Gy in 25 fx + capecitabine 825 mg/m² twice daily; SCRT-OP 25 Gy in five fractions
- 📊 cCR at second response assessment (16-20 weeks) 104 (64%) LCCRT-OP vs 61 (36%) SCRT-OP
- 📊 TAE for near-complete response more frequent after SCRT-OP: 38 (23%) LCCRT-OP vs 68 (40%) SCRT-OP
- 📊 Grade 3-4 serious adverse events by group

  | Event | LCCRT-OP | SCRT-OP | Primary TME |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Gastrointestinal disorders | 4 (2%) | 6 (4%) | 6 (8%) |
  | Procedural complications | 3 (2%) | 5 (3%) | 5 (6%) |

- 📐 SAE grade ≥3 within 4 wks of finishing RT: 9 (6%) of 163 LCCRT-OP vs 2 (1%) of 168 SCRT-OP
- ⚠️ Prespecified 30-month organ-preservation primary endpoint is NOT reported here; this is a governance-approved 12-month implementation analysis
- ⚠️ Phase 2 signal was far larger (HR 3.7 [1.7-8.0]) than the pooled 12-mo estimate, and post-hoc analyses of the phase 2 vs phase 3 difference were needed
- ⚠️ Phase 3 used a partially randomised patient-preference design: TME comparison is by choice, not randomisation, so TME-vs-OP toxicity contrasts are confounded
- ⚠️ Baseline MRI excluded nodal disease yet 17 (22%) of TME specimens had positive nodes, so nodal understaging is real in this population
