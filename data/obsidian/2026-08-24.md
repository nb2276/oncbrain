# 2026-08-24

**STAR-TREC: 12-mo TME-free survival 78.5% vs 60.6% (HR 1.90) with 50 Gy/25 fx LCCRT over 25 Gy/5 fx SCRT for organ preservation in mrT1-T3bN0 rectal ≤40 mm.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Lower GI carried the day: in mrT1-T3bN0 rectal cancer ≤40 mm, 50 Gy/25 fx LCCRT raised cCR 64% vs 36% over 25 Gy/5 fx SCRT, favouring long-course CRT when organ preservation is the goal. Interim 12-mo read; 30-mo organ-preservation primary pending, and 22% of TME specimens were node-positive despite MRI N0.

## 🌀 GI Lower

Organ preservation in MRI node-negative early rectal cancer: randomised data on which RT schedule to pair with a watch-and-wait strategy.

### STAR-TREC

> 12-mo TME-free survival 78.5% vs 60.6% (HR 1.90) favouring LCCRT over SCRT for organ preservation in mrT1-T3bN0 rectal cancer ≤40 mm.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> RT delivery does not explain the gap: completion was 99% with LCCRT and 100% with SCRT, with 18 (11%) needing a capecitabine dose change, so the difference tracks fractionation plus concurrent chemo, which this design cannot separate. Nodes in 22% of TME specimens despite MRI N0 make mesorectal coverage relevant; target volume is not in source text.

- 📊 Organ-preservation arms at 12 mo, mITT

  | Endpoint | LCCRT-OP (n=163) | SCRT-OP (n=168) | Effect |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | TME-free survival | 78.5% (95% CI 72.4-85.1) | 60.6% (53.6-68.4) | HR 1.90 (1.29-2.81), posterior P >99.5% |
  | cCR at 16-20 wk | 104 (64%) | 61 (36%) | not reported |
  | TAE for near-cCR | 38 (23%) | 68 (40%) | not reported |

- 💊 RT schedules
  - LCCRT-OP: 50 Gy/25 fx + oral capecitabine 825 mg/m² twice daily
  - SCRT-OP: 25 Gy/5 fx
  - RT completion 159/161 (99%) LCCRT vs 168 (100%) SCRT
- 🔍 Open-label randomised phase 2/3, 37 sites, 5 European countries, Bayesian framework
  - Phase 2: 1:1:1 LCCRT-OP vs SCRT-OP vs primary TME, N=120
  - Phase 3: patient-preference design; OP choosers randomised 1:1
  - 264 of 306 phase 3 pts (86%) chose OP
  - 409 mITT: 163 LCCRT-OP, 168 SCRT-OP, 78 primary TME
- 🔍 Eligible: rectal adenocarcinoma ≤40 mm, mrT1-T3bN0, no EMVI, ECOG 0-1
- 📊 Serious AEs by arm

  | Endpoint | LCCRT-OP | SCRT-OP | Primary TME |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | G3-4 GI SAEs | 4 (2%) | 6 (4%) | 6 (8%) |
  | G3-4 procedural SAEs | 3 (2%) | 5 (3%) | 5 (6%) |
  | G≥3 SAEs within 4 wk of RT/CRT | 9/163 (6%) | 2/168 (1%) | n/a |

- ⚠️ Primary TME morbidity (n=78)
  - G≥3 SAEs 14 (18%)
  - Intraperitoneal perforation from anastomotic leak 12 (15%)
  - 1 postoperative death, sepsis after anastomotic leak
- ⚠️ Nodes positive in 17 (22%) TME specimens despite MRI N0; OP arms never sampled
- ⚠️ Interim 12-mo read; 1° EP is 30-mo organ preservation (no TME, stoma, or local recurrence)
- ⚠️ Phase 2 HR 3.7 (1.7-8.0) contracted to 1.90 once phase 3 pooled
- 🔗 OPRA tested watch-and-wait in locally advanced disease; STAR-TREC tests earlier, MRI node-negative tumours
