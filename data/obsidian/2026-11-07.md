# 2026-11-07

**Dose-adapted single-isocenter SRS for 4-10 BM: 22 of 40 pts outlived DS-GPA prediction (bar was 17), 1-yr local failure 5%, no G3-5 AEs.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> All-CNS day on SRS technique for brain mets. The prospective dose-adapted SIMT trial (4-10 BM) cleared its DS-GPA survival bar with 1-yr local failure 5%; retrospective Minniti and HyperArc series report 1-yr LC 89% and 90.1%. The planning comparisons trade V12Gy against low-dose spill and report dosimetric surrogates only.

## 🧠 CNS

Five SRS technique studies for BM; only the dose-adapted SIMT trial is prospective, and the planning comparisons carry no clinical endpoints.

### DCAT vs CoVMAT vs NcVMAT for single brain metastasis (planning study)

> Non-coplanar VMAT gave the lowest normal-brain V12Gy (9.4 vs 11.6 DCAT vs 15.1 cm3 coplanar VMAT) for 2-3 cm single BM SRS plans.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Coplanar-only VMAT, the default at many LINAC centers, was the worst of the three for normal brain, with V12Gy 15.1 vs 11.6 cm3 for DCAT and V5Gy 96.8 vs 49.2 cm3. The gain from VMAT comes from the couch arcs, not modulation alone, which bears on technique choice for 2-3 cm lesions where V12Gy drives radionecrosis risk.

- 🔍 Planning study, 15 pts with single BM, longest diameter 2-3 cm, single institution (Kyoto)
- 🔍 Each pt re-planned 3 ways: DCAT, coplanar VMAT (1 arc), non-coplanar VMAT (1 coplanar + 2 couch arcs)
- 🔍 Planning parameters (identical across arms)
  - 20 Gy x 1 to PTV, normalized to D99.5%
  - PTV = GTV + 1mm; median PTV 6.4 cm3 (range 3.7-16.2)
  - 6 MV TrueBeam STx; Eclipse 15.6, AcurosXB, 1mm grid
  - VMAT plans used a ring contour + Normal Tissue Objective
- 📊 Normal-brain irradiated volume (cm3, mean ± SD)

  | Index | DCAT | CoVMAT | NcVMAT | p NcVMAT vs DCAT |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | V20Gy | 2.44 ± 1.78 | 2.48 ± 1.87 | 1.64 ± 1.13 | <0.05 |
  | V15Gy | 7.22 ± 4.08 | 8.52 ± 4.89 | 5.95 ± 3.10 | <0.05 |
  | V12Gy | 11.6 ± 6.5 | 15.1 ± 8.2 | 9.4 ± 4.9 | <0.05 |
  | V10Gy | 16.8 ± 9.0 | 22.7 ± 12.0 | 13.9 ± 6.8 | <0.05 |
  | V5Gy | 49.2 ± 28.4 | 96.8 ± 55.4 | 42.9 ± 20.3 | <0.05 |

- 📊 RTOG-CI and Paddick CI better with NcVMAT vs DCAT; GTV D98% higher with NcVMAT vs both
- ⚠️ Coplanar-only VMAT was worse than DCAT on V15Gy through V5Gy; low-dose spill was the largest at V5Gy
- 📐 Beam-on time longer for both VMAT techniques vs DCAT; MUs lower with CoVMAT
- ⚠️ Dosimetric surrogate only: no local control, radionecrosis, or neurocognitive outcome measured
- 🔗 Molinier et al. compared the same 3 techniques across mixed intracranial lesions but with different TPS/algorithms; here one TPS used throughout

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Dosimetric comparison among dynamic conformal arc therapy, coplanar and non-coplanar volumetric modulated arc therapy for single brain metastasis** — Torizuka, Daichi; Uto, Megumi; Takehana, Keiichi et al.. *Journal of Radiation Research* (2021-10). [doi:10.1093/jrr/rrab092](https://doi.org/10.1093/jrr/rrab092)
  > Abstract In the delivery of stereotactic radiosurgery (SRS) by linear accelerator (LINAC), dynamic conformal arc therapy (DCAT) with non-coplanar beams is conventionally used. However, volumetric modulated arc therapy (VMAT) can improve target conformity, thereby decreasing the dose to organs at risk by inversed planning methods, but few studies have directly compared DCAT and VMAT with and without non-coplanar beams in patients with single brain metastasis. We therefore conducted a planning study to compare the dose distribution in DCAT, VMAT using only a coplanar arc (CoVMAT) and VMAT with non-coplanar arcs (NcVMAT) in the treatment of single brain metastasis. DCAT, CoVMAT and NcVMAT plans were created for 15 patients. The three modalities were compared in terms of target conformity, target coverage, the dose to normal brain tissue, monitor units (MUs) and beam-on time. Both conformity indices (RTOG-CI and IP-CI) as well as the D98% of the gross target volume (GTV) were significantly better in the NcVMAT plans than in the DCAT plans. Comparisons of the doses to normal brain tissue revealed that the V20Gy, V15Gy, V12Gy, V10Gy and V5Gy were significantly smaller in the NcVMAT plans than in the plans based on the other two modalities. The MUs of the DCAT and NcVMAT plans were larger than those of the CoVMAT plans, and the beam-on time was longer in the NcVMAT and CoVMAT plans than in the DCAT plans. Compared to the CoVMAT and DCAT plans, NcVMAT plans significantly improved target conformity and reduced the doses to normal brain tissue at V20Gy, V15Gy, V12Gy, V10Gy and V5Gy.

### Elements MME vs RapidArc single-isocenter SRS plan quality

> RapidArc VMAT gave lower V12Gy (19.2 vs 23.7 cm3) and better conformity; Elements MME gave lower V5Gy and mean brain dose (2.57 vs 2.76 Gy).

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The V12Gy advantage for RapidArc depends on total target volume: every case where MME matched or beat it had total PTV under 2.1 cm3, and above that threshold the V12 ratio averaged 1.24. V12Gy is the metric tied to radionecrosis, so for multi-met SRS the planning platform matters most in larger-volume cases.

- 🔍 Two-institution treatment planning study: 30 pts, each replanned with both techniques (60 plans)
- 🔍 4 to 10 brain mets per pt (217 targets, median 7.5); total PTV median 7.05 cm3
- 🔍 Each center planned its own technique: TJU Elements MME v1.5, UAB Eclipse RapidArc 13.6
- 📐 Technique parameters
  - Rx 14 to 24 Gy single fraction, normalized to D99% = Rx
  - MME: 4 to 9 noncoplanar DCAs, 6 MV FFF, pencil-beam calc
  - RA: 2 to 4 noncoplanar VMAT arcs, 10 MV FFF, AAA calc
  - Both HD120 MLC, 1 mm dose grid, DVH analysis in third-party MIM
- 📊 Plan metrics, medians (paired Wilcoxon)

  | Metric | Elements MME | RapidArc | P |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | RTOG CI (per target) | 1.38 | 1.23 | <.0001 |
  | Paddick CI (per target) | 0.70 | 0.77 | <.0001 |
  | V12Gy (cm3) | 23.7 | 19.2 | <.0001 |
  | V8Gy (cm3) | 53.6 | 44.1 | .024 |
  | V5Gy (cm3) | 141.4 | 142.8 | .009 |
  | Mean brain dose (Gy) | 2.57 | 2.76 | <.0001 |

- 📊 RA lower V12Gy in 26 of 30 cases; MME lower V5Gy in 25 of 30
- 📊 Exceptions to the V12Gy pattern were all small total PTV (<2.1 cm3); there MME won V12Gy in 4 of 6
- 📊 Brain DVHs cross near 6 Gy: RA favorable above, MME favorable below
- 📊 Mean coverage 97.5% MME vs 96.9% RA (P=.007)
- 💊 Delivery: MME 7.3 arcs, 6871 MU, est. beam-on 6 to 9 min; RA 3.8 arcs, 10225 MU, est. 3 to 4 min
- 🔗 vs Gevaert (10 pts, 40 mets): Paddick CI 0.65/0.67 there vs 0.70/0.77 here, suggesting under-optimized plans in that series
- 🔗 vs Narayanasamy (8 pts, 40 targets): found the techniques comparable, likely underpowered
- ⚠️ Beam energy (6 vs 10 MV FFF) and planning institution are confounded with technique
- ⚠️ Dosimetric surrogates only; no radionecrosis, control or cognitive outcomes
- ⚠️ Abstract and Results disagree on RA median RTOG CI (1.21 vs 1.23)
- ⚠️ Several authors hold Varian research support or consulting ties

### Single-isocenter SRS for multiple brain mets with automated planning (Minniti)

> 1-yr LC 89%, 1-yr OS 68%, no G3-4 toxicity after single-isocenter DCA SRS for 4-10 BM; a 1-mm GTV-to-PTV margin kept dose coverage on every lesion.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Uncorrected intrafraction shifts dropped GTV coverage in 21 (28%) of 74 lesions, so the 1-mm GTV-to-PTV margin is only valid alongside 6D intrafraction imaging and correction. Coverage loss clustered in lesions <0.40 cm3 and ≥39 mm from isocenter, which is where the margin decision matters most.

- 🔍 Retrospective, 2 Italian centers, 31 consecutive pts, 204 BM <3 cm, Oct 2016 to Sep 2018
- 🔍 Median clinical f/u 11 mo (range 4-26); 13 (42%) pts alive at data cutoff
- 💊 Systemic therapy given at the time of SRS in 9 pts (IO n=4, targeted n=5)
- 📐 Technique
  - Brainlab Elements Multiple Brain Mets SRS v1.5, automated inverse-optimized DCA
  - 10 noncoplanar DCA beams at 5 preset couch positions, isocenter at center of mass of all targets
  - 20-22 Gy for lesions <2 cm; 16-18 Gy for ≥2 cm or near brainstem/chiasm
  - PTV = GTV + 1 mm (n=176) or 2 mm (n=28)
  - Frameless mask, ExacTrac 6D kV, tolerance 0.5 mm / 0.5°
  - Median conformity index 1.32, gradient index 3.94
- 📊 Local control and survival (KM)

  | Endpoint | 1-yr | 2-yr |
  |---|---|---|
  | LC, per patient | 89% | 77% |
  | LC, per lesion | 94% | 86% |
  | OS | 68% | 40% |

- 📊 Median OS 16.1 mo; distant brain failure median 8 mo, 1-yr rate 38%
- 📊 Salvage after brain progression: further SRS in 15 pts, WBRT in 3
- 📊 Coverage loss (V95 <95%) from residual setup error
  - Mean 3D residual error 0.34 mm (range 0.1-0.53)
  - 28 targets lost coverage; 7 lesions <39 mm vs 21 lesions ≥39 mm from isocenter (P=.0001)
  - 20 lesions <0.40 cm3 vs 8 lesions ≥0.40 cc (P=.015)
  - 1-mm margin restored coverage for all lesions; 0-mm simulation left 18 lesions compromised
- 📊 Intrafraction: 10 positionings in 7 (22%) pts out of tolerance, max 0.8° and 0.7 mm
- 📊 Uncorrected intrafraction shifts cost GTV coverage in 21 (28%) of 74 lesions; 1-mm margin held all but 2
- 📊 Safety
  - No G3-4 acute or late toxicity
  - Radionecrosis in 5 (16.1%) pts at median 10 mo, G2 motor deficit in 2
  - Per-lesion radionecrosis risk 11% at 12 mo
  - Individual V12Gy >10 cm3 correlated with radionecrosis (P=.01); lesion number and cumulative volume did not
- 📐 Simulated PTV for a spherical lesion by GTV-to-PTV margin (paper's Table 4)

  | Lesion diameter | 0 mm | 1 mm | 2 mm | 3 mm |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 0.5 cm | 0.07 cm3 | 0.18 cm3 | 0.38 cm3 | 0.7 cm3 |
  | 1 cm | 0.52 cm3 | 0.9 cm3 | 1.43 cm3 | 2.21 cm3 |
  | 1.5 cm | 1.76 cm3 | 2.57 cm3 | 3.59 cm3 | 4.85 cm3 |
  | 2 cm | 4.18 cm3 | 5.57 cm3 | 7.23 cm3 | 9.2 cm3 |

- 🔗 vs Korytko gamma knife series (cited in paper): RN 55.3% for V12Gy >10 cm3 vs 22.5% below, 198 tumors
- ⚠️ Internal inconsistencies in the paper
  - Abstract: 14 pts with 4-6 and 17 with 7-10 BM; Results: 20 with 4-7 and 11 with 8-10
  - Coverage loss in 28 PTVs: abstract says 10 pts, Results say 12 pts
- ⚠️ No neurocognitive testing; KPS was the only functional measure
- ⚠️ Margin findings depend on 6D intrafraction imaging; they may not transfer to setups without it

[doi:10.1016/j.adro.2020.06.008](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adro.2020.06.008)

### Dose-adapted single-isocenter multitarget SRS for 4-10 brain mets · [[NCT02886572]]

> mOS 8.1 mo, 1-yr OS 35.7%; 22 of 40 pts outlived DS-GPA prediction (bar was 17), 1-yr local failure 5%, no G3-5 AEs.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The transferable piece is the switch rule: if single-fraction brain V12Gy exceeded 20 mL or any lesion was in the brainstem, every lesion got 25 Gy/5 fx. Those 79 lesions had 98% LC and 1.3% radionecrosis, vs 93% and 3.4% with single fraction (P=NS). Aggregate volume, not lesion count, decides whether to fractionate.

- 📊 mOS 8.1 mo, 6-mo OS 57.3%, 1-yr OS 35.7% from protocol SRS
- 📐 DS-GPA predicted mOS 5.5 mo, 1-yr 20% for this cohort
- 📐 22 of 40 pts outlived DS-GPA prediction; prespecified noninferiority bar was 17
- 🔍 Prospective single-arm, N=40, Durham NC, accrued Feb 2017 to Aug 2019, median f/u 15.2 mo
- 📊 Per-lesion outcomes by fractionation

  | Measure | Single fraction | 5 fractions |
  |---|---|---|
  | Pts / lesions | 24 / 125 | 16 / 79 |
  | Prescription | 20 Gy (<2 cm) or 18 Gy (2 to <3 cm) | 25 Gy/5 fx |
  | Local control | 93% (8 of 119 failed, no dose adjustment) | 98% (2 of 79 failed) |
  | Radionecrosis | 3.4% (4 of 119) | 1.3% (1 of 79) |

- 📊 1-yr local failure 5% (2.6%, 8.7%): 10 of 204 lesions, in 4 of 32 pts
- 📊 Distant brain failure cumulative incidence 20% at 6 mo, 35.8% at 12 mo
- 📊 Safety and neuro outcomes
  - G1-2 SIMT-related AEs in 73%, no G3-5
  - Headache 50%, fatigue 45%, nausea 25%, alopecia 20%
  - Radionecrosis in 5 lesions (4 pts), 1-yr rate 1.5%
  - Neurologic death 20%
  - MMSE, Trail Making, HVLT, FACT-Br unchanged at 3 mo
- 🔍 Median beam-on 8 min; median table time 31.5 min/d
- ⚠️ Benchmark is DS-GPA predicted survival, not a randomized WBRT or multi-isocenter arm
- ⚠️ 11 pts had prior SRS/WBRT, while DS-GPA times survival from first brain met diagnosis
- ⚠️ Discussion cites 58% 6-mo distant brain failure; Results report 20%
- ⚠️ Funded by Varian Medical Systems

### HyperArc vs manual VMAT SRS: automated planning and delivery

> Paddick GI 5.42 vs 7.09 (P<.001) favoring automated HyperArc over expert manual VMAT SRS; conformity equal; 1-yr LC 90.1% in HyperArc brain mets.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The gradient advantage survives matching arc number: Paddick GI 3.68 vs 4.67 with 2 table angles and 7.53 vs 9.58 with 4, so extra noncoplanar arcs do not explain it. With PTV=GTV single-isocenter delivery and 1-yr LC 90.1%, this supports automated planning for multi-target SRS where planner experience is the bottleneck.

- 🔍 Single-institution retrospective comparison: 693 single-isocenter VMAT SRS pts, HyperArc vs historical manual VMAT (mVMAT) cohort
- 🔍 PTV=GTV, no margin, for both techniques; single isocenter for multiple targets
- 🔍 Brain mets dosing: 18-20 Gy x1, or 30 Gy/5fx if any lesion >2-3 cm or deep
- 📊 Plan quality, QA and workflow, HyperArc vs mVMAT (medians unless stated)

  | Metric | HyperArc | mVMAT | P |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | RTOG conformity index | 1.14 | 1.13 | .322 |
  | Paddick conformity index | 0.86 | 0.87 | .048 |
  | Paddick gradient index | 5.42 | 7.09 | <.001 |
  | Falloff index | 6.25 | 8.09 | <.001 |
  | Mean brain dose | 4.6% | 5.1% | .04 |
  | Gamma ≥95% at 3%/2 mm (TG-218 tolerance) | 97% | 94% | .024 |
  | Planning time | 77 min | 72 min | .61 |
  | Time in room | 10.5 min | 11.4 min | <.001 |
  | Median number of arcs | 3 | 2 | <.001 |

- 📐 Gradient index advantage holds at matched table-angle count

  | Table angles | Plans (HyperArc / mVMAT) | Paddick GI HyperArc | Paddick GI mVMAT | P |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 2 | 88 / 261 | 3.68 | 4.67 | <.001 |
  | 4 | 66 / 58 | 7.53 | 9.58 | <.001 |

- 📊 HyperArc clinical cohort: 107 pts, 377 mets, 1-yr LC 90.1% with 47 tumors at risk
- 📊 G3 seizures in 3 pts after median clinical f/u 7 mo; no G4/5 events
- ⚠️ LC cohort is HyperArc-only; no mVMAT clinical comparator, so 'no compromise' in LC is not tested
- ⚠️ Median imaging f/u 5 mo; 1-yr LC rests on 47 tumors at risk
- ⚠️ Confound: HyperArc on Eclipse 15.5 vs mVMAT on Eclipse 13.6, sequential eras
- ⚠️ Multiple authors report Varian grants, fees or patents
