# 2026-11-07

**Dose-adapted single-isocenter SRS for 4 to 10 brain mets (prospective): 22/40 outlived DS-GPA prediction, 1-yr local failure 5%, no G3-5 toxicity.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> A CNS-only day on linac single-isocenter SRS for multiple brain mets. The one prospective cohort shows 1-yr local failure 5% with no G3-5 toxicity. Four planning or retrospective studies favor HyperArc and non-coplanar VMAT on gradient and V12Gy, and a simulated 1 mm GTV-PTV margin restored coverage lost to residual setup error.

## 🧠 CNS

All five studies address single-isocenter linac SRS technique. Only one is prospective, and the rest rely on dosimetric surrogates or retrospective local control.

### Manual to automated (HyperArc) VMAT SRS transition

> Paddick GI 5.42 vs 7.09 (P<.001), time in room 10.5 vs 11.4 min, RTOG CI unchanged; HyperArc 1-yr LC 90.1% across 377 mets.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The gradient advantage is not just more arcs: at 2 table angles Paddick GI was 3.68 vs 4.67 (P<.001), and conformity was unchanged. Zero-margin single-isocenter multitarget delivery still gave 1-yr LC 90.1%, which supports keeping PTV=GTV when localization and intrafraction motion are tightly managed.

- 🔍 Single-institution retrospective: 693 single-isocenter VMAT SRS plans, HyperArc vs the prior 15 months of manual VMAT
- 🔍 HyperArc: 23-pt 2017 pilot + 350 pts over 15 months; mVMAT: 320 pts treated just before adoption
- 💊 GTV with no margin (PTV=GTV); 18-20 Gy x1, or 30 Gy/5 fx if any target >2-3 cm or deep
- 🔍 10 MV FFF on Edge with HD120 MLC; HyperArc used 2-4 arcs, Encompass open mask, 6DOF couch
- 📊 HyperArc vs mVMAT: plan quality, QA and workflow (medians)

  | Metric | HyperArc | mVMAT | P |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | RTOG conformity index | 1.14 | 1.13 | .322 |
  | Paddick conformity index | 0.86 | 0.87 | .048 |
  | Paddick gradient index | 5.42 | 7.09 | <.001 |
  | Falloff index | 6.25 | 8.09 | <.001 |
  | Mean brain dose | 4.6% | 5.1% | .04 |
  | Median no. of arcs | 3 | 2 | <.001 |
  | TG-218 tolerance (gamma ≥95%, 3%/2 mm) | 97% | 94% | .024 |
  | TG-218 action limit (gamma ≥90%) | 100% | 98% | .072 |
  | Planning time | 77 min | 72 min | .61 |
  | Time in room | 10.5 min | 11.4 min | <.001 |

- 📐 Gradient gain holds at matched table-angle count
  - 2 table angles (88 vs 261 plans): Paddick GI 3.68 vs 4.67, P<.001
  - 4 table angles (66 vs 58 plans): Paddick GI 7.53 vs 9.58, P<.001
- 📊 Clinical cohort (HyperArc only): 107 pts, 377 mets; 1-yr LC 90.1%, 47 tumors at risk
- 📊 Grade 3 seizures in 3 pts; no grade 4 or 5 events at median clinical f/u 7 mo
- ⚠️ Median imaging f/u 5 mo; 1-yr LC rests on 47 tumors at risk
- ⚠️ Different Eclipse versions (15.5 vs 13.6) confound technique vs software era
- ⚠️ Planning-time parity reflects 9 yrs of in-house mVMAT expertise; efficiency for novice planners is asserted, not measured
- ⚠️ Several authors report Varian grants, fees or patents

### Dose-adapted single-isocenter SRS for 4 to 10 brain mets (prospective) · [[NCT02886572]]

> mOS 8.1 mo, 1-yr OS 35.7%; 22/40 outlived DS-GPA prediction (≥17 needed for non-inferiority), 1-yr local failure 5%, no G3-5 toxicity.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The transferable piece is the dose-adaptation rule: switching every lesion to 5 Gy x5 when V12Gy exceeds 20 mL or a brainstem lesion is present gave 98% per-lesion LC with 1.3% necrosis, at aggregate PTV up to 32 mL. That supports hypofractionating the whole plan in high-volume cases instead of de-escalating single-fraction dose.

- 🔍 Prospective single-arm, N=40, single institution (Durham, NC), accrued Feb 2017 to Aug 2019
- 🔍 1° EP OS, benchmarked against DS-GPA-predicted survival; non-inferiority if ≥17/40 outlived prediction
- 📊 22 pts outlived DS-GPA-predicted survival from protocol SRS, clearing the 17-pt threshold
- 📊 mOS 8.1 mo (5-12.1), 6-mo OS 57.3%, 1-yr OS 35.7%; median f/u 15.2 mo
- 🔍 Dose-adapted SIMT algorithm (VMAT, 1 mm PTV margin)
  - PTV <2 cm: 20 Gy x1
  - PTV 2.0 to <3.0 cm: 18 Gy x1
  - PTV >3 cm, any brainstem lesion, or V12Gy >20 mL: 5 Gy x5 to all lesions
  - Eloquent-area lesions could drop to 18 Gy or 16 Gy at MD discretion
- 📊 Per-lesion outcomes by fractionation

  | Endpoint | Single fraction | 5 fractions |
  |---|---|---|
  | Local failure, lesions | 8 of 119 (no dose adjustment) | 2 of 79 |
  | Local control | 93% | 98% |
  | Radionecrosis per lesion | 3.4% (4 of 119) | 1.3% (1 of 79) |

- 📊 1-yr local failure 5% (10 of 204 lesions) in 4 of 32 evaluable pts; 1 NSCLC pt had 5 of the failures
- 📊 Distant brain recurrence cumulative incidence 20% at 6 mo, 35.8% at 12 mo; 19 of 40 pts
- 📊 AEs in 73%, all G1-2 (headache 50%, fatigue 45%); no G3-5 treatment-related AEs
- 📐 Median beam-on 8 min; median brain V12Gy 11.7 mL (1fx), V24Gy 3.5 mL (5fx); median hippocampal dose 2.4 Gy
- ⚠️ 8 of 40 pts died before first MRI, so LC/DBF denominators are 32 pts
- ⚠️ 11 pts had prior SRS/WBRT; neurocognitive testing attrition steep after 6 mo
- ⚠️ Discussion cites 58% 6-mo distant failure, contradicting the 20% 6-mo cumulative incidence in Results
- ⚠️ Varian-funded; PIs report Varian funding directly related to the study

[doi:10.1016/j.adro.2021.100760](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adro.2021.100760)

### Interinstitutional plan quality: Elements MME vs RapidArc single-isocenter SRS

> RapidArc lowered median V12Gy 19.2 vs 23.7 cc (P<.0001) and improved conformity; MME lowered V5Gy and mean brain dose (2.57 vs 2.76 Gy).

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The platforms trade dose bands rather than one beating the other. RA lowers the V12Gy/V8Gy band tied to radionecrosis, while MME lowers dose below roughly 6 Gy. The V12 advantage holds only above 2.1 cc total PTV (ratio mean 1.24); below that, MME matched or beat RA on V12 in 4 of 6 cases.

- 🔍 Paired replanning study: 30 pts, 4-10 brain mets (217 targets), each planned on both platforms
- 🔍 Each institution replanned the other's 15 cases with its own expert technique (TJU = MME, UAB = RA)
- 💊 Planning parameters by platform
  - RA (VMAT): 2-4 noncoplanar arcs, 10 MV FFF, Eclipse 13.6, AAA dose calc
  - MME (DCA): 4-9 noncoplanar DCAs, 6 MV FFF, Elements v1.5, pencil-beam dose calc
  - Both: single isocenter, HD120 MLC, 14-24 Gy single fraction, D99% = 100% Rx goal
  - All 60 plans exported to MIM for DVH analysis, removing inter-TPS calculation bias
- 📊 Dosimetric medians, MME vs RA (paired Wilcoxon)

  | Metric | MME (DCA) | RA (VMAT) | p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | RTOG CI (per target) | 1.38 | 1.23 (abstract: 1.21) | <.0001 |
  | Paddick CI (per target) | 0.70 | 0.77 | <.0001 |
  | V12Gy (cc) | 23.7 | 19.2 | <.0001 |
  | V8Gy (cc) | 53.6 | 44.1 | .024 |
  | V5Gy (cc) | 141.4 | 142.8 | .009 |
  | Mean brain dose (Gy) | 2.57 | 2.76 | <.0001 |
  | Mean target coverage | 97.5% | 96.9% | .007 |

- 📊 RA gave lower V12Gy in 26 of 30 cases; MME gave lower V5Gy in 25 of 30
- 📊 Total PTV <2.1 cc (6 cases): MME favorable for V8, V5, Dmean; V12 favored MME in 4 of 6
- 📊 Total PTV >2.1 cc (24 cases): V12 favored RA, Element/VMAT ratio mean 1.24
- 📐 Brain DVHs cross near 6 Gy (6.8 Gy in representative case): RA better above, MME better below
- 📐 Table 1 means widen the low-dose gap: V5Gy 158.3 vs 174.8 cc, Dmean 2.66 vs 3.03 Gy
- 🔍 Delivery: MME 7.3 ± 1.4 arcs, 6871 ± 1392 MU; RA 3.8 ± 0.6 arcs, 10225 ± 3449 MU
- 🔍 Estimated beam-on: MME ~6-9 min at 6 MV FFF; RA 3-4 min at 10 MV FFF
- ⚠️ Beam energy confound: 6 MV FFF (MME) vs 10 MV FFF (RA), by commissioning, not design
- ⚠️ Dosimetric surrogates only; no toxicity, radionecrosis, or cognitive outcomes
- ⚠️ Internal inconsistencies: V12 MME median 23.7 in text vs 24.0 in Table 1; RA RTOG CI 1.21 vs 1.23
- ⚠️ Software versions dated (Eclipse 13.6, MME 1.5); HyperArc not tested
- ⚠️ Several UAB authors report Varian research support or consulting

### Automated single-isocenter SRS for multiple brain mets: outcomes and margins

> 1y LC 89% per pt and 94% per lesion. Residual setup error caused coverage loss (V95 <95%) in 28 targets; simulated 1 mm GTV-PTV margin restored coverage.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Coverage loss clustered in small lesions far from isocenter: about 90% of V95 <95% misses were lesions <0.4 cm3 at ≥3.9 cm. Uncorrected intrafraction drift cost GTV coverage in 28% of lesions, so the 1 mm margin depends on 6D monitoring with mid-treatment correction.

- 🔍 Retrospective, 2 Italian centers, 31 consecutive pts, 204 BM <3 cm, 4-10 BM per pt, Oct 2016-Sep 2018
- 🔍 Frameless mask, Brainlab Elements Multiple Brain Mets (automated DCA planning), single isocenter, ExacTrac 6D IGRT
- 🔍 RT technique
  - 20-22 Gy single fx for lesions <2 cm; 16-18 Gy for ≥2 cm or near brainstem/chiasm
  - GTV-PTV margin 1 mm (n=176) or 2 mm (n=28)
  - Median CI 1.32, GI 3.94; median distance from isocenter 39 mm
  - Setup tolerance 0.5 mm / 0.5°; mean 3D residual error 0.34 mm (range 0.1-0.53)
- 📊 Clinical outcomes, median f/u 11 mo (range 4-26)

  | Endpoint | 1 yr | 2 yr |
  |---|---|---|
  | LC, per patient | 89% | 77% |
  | LC, per lesion | 94% | 86% |
  | OS | 68% | 40% |
  | Distant brain failure | 38% | n/r |

- 📊 mOS 16.1 mo; median time to distant brain failure 8 mo; salvage SRS in 15 pts, WBRT in 3
- 📊 No G3-4 toxicity. Radiologic necrosis in 5 (16.1%) pts; per-lesion risk 11% at 12 mo
- 📐 Individual V12Gy >10 cm3 predicted necrosis (P=.01); lesion number and cumulative volume did not
- 📊 Coverage loss (V95 <95%) from residual isocenter error, 28 targets

  | Factor | Lesions losing coverage | P |
  |---|---|---|
  | Distance <39 mm vs ≥39 mm | 7 vs 21 | .0001 |
  | Volume <0.40 cm3 vs ≥0.40 cm3 | 20 vs 8 | .015 |

- 📊 Simulated 0 mm margin: coverage compromised in 18 lesions; 1 mm margin: adequate coverage in all lesions
- 📊 Intrafraction: 10 positionings in 7 (22%) pts out of tolerance; uncorrected → GTV coverage loss in 21 (28%) of 74 lesions
- 📊 1 mm margin held GTV coverage under intrafraction drift in all but 2 lesions
- 📐 Volume cost of margin, spherical lesion (Table 4)

  | Diameter | 0 mm | 1 mm | 2 mm | 3 mm |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 0.5 cm | 0.07 cm3 | 0.18 cm3 | 0.38 cm3 | 0.7 cm3 |
  | 1 cm | 0.52 cm3 | 0.9 cm3 | 1.43 cm3 | 2.21 cm3 |
  | 1.5 cm | 1.76 cm3 | 2.57 cm3 | 3.59 cm3 | 4.85 cm3 |
  | 2 cm | 4.18 cm3 | 5.57 cm3 | 7.23 cm3 | 9.2 cm3 |

- 🔗 Roper et al: 0.5° simulated rotation spared coverage; up to 2° lost coverage in more than one-third of cases
- 🔗 Korytko et al: V12Gy >10 cm3 necrosis risk 55.3% vs 22.5% below, in 198 GK-treated tumors
- ⚠️ Margin sufficiency is dosimetric (CBCT re-computation), not linked to per-lesion LC by margin size
- ⚠️ Internal inconsistency: abstract says 14 pts with 4-6 and 17 with 7-10 BM; Table 1 says 20 with 4-7, 11 with 8-10
- ⚠️ Coverage-loss pts reported as 10 (abstract) vs 12 (results); intrafraction section says 1 mm held all but 2 lesions
- ⚠️ No formal neurocognitive testing; only 9 pts on concurrent systemic therapy, limiting IO/TKI-era transfer

### DCAT vs CoVMAT vs NcVMAT for single brain met

> Non-coplanar VMAT cut normal-brain V12Gy to 9.4 vs 11.6 (DCAT) vs 15.1 cm3 (coplanar VMAT) for 2-3 cm single brain mets, 20 Gy/1 fx.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The coplanar-only VMAT many centers default to was the worst plan for low and intermediate spill (V12Gy 15.1 cm3, V5Gy 96.8 cm3), not merely a wash vs DCAT. For 2-3 cm lesions, where necrosis risk rises, adding non-coplanar arcs is what earns VMAT its advantage, at the cost of longer beam-on time.

- 🔍 Planning study: DCAT, CoVMAT and NcVMAT plans built for each of 15 pts, single institution (Kyoto)
- 🔍 Single brain met, longest diameter 2-3 cm; median PTV 6.4 cm3 (range 3.7-16.2)
- 🔍 Planning parameters
  - 20 Gy/1 fx to PTV, normalized to D99.5%
  - PTV = GTV + 1 mm
  - 6 MV, TrueBeam STx, Eclipse 15.6 AcurosXB, 1 mm grid
  - PTV D2% set at ~142% of Rx (PTV covered by 70% isodose line)
  - DCAT and NcVMAT: 1 coplanar + 2 non-coplanar arcs; CoVMAT: 1 coplanar arc
- 📊 Normal-brain irradiated volume, cm3 (mean ± SD)

  | Index | DCAT | CoVMAT | NcVMAT | DCAT vs CoVMAT p | DCAT vs NcVMAT p | CoVMAT vs NcVMAT p |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | V20Gy | 2.44 ± 1.78 | 2.48 ± 1.87 | 1.64 ± 1.13 | 0.88 | <0.05 | <0.05 |
  | V15Gy | 7.22 ± 4.08 | 8.52 ± 4.89 | 5.95 ± 3.10 | <0.05 | <0.05 | <0.05 |
  | V12Gy | 11.6 ± 6.5 | 15.1 ± 8.2 | 9.4 ± 4.9 | <0.05 | <0.05 | <0.05 |
  | V10Gy | 16.8 ± 9.0 | 22.7 ± 12.0 | 13.9 ± 6.8 | <0.05 | <0.05 | <0.05 |
  | V5Gy | 49.2 ± 28.4 | 96.8 ± 55.4 | 42.9 ± 20.3 | <0.05 | <0.05 | <0.05 |

- 📊 RTOG-CI and Paddick CI significantly better with NcVMAT than DCAT
- 📊 GTV D98% better with NcVMAT than DCAT or CoVMAT
- 📊 Coplanar-only VMAT was worst on low-dose spill: V5Gy nearly doubled vs DCAT
- 📊 MU higher for DCAT and NcVMAT than CoVMAT; beam-on time longer for both VMAT plans than DCAT
- ⚠️ Dosimetric surrogate only: no radionecrosis, local control or neurocognitive outcomes
- ⚠️ Single TPS and optimizer; gains may depend on planner skill and Eclipse-specific tools (ring contour, NTO)
- 🔗 Molinier et al. is the only prior direct DCAT/CoVMAT/NcVMAT comparison, but used different TPS and algorithms between techniques

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Dosimetric comparison among dynamic conformal arc therapy, coplanar and non-coplanar volumetric modulated arc therapy for single brain metastasis** — Torizuka, Daichi; Uto, Megumi; Takehana, Keiichi et al.. *Journal of Radiation Research* (2021-10). [doi:10.1093/jrr/rrab092](https://doi.org/10.1093/jrr/rrab092)
  > Abstract In the delivery of stereotactic radiosurgery (SRS) by linear accelerator (LINAC), dynamic conformal arc therapy (DCAT) with non-coplanar beams is conventionally used. However, volumetric modulated arc therapy (VMAT) can improve target conformity, thereby decreasing the dose to organs at risk by inversed planning methods, but few studies have directly compared DCAT and VMAT with and without non-coplanar beams in patients with single brain metastasis. We therefore conducted a planning study to compare the dose distribution in DCAT, VMAT using only a coplanar arc (CoVMAT) and VMAT with non-coplanar arcs (NcVMAT) in the treatment of single brain metastasis. DCAT, CoVMAT and NcVMAT plans were created for 15 patients. The three modalities were compared in terms of target conformity, target coverage, the dose to normal brain tissue, monitor units (MUs) and beam-on time. Both conformity indices (RTOG-CI and IP-CI) as well as the D98% of the gross target volume (GTV) were significantly better in the NcVMAT plans than in the DCAT plans. Comparisons of the doses to normal brain tissue revealed that the V20Gy, V15Gy, V12Gy, V10Gy and V5Gy were significantly smaller in the NcVMAT plans than in the plans based on the other two modalities. The MUs of the DCAT and NcVMAT plans were larger than those of the CoVMAT plans, and the beam-on time was longer in the NcVMAT and CoVMAT plans than in the DCAT plans. Compared to the CoVMAT and DCAT plans, NcVMAT plans significantly improved target conformity and reduced the doses to normal brain tissue at V20Gy, V15Gy, V12Gy, V10Gy and V5Gy.
