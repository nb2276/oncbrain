# 2026-11-10

**CTV-P consensus: CTV-P1 = GTV-P + 5 mm, CTV-P2 = GTV-P + 10 mm, both anatomically edited, for definitive RT of laryngeal, hypopharyngeal, oropharyngeal and oral cavity SCC.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> This is a day of contouring guidelines with no outcome data. Four H&N documents set primary CTV margins (GTV + 5 and 10 mm), elective neck levels, node-level boundaries and 5 mm / ≥30 mm nerve-path CTVs for V1/V2/V3 PNI. ESTRO ACROP LA-NSCLC discourages elective nodal coverage beyond the hilum and adjacent stations. Margins rest largely on expert opinion.

## 👄 Head & Neck

Primary CTV guidance converges on GTV + 5 mm for high dose and + 10 mm for low dose, always edited at anatomical barriers, with neck levels drawn per the 2013 consensus atlas.

### Trigeminal nerve (V) contouring guidelines in head and neck cancers

> Practical CTV guidance for PNI along V1/V2/V3: CTV-P1 = GTV + 5 mm along nerve; CTV-P2 = nerve path ≥30 mm, skull base often recommended.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The ≥30 mm CTV-P2 extension exists because MRI underreads PNI extent: extent misdefined in 17% and 11% of surgical-correlation series, and radiologic skip lesions sit on pathologically continuous disease. That gap is the argument for elective retrograde nerve-path coverage toward skull base in ACC, extensive-PNI SCC, and PPF or masticator invasion.

- 🔍 Expert contouring guideline (Radiother Oncol 2019): radio-anatomy of V1/V2/V3, PNI spread routes, imaging, and CTV definitions
- 📊 Recommended PNI target volumes along the nerve path

  | Scenario | High-risk CTV (CTV-P1) | Low-risk CTV (CTV-P2) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Primary RT, unresected macroscopic PNI | GTV (post-contrast enhancement along nerve) + 5 mm along nerve path | Nerve path ≥30 mm; extension to skull base discussed, often recommended |
  | Post-op RT, resected macroscopic PNI with R1 margin | Positive margin + 5 mm along nerve path; PNI in entire tumor bed | Per histology/site risk (row below) |
  | High-risk histology or site (ACC, SCC with extensive PNI, PPF or masticator invasion, NPC, maxillary sinus) | PNI in tumor bed | Nerve path ≥30 mm per primary site; skull base discussed, often recommended |
  | Skull base or intracranial involvement | As above | Extend CTV-P2 to the brainstem |

- 🔍 Non-lateralized tumors: bilateral nerve-path coverage for at least 20–30 mm, up to skull base considered
- 🔍 Standard coverage is retrograde toward skull base; antegrade coverage if symptoms, operative report or imaging suggest it
- 🔍 CTV-P1 margin can be increased when MRI extent is in doubt
- 🔍 Pterygopalatine fossa is the hub linking V1/V2/V3 and the facial nerve (VII) via the Vidian nerve; cover adjacent branches at neural junctions
- 📐 MRI limits for macroscopic PNI extent
  - Sensitivity 95–100% for detection
  - Gandhi 2011: extent misdefined in 5 of 30 operated cases (17%)
  - Baulch 2015: missed 2 of 38 (5%); extent wrong in 4 of 36 detected (11%)
  - Radiologic skip lesions common; pathologic PNI usually continuous, so imaging underestimates extent
- 💊 Focal microscopic PNI alone in SCC: relative indication for adjuvant RT, case-by-case; extensive PNI a more systematic indication
- ⚠️ Margins are expert recommendation; no randomized or pattern-of-failure validation of the 5 mm or ≥30 mm extensions in source
- ⚠️ Zukauskaite series (1576 pts, primary RT HNSCC) found most recurrences in GTV but excluded ACC, NPC, sinonasal and skin, the PNI-prone sites

### Neck node level delineation: 2013 update consensus guidelines

> Joint DAHANCA/EORTC/HKNPCSG/NCIC CTG/NCRI/RTOG/TROG CT atlas defines neck node levels Ia through Xb by axial-CT boundaries, Robbins-modified nomenclature.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The boundary shifts that change a nodal CTV: level V now starts at the hyoid body, IVa stops 2 cm above the sternal manubrium with IVb and Vc filling the space below, and VIIa means the lateral retropharyngeal nodes only. Which levels to treat and to what dose is left to other guidance.

- 🔍 Panel: radonc from the 7 cooperative groups, plus a H&N surgeon and an anatomist
- 🔍 Nomenclature modified from Robbins; mapped against the TNM atlas neck node groups (Table 1)
- 📐 Cranial and caudal limits by level (Tables 2-9)

  | Level | Cranial | Caudal |
  |---|---|---|
  | Ia (submental) | Mylo-hyoid m. | Platysma m. (caudal edge of anterior belly of digastric mm) |
  | Ib (submandibular) | Cranial edge of submandibular gland; anteriorly, mylo-hyoid m. | Caudal edge of hyoid bone and mandible, or of submandibular gland (whichever more caudal) |
  | II (upper jugular) | Caudal edge of the lateral process of C1 | Caudal edge of the body of the hyoid bone |
  | III (middle jugular) | Caudal edge of the body of the hyoid bone | Caudal edge of cricoid cartilage |
  | IVa (lower jugular) | Caudal edge of cricoid cartilage | 2 cm cranial to sternal manubrium |
  | IVb (medial supraclavicular) | Caudal border of level IVa | Cranial edge of sternal manubrium |
  | V (posterior triangle) | Cranial edge of the body of hyoid bone | Plane just below transverse cervical vessels |
  | Vc (lateral supraclavicular) | Plane just below transverse cervical vessels | 2 cm cranial to sternal manubrium |
  | VIa (anterior jugular) | Caudal edge of hyoid bone or submandibular gland, whichever more caudal | Cranial edge of sternal manubrium |
  | VIb (prelaryngeal, pretracheal, paratracheal) | Caudal edge of thyroid cartilage | Cranial edge of sternal manubrium |
  | VIIa (retropharyngeal) | Upper edge of body of C1/hard palate | Cranial edge of the body of the hyoid bone |
  | VIIb (retro-styloid) | Base of skull (jugular foramen) | Caudal edge of the lateral process of C1 |
  | VIII (parotid) | Zygomatic arch, external auditory canal | Angle of the mandible |
  | IX (bucco-facial) | Caudal edge of the orbit | Caudal edge of the mandible |
  | Xa (retroauricular) | Cranial edge of external auditory canal | Tip of the mastoid |
  | Xb (occipital) | External occipital protuberance | Cranial border of level V |

- 📐 Level V cranial limit moved to the hyoid bone; uppermost nodes reassigned to occipital Xb
- 📐 Va vs Vb split at the caudal edge of the cricoid cartilage
- 📐 Level II divisible into IIa/IIb at the posterior edge of the IJV
- 📐 VIb cranial limit drops to the hyoid body only for anterior floor of mouth, tongue tip, lower lip
- 🔍 VIIa covers lateral retropharyngeal nodes only; the inconstant medial group is excluded
- 🔍 VIa coverage is limited to lower lip and advanced gingivo-mandibular primaries invading chin soft tissue
- ⚠️ Landmarks assume a neutral neck; hyperextension shifts the Ib/II relationship and skull-base landmarks
- ⚠️ Explicitly does not address which levels to select or the dose levels
- 🔍 110 DICOM-RT axial CT sections posted on the cooperative group websites as teaching material

### CTV-P delineation consensus guidelines (laryngeal, hypopharyngeal, oropharyngeal, oral cavity SCC)

> CTV-P1 = GTV-P + 5 mm and CTV-P2 = GTV-P + 10 mm, both anatomically edited, for definitive RT of laryngeal, hypopharyngeal, oropharyngeal and oral cavity SCC.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The volume is edited, not just expanded: air, mandible, hyoid, vertebrae and fascia trim it unless they are invaded. Two exceptions change the contour: T1 glottic disease gets no CTV-P2, and hypopharynx gets an optional 15 mm cranio-caudal CTV-P2 margin because submucosal spread reaches 20-30 mm.

- 🔍 Multi-society consensus (AIRO, DAHANCA, EORTC, GORTEC, NCIC CTG, NRG Oncology, TROG and others) on primary-tumour CTV
- 🔍 Core concept '5 + 5 mm': CTV-P1 carries the high dose, CTV-P2 the lower dose, CTV-P1 always within CTV-P2
- 📊 Primary CTV margins by subsite and T stage

  | Subsite / T | CTV-P1 | CTV-P2 | Editing note |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Glottic T1 | GTV-P + 5 mm | Not recommended | Excludes thyroid cartilage and air cavity |
  | Glottic T2 | GTV-P + 5 mm | GTV-P + 10 mm; may omit if small/superficial, not from anterior commissure | Excludes cricoid cartilage |
  | Glottic T3-T4 | GTV-P + 5 mm | GTV-P + 10 mm | Stops at strap muscles unless macroscopically invaded |
  | Supraglottic T1 | GTV-P + 5 mm | GTV-P + 10 mm | Includes pre-epiglottic and para-laryngeal space |
  | Supraglottic T4 | GTV-P + 5 mm | GTV-P + 10 mm | Stops at strap muscles unless invaded; T4b may enter vertebral body |
  | Subglottic T1 | GTV-P + 5 mm | GTV-P + 10 mm; optional | e.g. 2 mm into cricoid cartilage adequate |
  | Subglottic T2-T4 | GTV-P + 5 mm | GTV-P + 10 mm | Excludes vertebral body except T4b |
  | Hypopharynx T1 | GTV-P + 5 mm | GTV-P + 10 mm; optional if small/superficial | Excludes retro-pharyngeal space |
  | Hypopharynx T2-T4 | GTV-P + 5 mm | GTV-P + 10 mm; consider 15 mm cranio-caudal | Not through pre-vertebral fascia unless T4b |
  | Oropharynx T1 | GTV-P + 5 mm | GTV-P + 10 mm | Excludes air, bone, mobile tongue |

- 📐 CTV-P volume mapped to dose level (2 Gy equivalents)

  | Volume | 2 dose levels | 3 dose levels |
  |---|---|---|
  | CTV-P1 | 70 Gy in 2 Gy fx over 7 wk | 70 Gy in 2 Gy fx over 7 wk |
  | CTV-P2 | 50 Gy in 2 Gy fx over 5 wk (prophylactic) | 60 Gy in 2 Gy fx over 6 wk (intermediate) |
  | CTV-P3 | Not used | 50 Gy in 2 Gy fx over 5 wk (prophylactic) |

- 🔍 Geometric expansion always edited, across 4 themes
  - Air cavities of pharynx, larynx, oral cavity, sinuses
  - Abutting but non-contiguous subsites (soft palate vs mobile tongue)
  - Barriers: mandible, maxilla, cervical spine, hyoid, muscular fascia, unless infiltrated
  - Surgical series support smaller margins for small tumours (T1 glottic, T1 tonsillar fossa)
- 🔗 Zanoni et al.: 2.2 mm surgical margin optimal for loco-regional recurrence-free survival
- 🔗 Laser microsurgery with 2-3 mm mucosal margins: local control 85-95% without PORT in T1-T2 glottic/supraglottic
- ⚠️ Hypopharynx: submucosal extension up to 20-30 mm cranio-caudal drives the optional 15 mm margin
- ⚠️ Not valid postoperatively or for recurrent disease; PTV margin left to each centre

### Target volume selection and delineation (T and N) for primary RT of oral cavity, oropharyngeal, hypopharyngeal and laryngeal SCC

> Elective neck levels II-IVa for N0 pharyngolaryngeal SCC and Ib-V for N2a-b/N3; primary CTV is GTV + 5 and 10 mm, anatomically edited.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The nodal dose recipe is the part that transfers: SIB with 54-56 Gy to elective levels, 70 Gy in 33-35 fx to nodal GTV + 5 mm, and 10 mm into abutting muscle or parotid as expert opinion. Danish 0-10 mm margin data put relapses mainly inside the GTV, which supports tighter primary CTVs.

- 🔍 Narrative review of international consensus guidelines for nodal level selection, node delineation and primary CTV (Oral Oncology 2018)
- 🔍 Scope: primary RT for SCC of the oral cavity, oropharynx, hypopharynx and larynx. Not for the postoperative neck or adenoid cystic histology
- 🔍 Rationale for elective coverage: up to 30% microscopic nodal infiltration reported in the imaging-cN0 neck
- 📊 Ipsilateral elective nodal levels by N category (from review text)

  | Nodal category | Oral cavity | Oropharynx / hypopharynx / larynx |
  |---|---|---|
  | N0 | Levels I-III | Levels II-IVa; + VIIa for posterior pharyngeal wall |
  | N1 | As N0 | As N0, except hypopharynx (high nodal propensity) |
  | N2a, N2b, N3 | Ib (Ia if anterior) to V | Ib to V; + VIIa (RP) for oropharynx and hypopharynx |
  | N2c | Per N category on each side | Per N category on each side |

- 🔍 Conditional add-on levels
  - IVb and Vc if IVa or Vb infiltrated
  - VIIb (retrostyloid) if upper level II infiltrated
  - VI for esophageal, subglottic or transglottic extension, or piriform apex / post-cricoid involvement
  - N3: non-lymphatic structures (skin, muscle, parotid, bone) may need inclusion
- 🔍 Unilateral neck RT reasonable for well-lateralized primaries
  - Upper/lower gum and lateral floor of mouth not approaching midline
  - Lateral mobile tongue (not within 1 cm of midline), cheek, retromolar trigone
  - Tonsillar fossa without extension to base of tongue, soft palate or posterior pillar
  - Lateral wall of piriform sinus
  - Contralateral neck advisable for oral cavity N2a or higher
- 📊 Primary-tumor CTV: three schools compared

  | Approach | High-dose CTV | Low-dose CTV |
  |---|---|---|
  | Anatomic (Eisbruch; Grégoire/Daisne; Lapeyre) | GTV + 3-10 mm by barrier | Whole at-risk compartment; 2-3 cm where no barrier |
  | Geometric (DAHANCA) | GTV + 5 mm | Additional 5 mm |
  | International consensus | GTV + 5 mm, edited | GTV + 10 mm, anatomically edited (CTV-P2) |

- 💊 Nodal SIB: 54-56 Gy elective; 70 Gy in 33-35 fx (2.12-2.0 Gy) to therapeutic nodal CTV
- 🔍 Therapeutic nodal CTV = nodal GTV + 5 mm; add 10 mm into abutting muscle or parotid, excluding bone and airway
- 🔗 Danish 3-center series (0-10 mm GTV-to-CTV margins): recurrences mainly within GTV, no link between relapse rate and margin
- ⚠️ 10 mm extranodal expansion is expert opinion. The DAHANCA geographic-miss analysis was still ongoing
- ⚠️ p16+ oropharynx: same nodal selection, based on absence of data, not demonstrated equivalence

## 🫁 Thoracic / Lung

ESTRO ACROP LA-NSCLC: nodal GTV defined on PET-CT, CTV + 5-8 mm, and further elective nodal CTV graded as discouraged.

### ESTRO ACROP target volume guidelines for locally advanced NSCLC

> Graded consensus (M/R/O/D) for LA-NSCLC contouring: PET-CT-defined nodal GTV, CTV +5–8 mm, elective nodal coverage beyond hilum/adjacent stations discouraged.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The nodal rule is the decision point: a PET-positive node stays in GTV even with negative EBUS/EUS, given high false-negative rates, and drops out only with a benign biopsy explanation or negative mediastinoscopy. Elective coverage stops at the hilum or adjacent stations (optional); anything wider is discouraged.

- 🔍 Committee consensus: structured questionnaire, then discussion and voting; every item graded M/R/O/D
- 🔍 Key recommendations by step (M mandatory · R recommended · O optional · D discouraged)

  | Step | Recommendation | Grade |
  |---|---|---|
  | Staging | Diagnostic contrast CT + whole-body FDG-PET-CT | M |
  | Staging | FDG-PET-CT within 3 weeks before treatment start | R |
  | Sequential chemo | PET-CT before induction; repeat contrast chest CT before RT | M |
  | Nodal staging | Biopsy of enlarged or FDG-avid nodes if it changes TV | R |
  | Planning CT | Treatment position, 2–3 mm slices, cricoid to mid abdomen | M |
  | Motion | Respiratory-correlated 4D-CT assessment | R |
  | Planning PET-CT | Dedicated scan, preferably in planning position | R |
  | Nodal GTV post-chemo | All pre-chemo involved nodes/stations, even if vanished on imaging | M |
  | CTV primary | GTV + 5–8 mm, edited at anatomical barriers | M |
  | CTV nodal | Whole involved station or 5–8 mm geometric expansion | M |
  | Elective hilum / adjacent stations | May be included | O |
  | Further elective nodal CTV | Not recommended | D |
  | PTV | Manual editing of PTV | D |
  | PTV | Individualised anisotropic margins, primary vs nodes | R |
  | OARs | Lungs, whole heart, oesophagus, spinal canal PRV | M |
  | QA | Radiation oncologist approves all contours before planning | M |

- 🔍 PET+ nodes stay in GTV even when EBUS/EUS-negative, given high endosonographic false-negative rates
- 🔍 PET+ node omitted only with a clear benign biopsy explanation or negative mediastinoscopy
- 🔍 Diagnostic doubt on an FDG-avid mediastinal node: include rather than exclude
- 🔍 Post-induction: primary GTV on current CT, pre-chemo imaging considered (R)
- 🔍 PORT CTV: resected involved mediastinal stations, bronchial stump, ipsilateral hilum, stations 4 and 7
- 🔍 Motion management, three accepted policies
  - ITV per ICRU 62, from all 4D-CT phases or a MIP CT
  - Mid-ventilation or mid-position CT, motion folded into van Herk PTV margin
  - Gating or tracking with system-specific PTV margins
- ⚠️ No panel size or per-item agreement reported in source excerpt; grades reflect a vote, not evidence level
- ⚠️ Nodal CTV option 1 vs 2 left to local choice; supporting multicentre trials not named in excerpt
