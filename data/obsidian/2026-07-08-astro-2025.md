# 2026-07-08 — ASTRO Annual Meeting 2025

**NRG-GU005 quality of life: 5-fx SBRT cuts MCID bowel decline to 33% vs 46% at 1yr (p=0.002), with urinary incontinence 26% vs 35% at 2yr.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Prostate SBRT dominates: GU005 kills the superiority case on disease control while handing SBRT the toxicity argument (G3+ GU 0.6% vs 2.5%), and Meier's 10-yr 40 Gy/5 fx series shows two-thirds of relapses land after year 5, so 5-yr SBRT reads understate failure. ARS AUC and the ESTRO OCSCC volumes fill the delineation and salvage gaps.

## 👄 Head & Neck

First ESTRO post-op CTV consensus for oral cavity SCC, volumes and EQD2 by risk tier, no outcome data behind it.

### ESTRO OCSCC Post-op CTV Delineation Guidelines

> First ESTRO guideline for post-op CTV delineation in oral cavity SCC: GTV-P pre-op + 10 mm composited with surgical defect/flap + 5 mm.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The margin recipe is asymmetric and that is the operative detail: 10 mm around the re-created pre-op GTV-P but only 5 mm around the surgical defect or flap, composited rather than either alone. Fig 3.1/3.2 shows why, the re-created GTV-P extended superiorly beyond the defect into infratemporal fossa, a geographical miss if you contour the defect alone.

- 🔍 ESTRO multi-disciplinary expert group; no consensus PORT delineation guideline previously existed for mucosal HNSCC
- 📐 CTV-P = (GTV-P pre-op + 10 mm) ∪ (surgical defect/flap + 5 mm), edited for bone, fascia, air, teeth, intra-oral prosthesis
- 🔍 10 mm around GTV-P pre-op mirrors the 5+5 mm definitive-setting expansion; surgical series show microscopic infiltration within 5 mm of GTV-P edge in >95 % of resected OCSCC
- 📊 Recommended EQD2 dose by target volume

  | Volume | Indication | EQD2 dose |
  |---|---|---|
  | CTV-P post-op | PTV associated with post-op primary CTV | 60 Gy |
  | CTV-P high-risk | Positive (<1 mm) margin, whole CTV-P or localised strip | over 60 Gy, e.g. 64-66 Gy |
  | CTV-N1 | Involved nodal levels | 60 Gy |
  | CTV-N2 | Undissected at-risk levels | 50 Gy |
  | CTV-N2, dissected at-risk levels | Optimal dose unknown | 50 Gy to 60 Gy, clinician discretion |
  | CTV-N high-risk | Pathological extranodal extension | over 60 Gy, e.g. 64-66 Gy |

- 🔍 Nodal margin is pENE-dependent: GTV-N pre-op + 5 mm without pENE, + 10 mm with pENE to create CTV-N high-risk
- 🔍 Neck omission gate: pN0 after adequate (≥18 node) dissection generally needs no nodal PORT; pN1 may not either
- 🔍 pN1 with a single node >10 mm but ≤30 mm is treated as intermediate risk, an indication for selective neck irradiation to involved plus adjacent levels and Ib-III
- 🔍 Where nodes cannot be re-created on pre-op imaging or co-registration fails, whole involved level(s) plus seroma/clips go into CTV-N1 (or CTV-N high-risk if pENE)
- 🔗 Builds on the 2018 definitive-setting HNSCC CTV guidelines and cites GORTEC 2020 flap delineation as an adjunct
- ⚠️ Consensus document: expert discussion plus international reviewer feedback, no stated Delphi round, voting record or agreement metric in source
- ⚠️ No outcome data. The authors call for prospective audits of practice and outcomes to establish these volumes as standard of care
- ⚠️ Flap dose de-escalation is explicitly unsettled: a 'flap avoidance structure' is offered for standardisation, not endorsed, for lack of data

## 🌰 Prostate

Two GU005 readouts, a 10-yr single-arm SBRT series and an ARS salvage AUC: the fractionation question settles on QoL, not cure rates.

### NRG-GU005 (quality of life)

> Fewer MCID declines with SBRT at 1yr bowel (33% vs 46%, p=0.002) and sexual (34% vs 44%, p=0.026).

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The QoL separation is domain-specific, not global: bowel and sexual at 1yr, urinary incontinence at 2yr, with no longitudinal effect in sexual or hormonal. Rectal manipulation (SpaceOAR 55%) and the 38.78 Gy PTV max cap gate transfer, since the bowel and GU signals came from a spacer-heavy, urethra-constrained delivery.

- 🔍 Phase III randomized non-blinded, N=698 (SBRT 353, MH-IMRT 345); QoL is a secondary/PRO analysis
- 📐 SBRT 36.25 Gy/5 fx, 2-3 fx per week; PTV expansion 5mm except 3mm posteriorly and anteriorly
- 📐 MH-IMRT 70 Gy/28 fx or 60 Gy/20 fx; PTV expansion 8mm except 5mm posteriorly
- 🔍 CTV = prostate ± 1cm proximal SV; SBRT PTV max capped at 38.78 Gy without urethral contouring (acceptable variation 43.5 Gy)
- 📊 EPIC-26 MCID decline rates, SBRT vs MH-IMRT

  | Domain / timepoint | SBRT | MH-IMRT | p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Bowel, 1 yr | 33% | 46% | 0.002 |
  | Sexual, 1 yr | 34% | 44% | 0.026 |
  | Urinary incontinence, 2 yr | 26% | 35% | 0.023 |

- 📊 Longitudinal urinary incontinence favored SBRT, LS mean difference 2.91 (95% CI 0.85-4.97, p=0.0058)
- 📊 Investigator-reported toxicity, SBRT vs MH-IMRT

  | Event | SBRT | MH-IMRT | p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Treatment-related G≥3 GU | 0.6% | 2.5% | 0.04 |
  | Rectal hemorrhage, any grade | 10.5% | 17.3% | 0.01 |
  | Fatigue, any grade | 39.2% | 50.8% | 0.0025 |

- 🔗 vs PACE-B: both trials show lower urinary incontinence decline with SBRT, though MCID definitions differ
- ⚠️ Non-blinded trial with patient-reported endpoints; SBRT's 5-fraction convenience can color EPIC responses
- ⚠️ Rectal manipulation used in ~59% overall (SpaceOAR 55%), so bowel results reflect a spacer-heavy population
- ⚠️ EPIC completion 82.0% yr1 and 83.9% yr2, with more IMRT-arm early withdrawal (6.4% vs 1.1%)
- ⚠️ No significant treatment effect in sexual or hormonal domains on longitudinal analysis, despite the 1yr sexual MCID difference

### 10-yr SBRT Survival/Toxicity (Meier et al.)

> 10-yr RFS 90% overall (94% LR, 86% IR) with 40 Gy/5 fx; grade 3 late toxicity 1.4-1.5%, no grade 4-5.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Two-thirds of relapses fell between 5 and 10 yr, so the reassurance PACE-B gives at 5 yr is provisional. The unfavorable IR subgroup sits at 77% (60-93) vs 92% for favorable IR (p=0.002), which is where the ADT-with-SBRT question actually lives, not in the pooled 86% IR number.

- 🔍 Investigator-initiated phase 2 nonrandomized trial, N=310 evaluable (172 LR, 138 IR), 21 community/regional/academic sites, treated Jan 2008 to Apr 2010
- 🔍 Dose 40 Gy in 5 fractions (8 Gy x 5, ~100 Gy EQD2 at a/b=2), noncoplanar robotic platform, fiducial tracking with translational + rotational intrafractional correction mandatory
- 🔍 Androgen suppression not allowed; prostate volumes up to 100 cc eligible; no TURP or AUA-score exclusion; central pathologic review
- 📊 10-yr OS 84% (95% CI 76-91); LR 91% (85-97) vs IR 78% (67-89), p=0.22
- 📊 10-yr freedom from biochemical/clinical relapse by risk group

  | Group | 10-yr RFS (95% CI) | p |
  |---|---|---|
  | Whole group | 92% (87-96) | n/a |
  | Low risk | 94% (89-99) | 0.19 (LR vs IR) |
  | Intermediate risk | 86% (77-94) | 0.19 (LR vs IR) |
  | MSK favorable IR | 92% (90-100) | 0.002 (fav vs unfav) |
  | MSK unfavorable IR | 77% (60-93) | 0.002 (fav vs unfav) |

- 📊 Freedom from local failure 96% (93-99) whole group; 98% (95-100) LR, 94% (89-99) IR, p=0.12
- 📊 10-yr late toxicity (CTCAE v3, physician-reported)
  - Grade 3 GI or GU: 1.4% (0-3.4) LR, 1.5% (0-3.7) IR; no grade 4-5
  - All five grade 3 events in four pts were GU
  - Grade 2+ GI 2.1%, grade 2+ GU 14%
  - No new treatment-related late grade 3+ events after year 5
- 🔍 Grade group 3 was the only unfavorable risk factor independently predicting 10-yr biochemical/clinical failure, per the cited multivariate analysis
- 🔗 IR RFS compares favorably with the 74-78% 10-yr RFS reported with dose-escalated EBRT in RTOG 0126; toxicity similar to Fuller's CyberKnife SBRT trial, lower than PACE-B SBRT arm and PATRIOT
- ⚠️ Nonrandomized single-arm; the authors state a randomized trial is required for meaningful comparison with EBRT
- ⚠️ Sponsor-funded (Accuray) with a coauthor consulting for Accuray, and the platform-specific tracking argument favors the sponsor's device
- ⚠️ Two-thirds of relapses occurred between 5-10 yr, so 5-yr series (including PACE-B) systematically understate late failure

### American Radium Society AUC: Local Intraprostatic Recurrence

> Severe GU toxicity 20% after salvage RP vs 5.6% SBRT, 9.6% HDR: panel prefers biopsy-confirmed reirradiation.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The modality recommendation is a toxicity argument, not an efficacy one: MASTER found adjusted 5-yr recurrence-free survival of 50% to 60% across modalities with no survival difference vs RP, so reirradiation wins on severe GU toxicity (5.6% SBRT, 9.6% HDR vs 20% RP). Target volume then follows concordance, focal when mpMRI and systematic biopsy agree, whole-gland when they do not.

- 🔍 12-member ARS GU panel (radonc, urology, medonc); modified Delphi, two voting rounds, RAND disagreement methodology
- 📊 Panel position on local salvage modality, by clinical variant (Topic 2)

  | Variant | Panel position |
  |---|---|
  | Variant 1: isolated intraprostatic recurrence | Reirradiation usually appropriate; cryotherapy or HIFU may be appropriate; ADT alone not recommended |
  | Variant 2: short PSA doubling time, short interval to failure | HIFU may be appropriate, but disagreement across all interventions; ADT alone not recommended |
  | Variant 3: castrate-resistant local recurrence | Disagreement on intervention; androgen suppression uniformly not recommended |
  | Variant 4: second local salvage | Disagreement on which modality to select |
  | Variant 5: prior grade ≥3 toxicity | Disagreement; active surveillance may be appropriate; ADT can be considered |

- 📊 MASTER meta-analysis: adjusted severe toxicity by salvage modality, salvage RP as reference

  | Modality | Severe GU | Severe GI |
  |---|---|---|
  | Salvage RP (reference) | 20% | 1.8% |
  | SBRT | 5.6% | not reported in source |
  | HDR brachytherapy | 9.6% | 0.0%, p < 0.01 vs RP |
  | LDR brachytherapy | 9.1% | not reported in source |

- 📊 Reirradiation schemas the panel considers acceptable
  - SBRT whole-gland: 34 Gy in 5 fractions, heterogeneously dosed to mimic HDR (Fuller series, 50 pts)
  - SBRT focal: 40 Gy in 5 fractions, the MTD from a small NCI dose-finding study
  - EBRT: 36 Gy in 6 fractions, from the prospective phase 2 GETUG-AFU 31 study
  - LDR: minimum target dose 140 Gy (I-125) or 120 Gy (Pd-103), per RTOG 0526
  - HDR: 36 Gy in 6 fractions over two implants, 32 Gy in four fractions, or 27 Gy in 2 fractions
  - F-SHARP: one to two fraction HDR with escalation up to 30 Gy, accrual complete
- 📊 Focal vs whole-gland salvage by imaging and biopsy concordance (Topic 4)

  | Scenario | Target volume |
  |---|---|
  | mpMRI and systematic biopsy agree on lesion location | Focal favored |
  | History of grade ≥3 toxicity from initial RT course | Focal favored |
  | Lesion occult on mpMRI, localised by PET plus systematic biopsy | Focal or whole-gland both appropriate |
  | mpMRI and systematic biopsy disagree on lesion location | Whole-gland preferred |
  | Recurrent lesion in a different location to the index lesion | Whole-gland preferred, focal appropriate in selected cases |

- 🔍 Biopsy required before salvage: radiographic false positives are common
  - FORECAST mpMRI sensitivity 94% (95% CI 88-98%), specificity 18% (95% CI 7-35%), n=155
  - PSMA PET false-positive rate 8% against histopathology in 635 pts with BCR (Fendler)
  - Up to 59% of true radiorecurrences would be occult with MRI-targeted biopsy alone
  - Both systematic and targeted biopsies recommended; combined biopsy-plus-ablation approaches discouraged
- 💊 Short 4-6 mo LHRH agonist has moderate consensus as radiosensitizer with salvage SBRT, no cardiac history
- ⚠️ No agreement on relugolix: HERO's cardiac benefit was an unplanned secondary analysis, PRONOUNCE did not replicate it
- ⚠️ Evidence base predates PSMA PET; prior brachytherapy pts excluded; initial RT was conventionally fractionated EBRT

[doi:10.1016/j.euo.2024.09.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.euo.2024.09.005)
