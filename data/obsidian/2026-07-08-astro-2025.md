# 2026-07-08 — ASTRO Annual Meeting 2025

**NRG-GU005: prostate SBRT crosses DFS futility (HR 1.38, 0.91-2.09) yet beats MH-IMRT on 2-yr bowel HRQOL (35% vs 44% MCID decline).**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Prostate SBRT gets its first randomised head-to-head reality check: NRG-GU005 buys toxicity and bowel HRQOL, not efficacy, with 3-yr biochemical failure numerically worse (8% vs 4%), while Meier's 10-yr single-arm data show two-thirds of relapses land after year 5, so 5-yr SBRT datasets undercount failure. Head/neck gets its first ESTRO post-op CTV atlas for OCSCC.

## 🌰 Prostate

Two SBRT reads pulling opposite ways (randomised HRQOL win vs efficacy futility, and a 10-yr single-arm series whose late relapses indict short follow-up), plus an ARS AUC putting biopsy and reirradiation dose schemas on the record for local recurrence.

### 10-yr SBRT for Prostate Cancer (Meier et al., Eur Urol)

> 10-yr RFS 92% overall, 94% LR / 86% IR, with 1.5% cumulative Gr3 GU toxicity after 40 Gy/5 fx.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Two-thirds of relapses fell between 5 and 10 yr, so the 5-yr PACE-B read undercounts failure and this is the first 10-yr prospective SBRT dataset. Grade group 3 was the only independent predictor of 10-yr failure, and unfavorable IR sat at 77% RFS, which is where the ADT-addition question actually lives.

- 🔍 Phase 2 nonrandomized multi-institutional trial, N=310 evaluable (172 LR, 138 IR), 21 community/regional/academic sites, accrual Jan 2008 to Apr 2010
- 🔍 40 Gy in 5 fractions (8 Gy × 5, ~100 Gy EQD2 at α/β=2) on a noncoplanar robotic platform; fiducial tracking + translational and rotational intrafractional correction mandatory
- 🔍 Androgen suppression not allowed, so the RFS signal is RT-attributable rather than ADT-confounded
- 🔍 Eligibility LR (T1b-T2a, Gleason 6, PSA ≤10) or IR (T1b-T2b, Gleason 7 or Gleason 6 with PSA 10-20), central path review; prostate volume up to 100 cc, no TURP or AUA-score exclusion
- 📊 10-yr outcomes by risk group (Kaplan-Meier, median f/u 9 yr)

  | Endpoint | Whole group | Low risk | Intermediate risk | p |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Freedom from biochemical/clinical relapse | 92% (87-96) | 94% (89-99) | 86% (77-94) | 0.19 |
  | Freedom from local failure | 96% (93-99) | 98% (95-100) | 94% (89-99) | 0.12 |
  | Overall survival | 84% (76-91) | 91% (85-97) | 78% (67-89) | 0.22 |
  | Cumulative grade 3 GI or GU | not reported | 1.4% (0-3.4) | 1.5% (0-3.7) | ns |

- 📊 MSK favorable vs unfavorable IR 10-yr RFS 92% (90-100) vs 77% (60-93), p=0.002; the only split that separated
- 📊 Grade 2+ toxicity at 10 yr: GI 2.1%, GU 14%; four pts had five grade 3 events, all GU, none after year 5, no grade 4-5
- 🔗 vs RTOG 0126 dose-escalated EBRT: IR 10-yr RFS 86% here vs 74-78% reported there, per the authors' own comparison
- 🔗 vs PACE-B and PATRIOT: toxicity higher in those SBRT arms; PACE-B reported several-fold higher CTCAE rates on conventional linac vs the 41% treated with CyberKnife, consistent with MIRAGE
- ⚠️ Nonrandomized single-arm; the EBRT comparison is cross-trial and historical, and the authors state a randomized trial is required for meaningful comparison
- ⚠️ Sponsor-funded (Accuray) with a device-specific platform and mandatory intrafractional tracking; the toxicity result may not transfer to linac-based SBRT
- ⚠️ Two-thirds of relapses occurred between 5 and 10 yr, so 5-yr datasets (including PACE-B) systematically undercount failure

[doi:10.1016/j.eururo.2026.04.029](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eururo.2026.04.029)

> [!question] Open questions
> - Does ADT addition improve outcomes in unfavorable intermediate-risk grade group 3?
> - Does robotic tracking reduce late toxicity vs linac-based prostate SBRT?

### American Radium Society AUC: Local Intraprostatic Recurrence

> ARS AUC: biopsy mandatory before salvage, reirradiation usually appropriate, ADT alone not recommended, short ADT only with salvage RT.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The panel puts the reirradiation decision on a defined menu (34-40 Gy/5 fx SBRT, 36 Gy/6 fx, HDR 27 Gy/2 fx, LDR 140 Gy I-125) and gates focal vs whole-gland on mpMRI-biopsy concordance, not on lesion size. It also makes tissue mandatory: PSMA PET alone is not enough to reirradiate.

- 🔍 Modified Delphi, 12 voting members (rad onc, urology, med onc), 2 voting rounds, RAND disagreement methodology
- 🔍 PRISMA search of PubMed + Embase run 28 June 2022 across 4 topics; conference abstracts and <5-pt series excluded
- 📊 Panel recommendations by topic

  | Topic | Panel position |
  |---|---|
  | Staging workup | PSMA PET + mpMRI both recommended; biopsy always required before local salvage |
  | Biopsy technique | Strong consensus for BOTH systematic and targeted biopsies |
  | Local salvage | Reirradiation usually appropriate; cryotherapy or HIFU may be appropriate; ADT alone not recommended |
  | Hormonal therapy | Short course probably appropriate with salvage SBRT; long courses recommended against |
  | Target volume | Focal if mpMRI + systematic biopsy agree on location; whole-gland if they disagree |

- 📐 Reirradiation dose schemas the panel considers acceptable
  - SBRT 34-40 Gy in 5 fx (34 Gy/5 fx Fuller prospective series, n=50; 40 Gy/5 fx focal MTD in NCI dose-finding)
  - EBRT 36 Gy in 6 fx (GETUG-AFU 31 phase 1/2, focal, GTV by mpMRI + choline PET, 5-7mm CTV margin)
  - HDR 27 Gy in 2 fx, 32 Gy in 4 fx (single insertion, 30h), or 36 Gy in 6 fx over 2 implants
  - LDR 140 Gy (I-125) or 120 Gy (Pd-103), per RTOG 0526
- 📊 MASTER meta-analysis (150 studies, 11 322 pts): adjusted severe toxicity vs RP

  | Modality | Severe GU (adjusted) | Severe GI (adjusted) |
  |---|---|---|
  | RP (reference) | 20% | 1.8% |
  | SBRT | 5.6% | not reported in source |
  | HDR brachytherapy | 9.6% | 0.0% (p < 0.01 vs RP) |
  | LDR brachytherapy | 9.1% | not reported in source |

- 📊 MASTER adjusted 5-yr RFS 50-60% across modalities, no significant survival difference vs RP
- 📊 RTOG 0526 (salvage LDR, n=92): DFS 61% at 5 yr, 33% at 10 yr, 10-yr local failure 5%, median f/u 6.7 yr
- 🔍 FORECAST: mpMRI sens 94% (88-98%), spec 18% (7-35%); MRI-targeted biopsy sens 92% (83-97%), spec 75% (45-92%)
- 🔗 Liu et al (n=79 exclusively radiorecurrent): PSMA PET vs conventional concordant in 56%, 34% upstaged, 10% downstaged
- ⚠️ Evidence base predates PSMA PET, so the exact population the guideline targets was not identifiable in the source studies
- ⚠️ Data limited to pts initially treated with conventionally fractionated EBRT; prior brachytherapy excluded, and moderate/ultrahypofractionation is now SOC
- ⚠️ Panel disagreement on castrate-resistant local recurrence (variant 3), second salvage (variant 4), and prior grade 3 toxicity (variant 5)
- ⚠️ No agreement on relugolix in the radiorecurrent setting; HERO MACE signal was an unplanned secondary analysis and PRONOUNCE did not replicate
- ⚠️ Topics 3 and 4 had no studies meeting inclusion criteria, so those recommendations are expert opinion only

**Sources:**
- 📄 **American Radium Society Appropriate Use Criteria for the Workup and Treatment of Local Intraprostatic Recurrence of Prostate Cancer Following Definitive Radiotherapy** — Valle, Luca F.; Jiang, Tommy; Rosenbloom, Ashton et al.. *European Urology Oncology* (2025-06). [doi:10.1016/j.euo.2024.09.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.euo.2024.09.005)
  - 📎 [[papers/prostate/american-radium-society-appropriate-use-criteria-for-the-workup.pdf|American Radium Society AUC: Local Intraprostatic Recurrence (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions
> - Salvage safety after upfront moderate or ultrahypofractionated RT
> - Focal vs whole-gland salvage in the PSMA PET era
> - Optimal management of castrate-resistant local intraprostatic recurrence

### NRG-GU005

> DFS futility crossed (HR 1.38, 95% CI 0.91-2.09, p=0.13); SBRT not superior to MH-IMRT, but bowel HRQOL better.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The bowel HRQOL win is confounded: rectal spacer, used in 56% SBRT vs 55% IMRT, independently improved bowel scores (LS mean -2.81, p=0.0011). The harder number for an RT reader is 3-yr biochemical failure, 8% vs 4% (p=0.037) against 36.25 Gy/5 fx, which argues against treating five fractions as dose-equivalent in intermediate risk.

- 🔍 Randomized non-blinded phase III, N=698 (SBRT 353, MH-IMRT 345), co-primary DFS + EPIC-26 HRQOL
- 🔍 SBRT 36.25 Gy/5 fx (7.25 Gy, 2-3 fx/wk), CTV-PTV 5 mm radial, 3 mm posterior + anterior
- 🔍 MH-IMRT 70 Gy/28 fx or 60 Gy/20 fx, CTV-PTV 8 mm except 5 mm posterior
- 🔍 CTV = prostate ± 1 cm proximal SV; fiducials, MRI fusion and daily IGRT mandated
- 📊 PTV max dose capped 38.78 Gy without urethral visualization; acceptable variation 43.5 Gy
- 📊 EPIC-26 MCID decline rates, SBRT vs MH-IMRT

  | Domain (timepoint) | SBRT | MH-IMRT | p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Bowel, 2 yr (1° EP) | 35% | 44% | 0.034 |
  | Urinary irritative/obstructive, 2 yr (1° EP) | 35% | 34% | 0.68 |
  | Bowel, 1 yr | 33% | 46% | 0.002 |
  | Sexual, 1 yr | 34% | 44% | 0.026 |
  | Urinary incontinence, 2 yr | 26% | 35% | 0.023 |

- 📐 Longitudinal mixed-model treatment effect (least square mean, favors SBRT)
  - Bowel domain: LS mean 2.68 (95% CI 1.02, 4.34), p=0.0016
  - Urinary incontinence: LS mean 2.91 (95% CI 0.85, 4.97), p=0.0058
  - Sexual and hormonal domains: no significant treatment effect
- 📊 Investigator-reported toxicity, SBRT vs MH-IMRT

  | Event | SBRT | MH-IMRT | p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Treatment-related G3+ GU | 0.6% | 2.5% | 0.04 |
  | Rectal hemorrhage, any grade | 10.5% | 17.3% | 0.01 |
  | Fatigue, any grade | 39.2% | 50.8% | 0.0025 |

- ⚠️ 3-yr cumulative biochemical failure higher with SBRT: 8% (5.2-11.0) vs 4% (2.3-7.0), p=0.037
- ⚠️ DFS point estimate favors IMRT numerically; 5-yr DFS 89% (85-92) SBRT vs 92% (89-95) IMRT
- 🔗 vs PACE-B: both trials show lower urinary incontinence decline with SBRT, though MCID definitions differ
- ⚠️ Rectal spacer used in 56% SBRT vs 55% IMRT and independently improved bowel scores (LS mean -2.81, 95% CI -4.49, -1.13; p=0.0011), confounding the bowel read

## 👄 Head & Neck

First ESTRO consensus CTV atlas for post-op OCSCC, filling a gap where no delineation guideline existed.

### ESTRO Post-op CTV Delineation Guidelines (Oral Cavity SCC)

> First ESTRO post-op CTV atlas for OCSCC: CTV-P = (GTV-P pre-op + 10 mm) ∪ (surgical defect/flap + 5 mm), EQD2 60 Gy.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The operational change is delineating a surrogate GTV-P pre-op alongside the defect or flap, not the flap alone: Fig 3.1 shows a buccal case where the re-created GTV-P extended superiorly beyond the defect and a flap-only CTV would have geographically missed the infratemporal fossa. Same logic nodally, where GTV-N pre-op + 10 mm covers pENE spread that level-based landmarks undercover caudally.

- 🔍 ESTRO multidisciplinary expert group; no prior consensus guideline existed for post-op CTV in mucosal HNSCC
- 📐 CTV-P composite: GTV-P pre-op + 10 mm isotropic, plus surgical defect or flap + 5 mm isotropic, edited for bone, fascia, air, teeth, intra-oral prosthesis
- 🔍 10 mm around GTV-P pre-op mirrors the 5 + 5 mm definitive-setting expansion; grounded in surgical series showing microscopic infiltration within 5 mm of GTV-P edge in >95 % of resected OCSCC
- 📊 Dose prescription by target volume (EQD2)

  | Volume | EQD2 |
  |---|---|
  | CTV-P post-op | 60 Gy |
  | CTV-P high-risk (positive <1 mm margin) | over 60 Gy, e.g. 64–66 Gy |
  | CTV-N1 (involved levels) | 60 Gy |
  | CTV-N2 (undissected at-risk levels) | 50 Gy |
  | CTV-N2, dissected at-risk levels | clinician discretion, 50–60 Gy |
  | CTV-N high-risk (pENE) | over 60 Gy, e.g. 64–66 Gy |

- 📊 Nodal CTV margin around the re-created GTV-N pre-op depends on pENE
  - No pENE: GTV-N pre-op + 5 mm, composited with involved level(s) and post-op change → CTV-N1
  - pENE present: GTV-N pre-op + 10 mm → CTV-N high-risk, for radial spread from a disrupted capsule
  - Nodes not identifiable on pre-op imaging or poor co-registration: delineate whole involved level(s) + seroma/clips instead
- 🔍 Neck PORT thresholds after neck dissection
  - pN0 after adequate (≥18 node) dissection: neck PORT generally not required
  - pN1 after adequate dissection: PORT may not be required; single node >10 mm but ≤30 mm is 'intermediate risk'
  - Single node ≤10 mm: adjuvant RT not routinely recommended
  - pN2-N3: neck PORT required, CTV-N1 plus adjacent and undissected at-risk levels
- 🔍 Rigid co-registration of pre-op contrast CT and/or MRI to a 2.0 mm (range 1–3 mm) planning CT, matched to C1-C3 or mandible/maxilla, not to soft tissue
- 🔗 Extends the 2018 definitive-setting HNSCC CTV guidelines and complements GORTEC 2020 flap delineation, which outlined flap body and vascular pedicle separately
- ⚠️ Consensus by expert discussion and international peer review, not a scored Delphi; no agreement statistics reported in source
- ⚠️ Dose to dissected uninvolved levels is explicitly unresolved; the supporting MD Anderson observation dates to 1993 and was never tested prospectively
- ⚠️ Reduced-dose flap-core approaches are described but not recommended: the guideline states a lack of data and consensus on their value
- ⚠️ Scope is OCSCC only; R2 resection is excluded, and other head-and-neck subsites are deferred to future guidelines
