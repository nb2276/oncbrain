# 2026-07-08 — ASTRO Annual Meeting 2025

**NRG-GU005: 5-fx SBRT beats MH-IMRT on 2yr bowel MCID decline (35% vs 44%, p=0.034) but DFS superiority stopped for futility (HR 1.38).**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Prostate carried the day, and the read is fractionation, not efficacy: NRG-GU005 buys toxicity with SBRT while 3yr biochemical failure runs 8% vs 4% (p=0.037), and a 10-yr 40 Gy/5 fx cohort shows 90% RFS with two-thirds of relapses after year 5. Head/neck gets its first post-op OCSCC CTV atlas, consensus-only.

## 🌰 Prostate

Two ultrahypofractionation datasets plus an ARS consensus on salvaging the irradiated gland.

### SBRT for Prostate Cancer: 10-yr Nonrandomized Cohort (Meier et al.)

> 10-yr RFS 90% overall (94% LR, 86% IR) after 40 Gy/5 fx SBRT, late Gr3 GU 1.4-1.5%, no Gr4-5.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Two-thirds of relapses fell between 5 and 10 yr, so every 5-yr SBRT series (including PACE-B) is reading an immature curve. The unfavorable IR subgroup separated at 77% vs 92% (p=0.002) with ADT prohibited, which is where the trial actually moves a decision: whether to add ADT rather than whether to use 40 Gy/5 fx.

- 📊 10-yr OS 84% (95% CI 76-91); LR 91% (85-97) vs IR 78% (67-89), p=0.22
- 📊 10-yr freedom from biochemical/clinical relapse 92% (87-96) whole group
- 📊 10-yr freedom from local failure 96% (93-99); LR 98% (95-100), IR 94% (89-99), p=0.12
- 📊 10-yr RFS by risk stratum (Fig 2)

  | Subgroup | 10-yr RFS | 95% CI | p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Low risk | 94% | 89-99 | 0.19 (LR vs IR) |
  | Intermediate risk | 86% | 77-94 | 0.19 (LR vs IR) |
  | Favorable IR | 92% | 90-100 | 0.002 (fav vs unfav) |
  | Unfavorable IR | 77% | 60-93 | 0.002 (fav vs unfav) |

- 🔍 Dose 40 Gy/5 fx (8 Gy x5, ~100 Gy EQD2 at a/b=2) on a noncoplanar robotic platform
- 🔍 Fiducial tracking with translational + rotational intrafractional correction mandatory
- 🔍 Phase 2 nonrandomized, 21 community/regional/academic sites, N=310 evaluable, median f/u 9 yr
- 🔍 Eligibility: LR T1b-T2a GS6 PSA<=10, or IR T1b-T2b GS7 or PSA 10-20; ADT not allowed; prostate volume up to 100 cc
- 📊 10-yr Gr2+ toxicity 2.1% GI and 14% GU; 4 pts had 5 Gr3 events, all GU; none after year 5
- 🔗 IR result compares favorably to the 74-78% 10-yr RFS with dose-escalated EBRT in RTOG 0126 (authors' comparison)
- 🔗 Toxicity similar to Fuller's CyberKnife SBRT trial; higher in PACE-B SBRT arm and PATRIOT, both largely conventional-linac delivered
- ⚠️ Grade group 3 was the only unfavorable risk factor independently predicting 10-yr biochemical/clinical failure on multivariate analysis
- ⚠️ Two-thirds of relapses occurred between 5-10 yr, so 5-yr series systematically understate failure
- ⚠️ Nonrandomized, sponsor-funded (Accuray), physician-reported CTCAE v3 toxicity only, no patient-reported outcomes in source

[doi:10.1016/j.eururo.2026.04.029](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eururo.2026.04.029)

### American Radium Society AUC: Local Intraprostatic Recurrence

> Consensus AUC: PSMA PET + mpMRI, mandatory targeted AND systematic biopsy, then reirradiation over RP for isolated intraprostatic radiorecurrence.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The volumetric rule is the operational content: focal salvage when mpMRI and systematic biopsy agree on location, whole-gland when they disagree or the lesion sits away from the index lesion. Panel puts SBRT and HDR ahead of RP on therapeutic ratio (severe GU 5.6% and 9.6% vs 20%), with a short 4-6 mo LHRH agonist for radiosensitization.

- 📊 Panel recommendations by topic (modified Delphi, 12 voting members)

  | Topic | Panel position |
  |---|---|
  | Staging | PSMA PET + mpMRI to exclude metastatic disease and map local extent |
  | Biopsy | Required before salvage; both targeted AND systematic |
  | Local salvage | Reirradiation usually appropriate; cryo/HIFU may be appropriate |
  | ADT alone | Not recommended for confirmed local recurrence, any agent or duration |
  | ADT with RT salvage | Short 4-6 mo LHRH agonist appropriate; novel agents not endorsed |
  | Target volume | Focal if mpMRI + systematic biopsy concordant; whole-gland if discordant |

- 📐 Reirradiation dose schemas the panel considers acceptable
  - SBRT 34-40 Gy in 5 fx (34 Gy/5 whole-gland Fuller series; 40 Gy/5 focal MTD)
  - EBRT 36 Gy in 6 fx (GETUG-AFU 31)
  - HDR 27 Gy in 2 fx, 32 Gy in 4 fx, or 36 Gy in 6 fx over 2 implants
  - LDR minimum target dose 140 Gy (I-125) or 120 Gy (Pd-103), per RTOG 0526
- 📊 MASTER meta-analysis toxicity, RP as reference (adjusted rates)

  | Modality | Severe GU | Severe GI |
  |---|---|---|
  | Radical prostatectomy | 20% | 1.8% |
  | SBRT | 5.6% | not reported in source |
  | HDR brachytherapy | 9.6% | 0.0% (p < 0.01 vs RP) |
  | LDR brachytherapy | 9.1% | not reported in source |

- 📊 MASTER pooled 150 studies, 11 322 pts; adjusted 5-yr RFS 50-60%, no survival difference between any modality and RP
- 🔍 Biopsy mandate is driven by radiographic false positives: PSMA PET false-positive rate 8% in Fendler (n=635), post-RT change the usual culprit
- 🔍 Systematic biopsy cannot be dropped: FORECAST secondary analysis found up to 59% of true radiorecurrences would be occult with MRI-targeted cores alone
- 📐 mpMRI alone is a poor gate: sensitivity 94% (88-98%) but specificity 18% (7-35%) vs systematic biopsy reference (FORECAST, n=155)
- 🔗 RTOG 0526 (LDR salvage, n=92) reported DFS 61% at 5 yr and 33% at 10 yr with 10-yr local failure 5%, published after MASTER closed
- ⚠️ Evidence base predates PSMA PET, so the very patients this guideline defines (local-only) could not be identified in the trials it cites
- ⚠️ Evidence limited to prior conventionally fractionated EBRT; prior brachytherapy pts were excluded and moderate/ultrahypofractionated first courses are unstudied for salvage
- ⚠️ No consensus on second salvage (variant 4), on castrate-resistant local recurrence (variant 3), or on modality after prior grade 3 toxicity (variant 5)

**Sources:**
- 📄 **American Radium Society Appropriate Use Criteria for the Workup and Treatment of Local Intraprostatic Recurrence of Prostate Cancer Following Definitive Radiotherapy** — Valle, Luca F.; Jiang, Tommy; Rosenbloom, Ashton et al.. *European Urology Oncology* (2025-06). [doi:10.1016/j.euo.2024.09.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.euo.2024.09.005)
  - 📎 [[papers/prostate/american-radium-society-appropriate-use-criteria-for-the-workup.pdf|American Radium Society AUC: Local Intraprostatic Recurrence (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions
> - Salvage outcomes after prior hypofractionated or ultrahypofractionated RT
> - Focal vs whole-gland salvage reirradiation in a randomized comparison
> - Optimal management of castrate-resistant local intraprostatic recurrence

### NRG-GU005

> 2yr bowel MCID decline 35% vs 44% (p=0.034) favoring 5-fraction SBRT, but DFS superiority crossed interim futility (HR 1.38).

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The efficacy caution sits in the secondary data: 3yr cumulative biochemical failure was 8% (5.2-11.0) with SBRT vs 4% (2.3-7.0) with IMRT, p=0.037, under a QoL-positive headline. The bowel benefit came with mandated fiducials, MRI fusion, daily IGRT, 5 mm/3 mm PTV margins and a spacer in 56%, which is the setup a department has to reproduce.

- 📊 Co-primary and efficacy endpoints, SBRT vs MH-IMRT

  | Endpoint | SBRT | MH-IMRT | Stat |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 2yr bowel MCID decline | 35% | 44% | p=0.034 |
  | 2yr urinary irritative/obstructive MCID decline | 35% | 34% | p=0.68 |
  | 5yr DFS | 89% (85-92) | 92% (89-95) | HR 1.38 (0.91-2.09), p=0.13 |
  | 3yr cumulative biochemical failure | 8% (5.2-11.0) | 4% (2.3-7.0) | p=0.037 |
  | 5yr OS | 91% (85-95) | 94% (90-97) | p=0.66 |

- 🔍 Randomised non-blinded phase 3, N=698 (SBRT 353 / MH-IMRT 345), designed for superiority
- 📐 SBRT 36.25 Gy/5 fx at 7.25 Gy, 2-3 fx/wk vs 70 Gy/28 fx or 60 Gy/20 fx
- 🔍 Fiducials, MRI fusion for planning and daily advanced IGRT mandated in both arms
- 📊 Secondary EPIC-26 MCID declines, SBRT vs MH-IMRT

  | Domain / timepoint | SBRT | MH-IMRT | p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Bowel, 1yr | 33% | 46% | 0.002 |
  | Sexual, 1yr | 34% | 44% | 0.026 |
  | Urinary incontinence, 2yr | 26% | 35% | 0.023 |

- 📊 Investigator-reported toxicity, SBRT vs MH-IMRT

  | Event | SBRT | MH-IMRT | p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Treatment-related G≥3 GU | 0.6% | 2.5% | 0.04 |
  | Rectal hemorrhage, any grade | 10.5% | 17.3% | 0.01 |
  | Fatigue, any grade | 39.2% | 50.8% | 0.0025 |

- ⚠️ Stopped for DFS futility, not harm; no non-inferiority margin was prespecified
- 📐 Spacer use improved EPIC bowel scores (LS mean -2.81, 95% CI -4.49 to -1.13, p=0.0011)
- 🔗 vs PACE-B: both show lower urinary incontinence decline with SBRT, MCID definitions differ

## 👄 Head & Neck

ESTRO fills the post-op CTV gap with geometry, not outcome data.

### ESTRO CTV Delineation Guidelines (Post-op OCSCC)

> First consensus post-op CTV atlas for oral cavity SCC: CTV-P = GTV-P pre-op + 10 mm composited with surgical defect/flap + 5 mm.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The load-bearing recommendation is asymmetric: 10 mm around a re-created pre-op GTV, only 5 mm around the surgical defect or flap, unioned rather than nested. Fig 3.1/3.2 show why the GTV-P step is not optional, the pre-op tumour extended superiorly beyond the defect into the infratemporal fossa, a geographical miss if the defect alone had been used.

- 🔍 ESTRO multidisciplinary expert group; no consensus guideline previously existed for post-op CTV delineation in mucosal HNSCC
- 📐 Primary CTV construction (Table 1, three steps)
  - Step 1a: re-create GTV-P pre-op on diagnostic MRI/CT, edit on planning CT
  - Step 1b: delineate surgical defect (no reconstruction) or flap body (reconstructed)
  - Step 2a: GTV-P pre-op + 10 mm isotropic
  - Step 2b: surgical defect/flap + 5 mm isotropic, added independently
  - Step 2c: CTV-P = union of both, edited for bone, fascia, air, intra-oral prosthesis
- 📐 10 mm justified from surgical series: microscopic infiltration within 5 mm of GTV-P edge in >95% of resected OCSCC
- 📊 Dose prescription by volume (EQD2)

  | Volume | EQD2 dose |
  |---|---|
  | CTV-P post-op (PTV) | 60 Gy |
  | CTV-P high-risk (positive <1mm margin) | over 60 Gy, e.g. 64-66 Gy |
  | CTV-N1 (involved levels) | 60 Gy |
  | CTV-N2 (undissected at-risk levels) | 50 Gy |
  | CTV-N2 (dissected at-risk levels) | unknown optimum, 50-60 Gy typical |
  | CTV-N high-risk (pENE) | over 60 Gy, e.g. 64-66 Gy |

- 🔍 Nodal margin depends on pENE status, not level anatomy alone
  - No pENE: GTV-N pre-op + 5 mm, composited with involved level(s) and post-op change → CTV-N1
  - pENE: GTV-N pre-op + 10 mm → CTV-N high-risk
  - Nodes not identifiable pre-op or poor co-registration: outline whole involved level(s) + seroma/clips instead
- 🔍 When PORT to the neck can be omitted (adequate ≥18 node dissection)
  - pN0 after adequate dissection: neck PORT generally not required
  - pN1 after adequate dissection: may not be required; single node >10 mm but ≤30 mm is an indication
  - Single node ≤10 mm: adjuvant RT not routinely recommended
  - PORT indicated in neck but not primary: still treat both, to cover T-N tract and lingual nodes
- 🔗 Extends the 2018 definitive-setting HNSCC CTV guideline's 5 + 5 mm geometric expansion into the post-op setting
- 🔗 GORTEC 2020 flap delineation guidance cited as a useful adjunct for standardising flap outlining
- ⚠️ Consensus + expert opinion, no outcome data: no recurrence, toxicity or dosimetric endpoint tests these margins
- ⚠️ Whether central flap body can be dose-de-escalated is explicitly unresolved; a flap avoidance structure is offered without supporting data
- ⚠️ Dose to dissected uninvolved levels left to clinician discretion, resting on a 1993 MD Anderson observation never tested prospectively

[doi:10.1016/j.radonc.2025.111135](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.radonc.2025.111135)
  - 📎 [[papers/head-neck/delineation-of-the-post-operative-primary-tumour-and-nodal-clini.pdf|ESTRO CTV Delineation Guidelines (Post-op OCSCC) (full text)]]
