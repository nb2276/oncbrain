# 2026-10-17

**RT CHARM: PMRT at 42.56 Gy/16 fx noninferior to 50 Gy/25 fx for 24-mo reconstruction complications, 12.3% vs 10.2%, difference -2.1% (95% CI -6.5 to 2.2).**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Single-site day carried by breast: RT CHARM supports moderate hypofractionation for PMRT with reconstruction, with 3-yr local/LR RFS 97.0% vs 96.2% (HR 1.01) and less G2 dermatitis (20% vs 42%). Caveats: wide 10% NI margin, median f/u 3.9 yr, underpowered reconstruction subgroups. Delayed reconstruction predicted complications (OR 2.96).

## 🎀 Breast

PMRT fractionation after mastectomy with reconstruction, where reconstruction-complication concern has kept many on 50 Gy/25 fx.

### RT CHARM (Alliance A221505) · [[NCT03414970]]

> 24-mo reconstruction complications 12.3% (42.56 Gy/16 fx) vs 10.2% (50 Gy/25 fx), difference -2.1% (95% CI -6.5 to 2.2); noninferior.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Reconstruction timing mattered more than fractionation: delayed reconstruction carried OR 2.96 for complications in either arm, while arm was not a predictor. LRR at 3 yr was 1.5% vs 2.2% and G2 dermatitis halved (20% vs 42%), so this supports 16 fx PMRT for reconstructed pts, including implant-only.

- 🔍 Unblinded 2-arm randomized (1:1) noninferiority trial, 209 US/Canadian sites, N=898 enrolled, 825 evaluable
- 🔍 NI margin 10%, assuming a 2-yr complication rate of 25% with conventional PMRT
- 🔍 Stratified by immediate vs delayed and autologous vs implant-only reconstruction
- 💊 50 Gy/25 fx over 5 wk vs 42.56 Gy/16 fx over 3 wk
- 📊 Outcomes by arm

  | Endpoint | 42.56 Gy/16 fx | 50 Gy/25 fx | Effect |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 24-mo reconstruction complications (ITT) | 12.3% (52/422) | 10.2% (41/403) | -2.1% (95% CI -6.5 to 2.2), NI p=0.0001 |
  | Completed reconstruction within 18 mo (n=631) | 12.6% | 10.4% | -2.2% (95% CI -7.2 to 2.8), p=0.001 |
  | 3-yr local/LR recurrence, cumulative incidence | 1.5% (0.7-3.2) | 2.2% (1.1-4.3) | Gray's test ns |
  | 3-yr local/LR RFS | 97.0% (95.4-98.7) | 96.2% (94.3-98.2) | HR 1.01 (0.53-1.96), p=0.97 |
  | Acute G3+ non-reconstruction toxicity | 5.0% | 7.7% | p=0.11 |
  | Acute G2 dermatitis | 84 (20%) | 171 (42%) | p<.0001 |
  | Acute G3 dermatitis | 3 (1%) | 11 (3%) | p=0.048 |

- 📊 Composite components, all pts
  - Unplanned admission 5.2%
  - Unplanned re-operation 7.8%
  - Baker 3-4 capsular contracture 3.6%
  - Complete reconstruction failure 1.2%
- 📐 Multivariable predictors of complication (either arm)
  - Delayed vs immediate reconstruction: OR 2.96 (95% CI 1.4-6.2), p=0.004
  - Implant-only vs autologous: OR 1.65 (95% CI 1.0-2.8), p=0.07
  - Treatment arm, BMI, diabetes: not significant predictors
- ⚠️ Abstract calls implant-only complications 'higher'; full text OR 1.65 has p=0.07
- ⚠️ 10% absolute margin is wide against an observed 10.2% control rate
- ⚠️ Median f/u 3.9 yr; late capsular contracture and failure may still accrue
- ⚠️ Reconstruction subgroups too small to declare NI individually
- 🔍 RT QA: 805 (90%) per-protocol or acceptable variation; 40 (4%) unacceptable
- ⚠️ Target volumes, IMN coverage, bolus and boost use not stated in source excerpt

**Sources:**
- 📄 [[PMID 42863096]] **Randomized Trial of Hypofractionated Postmastectomy Radiation Therapy in Women With Breast Reconstruction (RT CHARM, Alliance A221505).** — Poppe; Le-Rademacher; Haffty et al.. *Journal of clinical oncology : official journal of the American Society of Clinical Oncology* (2026-10). [doi:10.1200/jco-26-00474](https://doi.org/10.1200/jco-26-00474). [article](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC13660432/)
  > PURPOSE: Patients with breast cancer requiring postmastectomy radiation therapy (PMRT) often desire breast reconstruction. Reconstructions can be adversely affected by PMRT, and fear of reconstruction complications has discouraged adoption of short-course radiation therapy used after lumpectomy. To fill the evidence gap, we conducted a noninferiority trial of hypofractionated versus conventional PMRT in patients undergoing breast reconstruction. The primary outcome was reconstruction complication rate, a composite of wound healing, readmission, capsular contracture, unplanned reoperation, and reconstruction failure.
  > METHODS: Women with unilateral invasive breast cancer with planned immediate or delayed reconstruction and PMRT were randomly assigned (1:1) to 50 Gy (25 fractions/5 weeks) or 42.56 Gy (16 fractions/3 weeks). This study was designed with a noninferiority margin of 10%, assuming a 2-year reconstruction complication rate of 25% with conventional PMRT. Randomization was stratified by immediate versus delayed and autologous versus implant-only reconstruction.
  > RESULTS: Between 2018 and 2021, 898 patients enrolled with a median follow-up of 3.9 years; 825 patients were evaluable for the primary end point. The 24-month reconstruction complication rates were 12.3% (52/422) with hypofractionated PMRT and 10.2% (41/403) with conventional PMRT, corresponding to an estimated difference of -2.1% (95% CI, -6.5 to 2.2). The complication rate was higher (regardless of arm) with implant-only versus autologous reconstruction (odds ratio = 1.65). There was no statistically significant difference in 36-month locoregional recurrence rates: 1.5% (95% CI, 0.7 to 3.2) with hypofractionated and 2.2% (95% CI, 1.1 to 4.3) with conventional PMRT. Acute and late toxicity rates were not statistically different.
  > CONCLUSION: A 16-fraction course of hypofractionated PMRT is safe and noninferior to conventional PMRT in women undergoing breast reconstruction after mastectomy.
