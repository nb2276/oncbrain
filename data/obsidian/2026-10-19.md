# 2026-10-19

**MSKCC T1-2N1 post-mastectomy: 10-yr LRR 7.0% without vs 4.0% with PMRT, but age <40 + LVI reached 28% without PMRT.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> A breast-only day on locoregional risk after surgery. MSKCC T1-2N1 and MGH node-negative cohorts support omitting PMRT at low risk, with age, LVI, margin and size picking out who still fails. NSABP pooled data put mortality HR 5.85 on oLRR. SAKK 23/82 TAM after RT for isolated LRR: 5y DFS 61% vs 40%, no OS gain.

## 🎀 Breast

Retrospective and pooled data dominate: these sharpen risk stratification for PMRT and regional RT, but none randomizes the RT decision itself.

### Locoregional recurrence after mastectomy in node-negative pts

> 10-yr LRR 6.0% in node-negative pts after mastectomy without PMRT, rising to 40.6% with 3 risk factors (>2cm, margin <2mm, premenopausal, LVI).

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Chest wall carried 80.4% of failures, so the authors' case for chest-wall-only fields in selected pN0 pts rests on pattern-of-failure data, not just risk level. Margin <2mm (close 22%, positive 21% at 10 yr) is the factor most directly actionable at PMRT consult, ahead of the 3-factor 40.6% estimate with its ±13.8% error.

- 🔍 Retrospective MGH cohort: 877 node-negative cases (870 pts), mastectomy + ALND, no PMRT, 1980-2000
- 🔍 T4 excluded. Median f/u 100 mo, median age 64, median 15 nodes examined
- 📊 10-yr cumulative incidence (CIF) of total LRR 6.0%, isolated LRR 4.3%
- 📊 Chest wall was the failure site in 80.4% of total LRR, 87.5% of isolated LRR
- 📊 10-yr total LRR by univariate prognostic factor

  | Factor | Adverse group | Favorable group | p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Menopausal status | Premenopausal 11.1% | Postmenopausal 5.1% | 0.01 |
  | Deep margin | Close 22% / positive 21% | Negative 5.1% | <0.001 |
  | LVI | Present 19.8% | Absent/not recorded 5.0% | 0.0001 |
  | Tumor size | >2 cm 11% | ≤2 cm 3.1% | 0.001 |

- 📊 10-yr LRR by number of independent risk factors (size, margin, LVI, menopausal status)

  | Risk factors | 10-yr LRR |
  |---|---|
  | 0 | 1.2% ± 0.9% |
  | 1 | 10.0% ± 2.9% |
  | 2 | 17.9% ± 7.5% |
  | 3 | 40.6% ± 13.8% |

- 📐 MVA (Cox): size, margin, LVI, menopausal status independent; HRs ranged 2.6 to 3.8
- 📊 Grade on univariate only: 10-yr LRR 0% G1, 5.3% G2, 10% G3 (p=0.03)
- ⚠️ 3-factor estimate carries ±13.8%; only 1 pt had all 4 factors
- ⚠️ Systemic therapy in 276/877 only; pre-modern era likely inflates LRR vs current practice
- ⚠️ LVI not recorded was pooled with LVI absent, risking misclassification
- ⚠️ No PMRT arm: benefit is inferred from risk level, not measured

[doi:10.1016/j.ijrobp.2004.12.014](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijrobp.2004.12.014)

> [!question] Open questions
> - Does PMRT reduce LRR or improve survival in multi-factor high-risk pN0 pts?
> - Do these LRR risks hold with modern systemic therapy?
> - Is chest-wall-only RT sufficient for high-risk pN0 pts?

### EBCTCG 20-year recurrence after stopping endocrine therapy at 5 years

> Distant recurrence 5-20y after stopping 5y ET ranged 13% (T1N0) to 41% (T2N4-9), with steady annual risk throughout, in ER+ early breast.

- 🔍 IPD meta-analysis, 88 EBCTCG trials, 62,923 ER+ women disease-free at 5y after scheduled 5y ET
- 🔍 T1-T2, 0-9 positive nodes, age <75 at diagnosis; all had lymph-node dissection
- 💊 Assigned ET: 63% tamoxifen, 17% AI, 20% tamoxifen-AI sequence
- 📊 Distant recurrence, years 5-20, by TN status

  | Nodal status | T1 | T2 |
  |---|---|---|
  | N0 | 13% | 19% |
  | N1-3 | 20% | 26% |
  | N4-9 | 34% | 41% |

- 📊 T1N0 risk, years 5-20, by tumor grade

  | Endpoint | Low grade | Moderate grade | High grade |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Distant recurrence | 10% | 13% | 17% |
  | Any recurrence or contralateral | 17% | 22% | 26% |

- 📊 20y risk from year 0 (n=74,194), by nodal status

  | Endpoint | N0 | N1-3 | N4-9 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Distant recurrence | 22% | 31% | 52% |
  | Breast-cancer death | 15% | 28% | 49% |

- 📊 T1N0 annual distant recurrence ~1% per year throughout years 5-20
- 📊 Contralateral BC ~0.3%/yr after stopping, independent of age or TN status
- 📐 Given TN status, grade and Ki-67 only moderately prognostic after year 5; PR and HER2 status not predictive
- ⚠️ ~50% diagnosed before 2000 (range 1976-2011); modern staging and therapy likely lower absolute risks
- ⚠️ Scheduled, not completed, 5y ET; authors estimate 5-10% of distant recurrences missed in trial records
- ⚠️ No gene-expression data; only 2% scheduled trastuzumab
- ⚠️ Locoregional recurrence followed the same risk factors as distant, but no LRR magnitudes reported in source text

**Sources:**
- 📄 **20-Year Risks of Breast-Cancer Recurrence after Stopping Endocrine Therapy at 5 Years** — Pan, Hongchao; Gray, Richard; Braybrooke, Jeremy et al.. *New England Journal of Medicine* (2017-11). [doi:10.1056/nejmoa1701830](https://doi.org/10.1056/nejmoa1701830)
  - 📎 [[papers/breast/2017-pan-20-year-risks-breast-cancer-recurrence-stopping.pdf|EBCTCG 20-year recurrence after stopping endocrine therapy at 5 years (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions

> - Absolute benefit of extended ET versus fracture and thromboembolic risk
> - Late recurrence risk in modern-era, sentinel-node-staged cohorts

### SAKK 23/82

> Mature 11.6-yr f/u: 5y DFS 61% vs 40% with TAM after excision + RT for isolated post-mastectomy LRR; OS unchanged (median 11.5 vs 11.2 y, P=0.79).

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The RT-relevant read is in-field durability: after excision plus 5000 cGy at 200 cGy/fx, further local recurrence was the only failure type TAM significantly reduced, while distant failure did not differ. Even without systemic therapy, 30% stayed free of further local and distant relapse, which supports treating ILRR with curative-intent local therapy.

- 🔍 Phase III randomized, 178 randomized, 167 eligible; median f/u 11.6 yrs for surviving pts
- 🔍 'Good risk' isolated locoregional recurrence (ILRR) after mastectomy
  - ER+ recurrence, OR unknown ER with DFI >12 mo and <4 nodules, max diameter 3 cm
  - Sites: ipsilateral chest wall, shoulder, neck, upper arm, or regional nodes
  - Prior adjuvant TAM excluded
- 💊 After local Rx: TAM 20 mg/d until progression vs observation; stratified by menopausal status, adjuvant chemo, axillary nodes
- 🔍 Local Rx both arms: radical excision + RT 5000 cGy at 200 cGy/fx to involved region
- 📊 Outcomes by arm

  | Endpoint | TAM | Observation | p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Median DFS | 6.5 y | 2.7 y | log-rank P = 0.053; Gehan-Wilcoxon P = 0.008 |
  | 5-yr DFS | 61% | 40% | n/a |
  | Median OS | 11.5 y | 11.2 y | P = 0.79 |
  | 5-yr OS | 73% | 79% | n/a |

- 📊 Of failure types, only further local recurrence was significantly reduced by TAM
- 📊 Outcomes by menopausal status

  | Subgroup · endpoint | TAM | Observation | p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Premenopausal 5-yr DFS | 60% | 60% | n/a |
  | Postmenopausal 5-yr DFS | 61% | 33% | P = 0.006 |
  | Premenopausal 5-yr OS | 67% | 90% | P = 0.175 |
  | Postmenopausal 5-yr OS | 75% | 75% | n/a |

- ⚠️ DFS gain borderline on log-rank (P=0.053); stronger P relies on early-weighted Gehan-Wilcoxon test
- ⚠️ Premenopausal subgroup small (n=35); OS deficit with TAM not statistically significant
- ⚠️ TAM-naive population; most modern ILRR pts have already had adjuvant endocrine therapy
- 🔗 Authors cite aromatase inhibitor activity in TAM-pretreated disease and IBCSG 27 (chemo vs local Rx alone) as the open follow-on

[doi:10.1093/annonc/mdg347](https://doi.org/10.1093/annonc/mdg347)

### NSABP node-positive trials: prognosis after IBTR and locoregional recurrence

> Mortality HR 2.58 after IBTR and 5.85 after oLRR in node-positive pts treated with lumpectomy + WBI + chemo; 5y OS 59.9% vs 24.1%.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Supraclavicular failures were the largest oLRR group (81 of 165) and carried 5y DDFS 12.1% vs 31.5% after axillary recurrence. 10y oLRR reached 14.6% with ≥10 positive nodes. Regional nodal RT use is not stated in source, so this is the natural-history case for nodal coverage in node-positive BCS, not a test of it.

- 🔍 Pooled analysis of the lumpectomy subset from five randomized NSABP node-positive trials (B-15, B-16, B-18, B-22, B-25)
- 🔍 N=2,669 node-positive pts treated with lumpectomy, WBI and adjuvant systemic therapy. Median time on study 13.3 yr
- 🔍 Negative margins were required for eligibility
- 📊 Locoregional failure in 424 (15.9%): IBTR 259 (9.7%), oLRR 165 (6.2%)
- 📊 Outcomes after locoregional failure, IBTR vs oLRR

  | Endpoint | IBTR | oLRR |
  |---|---|---|
  | 10-yr cumulative incidence | 8.7% | 6.0% |
  | Share occurring within 5 yr | 62.2% | 80.6% |
  | 5-yr DDFS after event | 51.4% | 18.8% |
  | 5-yr OS after event | 59.9% | 24.1% |
  | Mortality HR (95% CI) | 2.58 (2.11-3.15) | 5.85 (4.80-7.13) |
  | Adjusted distant-disease HR | 2.72 | 6.68 |

- 📊 Cumulative IBTR and oLRR incidence by protocol (Table 3)

  | Protocol | N at risk | 10-yr IBTR | 10-yr oLRR |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | B-15 | 624 | 10.0% | 7.6% |
  | B-16 | 340 | 4.8% | 5.4% |
  | B-18 | 210 | 9.1% | 8.7% |
  | B-22 | 596 | 10.1% | 6.1% |
  | B-25 | 899 | 8.3% | 4.5% |
  | Total | 2,669 | 8.7% | 6.0% |

- 📊 oLRR sites (n=165)
  - Supraclavicular: 81
  - Axillary: 47
  - Chest wall: 13
  - Scar only: 7
  - Other or multiple: 17
- 📊 5-yr DDFS 12.1% (95% CI 5.6-26.4%) after supraclavicular recurrence vs 31.5% (19.9-49.7%) after axillary recurrence
- 📊 10-yr oLRR by nodes: 4.4% (1-3), 7.2% (4-9), 14.6% (≥10), P<.0001
- 📊 IBTR was not associated with nodal count (P=.53)
- 📊 10-yr IBTR by age: 11.1% (<50), 6.1% (50-59), 5.1% (≥60), P<.0001
- 📊 Pathologic tumor >4 cm vs ≤2 cm: 10-yr IBTR 14.3% vs 7.5% (P=.045)
- 📊 Later IBTR carried a >40% reduction in 3-yr mortality risk vs earlier IBTR, across cutoffs
- 📊 3-yr OS only 15.3% when oLRR occurred within 2 yr of the primary
- ⚠️ Recurrence was not a randomized exposure. HRs are prognostic, not the survival gain better local control would recover
- ⚠️ WBI dose, boost and regional nodal fields not stated in source
- ⚠️ Premenopausal women got no tamoxifen regardless of ER, which confounds the age signal for IBTR
- ⚠️ IBTR not split into true recurrence vs new ipsilateral primary

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Prognosis After Ipsilateral Breast Tumor Recurrence and Locoregional Recurrences in Five National Surgical Adjuvant Breast and Bowel Project Node-Positive Adjuvant Breast Cancer Trials** — Wapnir, Irene L.; Anderson, Stewart J.; Mamounas, Eleftherios P. et al.. *Journal of Clinical Oncology* (2006-05). [doi:10.1200/jco.2005.04.3273](https://doi.org/10.1200/jco.2005.04.3273)
  > Purpose Locoregional failure after breast-conserving surgery is associated with increased risk of distant disease and death. The magnitude of this risk in patients receiving chemotherapy has not been adequately characterized. Patients and Methods Our study population included 2,669 women randomly assigned onto five National Surgical Adjuvant Breast and Bowel Project node-positive protocols (B-15, B-16, B-18, B-22, and B-25), who were treated with lumpectomy, whole-breast irradiation, and adjuvant systemic therapy. Cumulative incidences of ipsilateral breast tumor recurrence (IBTR) and other locoregional recurrence (oLRR) were calculated. Kaplan-Meier curves were used to estimate distant–disease-free survival (DDFS) and overall survival (OS) after IBTR or oLRR. Cox models were used to model survival using clinical and pathologic factors jointly with IBTR or oLRR as time-varying predictors. Results Four hundred twenty-four patients (15.9%) experienced locoregional failure; 259 (9.7%) experienced IBTR, and 165 (6.2%) experienced oLRR. The 10-year cumulative incidence of IBTR and oLRR was 8.7% and 6.0%, respectively. Most locoregional failures occurred within 5 years (62.2% for IBTR and 80.6% for oLRR). Age, tumor size, and estrogen receptor status were significantly associated with IBTR. Nodal status and estrogen and progesterone receptor status were significantly associated with oLRR. The 5-year DDFS rates after IBTR and oLRR were 51.4% and 18.8%, respectively. The 5-year OS rates after IBTR and oLRR were 59.9% and 24.1%, respectively. Hazard ratios for mortality associated with IBTR and oLRR were 2.58 (95% CI, 2.11 to 3.15) and 5.85 (95% CI, 4.80 to 7.13), respectively. Conclusion Node-positive breast cancer patients who developed IBTR or oLRR had significantly poorer prognoses than patients who did not experience these events.

### T1-2N1 mastectomy without PMRT (MSKCC)

> 10-yr LRR 7.0% without vs 4.0% with PMRT in T1-2N1 after mastectomy; age <40 + LVI reached 28% without PMRT.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The no-PMRT baseline only holds after full ALND (median 18 nodes): 10-yr LRR 2% at age ≥40 without LVI vs 28% at <40 with LVI. Failures clustered at chest wall (51%) and supraclavicular nodes (27%), so where PMRT is used here, the target is chest wall plus SCV. The axilla is not.

- 🔍 Retrospective single-institution (MSKCC prospective database), mastectomy 1995-2006, N=1087
- 🔍 PMRT at physician discretion: 163 (15%) PMRT vs 924 (85%) no PMRT
- 🔍 Excluded T3/T4, NAC, and isolated tumor cells only
- 🔍 ALND in 94.2%, median 18 nodes resected; median f/u 10.8 yr
- 📊 PMRT vs no PMRT, 10-yr outcomes (unadjusted, non-randomized)

  | Endpoint | PMRT (n=163) | No PMRT (n=924) | Log-rank p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | LRR | 4.0% | 7.0% | 0.32 |
  | RFS | 75% (95% CI 69-82) | 73% (95% CI 69-76) | 0.46 |
  | OS | 81% (95% CI 73-86) | 80% (95% CI 77-82) | 0.44 |

- 📊 No-PMRT group: LRR by age and LVI (Fig 2)

  | Subgroup | 5-yr LRR | 10-yr LRR |
  |---|---|---|
  | <40, LVI | 15% | 28% |
  | <40, no LVI | 6% | 11% |
  | ≥40, LVI | 6% | 8% |
  | ≥40, no LVI | 1% | 2% |

- 📐 MVA for LRR without PMRT: age <40 HR 4.15 (95% CI 2.42-7.11), LVI HR 3.47 (95% CI 1.92-6.29)
- 📊 Pattern of failure, 63 LRRs total
  - Chest wall 32/63 (51%)
  - Supraclavicular 17/63 (27%)
  - Axilla 5 (8%), IMN 3 (5%)
  - Axilla-involving recurrences 10/63 (15.9%)
- 🔍 PMRT fields: chest wall in all; SCV 137/163 (84%), axilla 7 (4%), IMN 6 (4%); dose not reported
- 🔗 vs EBCTCG 1-3 node subset: LRR 20.3 → 3.8% at 10 yr with PMRT, from 1965-1986 era trials
- ⚠️ PMRT arm enriched for ECE, LVI, 3 nodes, macrometastases; arm comparison is confounded by indication
- ⚠️ <40 + LVI subgroup: 28% printed with 95% CI 11.0-22.1, which does not contain the estimate
- ⚠️ Authors state omission does not apply to 1-3 positive sentinel nodes without completion ALND
