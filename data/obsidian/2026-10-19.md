# 2026-10-19

**EBCTCG (62,923 women, ER+): after stopping endocrine therapy at 5y, distant recurrence in years 5-20 runs 13% (T1N0) to 41% (T2N4-9), at a steady annual rate.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> EBCTCG IPD: ER+ recurrence persists 15 years past endocrine therapy, scaled by T and N. RT read: MSKCC T1-2N1 without PMRT, 10y LRR 7.0% but 28% with age <40 + LVI; NSABP node-positive oLRR mortality HR 5.85; SAKK 23/82 tamoxifen after excision + RT for ILRR, 5-yr DFS 61% vs 40%, OS unchanged.

## 🎀 Breast

Five datasets on late and locoregional risk after mastectomy or lumpectomy + WBI. Four are prognostic or retrospective and one is randomized (SAKK 23/82). All feed PMRT and regional nodal RT selection.

### EBCTCG 20-year recurrence after 5 years of endocrine therapy

> After stopping endocrine therapy at 5y, distant recurrence in years 5-20 ran from 13% (T1N0) to 41% (T2N4–9), at a steady annual rate.

- 🔍 EBCTCG IPD meta-analysis: 88 trials, 62,923 women with ER+ disease, disease-free at 5y of scheduled endocrine therapy
- 🔍 T1-T2, 0-9 positive nodes, diagnosed before age 75; Kaplan-Meier + Cox stratified by trial and treatment
- 📊 Distant recurrence, years 5 to 20, by TN status

  | Nodal status | T1 | T2 |
  |---|---|---|
  | N0 | 13% | 19% |
  | N1–3 | 20% | 26% |
  | N4–9 | 34% | 41% |

- 📊 T1N0 only, years 5 to 20, by tumor grade

  | Grade | Distant recurrence | Any recurrence or contralateral BC |
  |---|---|---|
  | Low | 10% | 17% |
  | Moderate | 13% | 22% |
  | High | 17% | 26% |

- 📊 20-yr risk from year 0 (74,194 women, 78 trials), by nodal status

  | Nodal status | Distant recurrence | Death from breast cancer |
  |---|---|---|
  | N0 | 22% | 15% |
  | N1–3 | 31% | 28% |
  | N4–9 | 52% | 49% |

- 📊 Contralateral BC ~0.3%/yr after stopping, independent of age or TN status
- 📊 Locoregional recurrence tracked the same risk factors as distant recurrence
- 🔍 Other prognostic factors after year 5, given TN status
  - Grade and Ki-67: strong in years 0-5, only moderate thereafter
  - PR status: prognostic years 0-5, not thereafter
  - HER2+ without trastuzumab: worse years 0-5, not thereafter
- ⚠️ ~50% diagnosed before 2000 (range 1976 to 2011); no gene-expression profiles available
- ⚠️ Risks are for women SCHEDULED for 5y, not those who completed it; adherence largely unrecorded
- ⚠️ Authors estimate 5 to 10% of distant recurrences missed in trial records

**Sources:**
- 📄 **20-Year Risks of Breast-Cancer Recurrence after Stopping Endocrine Therapy at 5 Years** — Pan, Hongchao; Gray, Richard; Braybrooke, Jeremy et al.. *New England Journal of Medicine* (2017-11). [doi:10.1056/nejmoa1701830](https://doi.org/10.1056/nejmoa1701830)
  - 📎 [[papers/breast/2017-pan-20-year-risks-breast-cancer-recurrence-stopping.pdf|EBCTCG 20-year recurrence after 5 years of endocrine therapy (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions
> - Does a genomic low-risk score identify pts safe to stop at 5 years?
> - Does extended endocrine therapy reduce breast-cancer mortality, not just recurrence?
> - How much do modern-era treatments lower these late risks?

### SAKK 23/82

> 5-yr DFS 61% vs 40% with tamoxifen after excision + RT for good-risk post-mastectomy ILRR; OS unchanged (median 11.5 vs 11.2 yr, P=0.79).

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> For the RT reader, failure after excision plus 5000 cGy at 200 cGy/fx was mostly further local recurrence, and TAM mainly reduced that, not distant spread. Endocrine therapy works here as a local-control partner to chest wall RT. ILRR is also not always a metastatic marker: about 30% stayed relapse-free on local therapy alone.

- 🔍 Phase III randomized, 178 randomized, 167 eligible 'good risk' women with ILRR after mastectomy
- 🔍 Good risk: ER+ recurrence, or ER unknown with DFI >12 mo and <4 nodules ≤3 cm
- 🔍 Local therapy both arms: radical excision, then 5000 cGy at 200 cGy/fx to the involved region
- 💊 Randomized to tamoxifen 20 mg/d until progression vs observation; prior adjuvant TAM excluded
- 🔍 Stratified by menopausal status, adjuvant chemo, axillary nodes at diagnosis. Median f/u 11.6 yr in survivors
- 📊 Outcomes by arm, mature analysis

  | Endpoint | TAM | Observation | P |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Median DFS | 6.5 yr | 2.7 yr | 0.053 log-rank; 0.008 Gehan-Wilcoxon |
  | 5-yr DFS | 61% | 40% | n/a |
  | Median OS | 11.5 yr | 11.2 yr | 0.79 |
  | 5-yr OS | 73% | 79% | n/a |

- 📊 5-yr outcomes by menopausal status

  | Subgroup | TAM | Observation | P |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Premenopausal DFS | 60% | 60% | n/a |
  | Postmenopausal DFS | 61% | 33% | 0.006 |
  | Premenopausal OS | 67% | 90% | 0.175 |
  | Postmenopausal OS | 75% | 75% | n/a |

- 📊 Per authors, further LOCAL recurrence was the only failure type TAM reduced on cumulative incidence analysis
- ⚠️ Log-rank DFS P=0.053; nominal significance rests on the early-weighted Gehan-Wilcoxon test
- ⚠️ Premenopausal subgroup only n=35; the OS signal against TAM is underpowered
- ⚠️ Accrual 1982-1991; most current ILRR pts have already had adjuvant TAM and would not have been eligible

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Adjuvant therapy after excision and radiation of isolated postmastectomy locoregional breast cancer recurrence: definitive results of a phase III randomized trial (SAKK 23/82) comparing tamoxifen with observation** — Waeber, M.; Castiglione-Gertsch, M.; Dietrich, D. et al.. *Annals of Oncology* (2003-08). [doi:10.1093/annonc/mdg347](https://doi.org/10.1093/annonc/mdg347)
  - 📎 [[papers/breast/2003-waeber-adjuvant-therapy-excision-radiation-isolated.pdf|SAKK 23/82 (full text)]]

### Node-negative post-mastectomy LRR and prognostic factors

> 10y LRR 6.0% overall in N0 post-mastectomy pts without PMRT, rising to 40.6% with 3 risk factors (size >2 cm, margin <2 mm, premenopausal, LVI).

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Chest wall was the failure site in 80.4% of total LRRs, which is the basis for the authors' chest wall-only field proposal in high-risk N0. The 3-factor stratum (10y LRR 40.6% ± 13.8%) sits near the node-positive LRR range where PMRT gained survival, but its wide error makes it a threshold argument, not an indication.

- 🔍 Retrospective MGH cohort, 877 node-negative cases (870 pts), mastectomy + ALND, 1980-2000
- 🔍 No pts received PMRT; T4 excluded; median f/u 100 mo
- 🔍 Median age 64; median 15 nodes examined
- 💊 Systemic therapy in 276 cases; none in 601
- 📊 10y cumulative incidence: total LRR 6.0%, isolated LRR 4.3%
- 📊 10y total LRR by number of independent risk factors (p<0.0001)

  | Risk factors | 10y LRR |
  |---|---|
  | 0 | 1.2% ± 0.9% |
  | 1 | 10.0% ± 2.9% |
  | 2 | 17.9% ± 7.5% |
  | 3 | 40.6% ± 13.8% |

- 📊 Univariate 10y total LRR by factor

  | Factor | Adverse | Favorable | p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Deep margin | Close 22%, positive 21% | Negative 5.1% | <0.001 |
  | LVI | Present 19.8% | Other 5.0% | 0.0001 |
  | Tumor size | >2 cm 11% | ≤2 cm 3.1% | 0.001 |
  | Menopausal status | Pre 11.1% | Post 5.1% | 0.01 |
  | Grade | G3 10%, G2 5.3% | G1 0% | 0.03 |

- 📐 MVA (Cox, stepwise): size, margin, LVI, menopausal status independent; HRs 2.6 to 3.8, similar in magnitude
- ⚠️ Grade associated on univariate only; dropped from MVA
- 📊 Sites of failure

  | Site | Isolated LRR | Total LRR |
  |---|---|---|
  | Chest wall | 28 (87.5%) | 37 (80%) |
  | Axilla | 2 (6%) | 3 (7%) |
  | Supraclavicular | 1 (3%) | 5 (11%) |
  | IMC | 1 (3%) | 1 (2%) |

- ⚠️ Systemic therapy subgroups: chemo + hormones 12.6% vs none 6.5% (p=0.04); selection bias per authors
- ⚠️ LVI not recorded pooled with LVI absent; possible misclassification
- ⚠️ Pre-modern systemic therapy era; receptor/HER2 status not reported in source
- 🔗 IBCSG analysis (1,275 N0 women) also found size, LVI, menopausal status predictive; margins not assessable there

### NSABP B-15/B-16/B-18/B-22/B-25 IBTR and LRR prognosis

> After lumpectomy + WBI in node-positive pts, IBTR (HR 2.58) and other LRR (HR 5.85) predicted death; 5-yr OS 59.9% vs 24.1%.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Supraclavicular failure is the RT-relevant read: it made up 81 of 165 oLRRs and carried 5-yr DDFS 12.1% vs 31.5% after axillary recurrence. oLRR reached 14.6% at 10 yrs with ≥10 positive nodes, so nodal burden frames the regional-field question after BCS; field design was not reported in source.

- 🔍 Pooled analysis of 2,669 lumpectomy pts from 5 NSABP node-positive adjuvant trials, median time on study 13.3 yrs
- 🔍 All had lumpectomy, whole-breast irradiation and adjuvant systemic therapy; negative margins required for eligibility
- ⚠️ WBI dose, fractionation and nodal field use not reported in source
- 📊 Outcomes after IBTR vs other LRR (oLRR)

  | Endpoint | IBTR | oLRR |
  |---|---|---|
  | Pts with event | 259 (9.7%) | 165 (6.2%) |
  | 10-yr cumulative incidence | 8.7% | 6.0% |
  | Events within 5 yrs | 62.2% | 80.6% |
  | 5-yr DDFS after event | 51.4% | 18.8% |
  | 5-yr OS after event | 59.9% | 24.1% |
  | Mortality HR (time-varying Cox) | 2.58 (95% CI 2.11-3.15) | 5.85 (95% CI 4.80-7.13) |
  | Adjusted HR, distant disease | 2.72 | 6.68 |

- 📊 oLRR sites and post-recurrence DDFS
  - Sites: 47 axillary, 81 supraclavicular, 13 chest wall, 7 scar only, 17 other/multiple
  - Axillary recurrence: 5-yr DDFS 31.5% (95% CI 19.9-49.7%)
  - Supraclavicular recurrence: 5-yr DDFS 12.1% (95% CI 5.6-26.4%)
  - oLRR within 2 yrs: 3-yr OS only 15.3%
- 📊 Predictors of 10-yr cumulative incidence
  - IBTR by age <50 / 50-59 / ≥60: 11.1%, 6.1%, 5.1% (P<.0001)
  - IBTR by pathologic size >4 cm vs ≤2 cm: 14.3% v 7.5% (P=.045)
  - IBTR lowest with ER >100 fmol or unknown: 4.5% (ER P=.0007)
  - IBTR not associated with nodal count (P=.53)
  - oLRR by 1-3 / 4-9 / ≥10 nodes: 4.4%, 7.2%, 14.6% (P<.0001)
  - oLRR associated with ER-neg (P=.011) and PgR-neg (P=.0073)
- 📊 10-yr cumulative IBTR and oLRR by protocol

  | Protocol | Pts at risk | 10-yr IBTR % | 10-yr oLRR % |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | B-15 | 624 | 10.0 | 7.6 |
  | B-16 | 340 | 4.8 | 5.4 |
  | B-18 | 210 | 9.1 | 8.7 |
  | B-22 | 596 | 10.1 | 6.1 |
  | B-25 | 899 | 8.3 | 4.5 |
  | Total | 2,669 | 8.7 | 6.0 |

- 📐 No between-protocol IBTR difference after age adjustment (P=.50)
- 📊 Later IBTR: >40% reduction in 3-yr mortality risk vs earlier IBTR, across cutoffs
- ⚠️ Prognostic association only: cannot show that preventing LRR would avert the excess deaths
- ⚠️ 1984-1994 regimens; premenopausal pts got no tamoxifen regardless of ER status

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Prognosis After Ipsilateral Breast Tumor Recurrence and Locoregional Recurrences in Five National Surgical Adjuvant Breast and Bowel Project Node-Positive Adjuvant Breast Cancer Trials** — Wapnir, Irene L.; Anderson, Stewart J.; Mamounas, Eleftherios P. et al.. *Journal of Clinical Oncology* (2006-05). [doi:10.1200/jco.2005.04.3273](https://doi.org/10.1200/jco.2005.04.3273)
  > Purpose Locoregional failure after breast-conserving surgery is associated with increased risk of distant disease and death. The magnitude of this risk in patients receiving chemotherapy has not been adequately characterized. Patients and Methods Our study population included 2,669 women randomly assigned onto five National Surgical Adjuvant Breast and Bowel Project node-positive protocols (B-15, B-16, B-18, B-22, and B-25), who were treated with lumpectomy, whole-breast irradiation, and adjuvant systemic therapy. Cumulative incidences of ipsilateral breast tumor recurrence (IBTR) and other locoregional recurrence (oLRR) were calculated. Kaplan-Meier curves were used to estimate distant–disease-free survival (DDFS) and overall survival (OS) after IBTR or oLRR. Cox models were used to model survival using clinical and pathologic factors jointly with IBTR or oLRR as time-varying predictors. Results Four hundred twenty-four patients (15.9%) experienced locoregional failure; 259 (9.7%) experienced IBTR, and 165 (6.2%) experienced oLRR. The 10-year cumulative incidence of IBTR and oLRR was 8.7% and 6.0%, respectively. Most locoregional failures occurred within 5 years (62.2% for IBTR and 80.6% for oLRR). Age, tumor size, and estrogen receptor status were significantly associated with IBTR. Nodal status and estrogen and progesterone receptor status were significantly associated with oLRR. The 5-year DDFS rates after IBTR and oLRR were 51.4% and 18.8%, respectively. The 5-year OS rates after IBTR and oLRR were 59.9% and 24.1%, respectively. Hazard ratios for mortality associated with IBTR and oLRR were 2.58 (95% CI, 2.11 to 3.15) and 5.85 (95% CI, 4.80 to 7.13), respectively. Conclusion Node-positive breast cancer patients who developed IBTR or oLRR had significantly poorer prognoses than patients who did not experience these events.

> - Does chemotherapy at isolated locoregional recurrence improve survival?

### T1-2N1 mastectomy without PMRT (MSKCC)

> 10y LRR 7.0% without PMRT vs 4.0% with it (p=0.32) in T1-2N1 after mastectomy; age <40 + LVI reached 28% without PMRT.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The RT read is the no-PMRT stratification: 10y LRR 28% for age <40 with LVI vs 2% for ≥40 without LVI. Supraclavicular nodes carried 27% of LRRs and the axilla only 15.9%, so where PMRT is chosen for the high-risk stratum, the regional field targets where these pts actually failed.

- 🔍 Single-institution retrospective, prospectively maintained MSKCC database, mastectomy 1995-2006
- 🔍 N=1087 T1-2N1: 924 (85%) no PMRT, 163 (15%) PMRT at physician discretion
- 🔍 Excluded T3/T4, neoadjuvant chemo, isolated tumor cells only
- 🔍 Median f/u 10.8 yr (range 0-21)
- 📊 10-yr outcomes, PMRT vs no PMRT (unadjusted, log-rank)

  | Endpoint | PMRT (n=163) | No PMRT (n=924) | p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | LRR | 4.0% | 7.0% | 0.32 |
  | RFS | 75% (95% CI 69-82) | 73% (95% CI 69-76) | 0.46 |
  | OS | 81% (95% CI 73-86) | 80% (95% CI 77-82) | 0.44 |

- 📊 LRR without PMRT by age and LVI (Fig 2)

  | Subgroup | 5-yr LRR | 10-yr LRR |
  |---|---|---|
  | <40, LVI | 15% | 28% |
  | <40, no LVI | 6% | 11% |
  | ≥40, LVI | 6% | 8% |
  | ≥40, no LVI | 1% | 2% |

- 📐 MVA for LRR in no-PMRT group
  - Age <40: HR 4.15 (95% CI 2.42-7.11), p<0.0001
  - LVI: HR 3.47 (95% CI 1.92-6.29), p<0.0001
  - Tumor size lost significance for LRR on MVA
- 📊 Pattern of failure, 63 LRRs total (7 PMRT, 56 no PMRT)
  - Chest wall 32/63 (51%)
  - Supraclavicular 17/63 (27%)
  - Axilla 5 (8%), IMN 3 (5%)
  - Axilla involved in only 10/63 (15.9%)
- 🔍 PMRT: chest wall in all; SCV 137/163 (84%), axilla 7 (4%), IMN 6 (4%). Dose/fractionation not reported
- 🔍 ALND in 94.2%, median 18 nodes resected; chemo 88%, anthracycline and/or taxane 76%
- ⚠️ PMRT group enriched for younger age, larger T, LVI, ECE, more +LNs: selection makes the arm comparison uninterpretable
- ⚠️ HER2-amplified: only 51/123 (41%) received trastuzumab
- ⚠️ Fig 1 prints PMRT 10y LRR as 4.2% while text states 4.0%
- 🔗 vs EBCTCG 1-3 node subset: LRR 20.3% → 3.8% at 10 yr with PMRT, in 1965-1986-era trials
- 🔗 vs EORTC 22922: mastectomy-subgroup death HR 0.91, authors question whether regional RT benefit holds post-mastectomy
