# 2026-11-03 — ESMO Congress 2026

**DASL-HiCaP: darolutamide added to 96-wk ADT plus prostate/bed and pelvic nodal RT, 4-yr MFS-CI 95% vs 88%, HR 0.42 (0.27-0.66) at interim in very high-risk disease.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> DASL-HiCaP adds a second ARSI-intensification signal on an RT backbone: 4y MFS-CI 95% vs 88%, HR 0.42 (0.27-0.66), though at interim with OS (HR 0.88) and PCSS immature.

## 🌰 Prostate

ARSI intensification on top of ADT plus pelvic nodal RT, definitive or salvage, in very high-risk disease.

### DASL-HiCaP · [[NCT04136353]]

> 4-yr MFS-CI 95% vs 88%, HR 0.42 (0.27-0.66), adding darolutamide to 96-wk ADT plus prostate/bed and pelvic nodal RT in very high-risk disease.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Every pt received pelvic nodal RT with 96 wk of LHRH analog, so darolutamide's MFS-CI HR 0.42 is measured on top of elective nodal coverage, not instead of it. 29% were node-positive on CT and the benefit held across primary vs salvage RT and N+ vs N0 (subgroup HRs not in source). The decision it moves is ARSI intensification alongside whole-pelvis RT, not RT field or dose.

- 🔍 Phase III, placebo-controlled, randomised 1:1, N=1,106, international (ANZUP1801)
- 💊 Darolutamide 600 mg BID vs placebo for 96 wk, both arms on LHRH analog for 96 wk
- 🔍 RT in both arms: prostate or prostate bed plus pelvic nodes, definitive or salvage
- 📊 Efficacy at interim, events (4-yr event-free)

  | Endpoint | Darolutamide N=553 | Placebo N=553 | HR (95% CI) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | MFS-CI | 27 (95%) | 63 (88%) | 0.42 (0.27-0.66), p=0.0001 |
  | PSA-PFS | 46 (91%) | 95 (83%) | 0.47 (0.33-0.67) |
  | PCSS | 4 (99%) | 7 (99%) | 0.58 (0.17-1.97) |
  | OS | 21 (97%) | 24 (95%) | 0.88 (0.49-1.59) |

- ⚠️ Grade ≥2 AE clusters of special interest and any grade ≥3 AE

  | AE | Darolutamide | Placebo |
  |---|---|---|
  | Fatigue/asthenia G≥2 | 14% | 10% |
  | Hypertension G≥2 | 7% | 9% |
  | Fractures G≥2 | 2% | 3% |
  | Cardiac G≥2 | 3% | 3% |
  | Any AE G≥3 | 43% | 36% |

- 📐 Single prespecified interim after 87 MFS-CI events; disclosure threshold 2-sided stratified log-rank p<0.012
- 📊 MFS benefit consistent across primary vs salvage RT and pelvic N+ vs N0 (OncLive); subgroup HRs not reported in source
- ⚠️ MFS-CI is a surrogate per ICECaP; PCSS and OS differences not established at interim
- ⚠️ Metastases defined on CT and/or Tc bone scan, not PSMA PET
- ⚠️ Prior RP in only 15%: salvage-setting estimate rests on a small subset
- 🔗 Same ARSI-intensification-with-RT question as ENZARAD (enzalutamide) and ATLAS (apalutamide); STAMPEDE abiraterone data frame the current high-risk bar

[article](https://cslide.ctimeetingtech.com/esmo2026/attendee/confcal/presentation/list)
  > ESMO Congress 2026 abstract LBA4 · Presidential Symposium I · 2026-10-24 · presented by Tamim Niazi (Montreal, Canada, QC) · [[NCT04136353]]
  > Background: We evaluated the addition of darolutamide to standard of care ADT and RT for very high-risk localised or biochemically persistent/recurrent prostate cancer within 1 year after radical prostatectomy (RP).
  > Methods: Participants (pts) were prescribed an LHRH analog for 96 weeks plus RT to prostate/prostate bed and pelvic nodes and randomised 1:1 to darolutamide 600 mg vs placebo, twice daily for 96 weeks. The primary endpoint was metastasis-free survival based on conventional imaging with CT and/or technetium bone scan (MFS-CI, as per ICECaP surrogacy for OS). PSA-progression free survival (PFS), prostate cancer specific survival (PCSS), and overall survival (OS) were secondary endpoints. The protocol specified a single interim analysis after 87 MFS-CI events, with IDSMC review for external disclosure if the 2-sided, stratified log-rank p-value for MFS-CI was <0.012.
  > Results: We randomised 1,106 pts from 30APR20 to 09AUG23 with median age 71 (IQR 65-75), pelvic nodal involvement on CT in 29%, Gleason score 9-10 in 73%, and prior RP in 15%. Median follow-up was 3.8 years (IQR 3.3 to 4.5). MFS-CI was longer amongst those assigned darolutamide vs placebo: 4-year MFS-CI 95% vs 88%, hazard ratio 0.42 (95% CI 0.27 to 0.66), 27 vs 63 events, p=0.0001. PSA-PFS, PCSS, and OS are shown below. The proportions of pts experiencing grade ≥2 of the following AE clusters of special interest (darolutamide vs placebo) were: fatigue/asthenia (14% vs 10%), hypertension (7% vs 9%), fractures (2% vs 3%), and cardiac (3% vs 3%). Any AE grade ≥3 was experienced by 43% vs 36% of pts, respectively.
  > Conclusions: Darolutamide significantly improved MFS-CI when added to ADT and loco-regional RT for very high-risk localised, or biochemically persistent/recurrent prostate cancer. Follow-up is ongoing to confirm the surrogacy of MFS-CI for OS.
