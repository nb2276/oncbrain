# 2026-10-16

**AUA 2026 MIBC debate: cCR after neoadjuvant EV+pembro equals true pCR in only 51% (95% CI 42–60); RT preservation, RC and surveillance remain untested against each other.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Bladder was the only site today. An AUA session treated management after a cCR to EV+pembro as unsettled. cCR equals true pCR in only 51%, and 67% of ctDNA-negative pts still had ≥pT2 at RC. Both phase III trials required cystectomy, so RT-based preservation (BRIGHT: 55 Gy/20 fx → pembro) has no randomized test against RC.

## 💧 Bladder

After a response to EV+pembro, the next step is an open RT question: bladder-preserving RT, RC or surveillance, with no head-to-head data.

### AUA 2026: Consolidative local therapy after EV+pembro response (surgery vs radiation vs surveillance)

> RT-based preservation, cystectomy and surveillance each argued for cCR after neoadjuvant EV+pembro in MIBC; no randomized data compare the three options.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> For RT, the key observation is the failure pattern: in RETAIN-2, no pt developed mets without a prior local recurrence, which is the premise behind both salvage RT and surveillance. BRIGHT (55 Gy/20 fx whole bladder, then pembro) is the trial that will test whether RT consolidation after cT0–T1 response adds bladder control beyond surveillance. No EV-era RT data exist yet.

> [!abstract] Presentation
> This point-counterpoint session asked how to manage the bladder in MIBC after cCR on neoadjuvant EV+pembro. The RT case held that clinical restaging misses residual disease, and that RT-based preservation keeps the bladder with better sexual function and body image than cystectomy. The surgical case held that all level 1 EV+pembro data include cystectomy, and that cCR predicts pCR only 51% of the time. The surveillance case shifted the question from pCR to MFS. It rested on a 97% PPV of cCR for 2-year MFS and on local recurrence coming before metastasis, which would allow salvage. ctDNA was presented as promising but not yet able to select pts.

> [!warning] Adversarial read
> - No data were shown on RT consolidation after EV+pembro; the RT case rests on chemo-era cohorts and observational TMT vs RC QOL comparisons, where pts chose their modality.
> - The 97% PPV comes from GC + nivo in HCRN GU16-257; applying it to EV+pembro assumes a cCR means the same thing under a different regimen.
> - The 51% cCR-pCR concordance measures residual pathology, not whether that residual changes MFS once surveillance and salvage are in place, so the surgical case treats pCR as if it were an outcome.
> - RETAIN-1 vs RETAIN-2 is a cross-trial comparison in small surveillance cohorts (N=22 in RETAIN-2) after AMVAC-based therapy, not EV+pembro, and no trial compares RT, cystectomy and surveillance head to head.

**Trials cited:** KEYNOTE-905/EV-303 · KEYNOTE-B15/EV-304 · SWOG 0219 · RETAIN-1 · RETAIN-2 · HCRN GU16-257 · ARCHER · BRIGHT

- 🔍 The three positions argued in the session

  | Option | Core argument | Evidence cited |
  |---|---|---|
  | Bladder-preserving RT | cCR does not guarantee eradication; RT gives durable bladder control with a QOL advantage | SWOG 0219, RETAIN-2, TMT vs RC patient-reported outcomes |
  | Radical cystectomy | Cystectomy is the backbone of all level 1 EV+pembro data; cCR is not pCR | KEYNOTE-905/EV-303, KEYNOTE-B15/EV-304, Roessler meta-analysis, RETAIN ctDNA |
  | Surveillance | Most pts had pCR at cystectomy anyway; the real question is whether cCR predicts MFS | HCRN GU16-257, RETAIN failure patterns, ctDNA (Epstein, ASCO GU 2026) |

- 📊 EV-303 (cis-ineligible/declining): EFS HR 0.40 (95% CI 0.28–0.57), OS HR 0.50 (95% CI 0.33–0.74) vs surgery alone
- 📊 EV-304 (cis-eligible): pCR 55.8% vs 32.5% with gem/cis; interim EFS HR 0.53, OS HR 0.65
- ⚠️ In both phase III trials every pt was meant to have cystectomy, so EV+pembro alone has never been tested against EV+pembro + RC
- 📊 cCR vs pCR: pooled true pCR among cCR was 51% (95% CI 42–60), across 10 studies and 419 pts
- 📊 SWOG 0219: 34 of 74 (46%) were cT0 after re-TURBT; 6 of 10 who went on to immediate RC (60%) had residual disease
- 📊 Active-surveillance outcomes, RETAIN-1 (AMVAC) vs RETAIN-2 (AMVAC + nivo)

  | Outcome | RETAIN-1 | RETAIN-2 |
  |---|---|---|
  | Local recurrence | 62% | 36% |
  | Metastatic recurrence | 36% | 14% |
  | MFS with intact non-irradiated bladder | 48% | 68% |

- 🔗 HCRN GU16-257 (GC + nivo): cCR PPV 97% for 2-yr MFS, sensitivity 0.89, specificity 0.97, NPV 0.89
- 📊 Pattern of failure: in RETAIN-2 no pt developed mets without a prior local recurrence; in RETAIN-1, local recurrence came first in 8 of 9
- ⚠️ ctDNA-negative status did not rule out residual disease: 67% of ctDNA-negative pts still had ≥pT2 at RC
- 🔍 BRIGHT schema: cT0–T1 response after ≥3 cycles NAT → RT 55 Gy/20 fx whole bladder → pembro 200 mg q21 days × 12 mo
- ⚠️ Trial IDs do not match: the write-up lists ARCHER as Alliance A032103 and BRIGHT as S2406; the slide shows NRG-GU015 and S2427
