# 2026-08-10 — ASCO Genitourinary Cancers Symposium 2022

**SFRO RecoRad 2025 OAR constraint update: normo, moderate hypofx and ablative tables across 6 sites, now with LAD and left ventricle as named cardiac substructures.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> A planning day, not a results day. The SFRO OAR update is the practical item: fractionation-stratified constraint tables anchored to START B and FAST-FORWARD for breast and the ASTRO guideline for liver, with reirradiation handled by principle and buried in the appendix. HERO's prostate signal is testosterone recovery kinetics (53.9% vs 3.2% at 90d), not an oncologic endpoint.

## ⚠️ Safety / Regulatory

Constraint framework, not evidence grading: most numbers derive from 3D conformal retrospective data, so IMRT/SBRT extrapolation carries the authors' own caveat.

### Organs at Risk Radiation Dose Constraints: 2025 Update

> SFRO 2025 OAR dose-constraint update: normofractionated, moderate hypofractionated and ablative tables across skull base, H&N, lung, breast, oesophagus, liver.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The practical shift is coverage of ablative regimens per fraction number (1, 3, 4, 5, 8) alongside normofractionation, plus cardiac substructures (LAD, left ventricle) and a proposed dorsal vagal complex OAR. Numbers are tiered optimal vs mandatory, so a plan that exceeds an optimal value is a documented conversation, not a violation.

- 🔍 SFRO (Société française de radiothérapie oncologique) consensus, RecoRad 4th edition; adult pts only
- 🔍 Constraints compiled from international consensus guidelines + trial data, validated by national reference experts
- 📐 Tables are organized by tumour location: skull/skull base, head and neck, lung, breast, oesophagus, liver
- 📐 Each site table splits by fractionation regimen: normofractionation, moderate hypofractionation, ablative (1/3/4/5/8 fractions)
- 📐 Constraint conventions used throughout
  - Normofractionation: Dmax approximated by D2 %
  - Stereotactic: D0.035 cm3 preferred as Dmax surrogate
  - Several hypofractionated entries expressed as EQD2
  - Constraints tiered as optimal vs mandatory, or ALARA where no number is set
- 💊 Breast tables are anchored to named trial schedules: START B (40 Gy/15) and FAST-FORWARD (26 Gy/5)
- 💊 Liver tables are anchored to the ASTRO clinical practice guideline across 3-, 5- and ≥ 20-fraction regimens
- 🔗 New structures added vs prior editions: cardiac substructures (LAD, left ventricle), correlated with late toxicity
- 🔗 Dorsal vagal complex proposed as a candidate brain OAR for radiation-induced nausea and vomiting
- ⚠️ Authors state most constraints derive from 3D conformal data, so extrapolation to IMRT/SBRT needs caution
- ⚠️ Most underlying data are retrospective and from heterogeneous populations; toxicity scoring (RTOG/EORTC/CTCAE) is subjective
- ⚠️ Authors explicitly do not validate the clinical significance of each constraint; this is a planning framework, not evidence grading
- ⚠️ Reirradiation is handled by principle, not table: ALARA for serial structures (cord, brainstem, chiasm), radiobiological modelling elsewhere
- ⚠️ Appendix (Tables S1–S12 EQD2/BED at α/β 2 and 3 Gy; S13–S16 reirradiation doses) is where the reirradiation numbers actually live

## 🌰 Prostate

Relevant to ADT choice around RT, where recovery timing gates re-treatment and salvage planning, though this is a non-randomised 184-pt subset.

### HERO

> 90-day testosterone recovery to ≥280 ng/dL 53.9% with relugolix vs 3.2% with leuprolide after 48wk ADT.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> For intermediate-risk prostate cancer treated with RT plus short-course ADT, the recovery kinetic is the deliverable: median 86.0 days to normal T on relugolix vs 2 of 47 recovering on leuprolide by day 90. That gates the choice of agent when ADT is a fixed 4 to 6 month adjunct to RT and the goal is off-treatment hypogonadism time, not depth of suppression.

- 📊 90-day cumulative incidence of T recovery ≥280 ng/dL: 53.9% relugolix vs 3.2% leuprolide, p=0.0017
- 🔍 Subset analysis of 184/934 HERO pts who completed 48wk of treatment and had no planned alternative ADT within 12wk (24wk after last leuprolide depot)
- 🔍 Parent trial randomized 2:1, relugolix 120mg PO daily after 360mg load vs leuprolide q12wk x 48wk
- 📊 Recovery detail by arm (verbatim from source)

  | Metric | Relugolix | Leuprolide |
  |---|---|---|
  | n in recovery subset | 137 | 47 |
  | Baseline T entering recovery (mean±SD) | 427±142 ng/dL | 404±127 ng/dL |
  | Recovered T | 74 | 2 |
  | Median time to recovery | 86.0 d (95% CI 65.0, 92.0) | 112.0 d (95% CI 112.0, NE) |
  | Median PSA at day 90 | 0.39 ng/mL (0 to 233.1) | 0.06 ng/mL (0 to 14.0) |

- 📐 Median recovery time in the leuprolide arm rests on only 2 events, upper CI bound not estimable
- 🔗 Parent HERO endpoint was sustained castration to wk48: 96.7% vs 88.8%, difference 7.9% (95% CI 4.1 to 11.8), P<0.001
- ⚠️ Non-randomised subset, and entry required no planned ongoing ADT, so selection favors pts whose clinicians already judged recovery likely
- ⚠️ Arms unbalanced 137 vs 47 and leuprolide pharmacology means depot coverage extends past the 90-day window; the comparison is partly a timing artifact
- ⚠️ Higher median PSA on relugolix at day 90 is the flip side of faster recovery, not reported with an oncologic endpoint
