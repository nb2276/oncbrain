# 2026-11-13

**RSS SRS guideline: up to 15 lesions, ≤3 cm and ≤14 cc, are SRS-suitable. Fractionate 2-4 cm lesions (27 Gy/3 fx or 30 Gy/5 fx); above 4 cm, surgery first.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> CNS carried the day: RSS and ISRS SRS guidelines both require planning MRI ≤7 days pre-SRS, each resting on largely observational or single-institution evidence. ASTRO think tank: germline DNA-repair variants should not alter indicated RT, except young deleterious ATM carriers. Breast: consecutive-day 30 Gy/5 fx VMAT PBI (N=50) shows G1-only toxicity at median 4.5 yr.

## 📊 Cross-cutting

### Implications of Genetic Testing on Radiation Therapy Decisions

> ASTRO think tank consensus: heterozygous germline DNA-repair variants (BRCA1/2, PALB2, CHEK2, RAD50) and VUS should not alter indicated RT; young deleterious ATM carriers are the exception.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The ATM carve-out is smaller than it sounds: an RR of 1.3 moves 5-yr contralateral breast cancer risk from 2.9% to an estimated 3.8%, and the panel calls toxicity data evenly split. Indicated RT in young ATM carriers still goes ahead with close CBC screening. Optional RT, such as omission-eligible adjuvant breast RT, is the decision this sharpens.

- 🔍 ASTRO think tank, Feb 2019: genetic screening, genomics, radiosensitivity, risk-estimate and clinical oncology experts
- 🔍 Consensus position by germline finding

  | Germline finding | RT recommendation | Basis in source |
  |---|---|---|
  | VUS | Treat as normal | Not actionable; RT risks similar to normal |
  | BRCA1/2 | No change to indicated RT | Consistent data: no excess toxicity or RT-induced second cancers |
  | PALB2, CHEK2, RAD50 | No change to indicated RT | Extended by analogy from BRCA |
  | Deleterious heterozygous ATM | Avoid optional RT in young pts; close CBC screening after indicated RT | Possible RT-attributable CBC excess; toxicity data mixed |
  | RB1, NF1, TP53, PTCH1 syndromes | Elevated in-field second malignancy risk | No known excess acute or late toxicity |

- 📊 VUS can be found in >50% of multigene panel results
- 📊 ATM carrier CBC risk, worked absolute-risk example (Box 3)
  - Adjusted RR 1.3 for contralateral breast cancer (Women's Environment, Cancer, and Radiation Epidemiology study)
  - 5-yr second breast cancer risk 2.9% (SEER) → estimated 3.8% in ATM carriers
  - Excess absolute risk 0.9% at 5 yrs
- 📊 647,672 adult 5-yr survivors: 9% second solid malignancy, only 8% attributed to RT
- 📊 RT-attributable excess absolute risk of second solid malignancy 0.5% by 15 yrs
- 📊 Hereditary retinoblastoma: 30-yr second cancer incidence 35% with EBRT vs 6% without RT
- 📊 ATM SNP rs1801516 in ~24% of 5456 breast/prostate pts: OR 1.5 acute, 1.2 late toxicity; not actionable
- 📊 Paired tumor-normal sequencing: 17% pathogenic germline variants in 1040 advanced cancers; 101 of 182 missed by standard screening
- 📊 Somatic ATM truncation tracked with better local control after palliative RT vs VUS/wildtype (P < .001); OS no different
- ⚠️ Consensus reached by expert discussion; no voting, agreement rates or evidence grading reported
- ⚠️ Panel concedes heterozygous ATM toxicity data are evenly split for and against excess risk
- ⚠️ PALB2/CHEK2/RAD50 reassurance rests on BRCA analogy, not direct RT outcome data

[doi:10.1016/j.ijrobp.2019.07.026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijrobp.2019.07.026)

## 🧠 CNS

Two SRS guidelines. The ISRS panel reached strong consensus on only 5 of 10 survey questions, and RSS states no panel process.

### ISRS Technical Guidelines: SRS for Small Brain Metastases (≤1 cm)

> Sub-mm geometric accuracy, ≤1 mm E2E tolerance, 5% dosimetric accuracy and MRI ≤7 days pre-SRS anchor ISRS physics consensus for ≤1 cm brain mets.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The margin is the lever on these targets: a 1-mm expansion takes a 0.8-cm met from 0.268 to 0.524 cc, and the one randomized comparison (1 vs 3 mm, 80 mets) showed no LC difference with more radionecrosis at 3 mm. Planning-MRI age also gates control: 12-mo local FFP 95% vs 56% at <14 vs ≥14 days.

- 🔍 PRISMA systematic review of [[PMID 2009]]-2020: 484 abstracts → 37 articles, plus 44 from references
- ⚠️ Evidence base thin: 57 of 81 papers (70%) single-institution
- 🔍 14-member ISRS committee survey, 10 questions: strong consensus (≥80%) on only 5, none on 3
- 🧭 ISRS summary recommendations by domain (Table 2)

  | Domain | Recommendation |
  |---|---|
  | Imaging | MRI ≤7 days from treatment is a prerequisite for contouring |
  | Imaging | Fine-cut MR, ≤1.5-mm slices; planning CT no thicker than MR slices |
  | Imaging | 10 to 15 min scan delay after contrast injection |
  | Imaging | SRS-optimized sequences; regular MRI QA for geometric distortion |
  | Contouring | Contour all targets and OARs; weigh margins per SRS platform |
  | Patient treatment | Sub-mm geometric accuracy; immobilization and IGRT chosen to achieve it |
  | Patient treatment | Lower energy beams may reduce normal tissue dose |
  | Dosimetry | Dosimetric accuracy within 5%; small-field dosimetry per IAEA and AAPM |

- 🔗 Margin evidence cited by the panel

  | Study | Comparison | Finding |
  |---|---|---|
  | Randomized, 49 pts / 80 mets, LINAC | 1 vs 3 mm PTV margin | No LC difference; more radionecrosis with 3 mm |
  | Retrospective, 93 mets, LINAC | With vs without 2-mm PTV margin | Severe parenchymal complications 7.1% and 19.6%; no LC effect |
  | Sneed, retrospective GK, 2200 treatments | ≤1 cm lesions, ≤20 Gy, no margin | 1-yr adverse radiation effect 1% or less |
  | Ma et al, GK modeling | Margins 0.5 to 3 mm | Necrosis risk up 6% to 25%; only 1 of 15 lesions <1 cm |

- 📐 1-mm margin on a 0.8-cm sphere: 0.268 → 0.524 cc, effectively doubling target volume
- 📊 Planning MRI <14 vs ≥14 days before SRS: 12-mo local FFP 95% vs 56% (retrospective, 151 mets)
- 📐 Median 20% tumor volume growth over a median 8-day interscan interval (531 lesions)
- 📊 Dose for 1 cm lesions in practice ranges 18 to 25 Gy; guideline sets no prescription
- 🔍 Platform and delivery parameters
  - MLC ≤5 mm width a prerequisite; 2.5 vs 5 mm superiority not settled
  - 6 MV FFF: 50% isodose volume 11% lower than 10 MV FFF (93 lesions, 35 pts)
  - Single-isocenter VMAT: uncertainty within 1 mm ≤4 cm from isocenter, 2 mm beyond
  - Dedicated SRS mask: median 0.38 mm displacement (max 1 mm) vs 0.74 mm (max 2 mm) conventional mask
  - Without real-time monitoring, imaging-to-beam-on delay preferably under 5 min
- ⚠️ Multiple authors disclose Elekta, BrainLAB, Accuray or Varian ties; no specific funding

[doi:10.1016/j.prro.2022.10.013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.prro.2022.10.013)

### Case-Based Radiosurgery Society Practice Guideline: SRS for Brain Metastases

> Up to 15 lesions, ≤3 cm and ≤14 cc are SRS-suitable. Fractionate 2-4 cm lesions (27 Gy/3 fx or 30 Gy/5 fx). Above 4 cm, surgery comes first.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The fractionation advice is driven by toxicity more than control. In 4-14 cc lesions, radionecrosis was 23.1% single-fraction vs 7.3% multifraction (P = .003). Above 14 cc and 3 cm, neither LC nor necrosis differed significantly, which is the guide's case for moving to surgery. Margin is the other lever: going from 0 to 2 mm triples V12.

- 🔍 Table 1 · SRS suitability grid

  | Category | Lesion number | Metastasis size | Specific scenarios |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Suitable | 1-15 | Diameter ≤3 cm, volume ≤14 cc | n/a |
  | Cautionary | >15 | Diameter 3-6 cm, volume >14 cc (operative management preferred) | SCLC, nodular leptomeningeal disease |
  | Unsuitable | n/a | >6 cm | Classical leptomeningeal disease |

- 📐 Fractionation by lesion size (intact mets)

  | Lesion size | Recommended approach |
  |---|---|
  | <2 cm | 20-24 Gy in 1 fx (ASTRO) |
  | 2-4 cm | 27 Gy in 3 fx or 30 Gy in 5 fx; staged SRS an alternative |
  | >4 cm | Surgery; multifraction over single-fraction SRS if inoperable |

- 🔗 Cited meta-analysis (24 studies, 1887 lesions): single- vs multifraction SRS for large mets

  | Group | 1-yr LC SF vs MF | Radionecrosis SF vs MF |
  |---|---|---|
  | A: 4-14 cc | 77.6% vs 92.9% (p not legible in source) | 23.1% vs 7.3%, P = .003 |
  | B: >14 cc, >3 cm | 77.1% vs 79.2%, P = .76 | 11.7% vs 6.5%, P = .29 |

- 📊 Single-fraction V12 of 5, 10, >15 cc → symptomatic RN ~10%, 15%, 20% (HyTEC)
- 🔍 PTV margin 1-2 mm if one is used, 1 mm whenever possible
- 📐 Margin cost: V12 doubles going 0 → 1 mm and triples going 0 → 2 mm (48 plans)
- 🔍 Postop cavity SRS targeting
  - CTV: whole cavity on contrast T1, edema excluded
  - Up to 5 mm along meningeal margin / bone flap
  - Up to 10 mm along bone flap if tumor touched dura preop
  - 1-5 mm along an abutting venous sinus
  - PTV typically 2 mm, 1 mm in select cases
  - SRS within 4 wk of surgery, planning MRI ≤7 days prior
- 📐 Table 2 · Common SRS dose constraints

  | Organ | 1 fx | 3 fx | 5 fx |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Brain | V12 <5-10 cc; V10 <12 cc | V18 <26 cc; V21 <21 cc; V24 <16.8 cc | V25 <16 cc; V28.8 <7 cc; V30 <10.5-30 cc |
  | Brain stem (not medulla) | Dmax <15 Gy; V10 <0.5 cc | Dmax <23.1 Gy; V15.9 <0.5 cc | Dmax <31 Gy; V23 <0.5 cc |
  | Spinal cord and medulla | Dmax <12.4-14 Gy; V10 <0.35 cc | Dmax <20.3-22.5 Gy; V15.9 <0.35 cc | Dmax <25.3-28 Gy; V22 <0.35 cc |
  | Cochlea | Dmax <9 Gy | Dmax <14.4 Gy | Dmax <22 Gy |
  | Optic pathway | Dmax <10 Gy; V8 <0.2 cc | Dmax <17.4-20 Gy; V15.3 <0.2 cc | Dmax <25 Gy; V23 <0.2 cc |

- 💊 Concurrent systemic therapy + SRS: no routine washout per most cited studies
- ⚠️ Exceptions flagged: BRAF inhibitors (intratumoral bleeding), erlotinib with WBRT + SRS (RN)
- 🔗 Preop SRS: meningeal disease 5.8% vs 16% to 21% postop, from initial studies only
- ⚠️ 15-lesion ceiling rests on an observational study and an early-terminated, abstract-only trial
- ⚠️ No panel process, evidence grading or agreement metric stated in source

[doi:10.1016/j.adro.2023.101402](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adro.2023.101402)

## 🎀 Breast

A DCIS natural-history review, plus a consecutive-day VMAT PBI update that reports toxicity but no local control.

### Natural History of DCIS of the Breast

> 14–53% of DCIS misdiagnosed as benign and left after biopsy alone progressed to invasive cancer over 10+ yr; precise progression rates not estimable.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The RT-relevant read is the recurrence gap: pooled recurrence 22.5% after CS alone vs 8.9% with CS+RT, widest for comedo type (34.4% vs 8.3%), with about half of recurrences invasive. Low-grade DCIS recurs with longer latency, so short follow-up flatters RT omission in exactly the lesions where omission is proposed.

- 📊 DCIS misdiagnosed as benign, biopsy only: 14–53% progressed to invasive cancer over 10+ yr
- 🔍 Misdiagnosed-DCIS follow-up series (from review text)
  - Page: 28 misdiagnosed of 11,760; 7 invasive by 15 yr, 2 more at 23 and 31 yr
  - Eusebi: 80 misdiagnosed; 11 invasive after mean 17.5 yr f/u
  - Nurses' Health study: 6 of 13 invasive, 2 each low, intermediate, high grade
  - Betsill: low grade micropapillary; 7 of 10 with f/u developed cancer, mean 9.7 yr
  - Rosen: micropapillary; 8 of 15 with f/u invasive in same breast, mean 9 yr f/u
- 📊 Boyages meta-analysis: DCIS recurrence by treatment and tumour feature

  | Group | CS alone | CS+RT | Mastectomy |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Overall | 22.5% (95% CI 16.9–28.2) | 8.9% (95% CI 6.8–11.0) | 1.4% (95% CI 0.7–2.1) |
  | Comedo type | 34.4% | 8.3% | NR |
  | Necrosis present | 24.7% | 8.2% | NR |
  | High nuclear grade | 23% | 9.5% | NR |
  | Intermediate nuclear grade | 22.7% | 7.5% | NR |

- 📊 Long-term BCS series: up to 40% ipsilateral recurrence, about half as invasive cancer
- 📊 Autopsy: median undiagnosed DCIS prevalence 8.9% (range 0–14.7%) vs invasive 1.3%
- 🔍 Kopans model: DCIS reservoir up to 20 times detected count expected even if all DCIS progresses
- ⚠️ Misdiagnosed series mostly low grade; progression for high grade likely underestimated
- ⚠️ Incomplete f/u (Rosen: 15 of 30) may enrich for women later diagnosed with cancer
- ⚠️ Autopsy prevalence likely inflated by ADH/DCIS misclassification and non-representative decedents

### APBI-IMRT-Florence Technique Update (VMAT)

> 30 Gy/5 consecutive-fraction VMAT PBI, N=50: G1-only toxicity (acute 32%, late 16%) at median 4.5 yr f/u, no G>1 events.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The PTV-to-ipsilateral-breast volume ratio predicts ipsilateral breast V15Gy (R2 .911), so a planner can tell before optimizing whether a pt will meet the V15Gy ≤50% constraint. Consecutive daily fractions gave numerically more G1 toxicity than the trial's alternate-day schedule (acute 32% vs 21.1%, late 16% vs 4.5%), none above G1.

- 🔍 Single-center consecutive series, N=50 (39 left, 11 right), free breathing
- 🔍 Dosimetric planning guide plus RTOG/EORTC toxicity; parent trial [[NCT02104895]]
- 💊 Delivery change vs original trial protocol

  | Parameter | Original protocol | Current practice |
  |---|---|---|
  | Delivery | 5 coplanar 6-MV step-and-shoot IMRT fields | Partial-arc VMAT, pt-specific gantry angles |
  | Schedule | 30 Gy/5 fx, nonconsecutive once-daily | 30 Gy/5 fx, consecutive days |

- 🔍 CTV now clips + 1.5 cm, PTV = CTV + 0.5 cm; authors call it formal, no actual volume change
- 🔍 PTV may extend max 4 mm into lung, cropped 3 mm from skin
- 📐 Plan optimization constraints, original vs updated (Table 1)

  | Structure | Original | Updated ideal | Accepted deviation |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | PTV | V28.5Gy = 100% · Dmax ≤105% · Dmin ≥28 Gy | V28.5Gy ≥98% · V31.5Gy ≤5% · Dmax ≤110% | V28.5Gy ≥95% · V31.5Gy ≤10% |
  | Ipsilateral uninvolved breast | V15Gy ≤50% | V15Gy ≤50% | V15Gy ≤60% |
  | Ipsilateral lung | V10Gy ≤20% | V10Gy ≤20% | not listed |
  | Contralateral lung | V5Gy ≤10% | V5Gy ≤10% | not listed |
  | Contralateral breast | Dmax ≤1 Gy | Dmax ≤1 Gy | Dmax ≤2 Gy |
  | Heart | V3Gy ≤10% | V3Gy ≤10% | not listed |

- 🔍 Plan missing the acceptable constraints → pt moved to WBI
- 📐 Ipsilateral breast V15Gy tracks PTV/ipsilateral breast volume ratio, R2 0.911
- 📊 Mean ipsilateral breast 731 cm3 (range 151-2205); mean PTV 139 cm3 (range 55-259)
- 📊 Toxicity at median 4.5 yr f/u (mean 3.4; range 0.6-5)
  - Acute any G1: 32% (n=16) · dermatitis 28%, fatigue 4%, edema 2%, breast pain 2%
  - Late any G1: 16% (n=8) · fibrosis 14%, telangiectasia 2%
  - No G>1 acute or late events
  - Physician cosmesis (Harvard): excellent 84%, good 14%, fair 2%
- 🔗 vs parent APBI-IMRT-Florence trial (every-other-day): more G1 toxicity with consecutive days

  | Endpoint | This series (consecutive) | Florence trial (alternate days) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Acute toxicity, any grade | 32% | 21.1% |
  | Early late toxicity, any grade | 16% | 4.5% |

- ⚠️ No local control or IBTR reported for this cohort; efficacy rests on the parent trial
- ⚠️ Median f/u 4.5 yr sits oddly with VMAT adopted 'in the past 3 years'; cohort timing unclear
- ⚠️ Some pts had a 1-day Sunday break, so not all strictly consecutive
