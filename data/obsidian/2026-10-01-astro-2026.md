# 2026-10-01 — ASTRO Annual Meeting 2026

**NRG/RTOG 0815: 6 mo STAD + dose-escalated RT in IR PCa missed OS (HR 0.87, p=0.13) but cut DM (HR 0.35) and PSA failure (HR 0.60).**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> ASTRO 2026 centers on biomarker selection for short-term ADT with RT: Decipher Int/High gets 8% absolute 10-yr DM benefit vs 1% Low GC in RTOG 0815, and PAM50 Luminal B predicts STADT benefit with salvage RT in SPPORT (5-y FFP 31% vs 10%). GETUG P05: brachy boost no bPFS gain over 80 Gy EBRT in IR PCa (88.0 vs 88.2%).

## 🌰 Prostate

All-prostate ASTRO day: who needs ADT with definitive or salvage RT (Decipher, PAM50, MMAI), whether brachy boost adds to 80 Gy EBRT, and how to predict and manage GU toxicity.

### NRG/RTOG 0815

> OS HR 0.87 (95% CI 0.72-1.04, p=0.13), not met; 6 mo STAD + dose-escalated RT cut PSA failure (HR 0.60) and DM (HR 0.35) in IR PCa.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Dose escalation did not erase the ADT benefit: with 79.2 Gy or a brachy boost, STAD still cut distant mets (40 vs 14 events, HR 0.35) and PCSM (HR 0.26). OS stayed flat because 422 of 446 deaths were non-prostate cancer, so the STAD call is a disease-control vs QoL/cardiac trade-off, not a survival one.

### RTOG 0815 Genomic Classifier (Decipher) Analysis

> Adding STAD to RT gave an 8% absolute 10-yr DM benefit with Int/High Decipher vs 1% with Low GC, in intermediate-risk prostate cancer.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Low GC was the largest stratum (44% of 395) and gained only 1% absolute 10-yr DM from STAD, versus 8% with Int/High GC. This points to Decipher as the gate for ADT omission alongside RT in intermediate-risk disease, pending the interaction statistics the source does not report.

### LUNAR vs SATURN

> IPTW-adjusted euPFS HR 1.39 (95% CI 0.56-3.45), p=0.47, SBRT+RLT vs SBRT+6mo AAT in M1 oligorecurrent PCa post-RP; n=63.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The SBRT-alone group progressed in 100% (18/18) at median 6.0 mo, versus 19.4 mo with RLT and 21.7 mo with AAT, so the RT read is that SBRT alone looks insufficient here and the open choice is the systemic partner. PSADT (HR 0.87 per month) is the selection variable to weigh when deciding whether SBRT alone is reasonable.

### NRG/RTOG 0534 SPPORT PAM50 Analysis

> PAM50 Luminal B predicts STADT benefit with salvage RT: 5-y FFP benefit 31% vs 10% non-Luminal B (p-int=.01).

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The ADT-with-salvage-RT decision may split by subtype: Luminal B gained 31% absolute 5-y FFP and 20% 10-y MFS from STADT, while non-Luminal B gained 10% FFP and no MFS (-3%). If validated, this flags a non-Luminal B group where STADT could be omitted from PBRT.

### GETUG P05

> 5-yr bPFS 88.0 vs 88.2% (p=0.90): 46 Gy EBRT + LDR/HDR boost no better than 80 Gy EBRT in IR prostate without ADT.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The toxicity ledger cuts against the boost: late GU G2 34.1% vs 17.9% (all-grade p=0.0006), against acute GI sparing only. With 5-yr bPFS flat in both GG2 and GG3, this questions offering a brachy boost over 80 Gy EBRT in IR disease treated without ADT.

### PROSTOX Ultra (SCIMITAR/EXCALIBUR)

> Continuous AUC 0.814 (SCIMITAR) and 0.824 (EXCALIBUR) for late G2+ GU toxicity after post-RP prostate bed SBRT; recalibrated 0.25 threshold gives pooled AUC 0.748.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The intact-prostate 0.50 cutoff failed post-op: in EXCALIBUR every pt with toxicity scored below it, while continuous ranking held (AUC 0.814 and 0.824). Clinical factors (pad use, IPSS, time from RP) predicted nothing, so a germline score at 0.25 is the only stratifier here for counseling or planning before prostate bed SBRT.

- 🔍 Germline 32-mirSNP signature, built for intact-prostate SBRT, tested in 2 prospective post-RP SBRT cohorts
- 🔍 SCIMITAR ([[NCT03541850]]): 100 enrolled, 89 evaluable, 30-34 Gy/5 fx ± pelvic nodes
- 🔍 EXCALIBUR ([[NCT04915508]]): 74 evaluable, 5-fx SBRT to prostate bed ± nodes
- 🔍 Saliva germline DNA, CLIA lab; scores from the original intact-prostate model, not refit
- 📊 SCIMITAR late G2+ GU AEs: 21/89 (23.6%); EXCALIBUR event rate not reported in source
- 📊 PROSTOX Ultra performance by cohort and threshold

  | Metric | SCIMITAR | EXCALIBUR |
  |---|---|---|
  | Continuous AUC | 0.814 | 0.824 |
  | Binary AUC at 0.50 (intact) | 0.755 | <0.50 |
  | Specificity at 0.50 | 0.985 | 0.926 |
  | Sensitivity at 0.50 | 0.546 | not reported |
  | Binary AUC at 0.25 (post-op) | 0.750 | 0.727 |
  | Median score, tox vs no tox | 0.547 vs 0.049, p<0.0001 | 0.338 vs 0.043, p=0.009 |

- 📐 Pooled AUC at 0.25: 0.748 (fixed-effects, 1,000-iteration bootstrap)
- 🔍 Age, baseline pad use, IPSS, time from RP to SBRT: no association with toxicity (all p>0.2)
- ⚠️ 0.25 threshold chosen by exploratory recalibration in these same cohorts; needs independent validation
- ⚠️ Conference report via trade press; nodal coverage split and dose by toxicity status not reported

### Biomarkers in the Post-operative Setting for Prostate Cancer (review)

> Spratt ASTRO 2026 review: PAM50 luminal B predicts apalutamide benefit with salvage RT in NRG GU006 (5y MFS 95% vs 82%, HR 0.27); non-luminal B no benefit.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The ADT decision with salvage RT now has three selectors: pre-RT PSA (OS benefit only above 0.50 ng/mL in POSEIDON), PAM50 luminal B (GU006 5y MFS 95% vs 82%), and MMAI-high. Adverse pathology count does not select (interaction p=0.40). SYMPHONY will test 5-fraction prostate-bed SBRT, dose de-escalation, and elective nodal RT.

- 🔍 Talk-level review (Spratt, ASTRO 2026, UroToday write-up); no new single-trial result

### GUAM International Consensus Guideline on GU Adverse Events

> First RT-specific GU AE guideline: 48-expert Delphi, 31 key questions; 76% favor moderate hypofractionation over SBRT at baseline IPSS 15-19.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Baseline IPSS, not a bladder or urethral constraint, becomes the gating variable for fractionation: 76% favored moderate hypofractionation over SBRT at IPSS 15-19, while no dose-volume constraint reached consensus. The technique lever the panel flagged is margin, citing MIRAGE late G≥2 GU 51% to 27% with 2-mm MRI-guided vs 4-mm CT-guided margins.

- 🔍 Systematic review (183 articles) + 3-round modified Delphi, May 2025 to Feb 2026
- 🔍 Panel: 48 experts, 15 countries; 34 radonc, 7 medonc, 7 urologic onc
- 🔍 Consensus bar ≥75% agreement; endorsed by ASTRO and ESTRO
- 📊 Unmet need per source: >30% acute G≥2 GU AEs, ≥15% late G≥2 GU AEs
- 💊 Acute GU symptom management by setting (UTI excluded first)

  | Setting | Preferred 1st-line | Subsequent options, in order |
  |---|---|---|
  | Intact, obstructive | Alpha blocker | PDE5-I → NSAID → steroid → anti-spasmodic |
  | Intact, irritative | Anti-spasmodic | Alpha blocker → NSAID → phenazopyridine → cranberry |
  | Post-prostatectomy | Anti-spasmodic | Not stated in source |

- 💊 Medication strategy
  - 80% against prophylactic medication before symptom onset
  - 80% add a 2nd agent rather than switch when 1st-line fails
  - 74%: alpha blockers do not or probably do not help post-op
- 📊 Panel IPSS threshold for favoring moderate hypofractionation over SBRT

  | Baseline IPSS | % panel selecting |
  |---|---|
  | 10-14 | 16% |
  | 15-19 | 61% |
  | 20-24 | 21% |
  | ≥25 | 2% |

- 📊 76% favor moderate hypofractionation over SBRT at baseline IPSS 15-19
- 📊 85% recommend full bladder at simulation and treatment
- ⚠️ No dose-volume constraint reached consensus
- 📊 Panel IPSS threshold for urology referral for outlet procedure

  | Baseline IPSS | % panel selecting |
  |---|---|
  | 15-19 | 34% |
  | 20-24 | 47% |
  | 25-29 | 15% |
  | ≥30 | 4% |

- 🔍 Bladder outlet obstruction (BOO) procedure timing
  - Recovery 8-12 wk before RT: 94% wait ≥8 wk, 66% ≥12 wk
  - 85% would consider prophylactic BOO procedure for severe LUTS pre-RT
  - 95% against prophylactic procedure when irritative symptoms predominate
  - 52% consider SBRT safe after BOO procedure once obstruction resolved
- 💊 HBOT reserved for late radiation cystitis with persistent hematuria after cystoscopy and failed medical mgmt
- ⚠️ Several reported splits (74%, 61%, 52%) sit under the ≥75% bar; source does not say how each was adopted
- ⚠️ Source is trade-press conference write-up, not the guideline manuscript
