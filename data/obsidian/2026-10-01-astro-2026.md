# 2026-10-01 — ASTRO Annual Meeting 2026

**GETUG P05: brachy boost vs 80 Gy EBRT, 5-yr bPFS 88% vs 88.2% (p=0.90) in intermediate-risk PCa without ADT, with more late G2 GU toxicity (34.1% vs 17.9%).**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Prostate-only day. Intensification for intermediate-risk disease is narrowing: GETUG P05 brachy boost added late GU toxicity without bPFS gain, and NRG/RTOG 0815 STAD cut DM (HR 0.35) with OS not met, benefit concentrated in Int/High Decipher GC. Post-RP, PAM50 Luminal B selects hormonal benefit with salvage RT (GU006 MFS HR 0.27).

## 🌰 Prostate

The day's theme is who needs intensification with RT: dose escalation via boost, STAD in intermediate risk, and biomarker-selected ADT or ARSI with salvage RT, plus a GUAM Delphi on GU toxicity management.

### NRG/RTOG 0815 Decipher GC score and STAD benefit

> Adding STAD to RT cut 10-yr DM by 8% absolute in Int/High GC vs 1% in Low GC, intermediate-risk prostate (n=395).

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Low GC (<0.45) covered 44% of this intermediate-risk cohort and gained only 1% absolute 10-yr DM reduction from STAD, vs 8% in Int/High GC. That pushes Decipher toward the ADT-omission decision with definitive RT, pending interaction testing and the prospective de-escalation trials.

### GETUG P05

> 5-yr bPFS 88% vs 88.2% (p=0.90) for brachy boost vs 80 Gy EBRT in intermediate-risk PCa without ADT; late GU toxicity higher with boost.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The read is toxicity, not control: boost cut acute GI toxicity (29.9% vs 41.2%) but raised late all-grade GU toxicity (82.2% vs 63.6%, p=0.0006), late G2 GU 34.1% vs 17.9%. Against 80 Gy in 2Gy fractions without ADT, the boost bought no 5-yr bPFS, which weighs against routine boost here.

### LUNAR vs SATURN

> Median time to progression 19.4 mo with SBRT + 177Lu-PNT2002 vs 21.7 mo with SBRT + 6 mo AAT; IPTW-adjusted HR 1.39 (0.56-3.45), p=0.47.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The MDT read is the SBRT-alone reference: median time to progression 6.0 mo with 18/18 progressing, vs 19.4 and 21.7 mo with either systemic partner. That frames the question as which partner to add to SBRT, not whether to add one, and RLT keeps pts eugonadal with numerically fewer grade ≥3 AEs (10% vs 20%, p=0.357).

### NRG/RTOG 0815 long-term follow-up

> OS HR 0.87, p=0.13 (not met), but 6 mo STAD cut PSA failure (HR 0.60) and DM (HR 0.35) in intermediate-risk PCa on dose-escalated RT.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Dose escalation (79.2 Gy or brachy boost) did not absorb the STAD benefit: distant metastases 40 vs 14 events (HR 0.35), prostate cancer deaths 19 vs 5. With 422 of 446 deaths non-prostate, OS was never going to decide this, so add-vs-omit STAD hinges on DM reduction weighed against cardiac and sexual toxicity.

- 🔍 Median f/u 9.4 y overall, 10.3 y in surviving pts

### PROSTOX Ultra post-prostatectomy SBRT (SCIMITAR, EXCALIBUR)

> Germline 32-mirSNP score kept continuous AUC 0.814/0.824 for late G2+ GU AEs after post-op SBRT; intact 0.50 cutoff failed, recalibrated 0.25 gave pooled AUC 0.748.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> SCIMITAR logged 23.6% late G2+ GU AEs after 30-34 Gy in 5 fx to the bed, and age, pad use, IPSS and time from RP did not predict them (all p > 0.2). The germline score was the only stratifier that separated pts, which makes it a candidate input for counseling and risk-adaptive bed SBRT planning once the 0.25 cutoff is validated externally.

- 🔍 PROSTOX Ultra: 32-mirSNP germline signature (saliva DNA, CLIA lab), original intact-prostate SBRT model
- 🔍 Two prospective post-RP SBRT cohorts: SCIMITAR ([[NCT03541850]]) and EXCALIBUR ([[NCT04915508]])
- 🔍 Cohorts and RT
  - SCIMITAR: 100 enrolled, 89 evaluable; 30-34 Gy in 5 fx ± pelvic nodes
  - EXCALIBUR: 74 evaluable; 5-fx SBRT to prostate bed ± nodes
- 📊 Discrimination for late G2+ GU AEs by cohort

  | Metric | SCIMITAR | EXCALIBUR |
  |---|---|---|
  | Late G2+ GU AEs | 21/89 (23.6%) | not reported in source |
  | Continuous AUC | 0.814 | 0.824 |
  | Dichotomized AUC at 0.50 | 0.755 | <0.50 |
  | Specificity at 0.50 | 0.985 | 0.926 |
  | Sensitivity at 0.50 | 0.546 | not reported in source |
  | Median score, tox vs no tox | 0.547 vs 0.049, p<0.0001 | 0.338 vs 0.043, p=0.009 |
  | Dichotomized AUC at 0.25 | 0.750 | 0.727 |

- 📊 Pooled dichotomized AUC at 0.25: 0.748 (bootstrapped, fixed-effects pooling)
- 📊 Age, baseline pad use, IPSS, time from RP to SBRT: not associated with toxicity (all p > 0.2)
- ⚠️ 0.25 threshold chosen by exploratory recalibration in the same cohorts it was tested in; no independent validation
- ⚠️ Toxicity grading scale and follow-up duration not stated in source
- ⚠️ ± pelvic nodes in both cohorts; nodal field not analyzed as a covariate in source
- 🔗 Intact-prostate SBRT: PROSTOX Ultra validated at prespecified 0.50 cutoff; post-op ordering preserved, trigger shifted down

### Post-operative biomarkers in prostate cancer (Spratt, ASTRO 2026)

> PAM50 luminal B selected apalutamide benefit with salvage RT in NRG GU006 (5yr MFS 95% vs 82%, HR 0.27); non-luminal B showed none.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Two selectors for adding hormone therapy to post-op RT now point the same way: pre-RT PSA above 0.50 ng/mL (POSEIDON OS HR 0.72 and 0.69) and PAM50 luminal B (GU006 MFS HR 0.27). Adverse pathology count did not predict benefit, so margin, SVI and GG4-5 alone should not drive the ADT add-on.

- 🔍 Review talk (Spratt, ASTRO 2026): biomarkers for systemic intensification with post-RP RT, via UroToday write-up

### GUAM international consensus guideline on GU adverse events after prostate RT

> Delphi panel (48 experts, ≥75% agreement): 76% favor moderate hypofx over SBRT at baseline IPSS 15–19; no dose-volume constraint reached consensus.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The technique read is that baseline IPSS now gates fractionation: 76% favored moderate hypofx over SBRT at IPSS 15–19, and MIRAGE (2-mm MRI-guided margins, late G≥2 GU 51% → 27%) was the margin evidence cited. Yet no bladder or urethral dose-volume constraint reached consensus, so planning-level GU sparing still rests on institutional choice.

- 🔍 Systematic review (183 articles) + 3-round modified Delphi, May 2025–Feb 2026
- 🔍 Panel: 48 experts, 15 countries; 34 radonc, 7 medonc, 7 urologic oncologists
- 🔍 Consensus defined as ≥75% agreement; ASTRO and ESTRO endorsed
- 🔍 Consensus reached on 31 key questions across 5 domains
- 💊 Acute GU symptom management by setting (assumes UTI excluded)

  | Setting | Preferred 1st line | Subsequent options (in order) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Intact prostate, obstructive | Alpha blockers | PDE5-Is, NSAIDs, steroids, anti-spasmodics |
  | Intact prostate, irritative | Anti-spasmodics | Alpha blockers, NSAIDs, phenazopyridine, cranberry |
  | Post-prostatectomy | Anti-spasmodics | Not specified in source |

- 📊 Panel agreement on key recommendations

  | Recommendation | Agreement |
  |---|---|
  | No prophylactic GU medication before symptom onset | 80% |
  | Add 2nd agent rather than switch if 1st line inadequate | 80% |
  | Alpha blockers do not/probably do not help post-RP | 74% |
  | Moderate hypofx over SBRT at baseline IPSS 15–19 | 76% |
  | Full bladder at simulation and treatment | 85% |
  | Refer for outlet procedure at IPSS ≥20 | 74% |
  | Wait ≥8 wk after outlet procedure before RT | 94% |
  | Wait ≥12 wk after outlet procedure before RT | 66% |
  | SBRT safe after outlet procedure once obstruction resolved | 52% |
  | Prophylactic outlet procedure for severe LUTS | 85% |
  | No outlet procedure when irritative symptoms predominate | 95% |

- 📊 Baseline IPSS threshold for choosing moderate hypofx over SBRT (panel votes)

  | IPSS threshold | Panelists |
  |---|---|
  | 10–14 | 16% |
  | 15–19 | 61% |
  | 20–24 | 21% |
  | ≥25 | 2% |

- 📊 IPSS threshold for urology referral for outlet procedure (panel votes)

  | IPSS threshold | Panelists |
  |---|---|
  | 15–19 | 34% |
  | 20–24 | 47% |
  | 25–29 | 15% |
  | ≥30 | 4% |

- 🔗 MIRAGE cited: MRI-guided 2-mm margins cut late G≥2 GU AEs 51% → 27% vs CT-guided 4-mm
- 🔗 CHHiP, PROFIT, RTOG 0415, HYDRA meta-analysis cited: no consistent late GU excess with moderate hypofx
- 💊 HBOT reserved for cystoscopy-confirmed late radiation cystitis with persistent hematuria after failed medical mgmt; RICH-ART cited
- ⚠️ Several reported items (74% referral, 66% ≥12 wk wait, 52% SBRT post-procedure) sit below the ≥75% consensus bar
