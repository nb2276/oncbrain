# 2026-10-01 — ASTRO Annual Meeting 2026

**NRG/RTOG 0815: 6 mo STAD + dose-escalated RT in IR PCa, OS HR 0.87 (p=0.13) not met, but PSA failure HR 0.60 and DM HR 0.35.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> The common thread in prostate is choosing who gets ADT with RT. In IR disease, RTOG 0815 STAD cut DM but not OS. GC ≥0.45 (8% vs 1% 10-yr DM benefit), PAM50 Luminal B and pre-SRT PSA >0.5 flag who gains. In the GUAM Delphi, 76% of panelists favor moderate hypofx over SBRT at baseline IPSS 15-19.

## 🌰 Prostate

Biomarker selection for ADT with definitive and salvage RT (GC, PAM50, MMAI, pre-RT PSA) led the day, alongside two GU-toxicity tools: a germline AE predictor and a Delphi consensus.

### RTOG 0815 — Decipher/GC Score Secondary Analysis

> Secondary analysis: adding STAD to RT cut 10-yr DM by 8% absolute in Int/High GC vs 1% in Low GC, intermediate-risk prostate.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Low GC was the largest group (44%), and adding STAD bought only 1% absolute 10-yr DM there, against 8% in Int/High GC. That puts RT alone on the table for nearly half of intermediate-risk men and points ADT toward the GC ≥0.45 group, the decision NRG GU010 is testing prospectively.

### NRG/RTOG 0815

> OS HR 0.87 (95% CI 0.72-1.04, p=0.13), not met; 6 mo STAD + dose-escalated RT cut PSA failure (HR 0.60) and DM (HR 0.35) in IR PCa.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Dose escalation did not erase the ADT benefit: with 79.2 Gy or a brachy boost, STAD still cut distant mets (40 vs 14 events, HR 0.35) and PCSM (HR 0.26). OS stayed flat because 422 of 446 deaths were non-prostate cancer, so the STAD call is a disease-control vs QoL/cardiac trade-off, not a survival one.

- 🔍 Ph 3 RCT, N=1493 (751 RT alone vs 742 RT + STAD); median f/u 10.3 y in survivors
- 🔍 RT: EBRT 79.2 Gy, or EBRT + LDR or HDR brachy boost; 89% received EBRT alone
- 💊 STAD = 6 mo LHRH agonist + antiandrogen
- 📐 Powered for 5-y OS 90% → 93% (HR 0.66), 85% power
- 📊 Secondary disease-control and mortality endpoints
- ⚠️ 422 of 446 deaths were non-prostate cancer; competing mortality dilutes any OS signal
- 📊 PSA failure benefit by subgroup (HR, STAD vs none)
  - 1 IR factor: HR 0.66
  - 2-3 IR factors: HR 0.52
  - ACE-27 <2: HR 0.58
  - EBRT: HR 0.58
- ⚠️ Authors could not identify a subgroup that does not benefit; smaller subgroups had wide CIs
- ⚠️ No PSMA PET, genomic classifier, or MMAI selection; 67% had a single IR factor

### NRG/RTOG 0534 SPPORT — PAM50 Analysis

> PAM50 Luminal B vs non-Luminal B: STADT with salvage RT added 31% vs 10% 5-y FFP (p-int=.01); 10-y MFS benefit 20% vs -3% (p-int=.05).

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The ADT add-on to prostate-bed RT concentrates in Luminal B: 31% vs 10% absolute 5-y FFP gain, and the 10-y MFS gain (20%) vanishes outside Luminal B (-3%). Whether subtype also sorts pelvic nodal RT benefit is unsettled; the per-arm panels lack legible subtype labels in source.

### PROSTOX Ultra (SCIMITAR/EXCALIBUR)

> Continuous AUC 0.814 and 0.824 for late G2+ GU AE after post-op prostate-bed SBRT; the intact 0.50 cutoff fails post-op, and a recalibrated 0.25 cutoff gives a pooled AUC of 0.748.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The intact 0.50 cutoff cannot be reused after prostatectomy. In SCIMITAR it caught only 0.546 of toxicity cases, and in EXCALIBUR every pt with toxicity scored below it. Age, pad use, IPSS and the RP-to-SBRT interval did not predict late G2+ GU AE (all p > 0.2). That leaves the germline score as the only stratifier here for counseling before 5-fx prostate-bed SBRT.

- 🔍 32-mirSNP germline signature, originally built on intact-prostate SBRT with a prespecified 0.50 threshold
- 🔍 Tested in 2 prospective post-op SBRT cohorts using the original intact-prostate model, no redevelopment
- 🔍 Saliva germline DNA, CLIA-certified lab
- 📊 Discrimination for late G2+ GU AE by cohort

  | Metric | SCIMITAR | EXCALIBUR |
  |---|---|---|
  | Evaluable n | 89 | 74 |
  | Late G2+ GU AE | 21/89 (23.6%) | not reported in source |
  | Continuous AUC | 0.814 | 0.824 |
  | AUC at 0.50 threshold | 0.755 | <0.50 |
  | Specificity at 0.50 | 0.985 | 0.926 |
  | Sensitivity at 0.50 | 0.546 | not reported in source |
  | AUC at 0.25 threshold | 0.750 | 0.727 |
  | Median score, AE vs no AE | 0.547 vs 0.049, p<0.0001 | 0.338 vs 0.043, p=0.009 |

- 📊 Pooled AUC at 0.25 was 0.748 (fixed-effects meta-analysis, 1,000 bootstrap iterations)
- 📐 Candidate post-op thresholds ~0.10-0.31 met sensitivity >=0.65 and specificity >=0.70; 0.25 selected
- 📊 Age, baseline pad use, IPSS, and RP-to-SBRT interval did not predict toxicity (all p > 0.2)
- ⚠️ The 0.25 threshold was chosen in the same cohorts it was tested on, so AUC at 0.25 is optimistic
- ⚠️ Conference abstract via trade press; follow-up duration and GU AE grading scale not reported in source

### GUAM Working Group — International Consensus Guideline on GU AEs

> 48-expert Delphi panel, ASTRO/ESTRO-endorsed: 76% favor moderate hypofx over SBRT at baseline IPSS 15-19; alpha blockers 1L for obstructive intact-prostate symptoms.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> No bladder or urethral dose-volume constraint reached consensus, so the technique lever left is margin: MIRAGE MRI-guided 2-mm margins cut late G≥2 GU AEs 51% → 27% vs CT-guided 4-mm. The IPSS ≥20 outlet-procedure referral (74%) and SBRT after an outlet procedure (52%) fall below the panel's own 75% bar.

- 🔍 Systematic review (183 articles) + 3-round modified Delphi, May 2025 to Feb 2026
- 🔍 48 experts, 15 countries: 34 radonc, 7 medonc, 7 urologic oncologists
- 🔍 Consensus defined as ≥75% agreement; 31 key questions reached consensus
- 🔍 Independently peer reviewed; endorsed by ASTRO and ESTRO
- 💊 Acute GU AE medication sequence by setting (UTI excluded first)

  | Setting | Preferred 1L | Subsequent options (in order) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Intact, obstructive | Alpha blocker | PDE5-I → NSAID → steroid → anti-spasmodic |
  | Intact, irritative | Anti-spasmodic | Alpha blocker → NSAID → phenazopyridine → cranberry |
  | Post-prostatectomy | Anti-spasmodic | 74% say alpha blockers do not or probably do not help |

- 💊 Medication strategy
  - 80% against prophylactic meds before symptom onset
  - 80% add a 2nd agent rather than switch when 1L inadequate
- 📊 Baseline IPSS threshold for choosing moderate hypofx over SBRT

  | IPSS threshold | Panelists selecting |
  |---|---|
  | 10-14 | 16% |
  | 15-19 | 61% |
  | 20-24 | 21% |
  | ≥25 | 2% |

- 📊 76% favor moderate hypofx over SBRT at baseline IPSS 15-19
- 📊 85% recommend full bladder at simulation and treatment
- 📊 Baseline IPSS threshold for urology referral re: outlet procedure

  | IPSS threshold | Panelists selecting |
  |---|---|
  | 15-19 | 34% |
  | 20-24 | 47% |
  | 25-29 | 15% |
  | ≥30 | 4% |

- 📊 Bladder outlet obstruction (BOO) procedure and RT
  - 74% refer at IPSS ≥20
  - Wait before RT: 94% ≥8 wk, 66% ≥12 wk
  - 52% consider SBRT safe post-procedure once obstruction resolved
  - 85% consider prophylactic procedure for severe LUTS
  - 95% against prophylactic procedure when irritative symptoms predominate
- 🔗 MIRAGE: MRI-guided 2-mm margins cut late G≥2 GU AEs 51% → 27% vs CT-guided 4-mm
- 🔗 CHHiP, PROFIT, RTOG 0415, HYDRA meta-analysis: no consistent late GU AE increase with moderate hypofx
- 💊 HBOT reserved for late radiation cystitis with persistent hematuria, cystoscopy-confirmed, after failed medical mgmt; RICH-ART cited
- ⚠️ Some items sit below the 75% bar: postop alpha blocker 74%, IPSS ≥20 referral 74%, SBRT post-BOO 52%
- ⚠️ No dose-volume constraint reached consensus
- ⚠️ Panel radonc-weighted (34 of 48)

### ASTRO 2026: Role of Biomarkers in the Post-operative Setting (Spratt)

> Pre-SRT PSA, PAM50 luminal B and MMAI-high status flag who gains from adding hormone therapy to post-op RT; adverse pathology count does not.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The ADT decision with salvage RT now has three selectors, one of them randomized: PSA above 0.50 ng/mL (POSEIDON HR 0.72 and 0.69), PAM50 luminal B (GU006 MFS HR 0.27) and MMAI-high. Adverse pathology count did not modify benefit (interaction p=0.40). Margin, SVI or ECE alone should not drive ADT addition at low pre-RT PSA.

- 🔍 UroToday write-up of Spratt's ASTRO 2026 talk; a review of post-RP biomarker data, not one new trial
- 📊 POSEIDON IPD MA: 6 RCTs, 6,057 pts, median f/u 9.0 yr (IQR 7.2-10.7)
- 📐 Hormone therapy + post-op RT vs RT alone, OS: HR 0.87 (95% CI 0.76-1.01), p=0.06
- 📐 Long-term (24 mo) vs short-term (4-6 mo) ADT, OS: HR 0.96 (95% CI 0.83-1.10), p=0.56
- 📊 POSEIDON OS benefit of adding hormone therapy, by pre-RT PSA

  | Pre-RT PSA (ng/mL) | OS HR (95% CI) | p |
  |---|---|---|
  | ≤0.20 | 1.14 (0.83-1.57) | 0.42 |
  | 0.21-0.50 | 0.94 (0.74-1.19) | 0.70 |
  | 0.51-1.0 | 0.72 (0.54-0.96) | 0.02 |
  | >1.0 | 0.69 (0.48-0.98) | 0.03 |

- 📊 Adverse pathology count (GG4-5, SVI, ECE, +margin) IPD MA: prognostic, not predictive
  - Per added feature: OS HR 1.25 (95% CI 1.16-1.36), MFS HR 1.29 (1.20-1.38), both p<0.001
  - Interaction with hormone therapy: OS HR 0.93 (0.79-1.10), p=0.40
  - Interaction with hormone therapy: MFS HR 0.88 (0.77-1.01), p=0.08
- 📊 NRG GU006 (BALANCE): SRT + 6 mo apalutamide vs placebo, by PAM50 subtype

  | Endpoint | Luminal B | Non-luminal B |
  |---|---|---|
  | 5-yr bPFS, apa vs placebo | 72% vs 54% | 70% vs 71% |
  | bPFS HR | 0.45 (95% CI 0.24-0.86), p=0.0062 | 0.95 (80% CI 0.65-1.41), p=0.44 |
  | 5-yr MFS, apa vs placebo | 95% vs 82% | 90% vs 89% |
  | MFS HR | 0.27 (95% CI 0.07-0.95), p=0.029 | 1.06 (95% CI 0.41-2.78), p=0.90 |

- 🔍 GU006 eligibility: post-RP, PSA 0.1-1.0 ng/mL, N0M0; double-blind, placebo-controlled, PAM50-stratified
- 🔗 RTOG 0534 PAM50 (post-hoc): luminal B HRs 0.63, 0.40 (p=0.004), 0.64 across arm comparisons; non-luminal B 0.98-1.12
- 📊 MMAI digital pathology (RTOG 9601/0534): MMAI-high 12-yr DM ~40% RT alone vs ~20% RT + hormone therapy; MMAI-low curves overlapped
- ⚠️ MMAI skipped standalone independent validation and clinical-genomic integration before randomized-trial validation
- ⚠️ GU006 luminal B MFS CI wide (0.07-0.95); few events behind the headline
- 🔍 NRG SYMPHONY salvage RT program (planned)
  - PRESTO: prostate bed 20-33 fx vs 5-fx SBRT, 948 pts
  - ADAGIO: normal vs reduced-dose RT, 948 pts
  - CRESCENDO: prostate bed alone vs + elective nodal RT, 870 pts
  - FULL-SCORE: MDT ± prostate-bed/nodal RT in N+/M+, 480 pts
