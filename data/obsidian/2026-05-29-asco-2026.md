# 2026-05-29 — ASCO Annual Meeting 2026

**Wait or Treat ([[NCT05236946]]): upfront cranial RT cut intracranial progression (sub-HR 0.35, 0.21-0.59) but 2y OS was 48% vs 60% for delayed RT.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Only CNS data today, and it cuts against reflexive upfront cranial RT in asymptomatic BM from EGFR/ALK+ NSCLC: a real intracranial control gain (2y 21.7% vs 50% progression) with OS numerically worse (HR 1.45, per attendee report). Timing, not omission, and dose/technique/target volume are absent from source.

## 🧠 CNS

### Wait or Treat (NCT05236946) · [[NCT05236946]]

> Upfront cranial RT cut intracranial progression (sub-HR 0.35, 0.21-0.59, p<0.001) but 2y OS favored delayed RT, 48% vs 60%.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The intracranial win is real (2y progression 21.7% vs 50%, sub-HR 0.35) yet does not convert: 2y OS 48% upfront vs 60% delayed, HR 1.45. With necrosis reported ~6% upfront vs none delayed, and RT dose/technique unstated in source, this moves the timing decision toward deferral with MRI q3m surveillance.

- 🔍 Phase III open-label RCT, N=208, 1:1, Tata Memorial; asymptomatic radiologically measurable BM in EGFR/ALK+ metastatic NSCLC, ECOG 0-2
- 🔍 Stratified by GPA (0-2 vs >2) and synchronous vs metachronous BM; MRI brain q3m for 1yr then q6m
- 🔍 Delayed arm got cranial RT at intracranial PD or patient's wish, so this is timing, not omission
- 📊 1° EP intracranial PFS: 20 events upfront (n=105) vs 47 delayed (n=103)
- 📊 Intracranial progression by timepoint (competing-risk, death as competing event)

  | Timepoint | Upfront RT (n=105) | Delayed RT (n=103) |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1-year | 8.7% (2.9%, 14.5%) | 25.7% (16.8%, 34.7%) |
  | 2-years | 21.7% (12.6%, 30.8%) | 50% (39.2%, 60.9%) |
  | Sub-HR (95% CI) | 0.35 (0.21, 0.59), p<0.001 | ref |

- 📊 OS numerically favored delayed RT: 2y OS 48% upfront vs 60% delayed, OS HR 1.45 (per @StephenVLiu; not in slide OCR)
- ⚠️ Radiation necrosis ~6% upfront vs none delayed (reported by attendees, not captured in source slide OCR)
- ⚠️ RT dose, fractionation, technique (WBRT vs SRS) and target volume not stated in source; transferability unknown
- ⚠️ Median f/u 30.6mo (28.7, 36) is short for TKI-era oncogene-driven NSCLC where OS runs multiple years; the OS split may not be stable
- ⚠️ Open-label with neurocognition and PROM as secondaries; those endpoints are unblinded and not reported in source
