# 2026-06-16

**DOREMY: 36 Gy/18fx preop RT in myxoid liposarcoma gives 5yr LRFS 97.4% at median f/u 66.4mo, a 14 Gy de-escalation off the 50 Gy standard.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Sarcoma carried the day on dose de-escalation: DOREMY holds near-total local control at 36 Gy preop in MLS, though single-arm against historic 50 Gy series and histology-specific. Prostate adds an ADT-selection read for men on pelvic RT, with relugolix showing 68.9 mm³ less 12-mo plaque accrual than leuprolide on a surrogate CCTA endpoint.

## 🦴 Sarcoma

Preop RT dose de-escalation in a radiosensitive histology, benchmarked against historic 50 Gy series rather than a randomised arm.

### DOREMY · [[NCT02106312]]

> 5yr LRFS 97.4% after 36Gy/18fx preop RT in myxoid liposarcoma, wound complications 21%, median f/u 66.4mo.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The number that moves the dose decision is 97.4% 5yr LRFS at 36 Gy, matched to a 21% wound complication rate, with only 3% late G3. Dose is 2 Gy daily to 36 Gy preop, standard fractionation, so it transfers directly. This is a de-escalation read confined to translocation-confirmed MLS.

- 📊 5yr local recurrence-free survival 97.4% (95% CI 93.9-100%) with 36 Gy preop
- 🔍 Preop RT 36 Gy in 2 Gy once-daily fractions (18 fx), then resection
- 🔍 Prospective single-group phase 2, N=90, 9 tertiary sarcoma centres (Europe + US)
- 🔍 Eligibility: adults, biopsy-proven translocation-confirmed localized MLS of trunk or extremity
- 📐 Median f/u 66.4 mo (IQR 48.8-87.5); accrual Nov 2010 to May 2020
- 📊 5-year outcomes (95% CI)

  | Endpoint | 5yr rate | 95% CI |
  |---|---|---|
  | Local recurrence-free survival | 97.4% | 93.9-100% |
  | Progression-free survival | 81.0% | 72.6-89.4% |
  | Disease-specific survival | 89.5% | 82.6-96.4% |
  | Overall survival | 88.5% | 81.2-95.8% |

- 📊 Toxicity and wound outcomes
  - Wound complication 18/90 (21%); 14 (16%) required intervention
  - Late G2 toxic effects 13 pts (15%)
  - Late G3 toxic effects 3 pts (3%)
- 🔍 3 pts (3%) never went to surgery due to intercurrent metastatic disease; RT per protocol in all 90
- ⚠️ Single-arm, no randomised comparator against standard 50 Gy; LRFS benchmarked against historic series only
- ⚠️ MLS is uniquely radiosensitive; this dose reduction does not read across to other STS histologies

**Sources:**
- 📄 [[PMID 42141895]] **Dose Reduction of Preoperative Radiotherapy in Myxoid Liposarcoma: The Phase 2 DOREMY Nonrandomized Clinical Trial.** — Lansu; Bov&#xe9;e; Braam et al.. *JAMA oncology* (2026-05). [doi:10.1001/jamaoncol.2026.1577](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaoncol.2026.1577)
  > IMPORTANCE: Prospective data from 2 phase 2 trials showed favorable wound complication rates and promising local control after a reduced preoperative radiotherapy dose for myxoid liposarcoma (MLS). However, long-term follow-up data are currently lacking.
  > OBJECTIVE: To determine the efficacy and toxicity profile of a reduced preoperative radiotherapy dose in patients with MLS with long-term follow-up.
  > DESIGN, SETTING, AND PARTICIPANTS: The Dose Reduction of Preoperative Radiotherapy in Myxoid Liposarcoma (DOREMY) trial is a prospective, single-group, phase 2 nonrandomized clinical trial conducted in 9 tertiary sarcoma centers in Europe and the US. Eligible patients were adults with biopsy-proven and translocation-confirmed localized MLS of the trunk or extremity who were enrolled from November 24, 2010, to May 14, 2020. Data were analyzed from January to December 2025.
  > INTERVENTION: Preoperative radiotherapy to a reduced dose of 36 Gy in once-daily 2-Gy fractions followed by resection.
  > MAIN OUTCOMES AND MEASURES: Long-term local recurrence-free survival, progression-free survival, disease-specific survival, overall survival, and late toxic effects.
  > RESULTS: Ninety patients (mean [SD] age, 47 [13.1] years; 50 [56%] male) were included and followed up for a median (IQR) of 66.4 (48.8-87.5) months. Preoperative radiotherapy was delivered according to protocol in all patients. Surgery was not performed in 3 patients (3%) due to intercurrent metastatic disease. Local recurrence-free survival, progression-free survival, disease-specific survival, and overall survival rates at 5 years were 97.4% (95% CI, 93.9%-100%), 81.0% (95% CI, 72.6%-89.4%), 89.5% (95% CI, 82.6%-96.4%), and 88.5% (95% CI, 81.2%-95.8%), respectively. In total, 18 patients (21%) experienced a wound complication, and 14 (16%) required intervention. Any grade 2 or grade 3 late toxic effects were seen among 13 patients (15%) and 3 patients (3%), respectively.
  > CONCLUSIONS AND RELEVANCE: This long-term analysis of the DOREMY nonrandomized clinical trial demonstrated excellent local control and a favorable toxicity profile following dose reduction of preoperative radiotherapy in patients with MLS. These compelling phase 2 findings support adoption of this regimen as an appropriate treatment option through shared decision-making with the patient, given the impracticality of conducting a phase 3 trial for a rare cancer.
  > TRIAL REGISTRATION: ClinicalTrials.gov Identifier: [[NCT02106312]].

## 🌰 Prostate

ADT agent choice as a cardiovascular decision for men already committed to pelvic RT, read on imaging rather than events.

### REVELUTION

> Adjusted 12-mo total plaque volume rose 68.9 mm³ more with leuprolide vs relugolix in men getting prostate RT.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Every man here got prostate ± pelvic nodal RT, so this is an RT-clinic decision, not a med-onc one: for the 6-month ADT course you prescribe alongside definitive RT, the agonist added 68.9 mm³ of adjusted total plaque volume at 12 months, driven by non-calcified plaque. A radiation-alone control cohort was enrolled in parallel, though its comparison was not reported in source.

- 🔍 Single-institution parallel-cohort open-label randomized trial, 4 Emory-affiliated centers, enrolled June 2020 to 2024
- 🔍 N=94 men, non-metastatic prostate cancer, all received pelvic RT to prostate ± pelvic nodes
- 🔍 ADT-eligible pts randomized 1:1 relugolix vs leuprolide; stratified by ASCVD 10-yr risk score
- 🔍 Parallel prospective control arm: lower-risk pts on definitive RT alone, no planned ADT
- 💊 Relugolix 360mg day 1 then 120mg daily; leuprolide as 3-month depots; ADT ≥6 months by risk tier
- 📊 1° EP change in total plaque volume on serial CCTA (baseline and 12 mo), blinded to arm, HeartFlow automated quantification
- 📊 12-month plaque volume change, leuprolide vs relugolix

  | Measure | Leuprolide | Relugolix |
  |---|---|---|
  | Total plaque volume, crude difference | 56 mm³ | 25 mm³ |

- 📐 Adjusted mean difference in total plaque volume 68.9 mm³ favoring relugolix; adjusted for age, statin use, baseline plaque volume
- 📊 Difference driven mainly by non-calcified plaque; calcified and low-attenuation plaque not significantly different, absolute changes low
- 🔗 Mechanistic follow-on to HERO (2020), which showed lower MACE with relugolix vs leuprolide but left mechanism unexplained
- ⚠️ Plaque volume is a surrogate biomarker, not a MACE endpoint; N=94 across three cohorts leaves each arm small
- ⚠️ Open-label; per-arm n, CIs and p-values not reported in source, and the source is a video interview, not the JAMA Cardiology paper
