# 2026-11-02

**AGITG MASTERPLAN: adding SBRT 40 Gy/5fx to chemo cut 12-mo LRR 20.2% vs 52.0% (P=0.022), mOS 22.0 vs 14.0 mo in high-risk operable/borderline/LA pancreas.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Local control led the day. MASTERPLAN isolates the SBRT benefit on LRR in pancreas. UNION's 3-yr EFS 91.3% vs 77.4% (HR 0.33) in LARC bundles SCRT with camrelizumab, so the RT share can't be separated. TOP-1 points the other way: omitting RT and ET at age ≥70 gave 5-yr LR 14.8%, with the lower CI bound above the 10% threshold.

## 👄 Head & Neck

I-124 PET-selected RAI de-escalation in DTC halves blood dose, but the primary endpoint is a surrogate and EFS has few events.

### CLERAD-PROBE · [[NCT01704586]]

> Mean blood dose 275 vs 521 mGy (p<0.001) with I-124 PET-guided risk-adapted vs broad adjuvant RAI in DTC; 5y EFS 96.6% vs 91.5%.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The decision-carrying detail is PET's upstaging: 11 of 78 (14.1%) pts with postop T1/T2 N0/Nx were N1 on I-124 PET and got RIT, so the de-escalation arm still treated occult nodal disease. Activity roughly halved (2.5 vs 5.7 GBq) with no EFS signal against it, informing omission in imaging-negative, low-risk DTC.

- 🔍 Prospective randomized bicentric trial, N=324 enrolled May 2015 to May 2021 (155 Arm 1, 169 Arm 2)
- 🔍 Adult DTC post-thyroidectomy; unifocal papillary microcarcinomas ≤10 mm excluded
- 🔍 Arm 1 RIT triggers (after I-124 PET staging)
  - Incomplete resection
  - Tumor >4 cm or extrathyroidal extent
  - LN metastases with age ≥45 years
  - Distant metastases or iodine-avid lesions on PET
- 🔍 Arm 2: RIT generally performed
- 📊 Outcomes, risk-adapted vs broad RIT (median f/u 64 mo)

  | Endpoint | Arm 1 risk-adapted | Arm 2 broad | Effect |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Mean blood dose (1° EP) | 275 mGy | 521 mGy | p<0.001 |
  | Administered activity | 2.5 GBq | 5.7 GBq | p<0.001 |
  | Progressive/recurrent disease | 5 cases | 14 cases | HR 0.38 (95% CI 0.14-1.05), p=0.06 |
  | 5y EFS | 96.6% | 91.5% | Log-rank p=0.05 |

- 📊 I-124 PET upstaged 11 of 78 (14.1%) postop T1/T2 N0/Nx pts to N1, triggering RIT
- ⚠️ Primary endpoint is blood dose, a surrogate for secondary malignancy risk, not oncologic outcome
- ⚠️ EFS is secondary with few events; not a powered non-inferiority comparison
- ⚠️ Sex imbalance: fewer males in Arm 1 (19% vs 34%, p=0.002)
- ⚠️ Arm 1 bundles PET staging with selective omission; can't separate PET's upstaging from de-escalation itself
- 🔗 Sits alongside ESTIMABL2 and IoN, which tested RAI omission in low-risk DTC; here selection is imaging-driven rather than pathology alone

**Sources:**
- 📄 **I-124 PET-guided risk-adapted versus broad adjuvant radioiodine therapy in differentiated thyroid carcinoma: Results from the randomized CLERAD-PROBE trial** — Kerstin Michalski. *ESMO Congress 2026* (2026-10). [article](https://cslide.ctimeetingtech.com/esmo2026/attendee/confcal/presentation/list)
  > ESMO Congress 2026 abstract 4210RO · Rapid oral: NETs and endocrine tumours · 2026-10-23 · presented by Kerstin Michalski (Wuerzburg, Germany) · [[NCT01704586]]
  > Background: The role of adjuvant radioiodine therapy (RIT) in differentiated thyroid carcinoma (DTC) remains controversial due to diverging international guidelines. The CLERAD-PROBE trial compares a risk-adapted with a broad RIT strategy regarding blood dose — as surrogate for secondary malignancy risk — and oncological outcomes.
  > Methods: CLERAD-PROBE is a prospective, randomized, bicentric study ([[NCT01704586]]) in adult DTC patients, excluding unifocal papillary microcarcinomas ≤10 mm. Following thyroidectomy, patients in Arm 1 underwent I-124 PET for staging; RIT was administered only if risk factors were present (incomplete resection, tumor >4 cm, extrathyroidal extent, lymph node metastases with age ≥45 years, distant metastases, or iodine-avid lesions on PET). In Arm 2, RIT was generally performed. The primary endpoint was mean blood dose at complete remission or 18 months; the secondary endpoint was event-free survival with a maximum follow-up of 10 years.
  > Results: 324 patients were enrolled from May 2015 to May 2021 (155 Arm 1, 169 Arm 2). Arms were well-matched for histology, age, and tumor stage; however, Arm 1 included fewer male patients (19% vs. 34%, p=0.002). Notably, I-124 PET upstaged 11 of 78 patients (14.1%) with postoperative T1/T2 and N0/Nx to N1, prompting RIT in these cases. Mean blood dose (275 vs. 521 mGy; p<0.001) and administered activity (2.5 vs. 5.7 GBq; p<0.001) were significantly lower in Arm 1. Median follow-up was 64 months. In Arm 1, risk of progressive or recurrent disease was lower (5 cases in Arm 1 vs. 14 in Arm 2; hazard ratio: 0.38; 95% CI: 0.14-1.05; p=0.06) and event-free survival higher (Log-rank test: p=0.05). Five-year event-free survival was 96.6% in Arm 1 vs. 91.5% in Arm 2.
  > Conclusions: CLERAD-PROBE demonstrates that a risk-adapted, PET-guided RIT strategy significantly reduces radiation exposure without evidence of inferior oncological outcomes. While the low number of events warrants cautious interpretation, the results support the oncological safety of RIT de-escalation and provide a rationale for a risk-adapted approach in appropriately selected DTC patients.
  > Clinical trial identification: [[NCT01704586]].

> [!question] Open questions
> - Does lower blood dose reduce second malignancies long term?
> - Is risk-adapted omission safe without I-124 PET staging?
> - Does EFS hold at the planned 10-year follow-up?

## 🌀 GI Lower

SCRT-based TNT plus PD-1 blockade beats LCCRT on EFS, but the pCR primary endpoint is not reported in source.

### UNION · [[NCT04928807]]

> 3-yr EFS 91.3% vs 77.4%, HR 0.33 (0.15-0.71), P=0.0029: SCRT → camrelizumab + CAPOX vs long-course CRT → CAPOX in LARC.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The control is long-course CRT, so the EFS gain (HR 0.33) comes with a switch to 25 Gy/5f. That makes SCRT the RT backbone for an IO-containing TNT, not a compromise. Because RT schedule and camrelizumab were changed together, the trial cannot say whether SCRT itself is what lets IO work in largely pMMR disease.

- 📊 Outcomes, SCRT + camrelizumab vs LCCRT (SOC)

  | Endpoint | SCRT + camrelizumab + CAPOX | LCCRT + CAPOX | Effect |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 3-yr EFS (investigator) | 91.3% (83.9-95.4) | 77.4% (67.9-84.4) | HR 0.33 (0.15-0.71), P=0.0029 |
  | G≥3 TRAE | 51.3% (59/115) | 48.2% (54/112) | n/a |
  | Median f/u | 36.8 mo (34.8-37.8) | 35.6 mo (34.3-36.9) | n/a |

- ⚠️ Primary endpoint was BIRC-assessed pCR rate; the pCR result is not reported in the source abstract
- 📊 EFS is a key secondary endpoint with prespecified α allocation; BIRC-assessed EFS also favored the experimental arm
- 🔍 Phase 3, 1:1, N=231 randomized (113 vs 118), July 2021 to March 2023
- 🔍 Regimens
  - Exp: SCRT 25 Gy/5f → 2 neoadjuvant + 6 adjuvant CAPOX, camrelizumab 200 mg d1 for up to 17 cycles
  - SOC: 50.4 Gy/28f with capecitabine 825 mg/m2 BID → same 2 + 6 CAPOX
  - Surgery type and timing not reported in source
- ⚠️ Two variables change at once (RT schedule and PD-1 inhibitor), so neither SCRT nor IO effect can be isolated
- ⚠️ Safety denominators (115 vs 112) differ from randomized counts (113 vs 118), so the as-treated populations differ from ITT
- ⚠️ OS not reported in source; MMR/MSI breakdown of the enrolled cohort not reported
- 🔗 SCRT-based TNT precedent: RAPIDO; LCCRT-based TNT: PRODIGE 23. UNION adds a PD-1 inhibitor to an SCRT backbone

- 📄 **Neoadjuvant short-course radiotherapy followed by camrelizumab and chemotherapy in locally advanced rectal cancer (LARC): The phase 3 UNION trial** — Zhen Yu Lin. *ESMO Congress 2026* (2026-10). [article](https://cslide.ctimeetingtech.com/esmo2026/attendee/confcal/presentation/list)
  > ESMO Congress 2026 abstract 3530O · Proffered paper: Gastrointestinal tumours, lower digestive · 2026-10-25 · presented by Zhen Yu Lin (Wuhan, China) · [[NCT04928807]]
  > Background: Despite the use of neoadjuvant chemoradiotherapy, recurrence and distant metastasis remain common in LARC. Effective immunotherapy-based strategies for the predominantly proficient mismatch repair/microsatellite-stable population remain limited. UNION is the first phase 3 trial to evaluate a neoadjuvant strategy incorporating radiotherapy, immunotherapy, and chemotherapy in LARC. Here we present the prespecified event-free survival (EFS) analysis.
  > Methods: Patients with cT3-4 or N+ rectal adenocarcinoma (tumor located ≤10 cm from the anal verge) were randomly assigned (1:1) to receive short-course radiotherapy (SCRT; 25 Gy/5f; experimental group) or long-course chemoradiotherapy (50.4 Gy/28f with concurrent capecitabine 825 mg/m2 twice daily; standard of care group) followed by 2 neoadjuvant cycles and 6 adjuvant cycles of capecitabine and oxaliplatin (CAPOX). The experimental group additionally received neoadjuvant and adjuvant camrelizumab (200 mg, day 1) since CAPOX treatment for up to 17 cycles. The primary endpoint was blinded independent review committee (BIRC)-assessed pathological complete response rate. Key secondary endpoints with prespecified α allocation were investigator-assessed EFS and overall survival.
  > Results: Between July 2021 and March 2023, 231 patients were randomized to the experimental group (n=113) and standard of care group (n=118). As of October 1, 2025, the median follow-up duration was 36.8 months (95% CI, 34.8-37.8) and 35.6 months (95% CI, 34.3-36.9), respectively. The 3-year EFS rate was significantly higher in the experimental group compared with standard of care (91.3% [95% CI, 83.9-95.4] vs 77.4% [95% CI, 67.9-84.4], hazard ratio=0.33 [95% CI, 0.15-0.71], P=0.0029). BIRC-assessed EFS consistently favored the experimental group. During the whole treatment period, the incidence of grade ≥3 treatment-related adverse events was 51.3% (59/115) and 48.2% (54/112), respectively.
  > Conclusions: UNION is the first phase 3 trial to demonstrate a significant improvement in EFS with an immunotherapy-based regimen in LARC. This neoadjuvant strategy represents a new standard of care.
  > Clinical trial identification: [[NCT04928807]].

## 🎀 Breast

TOP-1 tests omitting both RT and ET in low-risk elderly ER+ BC; 5-yr LR exceeds the bound the design was meant to exclude.

### TOP-1 (BOOG 2016-01)

> 5-yr local recurrence 14.8% (95% CI 12.3-17.5) after BCS alone without RT or ET in women ≥70 with low-risk ER+ HER2- BC.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The trial removed endocrine therapy as well as RT, which earlier omission trials never did, and LR reached 14.8%, above the 10% ceiling it was powered to exclude. Salvage was mostly feasible (86 repeat BCS), but 70 pts received RT at recurrence anyway. So in ET-untreated older women, omitting RT often postpones it rather than avoiding it.

- 📊 5-yr LR 14.8% (95% CI 12.3-17.5): 123 invasive ipsilateral events in 792 pts
- ⚠️ Lower CI bound 12.3% exceeds the 10% 5-yr LR the study was powered to exclude
- 🔍 Prospective, multicenter, observational, single-arm; N=792 (800 planned), accrual Jan 2017 to Sep 2021
- 🔍 Women ≥70, ER ≥50%, HER2-negative, ≤1cm grade 1/2 or ≤2cm grade 1, free margins
- 💊 No ET by design: Dutch guidelines do not advise ET in T1 low-grade early-stage BC
- 🔍 Median age 73.6y, median f/u 5.1y (IQR 4.9-5.2); cumulative incidence with death as competing event
- 📊 Outcomes at median 5.1y f/u (single arm)

  | Endpoint | Result |
  |---|---|
  | Local recurrence (invasive ipsilateral) | 123 pts · 5-yr 14.8% (95% CI 12.3-17.5) |
  | Contralateral BC | 32 (4.0%) |
  | Deaths, any cause | 81 (10.2%) |
  | Deaths, BC-related | 2 (2.5% of deaths) |

- 📊 Management of the 123 local recurrences
  - Surgery: 114 (92.7%), of whom 86 (75.4%) repeat BCS
  - RT at salvage: 70 (56.7%)
  - ET at salvage: 43 (35.0%)
- 🔗 Design assumption: 1.3% (99% CI 0.6-2.5%) 5-yr LR with RT from national registry; upper limit tripled to 7.5% without RT
- ⚠️ Comparator is a historical registry, not a concurrent RT arm; RT-attributable effect not directly estimable
- ⚠️ RT and ET both omitted: cannot apportion the excess LR between the two

[article](https://cslide.ctimeetingtech.com/esmo2026/attendee/confcal/presentation/list)
  > ESMO Congress 2026 abstract 545RO · Rapid oral: Breast cancer, early stage · 2026-10-23 · presented by Lotte Bouwman (Leiden, Netherlands)
  > Background: Adjuvant radiotherapy (RT) following breast-conserving surgery (BCS) is standard of care in women with early-stage estrogen-receptor positive (ER+) breast cancer (BC). Previous studies on RT omission involved older patients treated with BCS and endocrine therapy (ET), showing low local recurrence (LR) risks. According to Dutch guidelines, ET is not advised in T1, low-grade early-stage BC. This study assessed 5-year LR risk after BCS without RT, in older patients with ER+ BC without ET.
  > Methods: The prospective, multicenter, observational, single-arm TOP-1 study included women aged ≥70 years with low-risk BC (ER+ ≥50%, HER2-negative, ≤1cm and grade 1/2, or ≤2cm grade 1 with free surgical margins). Primary endpoint was 5-year LR risk, defined as a histologically confirmed invasive ipsilateral event. Secondary endpoints included contralateral BC, overall and BC specific survival. Cumulative incidence analysis was performed with death as competing event. With a 1.3% (99%CI 0.6-2.5%; national registry) 5-year LR risk with RT, and the upper CI limit tripled without RT (7.5%), 800 patients (power 80%) were needed to exclude a 10% 5-year LR risk, acceptable to patient advocates.
  > Results: Between January 2017 and September 2021, 792 patients were included. Median age was 73.6 years (IQI 71.9-75.5). Median follow-up was 5.1 years (IQR 4.9-5.2). In total, 123 patients developed a LR, resulting in a 5-year LR risk of 14.8% (95%CI 12.3-17.5). Treatment of LR was surgical in 114 (92.7%) patients, of whom 86 (75.4%) BCS, 70 (56.7%) patients received RT and 43 (35.0%) ET. Contralateral BC was diagnosed in 32 (4.0%) patients. Death occurred in 81 (10.2%) patients, of whom 2 (2.5%) BC related.
  > Conclusions: Omission of adjuvant RT resulted in a 5-year LR risk of 14.8% after BCS without ET, in women aged ≥70 with low-grade T1, ER+ BC. Although the LR risk exceeded prior estimates, BCS was possible in most patients and BC specific survival was excellent. Therefore, in a shared decision setting, RT omission is an option for a subgroup of older patients. Follow-up is needed to assess longer-term survival and other secondary endpoints.

## 🍽️ GI Upper

Randomized evidence that SBRT adds locoregional control to modern chemo in high-risk pancreas, with OS and PFS gains as secondary endpoints.

### AGITG MASTERPLAN

> 12-mo locoregional recurrence 20.2% vs 52.0% (P=0.022) adding SBRT 40 Gy/5fx to chemo in high-risk operable, borderline, or locally advanced pancreatic cancer.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The LRR benefit held in the potentially operable stratum (19.7% vs 52.9%, P=0.031) but not the inoperable one (P=0.16, n=26), so the strongest RT read is neoadjuvant SBRT 40 Gy/5fx after 3 mo of modern chemo in borderline or high-risk operable disease. That benefit came despite only 80% of the SBRT arm starting RT.

- 🔍 Randomized 2:1, chemo + SBRT vs chemo alone; 75 enrolled, 74 eligible, 14 centers
- 🔍 Phase not stated in source (trade-press report of ESMO 2026 Rapid Oral)
- 🔍 Two resectability strata
  - POPC (n=48): borderline resectable or operable with ≥1 high-risk feature (>4 cm, extrapancreatic extension, N+)
  - IPC (n=26): locally advanced or medically inoperable
- 💊 3 mo mFOLFIRINOX or gem/nab-paclitaxel (clinician choice), then surgical reassessment
- 💊 Resected → 3 mo adjuvant chemo; unresectable → 3 further mo systemic chemo
- 🔍 SBRT 40 Gy in 5 fx, fiducial guidance, real-time RT QA
- 📊 Outcomes by arm

  | Endpoint | Chemo + SBRT | Chemo alone | P |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 12-mo LRR, all | 20.2% | 52.0% | 0.022 |
  | 12-mo LRR, POPC | 19.7% | 52.9% | 0.031 |
  | 12-mo LRR, IPC | 21.4% | 50.0% | 0.16 |
  | mPFS | 11.9 mo | 6.3 mo | 0.002 |
  | mOS | 22.0 mo | 14.0 mo | 0.006 |
  | Resection (POPC) | 21/31 (68%) | 10/17 (59%) | 0.54 |
  | R0 (>1 mm), resected | 62% | 40% | 0.44 |
  | G3+ toxicity | 70.2% | 64.0% | 0.27 |

- ⚠️ Only 39 of 49 (80%) assigned to SBRT actually started it; ITT read dilutes RT effect
- ⚠️ Small N with mixed operable/inoperable pts; OS and PFS are secondary endpoints
- ⚠️ IPC stratum (n=26) underpowered; LRR difference there not significant
- ⚠️ G3+ toxicity high in both arms; RT-attributable late GI toxicity not reported in source
