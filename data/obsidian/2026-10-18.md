# 2026-10-18

**PMRT nomogram, T1-2N1: high-risk tier (score >18) 5y LRR 2.2% vs 14.9% with PMRT, HR 0.164; no significant LRR difference in low or intermediate tiers.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> A breast-only day of retrospective single-institution series on regional RT decisions. In T1-2N1, risk-stratified selection puts the PMRT LRR benefit in the high-risk tier. For isolated chest wall LRR, whole-chest-wall salvage fields beat small fields (5-yr freedom from re-recurrence 75% vs 36%). Nothing here is randomized, and selection and era confounding limit every read.

## 🎀 Breast

Five retrospective series bearing on PMRT selection, SCV field omission after positive SLN, and RT salvage of chest wall recurrence.

### Close or positive margin after mastectomy and chest wall irradiation

> 8yr cumulative chest wall recurrence 28% age ≤50 vs 0% age >50 (p=0.04) with close/positive margin, T ≤5cm, 0-3 nodes, no PMRT.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Margin distance itself did not separate risk (≤2mm 24% vs 2.1-6mm 7%, p=0.36); age did, with 28% vs 0% chest wall failure at 8yr. For the PMRT decision in an otherwise low-risk mastectomy pt, this argues for weighting age ≤50 over margin proximity alone, and supports omission in older women with a close margin.

- 📊 8yr cumulative chest wall recurrence, all 34 pts: 18% (5yr 9%)
- 📊 Age ≤50 vs >50 at 8yr: 28% vs 0%, p=0.04
- 📊 Crude chest wall recurrence by age band
  - Age ≤40: 27% (3 of 11)
  - Age 41-50: 18% (2 of 11)
  - Age 51+: 0 (0 of 12)
- 📐 Margin ≤2mm vs 2.1-6mm at 8yr: 24% vs 7%, p=0.36 (not significant)
- 📐 T2 vs T1: 24% vs 7%, p=0.98
- 🔍 Retrospective single-institution series (Fox Chase); 34 of 789 mastectomy pts met criteria
- 🔍 Margin distribution (microscopic, deep margin)
  - Positive: 2 pts
  - ≤2mm: 17 pts
  - >2 to ≤4mm: 7 pts
  - >4 to ≤6mm: 8 pts
- 💊 Chemo ± tamoxifen in 67%; tamoxifen alone 21% (CMF, CAF, AC era)
- 📊 5 of 5 chest wall failures: 1 isolated first failure; 3 associated with distant mets
- ⚠️ Age confounded with systemic therapy: chemo in 91% of ≤50 vs 25% of >50
- ⚠️ 5 events total; subgroup p=0.04 rests on 5 vs 0 failures
- 🔗 Ahlborn and Mentzer series (cited in source) found no significant margin effect, short f/u, crude rates only

**Sources:**

### Isolated local-regional recurrence after mastectomy: radiotherapeutic management

> 5-yr freedom from chest wall re-recurrence 75% with whole-chest-wall fields vs 36% with small fields (p=0.0001) in 224 pts irradiated for isolated LRR.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Field size carries the strongest signal: 67% of small-field failures landed outside the portal but within the chest wall, supporting whole-CW coverage. Elective SCV RT cut SCV failure 16% to 6%, while elective axillary RT raised arm edema 11% vs 1%, so cover the SCV fossa and leave the uninvolved axilla alone.

- 🔍 Single-institution retrospective, Mallinckrodt, 1964-1986, N=224 treated with curative intent
- 🔍 Excluded prior postmastectomy RT, failed outside salvage, and clinical/radiographic distant mets
- 🔍 Median f/u 46 mo (range 24-241); survival and failure timed from LRR
- 📊 Whole cohort 5/10-yr OS 43%/26%; DFS 25%/15%; 5-yr LRC 57%
- 📊 Outcomes at 5 yr by site of first recurrence

  | Site | 5-yr OS | 5-yr RFS | 5-yr LRC |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Chest wall alone | 50% | 35% | 63% |
  | Nodal alone | 36% | 15% | 45% |
  | Chest wall + nodal | 19% | 5% | 27% |
  | p | 0.0002 | 0.0001 | 0.0084 |

- 📊 Solitary CW recurrence: freedom from CW re-recurrence by field size

  | Field | 5-yr | 10-yr |
  |---|---|---|
  | Large (entire CW) | 75% | 63% |
  | Small (1-2 cm margin) | 36% | 18% |
  | p | 0.0001 | 0.0001 |

- 📊 Small-field failures: 33% in-field or marginal, 67% outside the portal but within the CW
- 📊 Failure in an uninvolved site, elective RT vs none

  | Uninvolved site | Elective RT | No RT | p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Supraclavicular fossa | 6% | 16% | 0.0489 |
  | Chest wall (any dose) | 17% | 27% | .32 |
  | Chest wall (≥5000 cGy) | 12% | 27% | .20 |

- 📊 Elective SCV RT: 5-yr survival 53% vs 35% (p=0.03)
- 📐 Dose response by recurrence size
  - Completely excised: control adequate across 4500-7000 cGy
  - <3 cm: 100% controlled at ≥6000 cGy vs 76% at lower doses
  - Larger, multiple or diffuse: no dose response; about 50% controlled even to 7000 cGy
- 🔍 Technique: tangents 1.5-6.0 MV to 4500-5000 cGy, 6-12 MeV electron boost; ~90% at 180-200 cGy/d
- ⚠️ RT-attributable late toxicity
  - Grade 2 in 9%, grade 3 in 1%, no fatal toxicity
  - Arm edema 8/75 (11%) with elective axillary RT vs 1/109 (1%) without
  - Pneumonitis 2% (2/92) tangents alone vs 8% (3/37) tangents + parasternal photons
  - Bone necrosis/fracture in 4 pts, all at >5400 cGy
- ⚠️ Field size confounded with era: small fields were the early policy, so staging and systemic therapy also changed
- ⚠️ Small-field group only 14 pts; dose chosen by physician preference, not protocol
- ⚠️ Elective CW p-value reported as .32 in abstract and .33 in discussion
- 🔗 Toonkel series cited in source: comprehensive nodal RT 54% vs 27% 5-yr survival with CW-only RT (p=0.029)

  - 📎 [[papers/breast/1990-halverson-isolated-local-regional-recurrence-breast-cancer.pdf|Isolated local-regional recurrence after mastectomy: radiotherapeutic management (full text)]]

### Nomogram for four or more involved nodes in SLN-positive breast cancer

> AUC 0.83 in derivation and 0.81 in external validation for predicting ≥4 involved nodes after 1-3 positive SLNs, using 7 pathologic factors.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The RT question is field design. With a <5% predicted ≥4-node risk as the threshold, 34% of the derivation and 37% of the validation cohort were candidates to drop the SCV/apex field. The source ties adding that field to lymphedema rising from 2% to 8% to approximately 9% to 24%. The case for omission rests on high tangents covering most of levels I-II.

- 🔍 Retrospective review at 2 academic centers (MGH n=248, BWH n=154), N=402
- 🔍 Eligibility: clinically node-negative invasive breast cancer, 1-3 H&E-positive SLNs, completion ALND (≥6 nodes)
- 🔍 No neoadjuvant systemic therapy; keratin-only SLN deposits excluded
- 📊 ≥4 involved nodes in 87 pts per the abstract; the results text says 81
- 📊 Nomogram score (Fig 2), with likelihood read off the total-score scale
  - Primary tumor size (cm) x 2.6
  - + number of positive SLNs x 9.2
  - +11 each for LVSI, lobular histology, extranodal extension
  - +16 if the largest SLN metastasis is a macrometastasis (>2 mm)
  - -8.6 if ≥1 SLN negative; then -58
- 📐 Derivation AUC 0.83 (n=344 with all 7 factors known)
- 📐 External validation AUC 0.81 (n=206, Sibley community hospital)
- 📐 At a 5% cutoff: NPV 94.7%, sensitivity 97.5%; 118 pts classified ≤5%
- ⚠️ The stated counts (113/118 and 54/57) don't clearly match the reported NPV and sensitivity
- 📊 <5% predicted risk: 34% of derivation and 37% of validation pts could skip the SCV/apex field
- ⚠️ The nomogram underestimated observed ≥4-node cases in the validation set
- ⚠️ Selection bias: only dissected pts included; 73 trial pts given nodal RT without ALND were excluded
- 🔗 Chagpar et al built a similar model (1,133 SLN+ pts) without LVSI or SLN metastasis size
- ⚠️ No regional-failure outcomes: tangent-only RT in the low-risk group was not tested

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Nomogram for the Prediction of Having Four or More Involved Nodes for Sentinel Lymph Node–Positive Breast Cancer** — Katz, Angela; Smith, Barbara L.; Golshan, Mehra et al.. *Journal of Clinical Oncology* (2008-05). [doi:10.1200/jco.2007.11.9479](https://doi.org/10.1200/jco.2007.11.9479)
  > Purpose The standard of care for patients with a positive (+) sentinel lymph node (SLN) is axillary dissection; however, for various reasons, some SLN+ patients do not undergo dissection. The purpose of this study was to define possible predictors of having four or more involved nodes to provide information for clinicians and patients making decisions about adjuvant chemotherapy and radiation. Patients and Methods We reviewed the records of 402 patients with invasive breast cancer and one to three involved SLNs who underwent completion axillary dissection at two academic cancer centers. None of these patients received neoadjuvant chemotherapy. Factors associated with having four or more involved axillary nodes (SLNs and non-SLNs) were evaluated by univariate and multivariate logistic regression analysis. Results Eighty-seven patients had four or more positive nodes. On multivariate analysis, having four or more SLNs was associated with tumor histology, primary tumor size, lymphovascular space invasion, extranodal extension, the number of involved SLNs, the number of uninvolved SLNs, and the size of the largest SLN metastasis. A nomogram to predict the probability of having four or more nodes based on patients’ pathologic data was developed from the multivariate logistic regression model. A separate previously published data set of 206 SLN+ patients treated at a community hospital in another city was used to validate this model. Conclusion Patients with a low probability of having four or more nodes can be identified from known pathologic features. The nomogram developed will be helpful to clinicians making adjuvant treatment recommendations.
  - 📎 [[papers/breast/2008-katz-nomogram-prediction-having-four-more-involved-nodes.pdf|Nomogram for four or more involved nodes in SLN-positive breast cancer (full text)]]

### Nomogram for locoregional recurrence in T1-2N1 breast cancer (PMRT decision-making)

> High-risk nomogram tier (score >18): PMRT 5y LRR 2.2% vs 14.9%, HR 0.164; no LRR association in low or intermediate tiers.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The omission case is the low-risk tier: 5y LRR 0.4% without PMRT and 0/160 vs 2/252 events, so any RT gain there is bounded near zero. Benefit concentrates above score 18 (14.9% → 2.2% LRR, 7.0% BCM reduction) with chest wall plus supraclavicular/infraclavicular fields at 46-50 Gy, i.e. standard comprehensive PMRT.

- 🔍 Retrospective single-institution cohort, N=1141 T1-2N1, mastectomy + ALND, 2001-2012
- 🔍 518 (45.4%) received PMRT; median f/u 74.9 mo
- 🔍 Nomogram built in 623 unirradiated pts from 5 factors: tumor size, positive nodes, ER, grade, LVI
- 📐 Bootstrap C index 0.73 (95% CI 0.68-0.78); calibration internal only
- 📊 Decision-tree cutoffs at scores 10 and 18: crude LRR without PMRT 0.8%, 6.1%, 15.9% by tier
- 📊 PMRT vs no PMRT by nomogram risk tier (5y cumulative incidence, multivariate HR)

  | Group | LRR | DR | BCM |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | All pts (n=1141) | 1.6% vs 6.0%, HR 0.248 (0.121-0.509), P<.001 | HR 0.608 (0.425-0.871), P=.006 | P=.130 |
  | Low risk (n=412) | P=.930 | P=.220 | P=.390 |
  | Intermediate risk (n=385) | P=.240 | P=.500 | P=.430 |
  | High risk (n=344) | 2.2% vs 14.9%, HR 0.164 (0.067-0.403), P<.001 | 8.4% vs 17.6%, HR 0.491 (0.288-0.837), P=.009 | 6.3% vs 13.3%, HR 0.371 (0.191-0.719), P=.003 |

- 📊 LRR events, PMRT vs no PMRT (Fig 3)
  - Low risk: 0/160 vs 2/252
  - Intermediate risk: 6/171 vs 13/214
  - High risk: 9/187 vs 25/157
- 🔍 RT: 46-50 Gy/23-25 fx, CT-planned; chest wall + infraclavicular + supraclavicular
- 🔍 IMN added for central tumors; axilla added for ECE
- ⚠️ PMRT not randomized: recipients were younger, earlier era, larger tumors, more nodes, higher grade
- ⚠️ Risk score and cutoffs derived and tested in the same cohort; no external validation
- ⚠️ Low-risk null rests on 2 LRR events; absence of benefit is underpowered, not demonstrated
- ⚠️ Trastuzumab in only 26.2% of HER2+; node size and margin width not captured
- 🔗 vs EBCTCG 1-3 node pts: control 10-yr LRR 21%, far above this cohort's 6.0% 5-yr unirradiated LRR

### Chest wall recurrence outcome by initial stage

> 5-yr OS after isolated CWR 79.9% vs 41.9% vs 29.1% (T1-T2N0 vs T1-T2 1-3 N+ vs T3-T4/>4 N+); 1-3 N+ salvage as poor as advanced stage.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The RT read has two parts. Omitting RT for the CWR carried OS OR 4.524 on MVA, and pts with T1-T2 1-3 N+ disease salvaged no better than T3-T4/>4 N+ (5-yr OS 41.9% vs 29.1%). The PMRT decision in 1-3 N+ therefore rests on preventing CWR, not on rescuing it. Salvage dose and field are not reported.

- 🔍 Single-institution retrospective (MD Anderson), CWR evaluated 1988-1998, N=96
- 🔍 All pts post-mastectomy, no prior PMRT, isolated CWR without distant mets
- 🔍 Of 155 identified: 25 excluded for distant mets at CWR, 34 for RT to the primary
- 🔍 Groups: T1-T2N0 (n=41) · T1-T2 1-3 N+ (n=20) · T3-T4 or >4 N+ (n=35)
- 🔍 Median f/u 41.4 mo from CWR (range 3.3-166.8)
- 🔍 Baseline and salvage treatment by group (Table 1)

  | Feature | T1-T2N0 | T1-T2 1-3 N+ | T3-T4/>4 N+ | p |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | LVI present | 8 (19.5%) | 8 (40.0%) | 22 (62.9%) | <0.001 |
  | Neoadjuvant therapy | 2 (4.9%) | 0 (0%) | 14 (40.0%) | <0.001 |
  | Adjuvant systemic therapy | 15 (36.6%) | 17 (85.0%) | 27 (77.1%) | <0.001 |
  | XRT for CWR | 29 (70.7%) | 15 (75.0%) | 24 (68.6%) | 0.879 |
  | Surgery for CWR | 29 (70.7%) | 9 (45.0%) | 16 (45.7%) | 0.045 |

- 📊 5-yr OS after CWR 79.9% vs 41.9% vs 29.1%, p=0.0001
- 📊 5-yr DMFS 75.2% vs 33.6% vs 25.9%
- 📐 Cox MVA for OS after CWR

  | Factor | OR (95% CI) | p |
  |---|---|---|
  | Group 1 vs 2 | 4.871 (1.756-13.510) | 0.002 |
  | Group 1 vs 3 | 7.521 (2.761-20.482) | <0.001 |
  | Group 2 vs 3 | 1.544 (0.645-3.699) | 0.330 |
  | No XRT for CWR | 4.524 (2.064-9.918) | <0.001 |
  | Interval to CWR ≤24 mo | 4.636 (2.106-10.206) | <0.001 |

- 📊 No XRT for CWR also predicted worse DMFS on MVA: OR 4.024 (1.825-8.874), p=0.001
- 📊 Second CWR 24.4% vs 15.0% vs 22.9%, p=0.680
- 🔗 Source cites PMRT trials: ~30% → ~10% 10-yr LRR, 9% absolute OS gain
- ⚠️ Reported as 'OR' from Cox models; these are hazard ratios mislabeled
- ⚠️ Group 3 5-yr OS is 29.1% in abstract/text but 21.8% in Table 2, which lists 29.1% as 10-yr
- ⚠️ Univariate pairwise DMFS ORs repeat the OS values exactly (3.516, 5.326, 1.515); likely copy error
- ⚠️ Group 2 n=20: null 2 vs 3 contrast is underpowered, not evidence of equivalence
- ⚠️ Group 3 omitted PMRT despite SOC (refusal, physician bias, often unknown): selected cohort
- ⚠️ Salvage RT dose, fields and technique not reported
