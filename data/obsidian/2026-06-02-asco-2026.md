# 2026-06-02 — ASCO Annual Meeting 2026

**mRCAT-III: pCR 61.0% vs 28.6% (P<0.001) omitting elective nodal RT plus tislelizumab in pMMR/MSS LARC, but two variables move at once.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Two randomised trials pull RT volume and RT addition in opposite directions. Lower GI carries the day: mRCAT-III doubles pCR with tumor-bed-only 5x5 plus PD-1, though nodal omission and IO are confounded and no recurrence data exist. Thoracic is null: consolidative 30Gy/10fx on chemo-IO gave mOS 10.0 vs 11.8mo, HR 1.14.

## 🌀 GI Lower

Nodal target omission tested only against pCR, with PD-1 blockade added in the same arm.

### mRCAT-III · [[NCT06507371]]

> pCR 61.0% vs 28.6% (P<0.0001) when elective nodal RT was omitted and tislelizumab added in pMMR/MSS LARC.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The RT variable here is target volume, not dose: both arms got 5Gy x 5d, and the experimental target was tumor bed alone with tumor-draining nodes deliberately spared. That inverts the usual de-escalation logic (smaller field proposed to IMPROVE efficacy by preserving nodal immunity), but tislelizumab moves with it, so the pCR gain cannot be assigned to the field change.

![mRCAT-III](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HJ13rE-XwAERv45.jpg)

| Endpoint (ITT) | Experimental (N=77) | Control (N=77) | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| pCR rate | 61.0 (47/77) | 28.6 (22/77) | <0.001 |
| MPR rate | 77.9 (60/77) | 50.6 (39/77) | <0.001 |

![Study design: 5Gy x 5d both arms, n=77 per group, NCT06507371](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HJ13qZ3W8AII7TS.jpg)
*Study design: 5Gy x 5d both arms, n=77 per group, NCT06507371*

- 🔍 Phase 3 open-label RCT, 17 Chinese hospitals, N=154 (77/77), 1:1, stratified by cN0 vs cN+
- 📐 RT both arms 5Gy x 5d; experimental target = tumor bed ONLY, no tumor-draining lymph nodes
- 🔍 Eligibility: rectal adenoCa ≤10cm from anal verge, cT3-4 N0/+ M0, MSS/pMMR, ECOG 0-1, age 18-75, no positive lateral node
- 💊 Experimental adds tislelizumab 200mg d1 to CAPOX (oxaliplatin 130mg/m² d1, capecitabine 1000mg/m² d1-14); control CAPOX alone
- 📊 1° EP pCR (ITT) by blinded independent central review: 61.0% (47/77) vs 28.6% (22/77), P<0.001
- 📊 MPR (TRG0+1): 77.9% (60/77) vs 50.6% (39/77), P<0.001
- ⚠️ Two variables move at once: nodal target omission AND PD-1 blockade. The design cannot attribute the gain to either
- ⚠️ Source reports reduced severe GI AEs in the experimental arm but gives no grade-specific rates
- ⚠️ No nodal recurrence, EFS, or OS data in source; omitting elective nodal RT is judged on pCR alone
- ⚠️ Open-label, though pathologic response was centrally read, which blunts the main bias risk for the 1° EP

## 🫁 Thoracic / Lung

CREST-era consolidative TRT fails to reproduce its benefit once durvalumab is in the backbone.

### LBA8005: Concurrent Thoracic RT + Chemoimmunotherapy in ES-SCLC

> mOS 10.0 vs 11.8 mo, HR 1.14 (0.84-1.56), p=0.40: adding 30Gy/10fx consolidative TRT to chemo-IO did not improve survival.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The consolidative-TRT habit carried over from CREST does not survive an IO backbone: OS HR 1.14, and both landmark subgroups (completers HR 1.02, no brain/liver mets HR 1.10) sit on or above 1.0, so there is no population here in which 30Gy/10fx earned its place. Source gives no local control or toxicity numbers, so the mechanism stays open.

| Arm | Median OS | 95% CI | HR (95% CI), p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chemoimmunotherapy plus TRT | 10.0 months | 8.3 - 11.7 | 1.14 (0.84 - 1.56), p=0.40 |
| Chemoimmunotherapy | 11.8 months | 10.0 - 13.6 | reference |

![LBA8005: Concurrent Thoracic RT + Chemoimmunotherapy in ES-SCLC](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HJyHFfxXMAAZ21N.jpg)

| Arm | Median PFS | 95% CI | HR (95% CI), p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chemoimmunotherapy plus TRT | 5.1 months | 4.7 - 5.4 | 1.10 (0.84 - 1.45), p=0.49 |
| Chemoimmunotherapy | 5.0 months | 4.6 - 5.4 | reference |

![Study design. TRT 30 Gy/10 fractions starting day 21-28. Durvalumab 1500 mg, carboplatin AUC=5, etoposide 100 mg/m BSA. PCI 25-30 Gy to responders.](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HJyHFUsXAAAGmvF.jpg)
*Study design. TRT 30 Gy/10 fractions starting day 21-28. Durvalumab 1500 mg, carboplatin AUC=5, etoposide 100 mg/m BSA. PCI 25-30 Gy to responders.*

- 🔍 Randomized phase III, N=228 (TRT 115, chemo-IO alone 113), stratified by liver and brain mets
- ☢️ TRT 30 Gy/10 fx starting day 21-28, i.e. concurrent with cycles 2-4 of durvalumab/platinum/etoposide
- 💊 Backbone identical both arms: durvalumab 1500mg + carboplatin AUC5 + etoposide x4, then durvalumab 1500mg Q4W maintenance
- 🔍 PCI 25-30 Gy allowed in responders per local routine, and WBRT 20-30 Gy permitted for brain mets, in BOTH arms
- 📊 PFS also flat: 5.1 vs 5.0 mo, HR 1.10 (0.84-1.45), p=0.49
- 📊 No subgroup rescued the RT arm (both prespecified-looking landmark analyses numerically favored control)

  | Population | TRT median OS | Control median OS | HR (95% CI), p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Completed all 4 chemo-IO courses | 11.9 mo (9.7-14.1) | 12.1 mo (9.4-14.8) | 1.02 (0.72-1.44), p=0.92 |
  | No brain or liver mets | 11.9 mo (6.2-17.7) | 13.2 mo (10.4-16.1) | 1.10 (0.65-1.87), p=0.72 |

- 🔗 Contrasts with CREST (Slotman 2015), where 30 Gy/10 fx consolidative TRT after chemo alone improved 2yr OS; that trial had no IO backbone
- ⚠️ Eligibility allowed stage III ineligible for curative CRT, so a fraction of enrollees carry a more locally-driven disease burden than pure stage IV
- ⚠️ Toxicity listed as a key secondary but no AE numbers in source; whether the null OS reflects added harm offsetting local benefit is not answerable from these slides
- ⚠️ No local control or pattern-of-failure data in source, so the mechanism of the null (no local benefit vs local benefit erased by toxicity) is unresolved

## 🎀 Breast

Education-session sequencing guidance for concurrent systemic agents with breast/CW + RNI, PK-driven, mostly retrospective.

### RT + Systemic Therapy: What to Continue vs Hold (Speers)

> ASCO 2026 education session slides triaging concurrent systemic agents with breast/CW + RNI into continue, caution, and hold/sequence.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The operational content is the PK column: talazoparib 5t½ 19 days and pembrolizumab 5t½ ~110 days mean a brief hold buys nothing, so the mitigation is field size, lung dose and monitoring rather than a washout. Field size, not agent class, gates the CDK4/6i and olaparib calls.

*T-DXd ILD ~12% at 5.4 mg/kg, fatal ~0.9%. DESTINY-Breast05 ILD 9.6% T-DXd vs 1.6% T-DM1. RT timing 10.7% seq vs 9.6% concurrent. COMBART 40 pts, acute tox 20%.*

*KEYNOTE-522 post-hoc: 1,174 pts, 715 received RT. Pembrolizumab t½ 22 days, 5t½ ~110 days. P-RAD preop RT 0 / 9 / 24 Gy.*

- 🔍 ASCO 2026 educational presentation (Speers), not a trial; adapted from Wong, Speers, Schaverien, ASCO Educational Book 2026, Table 5
- 📊 Traffic-light triage for concurrent systemic therapy with breast/CW + RNI

  | Agent | Call | Basis given |
  |---|---|---|
  | Endocrine therapy | Continue | Minimal radiosensitization |
  | Trastuzumab ± pertuzumab | Continue | Generally safe; modern heart-sparing |
  | T-DM1 | Continue | Dermatitis + pneumonitis vigilance; caution with CNS SRS |
  | Pembrolizumab | Continue | Pneumonitis vigilance + immune-toxicity workflows |
  | T-DXd | Caution | Sequence/hold for high lung-dose or active pulmonary disease |
  | CDK4/6 inhibitors | Caution | Usually hold for large fields; concurrent only in protocol |
  | Olaparib | Caution | Reasonable with limited fields; protocol settings only |
  | Cytotoxic chemo (anthracycline/taxane/platinum) | Hold / sequence | Sequence, do not give concurrently |
  | Capecitabine | Hold / sequence | Adjuvant paradigm non-concurrent |
  | Veliparib / talazoparib | Hold / sequence | Veliparib + RT severe acute/late tox |
  | mTOR / PI3K agents | Hold / sequence | Mucosal, skin, metabolic, inflammatory risk; ESMO-ESTRO advises caution |

- 💊 HER2 mAbs framed as settled: trastuzumab + pertuzumab concurrent with breast/CW + nodal RT is standard practice
- 💊 T-DM1 (t½ 4d, 5t½ 20d) continue during locoregional RT as on KATHERINE or ATEMPT
- ⚠️ T-DM1 caution list: large lung volumes, IMN coverage, bolus, reconstruction, CNS SRS
- 📊 T-DXd (t½ 6d, 5t½ 30d) ILD signals cited
  - PI at 5.4 mg/kg: ILD ~12%, fatal ~0.9%
  - DESTINY-Breast05: ILD 9.6% T-DXd vs 1.6% T-DM1
  - RT timing in T-DXd arm: 10.7% sequential vs 9.6% concurrent, no effect
  - COMBART: 40 pts concurrent RT/SRT, acute tox 20%
- 📊 KEYNOTE-522 post-hoc: 1,174 pts, 715 received RT; numerically fewer G3-5 and immune AEs concurrent vs sequential
- ⚠️ Highest AE rate was pembro-alone/no-RT arm, raising the question of RT immunosuppression; post-hoc, unadjusted
- 📐 Pembrolizumab t½ 22d, 5t½ ~110d, so holding by PK is rarely practical; mitigation shifts to volume, dose constraints, monitoring
- 🔍 P-RAD (TBCRC-053): preoperative 0 / 9 / 24 Gy + pembro in HR+/HER2-; 24 Gy + pembro increased T-cell infiltration at 2 wks with induced tertiary lymphoid structures
- 📐 PARPi PK drives the sequencing call
  - Olaparib t½ 15 hr, 5t½ 3 days: RT to complete 2-12 wks before olaparib
  - Veliparib t½ 5.5 hr, 5t½ 1 day: avoid concurrency, dose-limiting moist desquamation and fibrosis (TBCRC 024)
  - Talazoparib t½ 90 hr, 5t½ 19 days: long PK tail complicates brief holds, favors sequential
- ⚠️ RadioPARP suggests concurrent olaparib safety, but the early-stage paradigm on OlympiA remains sequential
- 💊 CDK4/6i: palbociclib t½ 28.8 hr (5t½ 6 days), abemaciclib/ribociclib t½ 24-55 hr (5t½ 5-11 days); marrow/GI exposure rises with field size
- ⚠️ CDK4/6i evidence is mostly retrospective; one prospective trial cited ([[NCT05996107]])
- 🔗 Slide's stated default outside protocol: PK-based washout → RT → resume
