# 2026-09-30 — ASTRO Annual Meeting 2026

**RTOG 3506 (STEEL): enzalutamide added to SRT + 24mo ADT in high-risk post-RP BCR, PFS HR 0.62 (80% CI 0.42-0.91, one-sided p=0.052); 2y BF 11% vs 19%.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Prostate RT carried the day. STEEL is a randomized phase II screening signal for adding an ARSI to salvage RT + 24mo ADT in high-risk post-RP BCR. SRT dose and volume were not reported. REVELUTION's post hoc PROs favor relugolix over leuprolide with RT at 12 mo (IPSS 5.66 vs 9.22).

## 🌰 Prostate

Both readouts change the hormonal partner given with RT, not the RT itself: an ARSI added to salvage RT + ADT, and oral vs injectable ADT alongside definitive RT.

### REVELUTION (relugolix vs leuprolide + RT, PROs)

> At 12 mo, relugolix vs leuprolide with RT: IPSS 5.66 vs 9.22 (p=0.039), EPIC-CP total 12.25 vs 17.25 (p=0.02); post hoc.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The PRO gap tracks with faster testosterone recovery on relugolix (mean 199.7 vs 142.0 ng/dL at 12 mo, P=.010), which plausibly explains better urinary and EPIC-CP total scores. For RT + short-course ADT, that recovery kinetic is the relevant variable when choosing GnRH agent; whether it costs anything in biochemical control is untested here.

### RTOG 3506 (STEEL)

> PFS HR 0.62 (80% CI 0.42-0.91, one-sided p=0.052) adding enzalutamide to SRT + 24mo ADT in high-risk post-RP BCR; BF at 2y 11% vs 19%.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Both arms received SRT + 24 months GnRHa, so the trial isolates ARSI intensification, not the RT contribution; SRT dose and field are not reported in source. BF at 2y dropped 19% to 11%, but Gr 3-4 lymphopenia rose 15% vs 5%, worth weighing if pelvic nodal fields are in the plan.

*Randomize 1:1: SRT + 24 months GnRHa ± enzalutamide 160 mg. Primary endpoint PFS (one-sided type I error=0.10).*

- 🔍 Randomized phase II, 1:1, n=188, mFU 33.1 mos
- 🔍 Primary endpoint PFS, designed with one-sided type I error=0.10 (screening design)
- 💊 Control: SRT + 24 months GnRHa · Exp: SRT + 24 months GnRHa + enzalutamide 160 mg
- 🔍 Eligibility: BCR after RP with PSA ≥0.2 ng/mL (or ≥0.1 ng/mL ultrasensitive) + ≥1 aggressive feature
  - Gleason 8-10
  - SVI
  - pN1
  - Persistently elevated PSA post-RP (>0.1 ng/mL)
  - PSA ≥0.7 ng/mL
- 📊 Biochemical failure at 2 yrs: 11% vs 19% (enza + ADT vs ADT)
- 📊 Gr 3-4 AEs higher with enza in the top two rows
  - Lymphocyte count decreased: 13 (15%) vs 5 (5%)
  - Hypertension: 7 (8%) vs 5 (5%)
- ⚠️ SRT dose, fractionation and target volume (bed vs pelvic nodes) not reported in source
- ⚠️ Tweet gives 80% CI 0.41-0.91; slide reads 0.42-0.91
- 🔗 Builds on the SRT + ADT evidence base (RTOG 9601, GETUG-AFU 16, SPPORT); tests ARSI intensification on a 24mo backbone
