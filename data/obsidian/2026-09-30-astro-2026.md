# 2026-09-30 — ASTRO Annual Meeting 2026

**RTOG 3506 (STEEL): enzalutamide added to SRT + 24mo GnRHa for high-risk BCR post-RP → PFS HR 0.62 (80% CI 0.42-0.91), randomized phase II screening signal.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Prostate was the only site today, and both trials change the hormonal partner given with RT. STEEL points to enza intensification of salvage RT + 24mo GnRHa, but only at a phase II screening threshold (one-sided p=0.052 vs α 0.10), and SRT parameters are unreported. REVELUTION favors relugolix over leuprolide on 12-mo IPSS and EPIC-CP total, in a post hoc analysis with N=65.

## 🌰 Prostate

Both studies modify the ADT backbone paired with RT, not the RT itself; RT dose, fractionation and nodal coverage go unreported in both sources.

### REVELUTION

> Relugolix vs leuprolide with RT: 12-mo IPSS 5.66 vs 9.22 (p=0.039) and EPIC-CP total 12.25 vs 17.25 (p=0.02), post hoc PRO analysis.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The PRO gap tracks testosterone: relugolix pts sat at 199.7 vs 142.0 ng/dL at 12 mo, and urinary (IPSS) plus EPIC-CP total scores favored relugolix, while sexual and continence domains did not separate. For a short-course ADT + RT plan, this informs agent choice on recovery and urinary symptom burden, not oncologic control.

### RTOG 3506 (STEEL)

> PFS HR 0.62 (80% CI 0.42-0.91, one-sided p=0.052) adding 24mo enzalutamide to SRT + 24mo GnRHa in high-risk BCR post-RP.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Biochemical failure at 2 yrs fell from 19% to 11% even against a control already carrying 24mo GnRHa, so enzalutamide adds on top of long-course ADT rather than replacing it. SRT dose and nodal volume were not reported, which leaves open whether the gain holds with modern pelvic nodal SRT.

*Primary endpoint: PFS (one-sided type I error=0.10). SRT + 24 months GnRHa ± enzalutamide 160 mg.*

- 🔍 Randomized phase II, 1:1, n=188, mFU 33.1 mos
- 🔍 Eligibility: BCR after RP plus ≥1 aggressive feature
  - PSA ≥0.2 ng/mL (or ≥0.1 ng/mL on ultrasensitive assay)
  - Aggressive features: Gleason 8-10, SVI, pN1
  - Persistently elevated PSA post-RP >0.1 ng/mL, or PSA ≥0.7 ng/mL
- 💊 SRT + 24mo GnRHa ± enzalutamide 160 mg
- 📊 1° EP PFS: HR 0.62, 80% CI 0.42-0.91, one-sided p=0.052
- 📐 Designed with one-sided type I error 0.10, so p=0.052 clears the prespecified bar
- 📊 Biochemical failure at 2 yrs: 11% vs 19% (enza + ADT vs ADT)
- ⚠️ 80% CI and one-sided α 0.10 are screening-design thresholds, not phase III evidence
- ⚠️ SRT dose, fractionation and pelvic nodal coverage not reported in source
- ⚠️ No metastasis, HRD or survival results reported in source; secondary EPs pending
- 🔗 Control arm already intensified (24mo GnRHa), longer than the ADT durations tested in RTOG 9601 or GETUG-AFU 16
