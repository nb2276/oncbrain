# 2026-06-18

**PRIMARY2: PSMA-PET triage avoided biopsy in 49%; csPCa 12% vs 16% with systematic biopsy (non-inferior, ITT) in biopsy-naive men with equivocal or high-risk non-suspicious MRI.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Prostate only: in PRIMARY2, PSMA-PET triage avoided biopsy in 49% and cut clinically insignificant PCa (14% vs 32%), with csPCa non-inferior in ITT. The NI read is fragile: per-protocol and post-hoc lower bounds cross the 10% margin, missing biopsy was 11% control vs 2% PET, and missed csPCa in unbiopsied PET-negative men is unreported at 6.0 mo median f/u.

## 🌰 Prostate

PSMA-PET tested as a biopsy triage tool in biopsy-naive men, the first RCT after the non-randomised PRIMARY study.

### PRIMARY2 · [[NCT05154162]]

> Biopsy avoided in 49%; csPCa 12% vs 16% with PSMA-PET vs systematic biopsy, non-inferior in ITT, in biopsy-naive men with equivocal or high-risk non-suspicious MRI.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The csPCa shortfall sits in GS 3+4 disease: GS ≥4+3=7 was found in 14 (4%) PSMA-PET vs 16 (5%) biopsy pts, while clinically insignificant diagnoses fell from 105 (32%) to 46 (14%). Men reaching definitive-treatment consults through this pathway are filtered away from low-grade disease, not from GS ≥4+3; only 7 (1%) have had RT so far.

- 📊 Co-primary: PSMA-PET avoided biopsy in 163 (49%) of 331 (95% CI 44 to 55), p<0.0001 vs 20% threshold
- 📊 Biopsy findings, ITT (PSMA-PET n=331 vs systematic TP biopsy n=329)

  | Endpoint | PSMA-PET | Systematic biopsy | Difference |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | csPCa (GS 3+4 with ≥10% pattern 4, or higher) | 39 (12%) | 51 (16%) | –3.7% (95% CI –8.9 to 1.5), p=0.0093, NI |
  | Clinically insignificant PCa | 46 (14%) | 105 (32%) | –18% (97.5% CI –25 to –11), p<0.0001 |
  | No cancer on biopsy | 73 (22%) | 138 (42%) | not reported |
  | GS ≥3+4=7 (any pattern 4 fraction) | 56 (17%) | 79 (24%) | –7.1% (95% CI –13.2 to –1.0) |
  | GS ≥4+3=7 | 14 (4%) | 16 (5%) | –0.6% (–3.8 to 2.5) |
  | Mean cores per biopsied pt | 19.6 (SD 12.1) | 24.8 (SD 9.0) | not reported |

- 📊 csPCa by baseline MRI category

  | MRI | PSMA-PET | Systematic biopsy |
  |---|---|---|
  | PI-RADS 2 | 15/155 (10%) | 22/180 (12%) |
  | PI-RADS 3 | 24/176 (14%) | 29/149 (19%) |

- 🔍 Phase 3 non-inferiority RCT, 7 Australian hospitals, 1:1, unmasked, NI margin 10%
- 🔍 PRIMARY score 3–5 → PSMA-targeted TP biopsy; score 1–2 → no biopsy, PSA surveillance
- ⚠️ Sensitivity analyses: per-protocol and post-hoc lower bounds fall beyond the 10% NI margin

  | Analysis | PSMA-PET | Systematic biopsy | Difference (95% CI) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Covariate-adjusted (prespecified) | 8% | 13% | –4.8% (–9.5 to –0.1) |
  | Per-protocol (prespecified) | 27/236 (11%) | 47/255 (18%) | –7.0% (–13.3 to –0.7) |
  | Biopsy per protocol (post-hoc) | 39/324 (12%) | 51/294 (17%) | –5.3% (–11.0 to 0.3) |
  | Best case (prespecified) | 14% | 16% | –1.6% (–7.3 to 4.1) |

- ⚠️ Missing biopsy was differential between arms
  - 35 (11%) control vs 7 (2%) PSMA-PET pts not biopsied per protocol
  - Worst case: 95% CI crosses 10% NI margin once 4 of 35 unbiopsied controls assumed csPCa
- ⚠️ Median f/u 6.0 mo; missed csPCa in 159 unbiopsied PET-negative men not yet reported
- 🔗 Prior evidence was the non-randomised PRIMARY study (improved NPV in MRI-triaged pts); no prior RCT
