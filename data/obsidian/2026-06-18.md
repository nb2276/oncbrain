# 2026-06-18

**PRIMARY2: PSMA-PET triage avoided biopsy in 49% with csPCa 12% vs 16% (difference -3.7%, 95% CI -8.9 to 1.5, non-inferiority p=0.0093).**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Prostate carried the day alone, and the question is diagnostic rather than therapeutic: PSMA-PET added to equivocal MRI (PI-RADS 2-3) halves the biopsy burden without missing more clinically significant disease at 6 months. Clinically insignificant cancer detection dropped 18%, so the gain is mostly in over-detection avoided. Differential missing biopsy data and 6.0-month median f/u are the caveats.

## 🌰 Prostate

One randomised diagnostic trial: PSMA-PET as a triage step before biopsy in men with equivocal MRI, upstream of any RT decision.

### PRIMARY2 · [[NCT05154162]]

> csPCa 12% vs 16% (difference -3.7%, 95% CI -8.9 to 1.5, p=0.0093 non-inferiority), and PSMA-PET avoided biopsy in 49%.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The RT-relevant read is downstream case-mix, not the PET itself: clinically insignificant cancer fell from 32% to 14% and no-cancer biopsies from 42% to 22%, so referrals arriving from this pathway are enriched for GS 3+4 with ≥10% pattern 4 or higher. GS 4+3 or higher was near-identical (4% vs 5%), so the high-risk pool a radiation oncologist actually treats does not shrink.

- 📊 Co-1° EP 1: csPCa (GS 3+4 with ≥10% pattern 4 or higher) in 39 (12%) of 331 experimental vs 51 (16%) of 329 control
- 📐 Risk difference -3.7% (95% CI -8.9 to 1.5), p=0.0093 against a 10% non-inferiority margin
- 📊 Co-1° EP 2: biopsy avoided in 163 (49%) of 331 (95% CI 44 to 55), p<0.0001 vs the prespecified 20% threshold
- 🔍 Phase 3, non-inferiority, 1:1, N=660, seven Australian hospitals, biopsy-naive, PI-RADS 2 or 3 with a clinical red flag, PSA ≤20 ng/mL, ≤cT2
- 🔍 PET protocol: [68Ga]Ga-PSMA-11 1.8-2.2 MBq/kg, pelvic-only two bed steps, imaging 60-70 min post-injection, PRIMARY score 1-2 negative and 3-5 positive
- 📊 Secondary yield, intention-to-treat
  - Clinically insignificant cancer 46 (14%) of 331 vs 105 (32%) of 329, difference -18% (97.5% CI -25 to -11), p<0.0001
  - No cancer on biopsy 73 (22%) experimental vs 138 (42%) control
  - GS 3+4=7 or higher 56 (17%) vs 79 (24%), difference -7.1% (95% CI -13.2 to -1.0)
  - GS 4+3=7 or higher 14 (4%) vs 16 (5%), difference -0.6% (-3.8 to 2.5)
- 📐 Central vs local PRIMARY score agreement (1-2 vs 3-5) κ=0.81 (95% CI 0.75-0.87)
- 📊 Mean cores per biopsied participant 19.6 (SD 12.1) experimental vs 24.8 (SD 9.0) control
- ⚠️ Missing biopsy data were differential: 35 (11%) control vs 7 (2%) experimental did not get protocol biopsy, and the trial's own worst-case analysis crosses the 10% margin once four of those 35 controls truly had csPCa
- ⚠️ Median follow-up 6.0 months (IQR 5.1-7.0): the 159 men who avoided biopsy have no maturity to confirm the negatives were true negatives
- ⚠️ Unmasked participants and investigators, and the control arm was systematic biopsy, so MRI-targeted-only comparators are not tested here
- 🔗 Only prior data were the non-randomised PRIMARY cohort (2021), which reported improved NPV in an MRI-triaged population; this is the first randomised test
