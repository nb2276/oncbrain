## 🌰 Prostate

First randomised test of PSMA-PET as a pre-biopsy triage tool, upstream of any RT decision but shaping who arrives at one.

### PRIMARY2 · [[NCT05154162]]

> csPCa 12% vs 16% (diff -3·7%, 95% CI -8·9 to 1·5; p=0·0093 non-inferiority) with PSMA-PET triage; biopsy avoided in 49%.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The number that gates transferability is the PET threshold, not the HR: PRIMARY score 1-2 sent men to PSA surveillance, and SUVmax ran 3·9 (IQR 3·3-4·6) in negatives vs 5·2 (4·3-7·1) in positives, with κ=0·81 between central and local readers. For a radiation oncologist this changes who arrives in clinic, halving insignificant Gleason 6 referrals (32% to 14%) without losing csPCa.

- 🔍 Phase 3 non-inferiority RCT, 1:1, N=660, seven Australian hospitals, unmasked, stratified by site
- 🔍 Biopsy-naive men, PI-RADS 2 or 3 MRI plus ≥1 clinical red flag (PSA density >0·1, family hx, abnormal DRE, BRCA, PSA >10, PSADT <36mo, PSA velocity >0·75/yr), PSA ≤20, ≤cT2
- 📊 Co-primary 1: csPCa (Gleason 3+4 with ≥10% pattern 4 or higher) 39/331 (12%) experimental vs 51/329 (16%) control; difference -3·7% (95% CI -8·9 to 1·5), p=0·0093 against a 10% margin
- 📊 Co-primary 2: PSMA-PET avoided biopsy in 163/331 (49%, 95% CI 44 to 55), p<0·0001 vs the 20% threshold
- 📊 Clinically insignificant cancer 105/329 (32%) control vs 46/331 (14%) experimental, difference -18% (97·5% CI -25 to -11), p<0·0001
- 📊 Alternate csPCa definitions and sensitivity analyses (ITT unless stated)

  | Analysis | Control | Experimental | Difference (CI) |

  | Gleason ≥4+3=7 | 16 (5%) | 14 (4%) | -0·6% (-3·8 to 2·5) |
  | Covariate-adjusted | 13% | 8% | -4·8% (95% CI -9·5 to -0·1) |
  | Per-protocol | 47 (18%) of 255 | 27 (11%) of 236 | -7·0% (95% CI -13·3 to -0·7) |
  | Received-protocol-biopsy post-hoc | 51 (17%) of 294 | 39 (12%) of 324 | -5·3% (95% CI -11·0 to 0·3) |

- 📐 PRIMARY score negative (1-2) in 166 (50%) of 329 scanned; positive (3-5) in 163 (50%); central vs local reader κ=0·81 (95% CI 0·75-0·87)
- 📐 Cores per biopsied participant 24·8 (SD 9·0) control vs 19·6 (12·1) experimental; no biopsy in 35 (11%) control vs 173 (52%) experimental
- ⚠️ Biopsy data missing differentially: 35 (11%) control vs 7 (2%) experimental did not get protocol biopsy, and the worst-case analysis crosses the 10% margin once four of those 35 control men truly harbour csPCa
- ⚠️ Median follow-up only 6·0 months (IQR 5·1-7·0); the 159 unbiopsied PSMA-negative men have PSA surveillance, not a histological reference standard
- ⚠️ No masking of participants or investigators, and control-arm PI-RADS 3 men could add MRI-targeted cores, so biopsy technique differed by arm beyond the imaging question
- 🔗 Only prior data were the non-randomised PRIMARY (2021) NPV study; this is the first randomised test of PSMA-PET as a pre-biopsy triage tool
