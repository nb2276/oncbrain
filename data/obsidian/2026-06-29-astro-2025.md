# 2026-06-29 — ASTRO Annual Meeting 2025

**BART: adjuvant pelvic RT after cystectomy cuts 2yr locoregional failure to 8% vs 26%, LRFFS HR 0.43 (0.20-0.96), p=0.04; OS flat.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Bladder carried the day: the first randomised evidence that post-cystectomy pelvic RT controls the pelvis in high-risk MIBC, with the benefit concentrated in T3+/N+ (HR 0.22-0.25) and no isolated locoregional failures in the RT arm. OS is underpowered at N=153 and the control arm predates adjuvant IO.

## 💧 Bladder

Adjuvant pelvic RT re-enters the post-cystectomy conversation on randomised locoregional data, not survival.

### BART

> 2yr LRFFS 87.1% vs 76.0% with adjuvant pelvic RT after cystectomy, HR 0.43 (0.20-0.96), p=0.04; OS unchanged.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The RT read is the per-protocol and subgroup magnitude: LRFFS HR 0.27 (0.10-0.71) among those actually irradiated, and HR 0.22 (0.06-0.75) in pN+. Target was cystectomy bed plus full pelvic nodes to 50.4Gy/28fx with stoma-sparing IMRT, a plan deliverable in standard practice, and late G3+ toxicity was 8.4% vs 10.5%. That moves the offer-RT decision for pN+ disease.

- 🔍 Phase III multicenter RCT, N=153 randomized 2016-2024, 1:1 adjuvant RT vs observation, stratified by nodal stage and chemo
- 🔍 Eligibility: high-risk T3-4, N1-3, R+ non-metastatic urothelial MIBC post radical cystectomy
- 📐 RT dose 50.4Gy/28fx, stoma-sparing IMRT with daily onboard IGRT
- 📐 CTV covered cystectomy bed plus common iliac, internal/external iliac, presacral, obturator nodes
- 📊 2-year outcomes, RT vs observation (ITT unless noted)

  | Endpoint | Adjuvant RT | Observation | Effect size |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | LRFFS (primary) | 87.1% | 76.0% | HR 0.43 (0.20-0.96), p=0.04 |
  | LRFFS per protocol | 93.2% | 75.0% | HR 0.27 (0.10-0.71), p=0.008 |
  | DFS | 71.6% | 58.7% | HR 0.62 (0.36-1.05), p=0.07 |
  | Bladder cancer-specific survival | 79.6% | 65.0% | HR 0.59 (0.33-1.10), p=0.09 |
  | Overall survival | 70.4% | 57.4% | HR 0.78 (0.49-1.26), p=0.31 |

- 📊 Subgroup 2yr locoregional recurrence-free survival benefit
  - T3+ and N+: HR 0.25 (0.07-0.84)
  - N+ disease: HR 0.22 (0.06-0.75)
- 📊 Locoregional recurrence 8% RT vs 26% observation, p=0.006; no isolated locoregional recurrences with RT
- 📊 Toxicity (previously reported, Red Journal 2025)
  - G3 GI: 1.6% RT vs 4.1% observation
  - G2 GI: 17.5% RT vs 1.4% observation, no toxicity-related discontinuation
  - Late G3+: 8.4% RT vs 10.5% observation, p=0.60
- ⚠️ 14/77 in the RT arm never got planned RT (8 refused/defaulted, 4 progressed, 2 unfeasible) and were analyzed with observation (n=90)
- ⚠️ Accrual fell short of target over 8 years; 153 pts leaves OS underpowered, and the OS CI (0.49-1.26) cannot exclude a meaningful benefit or harm
- ⚠️ No pt received adjuvant immunotherapy, so the control arm predates CheckMate 274 practice
- 🔗 Median age 57 and 28% variant histology mark this as a younger, more aggressive single-country population than Western adjuvant series
- ❓ MERCY IPD meta-analysis is the stated path to an OS answer
