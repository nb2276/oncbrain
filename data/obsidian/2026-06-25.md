# 2026-06-25

**ENZARAD: 8yr MFS 74% vs 72%, HR 0.88 (0.67-1.15), p=0.34 — enzalutamide adds nothing to 2yr ADT + definitive RT in high-risk localised prostate.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Prostate carried the day, and it was a negative one for intensification: adding enza to protocol-grade RT (78Gy or 46Gy + brachy boost) plus 2yr ADT missed MFS, with PFS positive only on an unadjusted p=0.044. The signal that survives is subgroup and confounded: cN1 (MFS HR 0.43) and planned pelvic RT (0.47, OS 0.53), both enriched for worse disease.

## 🌰 Prostate

### ENZARAD (ANZUP 1303)

> 8yr MFS 74% vs 72%, HR 0.88 (0.67-1.15), p=0.34: enzalutamide added to 2yr ADT + RT missed its primary endpoint.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The signal tracks the pelvic field, not the drug: MFS HR 0.47 (0.29-0.76) with pelvic RT planned and 0.43 (0.20-0.92) in cN1, both declared before randomization. RT was 78Gy or 46Gy plus brachy boost with 46Gy elective nodes for cN1, so this transfers directly. It moves the intensification decision toward pts you were already covering nodally.

- 📊 1° EP MFS negative: 8yr 74% vs 72%, HR 0.88 (0.67-1.15), p=0.34
- 📐 PFS positive: 8yr 67% vs 62%, HR 0.78 (0.61-0.99), p=0.044
- 📐 OS no difference: 8yr 83% vs 80%, HR 0.87 (0.63-1.20), p=0.40
- 📊 Prespecified subgroups where enza signal concentrates (MFS and OS)

  | Subgroup | MFS HR (95% CI) | OS HR (95% CI) |
  |---|---|---|
  | cN1 (regional nodes) | 0.43 (0.20-0.92) | 0.46 (0.17-1.26) |
  | Pelvic field RT planned | 0.47 (0.29-0.76) | 0.53 (0.30-0.95) |
  | 'Very high-risk' | 0.85 (0.64-1.13) | 0.81 (0.57-1.13) |

- 🔍 Control arm was an active NSAA x6mo + LHRH x24mo, not ADT alone
- 🔍 RT was protocol-grade: 78Gy, or 46Gy + brachy boost; cN1 required 46Gy elective nodes + gross nodal boost
- 🔍 Pelvic RT optional for cN0 but had to be declared before randomization, so the subgroup is allocation-time, not post-hoc selection
- 🔍 N=802, 8 countries, accrual Mar 2014 to Jun 2018, median f/u 8 yrs; 90% Gleason 8-10, 12% cN1, 36% PSA >20
- ⚠️ Pelvic-RT subgroup is confounded by risk: 28% N1 and 62% Gleason 9/10 vs 0% N1 and 49% Gleason 9/10 without pelvic RT
- ⚠️ Five subgroups prespecified, all p-values nominal without multiplicity adjustment; positive PFS at p=0.044 sits on an unadjusted alpha
- ⚠️ 1° EP was switched from OS to MFS mid-trial due to fewer deaths than anticipated
- 🔗 vs STAMPEDE abiraterone: all-comer MFS HR 0.88 here vs 0.53 there, with a lighter-risk population (cN1 11% vs 39%, median PSA 14 vs 35, cT3-4 47% vs 92%)
