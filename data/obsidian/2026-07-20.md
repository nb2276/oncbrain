# 2026-07-20

**RTOG 0848: adjuvant chemoRT missed OS overall (HR 0.96, P=.38) but N0 pts saw 5-yr OS 48.1% vs 28.6% (interaction P=.0063).**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Upper GI carried the day, and the RT read is entirely in the subgroup: modern QA'd 50.4 Gy/28 fx IMRT added nothing to gem-based chemo overall, but the treatment-by-nodal-status interaction (OS P=.0063, DFS P=.014) puts resected N0 pancreas back in play as the population worth testing. Grade 3 tox 38% vs 19%; hypothesis-generating, n=91.

## 🍽️ GI Upper

Post-resection pancreas: the first adjuvant CXRT trial with mandatory pre-treatment RT credentialing, and its only positive signal is a nodal-status interaction on a failed primary.

### RTOG 0848 · [[NCT01013649]]

> OS primary missed (HR 0.96, 90% CI 0.79-1.18, P=.38 1-sided), but treatment-by-nodal-status interaction favored CXRT in N0 (P=.0063).

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The RT-relevant read is the nodal interaction: N0 pts (n=91) had median OS 3.9 vs 3.0 yr and 5-yr OS 48.1% vs 28.6% with CXRT, at 50.4 Gy/28 fx to bed plus regional nodes with mandatory pre-treatment central review. That combination of dose, target and QA is deliverable today, and it moves the adjuvant RT question from whether to whom.

- 🔍 Phase IIR/III two-step: step 2 randomized 354 pts post-resection, no progression after 5 cycles gem-based chemo, ±CXRT after cycle 6
- 📊 1° EP OS not met: median 2.6 vs 2.3 yr; 5-yr 23.1% vs 27.9% (CXRT)
- 📐 DFS trend favors CXRT: HR 0.82 (95% CI 0.65-1.03), P=.089
- 📐 Nodal-status interaction: OS P=.0063, DFS P=.014; forest-plot N-stage subgroup P-interaction .022
- 📊 Node-negative subgroup (n=91), CXRT vs chemo alone

  | Endpoint | Chemo (n=42) | Chemo+CXRT (n=49) | 5-yr OS (95% CI) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Median OS | 3.0 yr (2.2-4.0) | 3.9 yr (2.5 to NR) | 28.6 (14.9-42.2) vs 48.1 (33.3-62.9) |
  | Median DFS | 1.5 yr (0.8-2.7) | 2.3 yr (1.4 to NR) | n/a |
  | OS events | 32 failed / 10 censored | 23 failed / 26 censored | n/a |

- 🔍 RT was tightly QA'd: 50.4 Gy/28 fx to tumor bed, pancreatojejunostomy, regional nodes; real-time central review before start
- 🔍 81% IMRT, 19% 3D conformal; 88% got ≥50 Gy; 79% no interruptions; per-protocol/acceptable in 92% of treated pts
- ⚠️ 14% randomized to CXRT never received it (18 refusals); as-treated sensitivity HR 0.98 (90% CI 0.80-1.20), unchanged
- ⚠️ Grade 3 toxicity higher with CXRT, grade 4/5 not
  - Any grade 3: 38% vs 19%, P<.001
  - Grade 4/5: 6% vs 5%, P=.68
  - Grade 3 nonheme: 22% vs 11%, P=.004
  - GI disorders grade 3-5: 11% vs 2%; diarrhea 6% vs 1%
  - Lymphocyte count decreased grade 3-5: 10% vs 2%
- ⚠️ No pt received FOLFIRINOX/mFOLFIRINOX; backbone was gemcitabine ± erlotinib, now superseded
- 🔗 vs ESPAC-1: CXRT was detrimental there (1994-2000, split-course low-dose 2D, no QA); 0848 modernized dose, IMRT, and credentialing
- 🔗 vs RTOG 9704: post-hoc showed RT protocol deviation tracked with worse survival; 0848 pre-empted that with mandatory pre-treatment review
- ⚠️ Nodal subgroup is a treatment-by-covariate interaction in a trial whose 1° EP failed; n=91 N0 pts, 55 events
- ⚠️ Accrual took ~9 yr; final analysis triggered at 5-yr follow-up with 270 (not 316) OS events, power 72%

- 📄 **Adjuvant Chemotherapy ± Chemoradiotherapy for Adenocarcinoma of the Pancreatic Head: Results of the Radiotherapy Random Assignment of NRG Oncology/RTOG 0848** — Abrams, Ross A.; Winter, Kathryn A.; Goodman, Karyn A. et al.. *Journal of Clinical Oncology* (2026-06). [doi:10.1200/jco-25-02520](https://doi.org/10.1200/jco-25-02520)
  > PURPOSE To assess whether adding fluoropyrimidine sensitized radiotherapy (CXRT) to adjuvant chemotherapy improves overall survival (OS) after curative intent resection of the pancreatic head. METHODS This was a multicenter, randomized phase III, two step trial. Step 1: gemcitabine versus gemcitabine + erlotinib (previously reported). Step 2: random assignment to sixth chemotherapy cycle ± CXRT after five cycles of step 1 chemotherapy without progression. Outcomes of step 2 random assignment are reported here. Assuming 17 months median OS (chemotherapy alone), the sample size was 354 patients (hazard ratio [HR], 0.76, 80% power, one-sided α = .05, 316 OS events). OS/disease-free survival (DFS) were estimated by Kaplan-Meier and arms compared using the log-rank test. RESULTS A total of 354 patients (median age 63, 55% male, 56% performance status, 1) were randomly assigned to chemotherapy (174) or chemotherapy + CXRT (180). Univariable median and 5-year OS (90% CIs) were 2.6 years (2.1-3.1) and 23.1% (17.7-28.6) for chemotherapy alone, and 2.3 years (2.0-2.6) and 27.9% (22.2-33.6) for chemotherapy + CXRT. The OS primary end point was not met (HR, 0.96 [90% CI, 0.79 to 1.18]; one-sided P = .38, two-sided P = .77). Chemotherapy + CXRT was associated with a trend for improved DFS (univariably; HR, 0.82 [95% CI, 0.65 to 1.03]; P = .089), without increase in grade 4/5 toxicities. However, grade 3 toxicity increased (38% v 19%, P &lt; .001). Significantly, treatment by nodal status interactions showed that CXRT improved OS ( P = .0063) and DFS ( P = .014) in node-negative patients. CONCLUSION Overall, the addition of adjuvant CXRT to adjuvant gemcitabine did not statistically significantly improve OS, DFS, or increase grade 4/5 toxicity. For node-negative patients, CXRT improved OS and DFS. These results, if confirmed in studies with current or future systemic therapies, would support the use of adjuvant/neoadjuvant CXRT for node-negative patients.
