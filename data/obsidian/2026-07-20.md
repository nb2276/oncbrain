# 2026-07-20

**RTOG 0848: adjuvant CXRT misses OS (HR 0.96), but N0 pts show 5y OS 48.1% vs 28.6% (interaction P=.0063).**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Upper GI carried the day alone. RTOG 0848 answers the adjuvant pancreas RT question negatively overall, then reopens it in node-negative disease, where 50.4 Gy/28 fx with modern QA (81% IMRT, 92% per-protocol) tracked with near-doubled 5y OS in 91 pts. Hypothesis-generating, not practice-changing, and the backbone was gemcitabine-era.

## 🍽️ GI Upper

First modern-QA randomised read on adjuvant chemoradiation after resected pancreatic cancer: negative overall, with an N0 signal that ESPAC-1's split-course 2D design could never have isolated.

### NRG Oncology/RTOG 0848 · [[NCT01013649]]

> OS HR 0.96 (90% CI 0.79-1.18), primary endpoint missed; node-negative interaction p=.0063 favors adjuvant CXRT.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The RT-relevant read is the nodal interaction: in N0 pts (n=91) median OS 3.9y vs 3.0y and 5y OS 48.1% vs 28.6%, with 50.4 Gy/28 fx to bed plus regional nodes under real-time central QA. That is the population where adjuvant CXRT is worth discussing, and the QA'd modern technique is what separates this from ESPAC-1.

- 📊 1° EP OS not met: HR 0.96 (90% CI 0.79-1.18), one-sided P=.38, two-sided P=.77
- 📊 Median OS 2.6y (2.1-3.1) chemo vs 2.3y (2.0-2.6) chemo+CXRT; 5y 23.1% vs 27.9%
- 📊 DFS trend favoring CXRT: HR 0.82 (95% CI 0.65-1.03), P=.089
- 📊 Treatment × nodal status interaction: OS P=.0063, DFS P=.014, benefit confined to N0
- 📊 Node-negative subgroup (n=91), CXRT vs chemo alone

  | Endpoint | Chemo (n=42) | Chemo+CXRT (n=49) |

- 🔍 Two-step phase III: step 2 randomized 354 pts (174 chemo, 180 chemo+CXRT) after 5 cycles without progression
- 🔍 RT was 50.4 Gy/28 fx to tumor bed, pancreatojejunostomy and regional nodes; 81% IMRT, 19% 3D conformal
- 🔍 Real-time central RT review pre-treatment; overall per-protocol/acceptable in 142/155 treated (92%)
- 🔍 Sensitizer 83% capecitabine, 15% infusional 5-FU; 88% of the 153 treated got ≥50 Gy
- ⚠️ 25 of 180 assigned to CXRT (14%) never received it, 18 by refusal; as-treated HR 0.98 (90% CI 0.80-1.20)
- ⚠️ Grade 3 AEs 38% vs 19%, P<.001, driven by GI/diarrhea and lymphopenia; grade 4/5 unchanged (6% v 5%, P=.68)
- ⚠️ Nodal-status interaction is a subgroup finding, hypothesis-generating; only 91 N0 pts (26%)
- ⚠️ Backbone is gemcitabine-era: 67% gemcitabine alone, 28% gem+erlotinib, zero FOLFIRINOX
- 🔗 vs ESPAC-1, where CXRT was detrimental: that trial used split-course lower dose, 2D planning, no QA, accrual 1994-2000
- 🔗 Failure pattern in this cohort: distant-only first failure 38%, local-only 23%

- 📄 **Adjuvant Chemotherapy ± Chemoradiotherapy for Adenocarcinoma of the Pancreatic Head: Results of the Radiotherapy Random Assignment of NRG Oncology/RTOG 0848** — Abrams, Ross A.; Winter, Kathryn A.; Goodman, Karyn A. et al.. *Journal of Clinical Oncology* (2026-06). [doi:10.1200/jco-25-02520](https://doi.org/10.1200/jco-25-02520)
  > PURPOSE To assess whether adding fluoropyrimidine sensitized radiotherapy (CXRT) to adjuvant chemotherapy improves overall survival (OS) after curative intent resection of the pancreatic head. METHODS This was a multicenter, randomized phase III, two step trial. Step 1: gemcitabine versus gemcitabine + erlotinib (previously reported). Step 2: random assignment to sixth chemotherapy cycle ± CXRT after five cycles of step 1 chemotherapy without progression. Outcomes of step 2 random assignment are reported here. Assuming 17 months median OS (chemotherapy alone), the sample size was 354 patients (hazard ratio [HR], 0.76, 80% power, one-sided α = .05, 316 OS events). OS/disease-free survival (DFS) were estimated by Kaplan-Meier and arms compared using the log-rank test. RESULTS A total of 354 patients (median age 63, 55% male, 56% performance status, 1) were randomly assigned to chemotherapy (174) or chemotherapy + CXRT (180). Univariable median and 5-year OS (90% CIs) were 2.6 years (2.1-3.1) and 23.1% (17.7-28.6) for chemotherapy alone, and 2.3 years (2.0-2.6) and 27.9% (22.2-33.6) for chemotherapy + CXRT. The OS primary end point was not met (HR, 0.96 [90% CI, 0.79 to 1.18]; one-sided P = .38, two-sided P = .77). Chemotherapy + CXRT was associated with a trend for improved DFS (univariably; HR, 0.82 [95% CI, 0.65 to 1.03]; P = .089), without increase in grade 4/5 toxicities. However, grade 3 toxicity increased (38% v 19%, P &lt; .001). Significantly, treatment by nodal status interactions showed that CXRT improved OS ( P = .0063) and DFS ( P = .014) in node-negative patients. CONCLUSION Overall, the addition of adjuvant CXRT to adjuvant gemcitabine did not statistically significantly improve OS, DFS, or increase grade 4/5 toxicity. For node-negative patients, CXRT improved OS and DFS. These results, if confirmed in studies with current or future systemic therapies, would support the use of adjuvant/neoadjuvant CXRT for node-negative patients.
