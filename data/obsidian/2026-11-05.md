# 2026-11-05

**kV RT over nasal cartilage, 671 BCC/SCC lesions: 5-yr cure 88.09%, 10-yr 86.51%, cosmesis good or acceptable in 96.84%. Retrospective single-institution series.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Skin RT carried the day. Retrospective nasal/facial BCC/SCC series report 5-yr local control or cure of 85% (orthovoltage), 88.09% (kV) and 89% (HDR mold), all single-institution with physician-rated cosmesis. Brantsch: desmoplasia (LR HR 16.11) and thickness >6.0 mm drive failure after margin-controlled excision without adjuvant RT. Prostate: GnRH antagonist ~37% numerically lower MACE, crude claims comparison.

## 🌰 Prostate

Real-world claims data on ADT class and MACE; no CI or adjusted HR in source for the antagonist signal.

### ADT Class and Cardiovascular Risk (Hahn, ASCO GU 2026)

> MACE ~four per 100 person-yrs overall; ~37% numerically lower with GnRH antagonist vs agonist; 11 per 100 person-yrs with preexisting CVD.

- 🔍 Retrospective real-world claims analysis, commercial + Medicare databases, ~17,000 pts starting ADT
- 🔍 Index date = first ADT prescription (GnRH agonist or antagonist); data through May 2024
- 🔍 Primary endpoints: two- and three-point MACE; secondary: MACE pre vs post-2021
- 💊 ADT class mix
  - GnRH agonist (e.g. leuprolide): ~90%
  - GnRH antagonist: ~10%
  - Antagonist split: degarelix ~two-thirds, relugolix ~a third
- 📊 Overall MACE ~four events per 100 person-yrs
- 📊 Antagonist vs agonist: MACE ~37% numerically lower; no CI or adjusted HR reported in source
- 📊 Preexisting CVD: ~11 MACE per 100 person-yrs, ~threefold the overall rate
- 📊 Post-2021 vs pre-2021: modest MACE reduction; no effect size reported in source
- ⚠️ Agonist pts older, more often Medicare, longer median f/u: crude comparison confounded
- ⚠️ Antagonist arm mostly degarelix; relugolix uptake after May 2024 not captured
- 🔗 vs HERO: same direction as relugolix's lower MACE signal, but claims data cannot test it

## 🌞 Skin / Melanoma

Five retrospective single-centre series: four RT series (orthovoltage, kV, two HDR contact techniques) and one surgical cohort that defines high-risk cSCC features.

### RT for nasal skin SCC, Princess Margaret experience

> Local relapse-free rate 90% at 2y and 85% at 5y, CSS 96%, no RTOG G4 late toxicity in 94 pts treated with mostly orthovoltage RT.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Failure pattern is the RT read: 6 of 10 local failures sat on the nasal bridge, and 3 of 4 bridge recurrences after CR had a marginal component, while 2 of 5 columella lesions failed. Curved surfaces and fusion planes argue for generous margins beyond the usual 1 cm.

- 🔍 Retrospective single-institution chart review, 100 pts treated 1982-1993; 6 lost to follow-up, 94 analyzed
- 🔍 T1 60, T2 11, T4 7, TX 16; only 1 pt node-positive at presentation
- 🔍 RT alone in 63 pts (67%); surgery then RT in 31 (33%) for gross or microscopic residual
- 💊 Size-based dose/fractionation (typical)
  - <2 cm: 35 Gy in 5 fractions
  - 2-5 cm: 45 Gy in 10 fractions
  - >5 cm or bone/cartilage invasion: 50 Gy in 20 fractions
  - Field edge typically 1 cm beyond the gross lesion
- 🔍 Modality: orthovoltage 76 pts (81%), electrons 13 (14%), cobalt 4 (4%)
- 📊 Actuarial outcomes from start of RT

  | Endpoint | 2 years | 5 years |
  |---|---|---|
  | Local failure-free | 90% (SE 3%) | 85% (SE 5%) |
  | Overall survival | 75% (SE 4%) | 51% (SE 6%) |
  | Cause-specific survival | 96% (SE 2%) | 96% (SE 2%) |

- 📊 Elective nodes not irradiated; regional failure in only 2 of 93 cN0 pts
- 📊 10 local failures: 6 on the nasal bridge, 2 columella (2 of 5 columella lesions failed)
- 📊 Salvage in 8 pts (4 excision, 4 re-RT); 5 reached disease-free status
- ⚠️ Late toxicity not formally scored; G4-free claim rests on retrospective chart review
- ⚠️ Median follow-up only 2.9y in survivors; OS 51% at 5y shrinks the at-risk denominator
- ⚠️ Univariate analysis found no prognostic factor, likely underpowered with 10 local events
- 🔗 vs Mazeron series cited in source (1676 nose/vestibule cases): 11% SCC local failure, similar to 10% here at 2y

### Risk factors determining prognosis of cutaneous SCC (Brantsch)

> No metastases in tumours ≤2.0 mm thick; >6.0 mm metastasized in 14/90 (16%); desmoplasia drove local recurrence (HR 16.11) after margin-controlled resection.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Desmoplasia, which here included every case of perineural or perivascular invasion, carried local recurrence of 12/51 (24%) vs 8/564 (1%) despite tumour-free 3D-histology margins and no adjuvant RT. That is an RT-naive surgical baseline. It places the adjuvant RT question in desmoplastic/PNI or >6.0 mm tumours (LR 11/90, 12%), not in clear-margin thin SCC.

- 🔍 Prospective single-centre cohort (Tübingen), resections 1990-2001; 653 enrolled, 615 assessable
- 🔍 Median f/u 43 mo (range 1-165); median age 73; 38 lost to follow-up
- 🔍 All resected with 3D-histology to tumour-free margins; no pt received adjuvant RT
- 📊 Events by tumour thickness

  | Thickness | Metastasis | Local recurrence |
  |---|---|---|
  | ≤2.0 mm (n=207) | 0 | 1 |
  | 2.1-6.0 mm (n=318) | 12 (4%) | 8 (3%) |
  | >6.0 mm (n=90) | 14 (16%) | 11 (12%) |

- 📐 Best multivariate Cox model, independent factors

  | Endpoint | Factor | HR (95% CI) | p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Metastasis | Tumour thickness | 4.79 (2.22-10.36) | <0.0001 |
  | Metastasis | Immunosuppression | 4.32 (1.62-11.52) | 0.0035 |
  | Metastasis | Ear location | 3.61 (1.51-8.67) | 0.0040 |
  | Metastasis | Horizontal size | 2.22 (1.18-4.15) | 0.0128 |
  | Local recurrence | Tumour thickness | 6.03 (2.71-13.43) | <0.0001 |
  | Local recurrence | Desmoplasia | 16.11 (6.57-39.49) | <0.0001 |

- 📊 Local recurrence 12/51 (24%) desmoplastic vs 8/564 (1%) non-desmoplastic
- 🔍 Perineural or perivascular invasion was always coded as desmoplasia, so the desmoplasia HR includes PNI
- 📐 Predictive accuracy (C-index), best model vs current TNM
  - Metastasis: 0.837 vs 0.743
  - Local recurrence: 0.907 vs 0.750
- 📊 First metastasis always in a regional LN; none after year 4, no LR after year 6
- ⚠️ Events per predictor 26:7 (metastasis) and 20:7 (LR), below the limit of 5 the authors cite
- ⚠️ Lip location and poor differentiation (both TNM-era criteria) were not independent prognostic factors

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Analysis of risk factors determining prognosis of cutaneous squamous-cell carcinoma: a prospective study** — Brantsch, Kay D; Meisner, Christoph; Schönfisch, Birgitt et al.. *The Lancet Oncology* (2008-08). [doi:10.1016/s1470-2045(08)70178-5](https://doi.org/10.1016/s1470-2045(08%2970178-5)

### Kilovoltage RT for carcinomas overlying nasal cartilage (671 lesions)

> 5-yr cure rate 88.09% and 10-yr 86.51% in 671 BCC and SCC over nasal cartilage; cosmesis good or acceptable in 96.84%.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Failure was mostly marginal (25 of 38 recurrences) with 0.5-1 cm field margins, which argues for generous margins over nasal skin; morpheaform lesions got 1.5 cm. With 5 Gy twice weekly (median 55 Gy), the authors report no cartilage sequelae. That supports kV RT as the nonsurgical option where excision would need complex reconstruction.

- 🔍 Retrospective single-institution series, 671 basal and squamous cell carcinomas, treated 1972-2007
- 🔍 Mean f/u 38.016 mo (range 1-351 mo); actuarial cure rate by life-table method
- 💊 CRT technique (668 of 671 lesions)
  - 55-60 kV, 4-6 mA, no filter, HVD 2-12 mm
  - 5 Gy/fx, 2 times per week
  - Total 30-75 Gy (mean 55.70 Gy, median 55 Gy)
  - Field margin 0.5-1 cm; 1.5 cm for 20 morpheaform BCC
  - Nostril lead rubber shield (1 mm Pb) for alae nasi lesions
  - HDRT + CRT in 3 lesions only (50 + 15 Gy or 60 + 20/25 Gy)
- 📊 Tumor outcomes

  | Endpoint | Lesions | % |
  |---|---|---|
  | CR at 1 mo | 663 | 98.80% |
  | PR (all BCC) | 3 | 0.44% |
  | Recurrence (13 central, 25 marginal) | 38 | 5.66% |
  | 5-yr cure rate | n/a | 88.09% |
  | 10-yr cure rate | n/a | 86.51% |

- 📊 Cosmesis, lesions in CR/PR

  | Rating | Lesions | % |
  |---|---|---|
  | Good | 496 | 74.47% |
  | Acceptable | 149 | 22.37% |
  | Not acceptable | 16 | 2.40% |

- 📊 Recurrence onset 3-96 mo (mean 37.96 mo); 37 of 38 relapses BCC
- 📊 No relapse association with dose or lesion size
- 📊 Authors report 0 complications or sequelae
- ⚠️ Crude 5.66% recurrence understates failure; actuarial 5-yr cure 88.09% is the real figure
- ⚠️ Toxicity is clinician-rated cosmesis; no cartilage-necrosis grading scale stated
- ⚠️ Superiority over other modalities rests on cross-literature comparison only, with no surgical arm
- 🔗 Authors cite randomised facial BCC data favouring surgery overall, with cosmesis similar on nasal skin

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Radiotherapy of carcinomas of the skin overlying the cartilage of the nose: our experience in 671 lesions** — Caccialanza, M; Piccinno, R; Percivalle, S et al.. *Journal of the European Academy of Dermatology and Venereology* (2009-09). [doi:10.1111/j.1468-3083.2009.03247.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1468-3083.2009.03247.x)

### Facial cutaneous carcinoma HDR contact brachytherapy with customized molds

> 5-yr actuarial LC 89% (100% flat surfaces, 83% nose) with Ir-192 HDR contact mold BT, 48 Gy/12 fx, for facial BCC/SCC ≤3 mm thick.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Failure tracked the applicator, not the dose: all five relapses had only three catheters, and four sat on the nasal tip (LC 73%) versus 95% on pyramid/wing and 100% on flat skin. The 3 mm prescription depth only fits lesions ≤3 mm, so nasal-tip lesions or thicker tumors argue for electrons, interstitial BT or surgery.

- 🔍 Retrospective single-institution series (FIVO Valencia), 51 lesions in 48 pts, treated 1999-2005
- 🔍 BCC 42 (82.4%), SCC 9 (17.6%); mean diameter 1.5 cm (range 0.5-4 cm); all ≤3 mm thick
- 🔍 Median age 78; radical intent 95%, adjuvant 5% (lateral margin involved after incomplete surgery)
- 💊 HDR schedules used (Ir-192, every other day, 3 fx/wk)

  | Schedule | Share of cases | EQD α/β10 | EQD α/β2.5 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 44-48 Gy, 4 Gy x 11-12 fx | 57% | 57.75-63 Gy | 61.5-67 Gy |
  | 51-57 Gy, 3 Gy x 17-18 fx | 37% | 62-69.3 Gy | 61.2-64.8 Gy |
  | 35 Gy, 7 Gy x 5 fx (very old pts) | 6% | 55.5 Gy | 72 Gy |

- 🔍 Prescription at 3 mm depth; 1 cm margin on lesion; skin surface dose ~150%, never higher
- 🔍 Nose: custom 5 mm wax mold on plaster cast, CT-planned; flat sites: 5 mm bolus with tubes 1 cm apart
- 📊 Local control by site (median f/u 45 mo, range 4-102)
- 📊 All 5 relapses treated with only three tubes; 2 at field border, 3 in-field
- 📊 All relapses salvaged with surgery; no nodal or distant spread; no cancer deaths
- 📊 Toxicity
  - Acute G1-2 dermatitis: 30 pts (78%), resolved in days
  - Acute G4 dermatitis: 11 pts (21%), healed in 3 wk as outpatient
  - No late complications reported, including nasal chondritis
  - Some G1 hypopigmentation, G2 telangiectasia, mainly large tumors
  - Cosmesis good or very good in all cases
- ⚠️ G4 dermatitis rate inconsistent within paper: 21% in Results, 12% in Discussion
- ⚠️ Cosmesis graded on a physician scale (poor to very good), no validated pt-reported instrument
- 🔗 vs Guix et al. (contact HDR-BT, 60-65 Gy at 1.8 Gy/fx, 136 lesions): 5-yr LC 98% per source

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Treatment of facial cutaneous carcinoma with high–dose rate contact brachytherapy with customized molds** — Maroñas, Maria; Guinot, Jose Luis; Arribas, Leoncio et al.. *Brachytherapy* (2011-05). [doi:10.1016/j.brachy.2010.08.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brachy.2010.08.005)
  - 📎 [[papers/skin/2011-maronas-treatment-facial-cutaneous-carcinoma-high-dose-rate.pdf|Facial cutaneous carcinoma HDR contact brachytherapy with customized molds (full text)]]

### Hypofractionated HDR plesiotherapy in nonmelanoma skin cancer

> 3y/5y DFS 95.12%/93.36% after 3 Gy/fx HDR surface brachy for 134 BCC/SCC lesions; CR 98%, recurrent lesions did worse.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Disease status, not applicator, split control: recurrent lesions reached 3y/5y DFS 85.9%/85.9% vs 97.9%/95.6% for primary (p=0.003). Acute G3 was 40.3% at 3 Gy/fx three times weekly, higher with definitive intent and molds, so fraction size and applicator choice gate tolerability more than control for larger or curved lesions.

- 🔍 Retrospective single-institution series: 114 pts, 134 lesions (92 BCC, 42 SCC), Nov 2006 to Dec 2011
- 🔍 All lesions clinically N0; median f/u 33 mo
- 🔍 Treatment setting and technique
  - Definitive: 75 BCC + 27 SCC (77.3%)
  - Adjuvant after excision with positive/close margins: 32 lesions (22.7%)
  - Leipzig applicator (2 or 3 cm), ≤2 cm flat lesions: 101 lesions (75.4%)
  - Customized wax/plaster mold, raised areas or >2 cm lesions
  - Molds treated larger lesions: mean 19.38 vs 13.07 mm, p=0.004
- 💊 RT parameters
  - Ir-192 HDR, 3 Gy/fx, 3 fx/wk
  - Mean dose 48.79 Gy (45-54) BCC, 50.55 Gy (45-57) SCC
  - Dose prescribed 3-5 mm below skin surface
  - PTV: lesion + 0.5-1 cm margin
  - Lower doses for BCC and adjuvant setting than SCC and definitive
- 📊 DFS by subgroup (per lesion, KM)

  | Group | 3y DFS | 5y DFS |
  |---|---|---|
  | Overall | 95.12% | 93.36% |
  | Leipzig applicator | 94.9% | 94.9% |
  | Customized mold | 93.1% | 88% |
  | Primary lesion | 97.9% | 95.6% |
  | Recurrent lesion | 85.9% | 85.9% |
  | BCC | 94.1% | 91.3% |
  | SCC | 97.5% | 97.5% |

- 📐 Primary vs recurrent DFS p=0.003; definitive vs adjuvant 94.86% vs 96.3%, p=0.412
- 📊 CR 98%; 2 persistent lesions, both Leipzig; no regional or distant mets
- 📊 Acute toxicity (RTOG, per lesion)
  - G1-2 57.5%, G3 40.3%, G4 2.2%
  - Definitive > adjuvant: OR 2.928 (95% CI 1.287-6.659), p=0.013
  - Mold vs Leipzig: OR 2.25 (95% CI 1.012-5.003), p=0.067
  - Lesions 5-25 mm: OR 3.533 (95% CI 1.318-9.467), p=0.016
- 📊 Late G0-1 95.2%; one G4 ulcer (recurrent 40 mm lower-leg SCC, venous insufficiency, 54 Gy)
- 📊 Cosmesis excellent/good 82%, fair 13%, not available 5%
- ⚠️ Applicator comparison confounded by lesion size and site; not randomized
- ⚠️ Internal inconsistencies: "six" recurrences listed as five Leipzig + three mold; abstract acute toxicity 57.3% vs 57.5% in results
- ⚠️ Median f/u 33 mo is short for NMSC late recurrence and late cosmesis
