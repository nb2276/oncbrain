# 2026-11-05

**Brantsch prospective cSCC cohort (n=615): 0/207 metastases at ≤2.0 mm thickness vs 16% >6.0 mm; desmoplasia drove local recurrence (HR 16.11) after clear-margin surgery alone.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Skin-only day. Four retrospective nasal/facial BCC/SCC RT series (kV, orthovoltage, HDR contact and plesiotherapy) report 5y local control or cure 85-93%. The nose and the field margin are where they fail. The prospective Brantsch surgery-alone cohort names thickness >6.0 mm and desmoplasia as the risk factors for selecting adjuvant RT.

## 🌞 Skin / Melanoma

Mostly single-institution retrospective RT series without a comparator. Short follow-up and treating-team cosmesis grading limit every one.

### Kilovoltage RT for skin carcinomas over nasal cartilage (671 lesions)

> 5-yr cure 88.09%, 10-yr 86.51% after kV RT for 671 nasal BCC/SCC lesions; 96.84% good/acceptable cosmesis, no complications reported.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Failure pattern is the RT read: 25 of 38 recurrences were marginal, with no dose or size relationship across 30–75 Gy at 5 Gy/fx. That argues field margin (0.5–1 cm, 1.5 cm for morpheaform) gates control more than total dose, supporting moderate-dose hypofractionated kV with generous margins and nostril shielding at the ala.

- 🔍 Retrospective single-series, 671 BCC and SCC lesions over nasal cartilage, treated 1972–2007
- 💊 Kilovoltage RT: 668 lesions conventional roentgentherapy (CRT), 3 HDRT + CRT
- 🔍 CRT technique
  - 55–60 kV, 4–6 mA, no filter, HVD 2–12 mm
  - 5 Gy/fx, 2 times per week
  - Total 30–75 Gy (mean 55.70 Gy, median 55 Gy)
  - Field margin 0.5–1 cm; 1.5 cm for 20 morpheaform BCCs
  - Ala nasi: 1 mm Pb lead rubber nostril shield to protect septal cartilage
- 📊 Outcomes (all 671 lesions)

  | Endpoint | n | % |
  |---|---|---|
  | CR at 1 mo | 663 | 98.80% |
  | PR | 3 (BCC) | 0.44% |
  | Recurrence | 38 (13 central, 25 marginal) | 5.66% |
  | 5-yr cure rate (actuarial) | n/a | 88.09% |
  | 10-yr cure rate (actuarial) | n/a | 86.51% |
  | Cosmesis good | 496 | 74.47% |
  | Cosmesis acceptable | 149 | 22.37% |
  | Cosmesis not acceptable | 16 | 2.40% |

- 📊 Recurrence onset 3–96 mo (mean 37.96 mo); 37 of 38 relapses were BCC
- 📐 No statistically significant link between relapse risk and dose or lesion size
- 📊 No complications or sequelae to RT observed to date
- ⚠️ Mean f/u 38.016 mo (range 1–351) roughly equals mean time to relapse; late failures likely undercounted
- ⚠️ 25 of 38 recurrences marginal: field margin, not dose, is the visible failure mode
- ⚠️ Cosmesis graded by treating team, no validated scale; no stats on dose vs cosmesis (sample too inhomogeneous)
- ⚠️ 'Superior to any other modality' rests on literature comparison, not a controlled comparator
- 🔗 Authors cite a randomized facial BCC trial in which surgery outperformed RT for lesions <4 cm, with mixed RT techniques

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Radiotherapy of carcinomas of the skin overlying the cartilage of the nose: our experience in 671 lesions** — Caccialanza, M; Piccinno, R; Percivalle, S et al.. *Journal of the European Academy of Dermatology and Venereology* (2009-09). [doi:10.1111/j.1468-3083.2009.03247.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1468-3083.2009.03247.x)
  - 📎 [[papers/skin/2009-caccialanza-radiotherapy-carcinomas-skin-overlying-cartilage-nose.pdf|Kilovoltage RT for skin carcinomas over nasal cartilage (671 lesions) (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions
> - Control vs margin-controlled excision at the nasal cartilage site
> - Late recurrence and cosmesis beyond 5 years
> - Optimal field margin for infiltrative histology

### Hypofractionated HDR plesiotherapy in nonmelanoma skin cancer

> 5y DFS 93.36% overall (Leipzig 94.9% vs mold 88%) with 3 Gy/fx HDR surface brachy across 134 BCC/SCC lesions; recurrent lesions fared worse.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The RT-relevant read is disease status, not applicator: recurrent lesions reached only 85.9% 5y DFS vs 95.6% for primaries (p=0.003). Acute G3 was 40.3% at 3 Gy x3/week, and definitive treatment carried higher acute toxicity than adjuvant (OR 2.928). Counsel recurrent and definitive-intent pts accordingly.

- 🔍 Retrospective single-institution series: 114 pts, 134 lesions (92 BCC, 42 SCC), Nov 2006 to Dec 2011
- 🔍 All clinically N0; analysis per tumor, not per patient
- 🔍 Treatment setting
  - Definitive: 75 BCC + 27 SCC (77.3%)
  - Adjuvant after excision with positive/close margins: 32 lesions (22.7%; 17 BCC, 15 SCC)
- 🔍 RT technique
  - Ir-192 HDR, 3 Gy/fx, 3 fx/week
  - Mean dose 48.79 Gy (45-54) BCC; 50.55 Gy (45-57) SCC
  - PTV = lesion + 0.5-1 cm margin; dose prescribed 3-5 mm below skin
  - Leipzig 2- or 3-cm applicator for flat lesions ≤2 cm (101 lesions, 75.4%)
  - CT-planned custom wax/plaster mold for raised surfaces or lesions >2 cm
- 📊 DFS by applicator (median f/u 33 mo)

  | Endpoint | Leipzig | Custom mold |
  |---|---|---|
  | 3y DFS | 94.9% | 93.1% |
  | 5y DFS | 94.9% | 88% |
  | Mean lesion size | 13.07 mm | 19.38 mm (p=0.004) |

- 📊 DFS by disease status

  | Endpoint | Primary | Recurrent | p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 3y DFS | 97.9% | 85.9% | 0.003 |
  | 5y DFS | 95.6% | 85.9% | 0.003 |

- 📊 Complete regression 98%; no regional or distant metastases
- 📊 BCC 3y/5y DFS 94.1%/91.3%; all 6 BCC failures after Leipzig applicators
- 📊 SCC: 2 local relapses (4.8%), 3y and 5y DFS 97.5% at median f/u 35 mo
- 📊 Acute toxicity (RTOG) per lesion
  - G1-2 57.5%, G3 40.3%, G4 2.2%
  - Definitive vs adjuvant: OR 2.928 (95% CI 1.287-6.659), p=0.013
  - Mold vs Leipzig: OR 2.25 (95% CI 1.012-5.003), p=0.067
  - Lesions 5-25 mm: OR 3.533 (95% CI 1.318-9.467), p=0.016
- 📊 Late G0-1 in 120 cases (95.2%); one G4 ulcer (0.8%)
- 📊 Cosmesis excellent/good 82%, fair 13%, not available 5%
- ⚠️ Applicator chosen by lesion size and contour, so the mold vs Leipzig comparison is confounded by size
- ⚠️ Internal inconsistency: 'six local recurrences (five Leipzig and three molds)' does not add up
- ⚠️ Acute ≥G2 reported as 57.3% in abstract vs G1-2 57.5% in results; G3 40.3% is high for 3 Gy/fx
- ⚠️ Toxicity OR 2.25 called borderline (p=0.067) though its CI 1.012-5.003 excludes 1
- ⚠️ Median f/u 33 mo is short for 5y DFS claims in BCC, where late recurrence is common

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Hypofractionated high-dose-rate plesiotherapy in nonmelanoma skin cancer treatment** — Arenas, Meritxell; Arguís, Monica; Díez-Presa, Lorena et al.. *Brachytherapy* (2015-11). [doi:10.1016/j.brachy.2015.09.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brachy.2015.09.001)
  - 📎 [[papers/skin/2015-arenas-hypofractionated-high-dose-rate-plesiotherapy.pdf|Hypofractionated HDR plesiotherapy in nonmelanoma skin cancer (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions
> - HDR plesiotherapy vs excision, Mohs or electrons in a randomized comparison
> - Optimal fractionation for HDR skin brachytherapy
> - Dose or margin escalation for recurrent NMSC

### Radiotherapy for nasal skin SCC, Princess Margaret experience

> Local failure-free rate 90% at 2y and 85% at 5y with mostly hypofractionated orthovoltage RT for nasal skin SCC; CSS 96%, no G4 late toxicity.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Failures cluster where tumor extent is easy to underestimate: 6 of 10 local failures were on the bridge, 3 of 4 bridge recurrences after CR had a marginal component, and 2 of 5 columella lesions failed. That argues for generous margins (1 cm to field edge was typical) and careful depth estimation at those sites.

- 🔍 Retrospective single-institution chart review, 1982-1993; 100 referred, 94 assessable (6 lost to f/u)
- 🔍 T1 60, T2 11, T3 0, T4 7, TX 16; only 1 pt node-positive at presentation
- 🔍 RT alone 63 (67%); surgery then postop RT 31 (33%)
- 💊 Postop RT indications (n=31)
  - Gross residual after surgery: n=15
  - Positive margin: n=15
  - Submandibular nodal failure after surgical primary control: n=1
- 🔍 Typical dose by lesion size
  - <2 cm: 35 Gy/5 fx
  - 2-5 cm: 45 Gy/10 fx
  - >5 cm or bone/cartilage invasion: 50 Gy/20 fx
- 🔍 Modality: orthovoltage 76 (81%), electrons 13 (14%), cobalt 4 (4%), MV photons 1
- 📊 Actuarial outcomes from start of RT

  | Endpoint | 2y | 5y |
  |---|---|---|
  | Local failure-free rate | 90% (SE 3%) | 85% (SE 5%) |
  | Overall survival | 75% (SE 4%) | 51% (SE 6%) |
  | Cause-specific survival | 96% (SE 2%) | 96% (SE 2%) |

- 📊 Local failures (n=10) by site and pattern
  - Bridge 6, columella 2, side of nose to cheek/canthus 1, tip 1
  - Persistent disease despite RT: 4 pts
  - Relapse after no gross disease: 3 marginal, 2 in-field, 1 both
  - Columella: 2 of 5 lesions failed
- 📊 No elective nodal RT; regional failure in only 2 of 93 pts without nodes at presentation
- 📊 Salvage in 8 pts (surgery 4, re-RT 4); 5 disease-free at last f/u
- 📊 No RTOG G4 late toxicity; no cartilage or bone necrosis despite 81% orthovoltage
- ⚠️ Late toxicity and cosmesis not formally scored
- ⚠️ Univariate analysis found no prognostic factor; only 10 local failure events
- 🔗 vs Mazeron et al. (1676 nose/vestibule skin cases): 11% local failure in SCC, similar to 10% at 2y here

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Radiotherapy Management for Squamous Cell Carcinoma of the Nasal Skin: The Princess Margaret Hospital Experience** — May N. Tsao; Richard W. Tsang; Fei-Fei Liu et al.. *International Journal of Radiation Oncology Biology Physics* (2002)
  - 📎 [[papers/skin/2002-tsao-radiotherapy-management-squamous-cell-carcinoma-nasal.pdf|Radiotherapy for nasal skin SCC, Princess Margaret experience (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions
> - Optimal dose-fractionation balancing cosmesis and local control
> - Better margin and depth definition for bridge and columella lesions
> - Which cN0 nasal SCC pts warrant elective nodal RT

### Brantsch cSCC prognostic risk factors (prospective, n=615)

> No metastases at ≤2.0 mm thickness (0/207); >6.0 mm metastasized in 16%, and desmoplasia drove local recurrence (HR 16.11) after clear-margin surgery.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> No pt received adjuvant RT and every margin was clear, so this is the surgery-alone baseline adjuvant RT is weighed against: desmoplastic SCC (where all PNI sat) recurred locally in 12/51 (24%), and >6.0 mm tumours metastasized in 14/90 (16%), always first to regional nodes.

- 🔍 Prospective single-centre cohort (Tübingen), 653 enrolled, 615 assessable, 1990-2001
- 🔍 All pts resected with complete 3D-histology margin control; all margins tumour-free
- 🔍 No pt received adjuvant RT: this is a surgery-alone natural history
- 📊 Median f/u 43 mo; 26 (4%) metastases, 20 (3%) local recurrences
- 📊 Events by tumour thickness

  | Thickness | Metastasis | Local recurrence |
  |---|---|---|
  | ≤2.0 mm (n=207) | 0 | 1 |
  | 2.1–6.0 mm (n=318) | 12 (4%) | 8 (3%) |
  | >6.0 mm (n=90) | 14 (16%) | 11 (12%) |

- 📐 Best multivariate Cox model, independent factors (HR, 95% CI)

  | Factor | Metastasis | Local recurrence |
  |---|---|---|
  | Tumour thickness | 4.79 (2.22–10.36), p<0.0001 | 6.03 (2.71–13.43), p<0.0001 |
  | Immunosuppression | 4.32 (1.62–11.52), p=0.0035 | not in model |
  | Ear location | 3.61 (1.51–8.67), p=0.0040 | not in model |
  | Horizontal size | 2.22 (1.18–4.15), p=0.0128 | not in model |
  | Desmoplastic growth | not in model | 16.11 (6.57–39.49), p<0.0001 |

- 📊 Desmoplastic SCC: local recurrence 12/51 (24%) vs 8/564 (1%) non-desmoplastic
- 🔍 Perineural or perivascular invasion was always accompanied by desmoplasia in this series
- 📐 Discrimination: thickness-based best model vs current TNM model (C-index)

  | Endpoint | Best model | TNM model |
  |---|---|---|
  | Metastasis | 0.837 | 0.743 |
  | Local recurrence | 0.907 | 0.750 |

- 📐 Internal validation sample (n=415)
  - Mets, thickness >6.0 mm: HR 5.88 (2.36–14.69), p<0.0001
  - Mets, poor differentiation: 0.72 (0.26–2.02), p=0.5296
  - LR, thickness >6.0 mm: HR 5.98 (2.06–17.37), p=0.0010
  - LR, desmoplasia: 15.16 (5.20–44.20), p<0.0001
- 📊 First metastasis always regional nodal; 19 within year 1, none after year 4
- ⚠️ Events per variable 26:7 (mets) and 20:7 (LR), below the accepted floor of 5
- ⚠️ White pts only, university referral centre; 38 lost to follow-up

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Analysis of risk factors determining prognosis of cutaneous squamous-cell carcinoma: a prospective study** — Brantsch, Kay D; Meisner, Christoph; Schönfisch, Birgitt et al.. *The Lancet Oncology* (2008-08). [doi:10.1016/s1470-2045(08)70178-5](https://doi.org/10.1016/s1470-2045(08%2970178-5)
  - 📎 [[papers/skin/2008-brantsch-analysis-risk-factors-determining-prognosis-cutaneous.pdf|Brantsch cSCC prognostic risk factors (prospective, n=615) (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions
> - Role of sentinel-lymph-node biopsy in tumours >6.0 mm
> - Value of structured nodal ultrasound surveillance in high-risk pts
> - Does adjuvant RT reduce local failure in desmoplastic cSCC?

### HDR contact brachytherapy with customized molds for facial cutaneous carcinoma

> 5y actuarial LC 89% for ≤3 mm thick facial BCC/SCC: 100% on flat skin, 83% on nose, with nasal tip failing 4 of 15.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The failure was geometric: all 5 relapses were on the nose, all with 3-catheter molds, two at the field border, in tumors of only 7-20 mm. Nasal tip LC was 73% vs 95% for pyramid/wing. That changes mold design (more than three catheters, full lateral-margin coverage) and sends nasal tip lesions to another technique.

- 🔍 Retrospective, single institution (Valencia), 51 facial lesions in 48 pts, treated 1999 to 2005
- 🔍 Population
  - BCC 42 (82.4%), SCC 9 (17.6%)
  - Mean diameter 1.5 cm (range 0.5-4 cm)
  - Thickness ≤3 mm in all; thicker lesions went to electrons
  - Radical intent 95%, adjuvant 5% (lateral margin involved)
  - Median age 78 (range 53-91)
- 💊 Ir-192 HDR contact fractionation used (3 fx/wk)

  | Dose/fx | Fractions | Total dose | Share of cases |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 4 Gy | 11-12 | 44-48 Gy | 57% |
  | 3 Gy | 17-18 | 51-57 Gy | 37% |
  | 7 Gy (2 fx/wk) | 5 | 35 Gy | 6% |

- 🔍 Standard schedule 48 Gy in 12 fx of 4 Gy, overall time 4 weeks
- 📐 Prescribed 3 mm below skin, 1 cm margin; skin surface usually around 150% of dose, never more
- 🔍 Nasal lesions: wax molds, CT-planned; flat surfaces: 0.5 cm bolus with tubes 1 cm apart
- 📊 Local control by subsite
- 📊 Actuarial LC 91% at 2y, 89% at 5y and 8y; median f/u 45 mo (range 4-102)
- 📊 5 relapses (9.8%) at 6-32 mo: 3 in-field, 2 at field border
- ⚠️ All 5 relapses were on the nose and all had used only 3 catheters
- 📊 All relapses salvaged by surgery; no nodal or distant spread, no cancer deaths
- 📊 Toxicity (RTOG/EORTC)
  - G1-2 dermatitis: 30 pts (78%), resolved in days
  - G4 dermatitis: 11 pts (21%), healed in 3 weeks as outpatients
  - No late complications reported, including no nasal chondritis
  - Cosmesis good or very good in all cases
- ⚠️ G4 dermatitis rate is internally inconsistent: 21% in Results, 12% in Discussion
- 🔗 vs Guix et al contact HDR-BT (136 lesions, 60-65 Gy at 1.8 Gy/session): 5y actuarial LC 98%
- ⚠️ Three schedules applied without protocol assignment, so no dose-response read is possible

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Treatment of facial cutaneous carcinoma with high–dose rate contact brachytherapy with customized molds** — Maroñas, Maria; Guinot, Jose Luis; Arribas, Leoncio et al.. *Brachytherapy* (2011-05). [doi:10.1016/j.brachy.2010.08.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brachy.2010.08.005)
  - 📎 [[papers/skin/2011-maronas-treatment-facial-cutaneous-carcinoma-high-dose-rate.pdf|HDR contact brachytherapy with customized molds for facial cutaneous carcinoma (full text)]]
