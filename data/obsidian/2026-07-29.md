# 2026-07-29

**DBCG Skagen 1: nodal RT hypofractionation confirmed — 3yr lymphedema 8.0% (40Gy/15fx) vs 9.4% (50Gy/25fx), OR 0.84, noninferior.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Hypofractionation crosses into the regional nodal volume: 15 fractions matched 25 for arm lymphedema at 3yr with no locoregional recurrence penalty at 8yr (HR 0.96). Breast carried the day's only signal. BC mortality HR 1.25 (0.93-1.66) is the number to watch, on a trial not powered for it.

## 🎀 Breast

The START/FAST-Forward hypofractionation case extended to locoregional RT, where node-positive pts were thinly represented in the original trials.

### DBCG Skagen Trial 1

> 3yr lymphedema 8.0% (40Gy/15fx) vs 9.4% (50Gy/25fx), OR 0.84 (0.62-1.14), noninferior; no recurrence or mortality differences at 8yr.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The lymphedema signal that kept 50Gy/25fx alive for nodal volumes does not appear: 8.0% vs 9.4% at 3yr, OR 0.84 (0.62-1.14). Locoregional recurrence HR 0.96 (0.62-1.51) says the shorter course does not trade control for convenience, so 15 fractions becomes defensible when the nodes are in the field.

- 📊 1° EP arm lymphedema at 3yr: 8.0% with 40Gy/15fx vs 9.4% with 50Gy/25fx
- 📐 OR 0.84 (95% CI 0.62-1.14), P=.27, inside the prespecified +5-percentage-point noninferiority margin
- 🔍 Phase III noninferiority, 17 Danish centers, 2015-2021, ITT n=2,908 (1,464 40Gy vs 1,444 50Gy)
- 🔍 Locoregional RT (not breast/chest wall alone) in high-risk BC; median age 57 (range 23-86)
- 📊 8-year cancer outcomes, 40Gy/15fx vs 50Gy/25fx (HR, 95% CI)

  | Endpoint | HR | 95% CI |
  |---|---|---|
  | Locoregional recurrence | 0.96 | 0.62 to 1.51 |
  | Distant recurrence | 1.10 | 0.89 to 1.37 |
  | BC mortality | 1.25 | 0.93 to 1.66 |
  | All-cause mortality | 1.08 | 0.85 to 1.36 |

- 🔍 Accrual continued until 3yr lymphedema estimates were available in 1,012 pts; cancer follow-up median 5.25 yrs (IQR 4.26-6.96)
- ⚠️ Lymphedema follow-up (median 4.1 yrs) is short for a late RT endpoint; brachial plexopathy and cardiac/lung late effects not reported in source abstract
- ⚠️ BC mortality HR 1.25 (0.93-1.66) is directionally unfavorable and the CI does not exclude a real excess; the trial was not powered for mortality
- 🔗 Extends the UK hypofractionation evidence (START/FAST-Forward lineage) into the nodal volume, where the START trials enrolled few node-positive pts with regional coverage

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Hypo- Versus Standard Fractionated Locoregional Radiotherapy of Patients With High-Risk Breast Cancer in the Randomized Phase III Trial: The Danish Breast Cancer Group Skagen Trial 1** — Offersen, Birgitte V.; Alsner, Jan; Høgsbjerg, Kristine et al.. *Journal of Clinical Oncology* (2026-07). [doi:10.1200/jco-25-02705](https://doi.org/10.1200/jco-25-02705). [article](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO-25-02705)
  > PURPOSE Adjuvant radiotherapy for node-positive breast cancer (BC) using 50Gy/25fx has been Danish Breast Cancer Group (DBCG) standard. Hypofractionated radiotherapy based on 40Gy/15fx has been increasingly used; however, it is less frequently for locoregional therapy because of concern over more morbidity. DBCG Skagen trial 1 hypothesized that 40Gy/15fx did not cause more lymphedema than 50Gy/25fx 3 years after radiotherapy without compromising the pattern of failure. METHODS Skagen trial 1 is a phase III, noninferiority trial randomly assigning high-risk BC patients with an indication for locoregional radiotherapy to standard 50Gy/25fx versus experimental 40Gy/15fx. The primary end point was arm lymphedema; assuming a 3-year incidence with 50Gy/25fx of 10%, noninferiority was predefined to maximum 5% excess incidence with 40Gy/15fx. Accrual continued until 3-year estimates were reported in 1,012 patients. RESULTS Between 2015 and 2021, 2,963 patients consented from 17 centers; the intention-to-treat cohort comprised 2,908 patients: 1,444 had 50Gy (50%), and 1,464 had 40Gy (50%). The median age was 57 years (range, 23-86). At a median follow-up of 4.1 years (IQR, 3.0-5.0), the 3-year rates of lymphedema were 9.4% (50Gy) versus 8.0% (40Gy), odds ratio 0.84 (95% CI, 0.62 to 1.14), and P = .27, thus within the +5-percentage-point noninferiority margin. The median follow-up for cancer outcomes was 5.25 years (IQR, 4.26 to 6.96). Within 8 years, the hazard ratio for locoregional recurrence was 0.96 (95% CI, 0.62 to 1.51), that for distant recurrence was 1.10 (95% CI, 0.89 to 1.37), that for BC mortality was 1.25 (95% CI, 0.93 to 1.66), and that for all-cause mortality was 1.08 (95% CI, 0.85 to 1.36), thus all with no differences by random assignment. CONCLUSION 40Gy/15fx for locoregional radiotherapy of BC did not result in more lymphedema compared with 50Gy/25fx. There were no differences in locoregional or distant recurrences, breast cancer mortality, nor all-cause mortality between the random assignment arms.
