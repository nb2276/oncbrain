# 2026-09-26

**LAVA-CRLM: SBRT vs MWA 1-yr FFLP 83.0% vs 77.7% (HR 0.87, p=0.70), acute G2+ toxicity 3% vs 25% in 1-3 oligometastatic CRLM.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Oligo-mets carried the day. In a randomised phase II, SBRT (45 Gy/3 fx, 88% MR-linac adaptive) showed no local control difference vs MWA for small CRLM, with less early toxicity. It was powered for HR 0.38, not equivalence, and median lesion size was 1.2 cm. Intermediate-size lesions await COLLISION-XL.

## 🎯 Oligometastatic / Mets

SBRT vs thermal ablation for small CRLM: first randomised LC data, but the trial was not built to show equivalence.

### LAVA-CRLM · [[NCT03654131]]

> 1-yr freedom from local progression 83.0% SBRT vs 77.7% MWA (HR 0.87, p=0.70); treatment-related G2+ toxicity 3% vs 25% in oligometastatic CRLM.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The dose and platform set the transfer bar: 45 Gy/3 fx at the 67% isodose, BED10 >173 Gy to GTV, 88% on MR-linac with daily adaptation, and no G3+ toxicity vs 4 (8%) after MWA. SBRT's case rests on that toxicity gap for small lesions, not on a local control win.

- 🔍 Randomised phase II, 1:1, two Copenhagen centres, N=100 randomised, mITT 92 (SBRT 45, MWA 47)
- 🔍 1-3 oligometastatic CRLM, each ≤4.0 cm, Child-Pugh A, MDT-judged suitable for BOTH MWA and SBRT
- 📊 1° EP: freedom from local progression, mITT; masked radiology review, RECIST 1.1
- 📊 Efficacy by arm (median f/u 35.0 mo SBRT, 34.8 mo MWA for LC)

  | Endpoint | SBRT | MWA | Effect |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1-yr FFLP, mITT | 83.0% (72.3-95.4) | 77.7% (66.5-90.9) | HR 0.87 (0.43-1.74), p=0.70 |
  | 1-yr FFLP, per-protocol | 86.0% (75.3-98.2) | 77.7% (66.5-90.9) | HR 0.78 (0.37-1.63), p=0.50 |
  | 3-yr OS, mITT | 71.5% (58.3-87.7) | 63.3% (49.3-81.2) | HR 0.91 (0.48-1.70), p=0.80 |

- 📊 Treatment-related toxicity by arm (safety population, SBRT n=40, MWA n=52)

  | Toxicity | SBRT | MWA | Test |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | G2+ acute (≤1 mo) | 1 (3%) | 13 (25%) | n/a |
  | G3+ acute (≤1 mo) | 0 (0%) | 4 (8%), incl 1 death | Fisher p=0.13 |
  | 1-yr cumulative G2+ | 7.5% (1.9-18.4) | 20.7% (10.6-33.1) | Fine-Gray HR 0.51 (0.21-1.24) |
  | 3-yr cumulative G2+ | 24.6% (9.8-42.9) | 32.7% (18.5-47.6) | Gray's p=0.13 |

- 🔍 SBRT technique
  - Preferred 45 Gy/3 fx over 1 wk (32 pts); 50 Gy/5 fx (8 pts) when OAR constraints forced it
  - Prescribed to PTV at 67% isodose, normalised to mean 100% GTV
  - PTV margin 10 mm craniocaudal, 5 mm radial; target cropping permitted
  - All regimens BED10 >173 Gy to GTV
  - 35 (88%) on MR-linac with daily online adaptation; 5 (13%) on conventional linac
- ⚠️ Powered for a large difference (assumed HR 0.38), not equivalence; CIs compatible with clinically relevant gaps
- ⚠️ 5 pts randomised to SBRT crossed to MWA (preference, breath-hold, OAR constraints)
- ⚠️ KRAS-mutant imbalance: 21 (45%) MWA vs 11 (24%) SBRT; no stratification, no systematic profiling
- ⚠️ All treated lesions ≤3.0 cm despite ≤4.0 cm eligibility; median size 1.2 cm
- 🔗 Authors cite Kang: BED10 >117 Gy needed for ~90% 1-yr LC; this trial exceeded it
- 🔗 COLLISION-XL (ongoing) addresses intermediate-size lesions this trial did not reach
