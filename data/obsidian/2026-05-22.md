# 2026-05-22

**PEACE V-STORM: elective pelvic nodal RT lifts 4yr MFS 76% vs 63% over MDT alone, HR 0·62 (80% CI 0·44-0·86), p=0·063.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Two field-size questions, same answer direction: covering more elective nodal volume bought outcome in both PEACE V-STORM (pelvic ENRT over MDT, locoregional RFS 85% vs 62%) and DBCG IMN2 (IMNI, 15y OS 65.0% vs 60.8%, HR 0.85). Prostate and breast carried the day, though neither is a conventionally powered randomised read: α=0·20 in one, laterality-allocated in the other. Lymphoma adds expert-opinion dose guidance only.

## 🌐 Lymphoma

Reduced-dose RT algorithm across cutaneous lymphoma subtypes, built entirely on retrospective and small single-arm data.

### EORTC Cutaneous Lymphoma Tumour Group RT Recommendations

> Expert-opinion dose recommendations consolidating reduced-dose RT (4-12 Gy) across cutaneous lymphoma subtypes; no randomised trials underpin any of it.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The operative number for an RT reader is the dose floor, not the ceiling: 8-12 Gy in two or three fractions holds ≥92% 1-yr local control in MF, while 4 Gy underperforms there but suffices for CD4+ small/medium T-LPD (100% remission, no relapses). That split is the prescribing decision, and it argues against one ultra-low-dose default across subtypes.

- 🔍 EORTC cutaneous lymphoma tumour group expert opinion, not a systematic review or Delphi panel; output is a dose algorithm (Fig 2A/2B)
- 💊 MF plaques/tumours: 8-12 Gy in two or three fractions, 1-yr local control ≥92%
- ⚠️ 4 Gy in one or two fractions gave inferior local control in MF per the same source
- 💊 pcALCL: RT effective with few relapses at doses of 20 Gy; palliative 2×4 Gy for symptom relief
- 💊 CD4+ small/medium-sized T-LPD: 4 Gy in two fractions, 100% remission, no local relapses
- 💊 pcMZL/pcFCL: 4 Gy can be curative in many cases; lesions >5 cm treated with 4 Gy did worse
- ⚠️ The ultra-low-dose (4 Gy) evidence in indolent B-cell disease is contested, not settled
  - Small retrospective series found comparable response and in-field recurrence vs higher dose
  - A longer-follow-up retrospective found 4 Gy gave inferior response and more in-field relapse
  - Authors' fix is a response-adapted escalation to a cumulative 24 Gy for residual/failure after 4 Gy
- 💊 TSEBT: 8-12 Gy to palliate advanced skin disease; up to 24 Gy pre-autologous transplant
- ⚠️ Sézary syndrome: TSEBT stays controversial, rarely achieves long-term remission
- 💊 DLBCL leg type: post-systemic consolidation dose may drop to 30 Gy; 20 Gy reported with lower toxicity
- ⚠️ Every table row is retrospective or small single-arm prospective; authors state no randomized trials support choosing RT over other modalities

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Radiotherapy in cutaneous lymphomas: Recommendations from the EORTC cutaneous lymphoma tumour group** — Khaled Elsayad; Emmanuella Guenova; Chalid Assaf et al.. *European Journal of Cancer* (2024). [doi:10.1016/j.ejca.2024.115064](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejca.2024.115064)
  - 📎 [[papers/lymphoma/radiotherapy-in-cutaneous-lymphomas-recommendations-from-the-eor.pdf|EORTC Cutaneous Lymphoma Tumour Group RT Recommendations (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions
> - Dose floor for ultra-low-dose RT in indolent cutaneous B-cell lymphoma
> - Whether low-dose TSEBT plus immunotherapy prolongs remission in advanced MF/SS
> - RT dose after systemic therapy in DLBCL leg type

## 🎀 Breast

IMNI benefit at 15y, including the guideline-contested 1-3 node group, but allocation was by laterality rather than randomisation.

### DBCG IMN2 · [[NCT06549920]]

> IMNI cut 15y mortality: OS 65.0% vs 60.8%, adjusted HR 0.85 (0.76-0.94), p=0.0016, in 4541 node-positive pts.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The 1-3 node group (n=3100, HR 0.85, 0.73-0.97) is the whole point: that is exactly where guidelines allow IMNI omission, and no measured factor identified a safe-omission subgroup. Right-sided IMN CTV V90% coverage was 94.6% with 25% under 64.8%, so a modern gated VMAT plan should exceed the dose separation that produced this 4.2% 15y OS gain.

- 🔍 Nationwide prospective cohort 2007-14, N=4541; laterality-allocated (right-sided → IMNI, left-sided → no IMNI), ITT by side
- 📐 RT dose 48Gy/24Fx before Jan 2009, 50Gy/25Fx after; IMN target intercostal space 1-4
- 📐 3D conformal wide tangents, free-breathing; no gating in study era
- 📊 IMNI vs no IMNI, adjusted HRs across the three endpoints

  | Endpoint | IMNI | No IMNI | Adjusted HR (95% CI), p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | OS at 15y | 65.0% | 60.8% | 0.85 (0.76-0.94), p=0.0016 |
  | BC mortality at 15y | 21.4% | 23.6% | 0.84 (0.74-0.95), p=0.0077 |
  | Distant mets at 15y | 25.1% | 26.9% | 0.87 (0.78-0.98), p=0.026 |

- 📊 1-3 positive nodes (n=3100): HR 0.85 (0.73-0.97), the guideline-contested group
- 📊 15y RD for OS 4.2% (95% CI 0.9-7.5); 10y RD 3.1% (0.7-5.6)
- 📊 IMN CTV V90% dose coverage by side (RT QA, n=2837 plans)
  - Right-sided median 94.6% (IQR 64.8-100.0)
  - Left-sided median 20.4% (IQR 0.9-55.8)
  - IMNI reported in 10.1% of left-sided pts (n=238), diluting the contrast
- 📐 15y death from ischemic/valvular heart disease 0.2% right-sided vs 0.7% left-sided
- 📐 15y death from second lung cancer 1.8% right vs 2.2% left
- 🔗 vs EBCTCG regional-node meta-analysis rate ratio 0.90 (0.84-0.96); vs KROG 08-06 HR 0.87 (0.57-1.31), underpowered
- ⚠️ Not randomised: allocation by laterality (Mendelian-randomisation framing), so left-sided efficacy is never directly demonstrated
- ⚠️ ER-/HER2+ subgroup HR 1.49 (0.98-2.25) disfavouring IMNI, interaction p=0.021, explorative and unadjusted for multiplicity
- ⚠️ No smoking, cardiac comorbidity, or lung morbidity data; cardiac harm assessed only as death, not events

Mølby Nielsen; Lise B. J. Thorsen; Demet Özcan et al.. *The Lancet Regional Health - Europe* (2025-02). [doi:10.1016/j.lanepe.2024.101160](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lanepe.2024.101160)
  - 📎 [[papers/breast/internal-mammary-node-irradiation-in-4541-node-positive-breast-c.pdf|DBCG IMN2 (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions
> - Effect of IMNI alongside immunotherapy and antibody-drug conjugates
> - Is ER-/HER2+ a genuine predictive subtype for IMNI harm
> - Safe RT omission in cN+ pts with pCR after neoadjuvant therapy

## 🌰 Prostate

First randomised comparison of pelvic ENRT vs MDT alone for PET-detected nodal oligorecurrence, read at α=0·20 with 80% CIs.

### PEACE V-STORM · [[NCT03569241]]

> 4yr MFS 76% vs 63% with elective pelvic nodal RT over MDT, HR 0·62 (80% CI 0·44-0·86), p=0·063.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The clean radiation read is locoregional control, not MFS: 85% vs 62% at 4 years, HR 0·45 (80% CI 0·31-0·65), and pelvic nodal relapse fell from 29% to 8%. Toxicity did not follow the field: grade 2+ GI 9% vs 7%. Prostate bed inclusion, not pelvic volume, drove GI events (OR 4·6 [95% CI 1·5-15·8]).

- 🔍 Phase 2 open-label RCT, 21 hospitals in 6 countries, N=196 randomised, 190 evaluable (MDT 97, ENRT 93)
- 🔍 Eligibility: up to 5 PET-detected pelvic nodes (choline or PSMA) after radical local treatment; pelvis defined to aortic bifurcation, common iliac included
- 🔍 Both arms got 6 months ADT, so the randomised question is nodal field size, not systemic intensity
- 📐 RT prescriptions by arm
  - MDT: SBRT 30 Gy/3 fx every other day, 80% of max dose, covering 90% of PTV, 3 mm PTV margin
  - MDT alternative: salvage LND, bilateral extended dissection of the true pelvis (12 pts total across both arms)
  - ENRT: 45 Gy/25 fx whole pelvis (modified RTOG 2009 template, upper limit L4-L5) with SIB 65 Gy to PET-positive nodes
  - Prostate bed RT advised for high-risk features in either arm, at least 66 Gy/33 fx
- 📊 Oncological endpoints, 4-year rates (80% CIs, per the trial's α=0·20 design)

  | Endpoint | MDT | ENRT | HR (80% CI) | p |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | MFS (1°) | 63% (56-69) | 76% (69-81) | 0·62 (0·44-0·86) | 0·063 |
  | Biochemical RFS | 41% (34-47) | 57% (50-64) | 0·62 (0·48-0·80) | 0·014 |
  | Locoregional RFS | 62% (55-69) | 85% (80-90) | 0·45 (0·31-0·65) | 0·0047 |
  | ADT-free survival | 60% (53-67) | 77% (70-82) | 0·60 (0·43-0·83) | 0·049 |

- 📊 Pattern of failure explains the MFS gain: pelvic nodal relapse 29% MDT vs 8% ENRT, M1a nodal spread 28% vs 14%
- ⚠️ The design lets a lot through, and the primary endpoint is the weakest signal on the card
  - α set at 0·20 two-sided, so p=0·063 counts as positive here but would not at conventional α
  - 80% CIs throughout, not 95%, so the intervals understate uncertainty against usual reading
  - MFS counts pelvic nodal and local relapse as non-events, and PET was not centrally reviewed
  - 6 pts who received no protocol treatment excluded from all analyses (modified ITT)
- ⚠️ Subgroup benefit concentrated in PSA <1 (HR 0·27 [0·14-0·52], interaction p=0·029) and PSMA-staged pts (HR 0·48 [0·32-0·72]); post-hoc for PSA, wide CIs
- 🔗 vs GETUG-P07 OLIGOPELVIS (single-arm ENRT): 3yr bRFS 45% vs 57% at 4yr here, but that trial had higher median PSA (3-4 ng/mL) and choline staging
- 🔗 POINTER-PC phase 3 is now recruiting; GETUG-P12 OLIGOPELVIS 2 tests ENRT+ADT vs intermittent ADT alone
