# 2026-05-22

**PEACE V-STORM: 4-yr MFS 76% vs 63% with elective nodal RT vs MDT, HR 0·62, and DBCG IMN2 puts 15y OS 65.0% vs 60.8% behind internal mammary nodal irradiation.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Two nodal-coverage trials, same lesson: treating the at-risk nodal basin rather than the visible lesion buys durable control. Prostate leads (PEACE V-STORM cuts pelvic relapse 29%→8%, ADT-free survival 77% vs 60%, no added G2+ GI/GU), breast follows (IMN2 extends the OS gain to 1-3 positive nodes, HR 0.85). Lymphoma is dose de-escalation, not coverage.

## 🌐 Lymphoma

Expert opinion, not evidence: 8-12 Gy is the working standard for indolent cutaneous lymphoma, and the 4 Gy question stays contested.

### EORTC Cutaneous Lymphoma Tumour Group RT Recommendations

> EORTC expert opinion consolidates reduced-dose RT (4-12 Gy) as standard for indolent cutaneous lymphomas, with a dose algorithm across subtypes.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The actionable content is the dose ladder, not a result: 8-12 Gy in 2-3 fractions for MF lesions, 4 Gy in 2 fractions for CD4+ SMPLPD, 20 Gy for pcALCL, and a response-adapted climb to 24 Gy when 4 Gy fails in pcMZL/pcFCL. The 4 Gy-vs-higher-dose relapse gap (28 % vs 5 %) is the number that should gate ultra-low-dose use outside palliation.

- 🧭 Expert-opinion recommendations, not a systematic review; no randomised trials support radiation-based therapy selection over other modalities (per the paper)
- 📐 MF plaques/tumours: usual dose 8-12 Gy in two or three fractions, with 1-year local control ≥ 92 % reported after low-dose RT
- ⚠️ 4 Gy (one or two fractions) gave an inferior local control rate in MF, so the paper does not treat 4 Gy as interchangeable with 8-12 Gy
- 📐 Low-dose TSEBT 8-12 Gy for palliation in advanced disease; up to 24 Gy to induce remission before autologous stem cell transplant
- 📐 pcALCL: RT effective with few relapses at doses of 20 Gy; palliative 2×4 Gy for prompt symptom relief
- 📐 CD4+ small/medium-sized T-cell lymphoproliferative disorder: 4 Gy in two fractions, 100 % remission and no local relapses in the cited series
- 📐 pcMZL/pcFCL: 4 Gy can be curative in many cases; lesions > 5 cm treated with 4 Gy had worse outcomes
- ⚠️ Ultra-low-dose 4 Gy is contested in pcMZL/pcFCL: the ILROG register series (n=440) reported local relapse 28 % vs 5 % (p < 0.001) against 8-50 Gy
- 🔗 Response-adapted escalation to a cumulative 24 Gy is proposed for residual disease or local failure after 4 Gy
- 📐 DLBCL leg type: consolidation after systemic therapy may be reduced to 30 Gy; 20 Gy consolidation reported with relatively lower toxicity
- ⚠️ Tables 1 and 2 arrived with uncertain row/value association in the source extraction, so per-series dose-to-outcome pairings beyond those stated in the body text are not attributed here

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Radiotherapy in cutaneous lymphomas: Recommendations from the EORTC cutaneous lymphoma tumour group** — Khaled Elsayad; Emmanuella Guenova; Chalid Assaf et al.. *European Journal of Cancer* (2024). [doi:10.1016/j.ejca.2024.115064](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejca.2024.115064)
  - 📎 [[papers/lymphoma/radiotherapy-in-cutaneous-lymphomas-recommendations-from-the-eor.pdf|EORTC Cutaneous Lymphoma Tumour Group RT Recommendations (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions
> - Ultra-low-dose 4 Gy vs higher dose in pcMZL/pcFCL: durability unresolved
> - Prolonging remission after low-dose TSEBT in advanced MF and SS
> - Whether low-dose TSEBT plus immunotherapy improves response duration

## 🎀 Breast

IMNI's OS benefit holds on modern systemic therapy and extends into the 1-3 node subgroup most guidelines still exclude, from a laterality-allocated cohort with real contamination in both directions.

### DBCG IMN2 · [[NCT06549920]]

> 15y OS 65.0% vs 60.8% with IMNI, adjusted HR 0.85 (0.76-0.94), p=0.0016, in 4541 node-positive pts on modern systemic therapy.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The 1-3 node subgroup is the whole point: HR 0.85 (0.73-0.97) in 3100 pts, the population several guidelines exclude from IMNI. Delivered with plain 3D wide tangents, 48-50Gy, free-breathing, so the benefit does not require gating or protons. Cardiac death 0.2% vs 0.7% at 15y argues the historic trade-off has largely dissolved.

- 📊 1° EP OS: adjusted HR 0.85 (95% CI 0.76-0.94), p=0.0016; crude HR 0.85 (0.77-0.95), p=0.0024
- 📐 Absolute 15y OS gain 4.2% (95% CI 0.9-7.5); 10y RD 3.1% (0.7-5.6); 5y RD 1.7% (-0.2;3.5), ns
- 🔍 Nationwide prospective cohort, N=4541, 2007-14, six RT centres; IMNI allocated by LATERALITY (right yes, left no), ITT by side, median f/u 13.7y
- 📐 RT: 48Gy/24Fx (26.2%) or 50Gy/25Fx (73.2%), 3D conformal wide tangents in free-breathing; IMN target intercostal space 1-4; axilla II-III + level IV all pts
- 🔗 IMNI effect held in 1-3 positive nodes (n=3100): HR 0.85 (0.73-0.97), the exact subgroup most guidelines currently exclude
- ⚠️ ER-/HER2+ subgroup ran the other way: HR 1.49 (0.98-2.25), interaction p=0.021, explorative and unadjusted for multiplicity
- ⚠️ Contamination both directions: IMNI reported in 10.1% of left-sided pts (n=238) and 344 right-sided pts treated without it, biasing the ITT estimate toward null
- ⚠️ Dose separation imperfect: median IMN CTV V90% 94.6% (IQR 64.8-100.0) right vs 20.4% (IQR 0.9-55.8) left, so left-sided pts got real incidental dose
- 🔗 vs KROG 08-06 (n=735), the only other 3D-based IMNI study: OS HR 0.87 (0.57-1.31), ns but underpowered; vs EBCTCG regional nodal RT rate ratio 0.90 (0.84-0.96)
- 📊 Cardiac + lung safety at 15 years, right-sided (IMNI) vs left-sided (no IMNI)

  | Cause of death, 15y cumulative incidence | Right-sided (IMNI) | Left-sided (no IMNI) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Ischemic or valvular heart disease | 0.2% (95% CI 0.0-0.5) | 0.7% (95% CI 0.4-1.2) |
  | Secondary lung cancer | 1.8% (95% CI 1.2-2.5) | 2.2% (95% CI 1.6-2.9) |

- 📊 Secondary endpoints, adjusted

  | Endpoint | IMNI 15y | No IMNI 15y | Adjusted HR (95% CI), p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Breast cancer mortality | 21.4% | 23.6% | 0.84 (0.74-0.95), p=0.0077 |
  | Distant metastasis | 25.1% | 26.9% | 0.87 (0.78-0.98), p=0.026 |

- 📊 OS by nodal burden and tumour location (Fig 3/4, adjusted HRs)

  | Subgroup | n IMNI / no IMNI | HR (95% CI) | 15y OS IMNI vs no IMNI |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1-3 nodes | 1490 / 1610 | 0.85 (0.73, 0.97) | 72.3% vs 68.0% |
  | 4-9 nodes | 469 / 512 | 0.92 (0.75, 1.12) | 55.3% vs 51.6% |
  | ≥10 nodes | 235 / 225 | 0.75 (0.59, 0.96) | 39.2% vs 31.1% |
  | Lateral ≥4 nodes | 425 / 429 | 0.77 (0.64, 0.93) | 53.8% vs 41.7% |
  | Medial/central ≥4 nodes | 279 / 308 | 0.98 (0.79, 1.21) | 43.6% vs 50.4% |

- ⚠️ Location signal inverted vs prior teaching: lateral tumours HR 0.81 (0.71-0.93) vs medial/central 0.89 (0.76-1.05), opposite of the EBCTCG/IMN1 expectation (interaction p=0.37)
- 📐 Premenopausal HR 0.64 (0.50-0.82) vs postmenopausal 0.90 (0.80-1.01), interaction p=0.014; age 35-49 HR 0.57 (0.43-0.77)

## 🌰 Prostate

The nodal-coverage question in oligorecurrent disease gets its first randomised read: pelvic ENRT beats metastasis-directed SBRT on every control endpoint, though at α=0·20 the MFS primary is not significant by conventional standards.

### PEACE V-STORM · [[NCT03569241]]

> 4-yr MFS 76% vs 63% with elective nodal RT vs MDT, HR 0.62 (80% CI 0.44-0.86), p=0.063.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Locoregional RFS is the RT read: 85% vs 62% at 4 yr, HR 0·45 (80% CI 0·31-0·65), and pelvic nodal relapse fell from 29% to 8%, so the elective field converts nodal salvage into durable regional control. 45 Gy/25 fx with a 65 Gy SIB added no GI toxicity over SBRT alone; the prostate bed, not the pelvis, drove G2+ GI (OR 4·6).

- 🔍 Phase 2 open-label RCT, 21 hospitals in 6 countries, 196 randomised, 190 evaluable (MDT 97, ENRT 93)
- 📐 α set at 0·20 two-sided, 80% power, so 80% CIs are reported and p≤0·20 counts as significant for the 1° endpoint
- 📊 RT prescription by arm
  - MDT: SBRT 30 Gy/3 fx every other day, 90% of PTV, 3 mm margin, IMRT/VMAT with daily IGRT
  - MDT alternative: bilateral extended sLND of the true pelvis (12 pts total chose surgery)
  - ENRT: 45 Gy/25 fx whole pelvis with SIB 65 Gy to PET-positive nodes; 45 Gy/25 fx alone post-sLND
  - Pelvic CTV per modified RTOG 2009 template, upper limit L4-L5 interspace
  - Both arms: 6 months of ADT; prostate bed 66 Gy/33 fx advised for pT3-4, Gleason ≥8, or positive margin
- 📊 4-year outcomes by arm (80% CIs throughout)

  | Endpoint | ENRT | MDT | HR (80% CI) | p |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Metastasis-free survival | 76% (69-81) | 63% (56-69) | 0·62 (0·44-0·86) | 0·063 |
  | Biochemical RFS | 57% (50-64) | 41% (34-47) | 0·62 (0·48-0·80) | 0·014 |
  | Locoregional RFS | 85% (80-90) | 62% (55-69) | 0·45 (0·31-0·65) | 0·0047 |
  | ADT-free survival | 77% (70-82) | 60% (53-67) | 0·60 (0·43-0·83) | 0·049 |

- 📊 Site of relapse, MDT (n=97) vs ENRT (n=93)

  | Site | MDT | ENRT |
  |---|---|---|
  | Pelvic nodes | 28 (29%) | 7 (8%) |
  | Prostate bed | 10 (10%) | 9 (10%) |
  | Distant metastases | 36 (37%) | 20 (22%) |
  | Lymph nodes (M1a) | 27 (28%) | 13 (14%) |
  | Bone (M1b) | 7 (7%) | 6 (6%) |

- 🔍 Benefit is a pattern-of-failure story: M1a nodal spread 28% MDT vs 14% ENRT, bone and lung near-identical
- 📐 Prespecified subgroups (MDT type, tracer) plus post-hoc ones; PSA <1 favoured ENRT HR 0·27 (0·14-0·52) vs ≥1 HR 1·00 (0·67-1·50), interaction p=0·029
- 📐 PSMA-staged pts HR 0·48 (0·32-0·72) vs choline HR 1·21 (0·64-2·28), interaction p=0·12
- ⚠️ Adding the pelvic field did not raise GI toxicity: 4-yr G2+ GI 7% MDT vs 9% ENRT (absolute difference 2%, p=0·93)
- ⚠️ GU also flat between arms: 4-yr G2+ GU 28% MDT vs 31% ENRT (absolute difference 3%, p=0·73)
- ⚠️ The toxicity driver was the prostate bed, not the pelvis: bed inclusion gave OR 4·6 (95% CI 1·5-15·8, p=0·0085) for G2+ GI and OR 1·85 (0·95-3·60, p=0·068) for G2+ GU, both post hoc
- ⚠️ Sceptic's read: p=0·063 only clears the bar because α was set at 0·20; at conventional α this MFS result is not significant
- ⚠️ Open-label with investigator-attributed AEs and no central PET review at baseline or follow-up
- ⚠️ OS, cancer-specific survival and time to castration resistance had too few events (11 deaths, 1 prostate cancer-related) to report
- 🔗 vs GETUG-P07-OLIGOPELVIS (single-arm ENRT): 3-yr bRFS 45% there vs 57% at 4 yr here, but that trial used choline and a higher median PSA (3-4 ng/mL)
- 🔗 vs RADIOSA / EXTEND: ADT-free interval >48 mo (median not reached with ENRT) vs 33 mo and >24 mo respectively

[doi:10.1016/s1470-2045(25)00197-4](https://doi.org/10.1016/s1470-2045(25%2900197-4)
