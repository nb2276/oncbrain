# 2026-05-22

**DBCG IMN2: IMNI in node-positive breast cancer → adjusted OS HR 0.85 (0.76-0.94), 15y OS 65.0% vs 60.8%; cohort allocated by laterality, not randomized.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Elective nodal RT led in breast and prostate. DBCG IMN2, a nonrandomized population-based cohort, links IMNI in node-positive breast cancer to an adjusted OS HR of 0.85. PEACE V-STORM, a phase 2 RCT (α 0.20), favours whole-pelvis ENRT over MDT for pelvic nodal oligorecurrence (4yr MFS 76% vs 63%, HR 0.62). EORTC endorses reduced-dose RT for cutaneous lymphoma without randomized evidence.

## 🌐 Lymphoma

EORTC expert opinion recommends reduced-dose RT across indolent cutaneous lymphomas, but registry data conflict on 4 Gy in pcMZL/pcFCL.

### EORTC cutaneous lymphoma RT recommendations

> Reduced-dose RT recommended across indolent cutaneous lymphomas; local MF lesions 8-12 Gy in 2-3 fx, 1-yr local control ≥92%, no randomized evidence.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The de-escalation is not uniform: 4 Gy is endorsed for CD4+ small/medium T-cell LPD and many pcFCL/pcMZL, but gave inferior MF local control and worse outcomes in B-cell lesions >5 cm. The algorithm handles this with response-adapted escalation to cumulative 24 Gy rather than upfront conventional dosing.

- 🔍 EORTC cutaneous lymphoma tumour group expert opinion (EJC 2024); dose algorithm to standardize RT across EORTC centres
- ⚠️ Authors state no randomized trials support RT selection over other modalities
- 🔗 Conventional 30-40 Gy was SOC until ~20 yrs ago; current regimens across Europe highly heterogeneous
- 📊 Mycosis fungoides, local RT
  - Usually 8-12 Gy in 2-3 fx
  - 1-yr local control ≥92% after low-dose RT
  - 4 Gy (1-2 fx) gave inferior local control
- 📊 TSEBT in MF/SS
  - Low-dose 8-12 Gy to palliate skin disease in advanced MF
  - Up to 24 Gy to induce remission before autologous SCT
  - SS: controversial, rarely durable; lowers circulating Sézary counts
- 📊 pcALCL: few relapses at doses of 20 Gy; palliative 2×4 Gy for symptoms
- 📊 CD4+ small/medium T-cell LPD: 4 Gy in 2 fx, 100% remission, no local relapses
- 📊 Cutaneous B-cell lymphoma
  - pcFCL/pcMZL: 4 Gy curative in many cases
  - Lesions >5 cm treated with 4 Gy had worse outcomes
  - Response-adapted escalation to cumulative 24 Gy for residual disease or local failure after 4 Gy
  - DLBCL leg type: consolidative RT after systemic; 30 Gy post-systemic reported equivalent
- ⚠️ Registry data conflict on 4 Gy in pcMZL/pcFCL: one long-f/u retrospective series found inferior response and more in-field relapse vs higher doses
- ⚠️ Tables 1-2 pool small retrospective series with heterogeneous doses; row-level values not reliably extractable here

> [!question] Open questions
> - Is 4 Gy with salvage escalation equivalent to upfront higher dose in pcFCL/pcMZL?
> - Can systemic agents prolong remission after low-dose TSEBT?

## 🎀 Breast

Population-based OS data now back IMNI in node-positive disease, including 1-3 LN (post hoc HR 0.85).

### DBCG IMN2 · [[NCT06549920]]

> Adjusted OS HR 0.85 (0.76-0.94) with IMNI in node-positive breast cancer; 15y OS 65.0% vs 60.8% despite taxanes, trastuzumab, AIs and 3D RT.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The benefit held in the 1-3 node group, HR 0.85 (0.73-0.97), the population where guidelines still hedge on IMNI. It was delivered with free-breathing 3D wide tangents at 50 Gy/25 Fx, and dilution (20.4% incidental coverage left-sided) biases toward the null, so modern gated or VMAT coverage likely does no worse.

- 🔍 Prospective nationwide population-based cohort, 6 RT centres, N=4541, treated 2007-2014
- ⚠️ Not randomized: IMNI allocated by laterality (right-sided IMNI, left-sided no IMNI) under national guideline
- 🔍 Macrometastatic node-positive, all ALND; excluded neoadjuvant systemic therapy, bilateral BC, prior malignancy
- 📊 Efficacy by endpoint, ITT by laterality (median f/u 13.7y OS, 13.2y DM)

  | Endpoint | IMNI (n=2194) | No IMNI (n=2347) | Adjusted HR (95% CI), p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | OS at 15y | 65.0% | 60.8% | 0.85 (0.76-0.94), p=0.0016 |
  | Breast cancer mortality, 15y CI | 21.4% | 23.6% | 0.84 (0.74-0.95), p=0.0077 |
  | Distant metastasis, 15y CI | 25.1% | 26.9% | 0.87 (0.78-0.98), p=0.026 |

- 📐 15y OS risk difference 4.2% (95% CI 0.9-7.5)
- ⚠️ Crude DM HR 0.91 (0.81-1.02), p=0.11; DM signal depends on adjustment
- 📊 OS subgroups relevant to the nodal-RT decision (adjusted HR)

  | Subgroup | HR (95% CI) | 15y OS IMNI vs no IMNI |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1-3 LN (n=3100, post hoc) | 0.85 (0.73-0.97) | 72.3% vs 68.0% |
  | 4-9 LN | 0.92 (0.75-1.12) | 55.3% vs 51.6% |
  | ≥10 LN | 0.75 (0.59-0.96) | 39.2% vs 31.1% |
  | Lateral ≥4 LN | 0.77 (0.64-0.93) | 53.8% vs 41.7% |
  | Medial/central ≥4 LN | 0.98 (0.79-1.21) | 43.6% vs 50.4% |
  | ER-/HER2+ | 1.49 (0.98-2.25) | 57.7% vs 68.2% |

- ⚠️ ER-/HER2+ interaction p=0.021 with no multiplicity correction; exploratory
- 🔍 RT technique
  - 48 Gy/24 Fx before Jan 2009, 50 Gy/25 Fx thereafter
  - IMN target ICS 1-4; 3D conformal wide tangents, free breathing
  - Axilla level II-III, interpectoral and level IV in all; level I if ≥6 LN+ or <10 removed
- ⚠️ Dose separation diluted: median IMN CTV V90% 94.6% right vs 20.4% left; 10.1% of left-sided got IMNI
- 📊 15y death from ischemic/valvular heart disease 0.2% right vs 0.7% left; left-sided median MHD 2.3 Gy
- 🔗 vs EBCTCG regional RT meta-analysis OS RR 0.90 (0.84-0.96) and KROG 08-06 HR 0.87 (0.57-1.31): same point estimate

Mølby Nielsen; Lise B. J. Thorsen; Demet Özcan et al.. *The Lancet Regional Health - Europe* (2025-02). [doi:10.1016/j.lanepe.2024.101160](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lanepe.2024.101160)
  - 📎 [[papers/breast/internal-mammary-node-irradiation-in-4541-node-positive-breast-c.pdf|DBCG IMN2 (full text)]]

## 🌰 Prostate

For pelvic nodal oligorecurrence, elective pelvic RT beats MDT on MFS and locoregional RFS with no G2+ GU/GI toxicity difference. The benefit is concentrated in PSMA-staged pts with PSA <1.

### PEACE V-STORM · [[NCT03569241]]

> 4yr MFS 76% vs 63% (HR 0.62, 80% CI 0.44-0.86) favoring whole-pelvis ENRT over MDT for pelvic nodal oligorecurrence, both with 6mo ADT.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The RT signal is regional: 4yr locoregional RFS 85% vs 62% (HR 0.45), consistent with MDT failing mostly in the pelvis. The benefit sat in PSMA-staged pts (HR 0.48) and PSA <1 (HR 0.27), so it moves the choice from nodal SBRT to whole-pelvis 45/25 with 65 Gy SIB. Prostate bed inclusion, not the pelvic field, drove added GI toxicity.

- 🔍 Phase 2 open-label RCT, 21 centres in 6 countries, N=196 randomised, 190 in mITT
- 📐 Two-sided α set at 0.20 with 80% CIs; p=0.063 clears that bar, not a conventional 0.05
- 🔍 ENRT: 45 Gy/25 fx whole pelvis (modified RTOG 2009, upper limit L4/L5) + SIB 65 Gy to PET+ nodes
- 🔍 MDT: SBRT 30 Gy/3 fx every other day (3 mm PTV margin) or sLND; sLND used in only 12 pts
- 💊 Both arms: 6 mo LHRH agonist/antagonist ADT
- 📊 4yr outcomes, ENRT vs MDT (median f/u 50 mo)

  | Endpoint | ENRT | MDT | HR (80% CI), p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | MFS (1°) | 76% (69-81) | 63% (56-69) | 0.62 (0.44-0.86), p=0.063 |
  | bRFS | 57% (50-64) | 41% (34-47) | 0.62 (0.48-0.80), p=0.014 |
  | Locoregional RFS | 85% (80-90) | 62% (55-69) | 0.45 (0.31-0.65), p=0.0047 |
  | ADT-free survival | 77% (70-82) | 60% (53-67) | 0.60 (0.43-0.83), p=0.049 |

- 📊 MFS subgroups (PET tracer prespecified; PSA and nodal level post hoc)

  | Subgroup | HR (80% CI) | Interaction p |
  |---|---|---|
  | PSMA PET | 0.48 (0.32-0.72) | 0.12 |
  | Choline PET | 1.21 (0.64-2.28) | 0.12 |
  | PSA <1 | 0.27 (0.14-0.52) | 0.029 |
  | PSA ≥1 | 1.00 (0.67-1.50) | 0.029 |
  | No common iliac node | 0.49 (0.33-0.74) | 0.20 |
  | Common iliac node | 1.04 (0.55-1.95) | 0.20 |

- 📊 Toxicity, MDT vs ENRT

  | Endpoint | MDT | ENRT | p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 4yr cumulative G2+ GU | 27/97 (28%) | 29/93 (31%) | 0.73 |
  | 4yr cumulative G2+ GI | 7/97 (7%) | 8/93 (9%) | 0.93 |
  | G3 urinary incontinence | 6 (6%) | 9 (10%) | n/a |
  | G3 diarrhoea | 1 (1%) | 2 (2%) | n/a |

- ⚠️ Prostate bed RT imbalance: 24 (25%) MDT vs 38 (41%) ENRT
- ⚠️ Post hoc: prostate bed in field → G2+ GI OR 4.6 (95% CI 1.5-15.8), p=0.0085
- ⚠️ No central PET review; ~1 in 5 staged with choline, not PSMA
- 🔗 vs GETUG-P07-OLIGOPELVIS (single-arm ENRT): 3yr bRFS 45% there, higher baseline PSA and choline staging
