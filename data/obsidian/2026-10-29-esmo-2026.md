# 2026-10-29 — ESMO Congress 2026

**PEACE-1 at 7.7y: abiraterone OS HR 0.75 (0.65-0.87) in de novo mCSPC; triplet vs ADT+docetaxel HR 0.68, 8y OS 40.3% vs 27.1%.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Prostate carries the day: PEACE-1 long-term f/u supports abiraterone added to ADT+docetaxel in de novo mCSPC, while SOC+RT median OS 4.6y matched SOC, with no RT-attributable OS HR in source. Breast brings two single-arm HER2+ CNS phase IIs pairing RT with HER2-directed therapy: BRIDGET (SRS then tucatinib, 18-mo iPFS 59%) and INSPIRE-LM (RT then IT HP, mOS 36.2 mo).

## 🎀 Breast

Both HER2+ CNS studies are single-arm phase II with no RT-omitted arm, so RT's contribution can't be separated from the HER2-directed therapy.

### INSPIRE-LM · [[NCT04588545]]

> mOS 36.2 mo (95% CI 19.4-NR), 12-mo OS 79% after RT then IT trastuzumab/pertuzumab in HER2+ breast LM; single-arm phase II, N=30.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Nearly every pt (97%) had WBRT or proton CSI before or at entry, so the survival figure describes a combined RT then IT sequence, not IT therapy alone. Dose, fractionation and the CSI vs WBRT split are not reported, which leaves open whether whole-neuraxis coverage is required before IT HER2 therapy.

- 📊 Survival endpoints (median f/u 26 mo)

  | Endpoint | Median (95% CI) | 12-mo rate |
  |---|---|---|
  | OS | 36.2 mo (19.4-NR) | 79% |
  | LM-PFS | 19.3 mo (7.2-NR) | 61% |

- 📊 Primary objective OS; source states predetermined OS endpoint met
- 📊 Best LM response
  - CR 5 (17%)
  - PR 5 (17%)
  - SD 17 (57%)
- 🔍 Multi-institutional single-arm phase II, N=30, enrolled Nov 2022 to May 2026
- 🔍 Eligibility: HER2+ breast LM, life expectancy >8 wk, KPS ≥60; 50% baseline KPS ≤70
- 💊 IT trastuzumab 80 mg + IT pertuzumab 80 mg (RP2D); concurrent systemic therapy permitted
- 🔍 RT before IT therapy: WBRT or proton CSI in 29 (97%), on study or prior to entry
- ⚠️ RT dose, fractionation and WBRT vs CSI split not reported in source
- ⚠️ RT's contribution not separable: no RT-omitted arm, and prior intracranial RT exempted pts
- ⚠️ Source prints median prior systemic lines as 34 (range 1-9): internal inconsistency, true median unknown
- 📊 G≥2 AEs attributed to IT therapy
  - Nausea/vomiting n=3 (10%)
  - Fatigue n=2 (7%)
  - Headache n=2 (7%)
- 📊 12 pts (40%) still on study therapy; longest treatment duration 38.7 mo
- ⚠️ Upper CI bounds NR for both OS and LM-PFS; estimates immature at N=30

- 📄 **Radiation therapy followed by intrathecal (IT) trastuzumab/pertuzumab in the management of HER2+ breast leptomeningeal metastasis: Results from a phase II multi-institutional study, (INSPIRE-LM)** — Kamran Ahmed. *ESMO Congress 2026* (2026-10). [article](https://cslide.ctimeetingtech.com/esmo2026/attendee/confcal/presentation/list)
  > ESMO Congress 2026 abstract 1768RO · Rapid oral 2: Breast cancer, advanced stage · 2026-10-26 · presented by Kamran Ahmed (Tampa, United States of America) · [[NCT04588545]]
  > Background: Treatment options for HER2+ leptomeningeal metastasis (LM) remain limited. We previously reported 80 mg as the recommended phase II dose of IT pertuzumab alongside 80 mg IT trastuzumab and now report preliminary results from the phase II study.
  > Methods: The study is a multi-institutional, phase II trial of radiation therapy (RT) followed by IT trastuzumab/pertuzumab in the management of HER2+ breast LM. HER2+ LM patients with a life expectancy > 8 weeks, and KPS ≥ 60 were eligible. RT was completed prior to the delivery of IT therapy but was not required in patients that received prior intracranial RT. Patients could continue treatment with systemic agents if controlling disease and LM developed while on these therapies. The primary objective was to evaluate overall survival (OS) following study treatment. Secondary objectives included LM progression free survival (LM-PFS) and LM response.
  > Results: A total of 30 patients were enrolled between November 2022 and May 2026. Median age was 48 (range: 33-65) and 15 patients (50%) had a baseline KPS </= 70. The median number of prior systemic therapies was 34 (range: 1-9). Most patients received whole brain radiation therapy or proton cranial spinal irradiation (n=29; 97%) either prior to IT therapy or before study entry. Median follow-up is 26 months. The study met its predetermined endpoint for OS. Median survival is 36.2 months (95% CI: 19.4 – Not reached (NR)) with a 12-month OS of 79%. Median LM progression free survival (LM-PFS) is 19.3 months (95% CI 7.2 – NR) with a 12-month LM-PFS of 61%. The best LM response was a complete response in 5 (17%), partial response in 5 (17%), and stable disease in 17 (57%). The most common grade ≥ 2 adverse events attributed to IT trastuzumab/pertuzumab included nausea/vomiting (n=3; 10%), fatigue (n=2; 7%), and headache (n=2; 7%). Currently, twelve patients (40%) continue study therapy with 38.7 months as the longest treatment duration.
  > Conclusions: The study met its predetermined endpoint for OS with the longest median OS reported to date in HER2+ LM. Complete responses were noted in a subset of patients. IT trastuzumab/pertuzumab should be considered in the management of HER2+ LM.
  > Clinical trial identification: [[NCT04588545]].

### BRIDGET · [[NCT05323955]]

> Median iPFS not reached vs 5.0 mo historical control (p<0.0001); 18-mo iPFS 59% adding tucatinib to HP/T-DM1 after SRS for isolated intracranial progression.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The RT-relevant read is distant brain control after SRS-only management: with WBRT excluded, iPFS held at 64% at 12 mo and 59% at 18 mo after adding tucatinib. That frames tucatinib as a partner to SRS rather than an argument for WBRT. Local vs distant intracranial failure is not split in the source.

- 🔍 Single-arm phase II, N=48 enrolled, 47 evaluable; [[NCT05323955]]
- 🔍 Key eligibility
  - HER2+ MBC, first or second isolated intracranial event
  - Stable or no extracranial disease on HP or T-DM1
  - SRS to all brain lesions ≥5mm, within 12 wks of enrollment
  - WBRT exclusionary; no prior HER2/EGFR TKI; ECOG PS 0-2
- 💊 Tucatinib 300 mg BID added continuously to ongoing HP or T-DM1
- 🔍 Population: median age 53.5 yrs, 66% ER+, 66% no ECD, 93.6% HP backbone, 80.9% after first intracranial event
- 📊 iPFS (RANO-BM) by timepoint, median f/u 19.6 mo (95% CI 15.6-26.1)

  | Timepoint | iPFS rate |
  |---|---|
  | 6 mo | 91% |
  | 12 mo | 64% |
  | 18 mo | 59% |

- 📊 Intracranial progression in 17 pts (36.2%); median iPFS NR
- 📐 One-sample log-rank p<0.0001 vs H0 median iPFS 5 mo (one-sided α=0.05, 90% power)
- 📊 Extracranial progression rare: 2 pts (4.3%)
- 📊 G≥3 AEs 15% (7/47)
- ⚠️ Historical exponential control, not a concurrent SRS-alone arm; benefit size vs SRS + unchanged therapy unknown
- ⚠️ 12-to-18-mo iPFS drop 64% to 59%, yet 36.2% still progressed intracranially; no breakdown of local vs distant brain failure in source
- 🔗 Builds on HER2CLIMB intracranial activity (tucatinib + capecitabine/trastuzumab); here tucatinib without capecitabine

- 📄 **BRIDGET: A phase II trial of tucatinib added to trastuzumab/pertuzumab or ado-trastuzumab emtansine after isolated intracranial progression in patients with advanced HER2-positive breast cancer** — Sarah Sammons. *ESMO Congress 2026* (2026-10). [article](https://cslide.ctimeetingtech.com/esmo2026/attendee/confcal/presentation/list)
  > ESMO Congress 2026 abstract 1767RO · Rapid oral 2: Breast cancer, advanced stage · 2026-10-26 · presented by Sarah Sammons (Baltimore, United States of America) · [[NCT05323955]]
  > Background: Isolated CNS relapse/progression (ICP) is a frequent occurrence in patients (pts) with HER2+ MBC. Current guidelines recommend local therapy and continuation of same systemic regimen. The impact of adjusting the systemic regimen to include a CNS-active agent is unknown. Tucatinib demonstrated significant intracranial activity in HER2CLIMB when delivered with capecitabine/trastuzumab. BRIDGET is a prospective trial to add a brain-penetrant TKI post radiation to ongoing therapy in attempts to prolong intracranial disease control in pts with stable or no extracranial disease (ECD).
  > Methods: Single-arm phase II trial of pts with HER2+ MBC experiencing a first or second isolated intracranial event with stable/no ECD on HP or T-DM1 within 12 wks of stereotactic radiosurgery (SRS). Key eligibility: ECOG PS 0–2; SRS to all brain lesions ≥5mm, no prior HER2/EGFR TKI. Whole-brain radiation was exclusionary. Tucatinib 300 mg BID was added continuously to ongoing HP or T-DM1 therapy. Primary endpoint: intracranial PFS (iPFS) per RANO-BM compared with a historical exponential control (median of 5.0 mos) using a one-sample log-rank test (one-sided α=0.05; 90% power; N=43 evaluable).
  > Results: Of 48 enrolled pts, 47 were evaluable (median age 53.5 yrs; 66% ER+). Most pts (66%) had no ECD at enrollment, were treated with backbone HP (93.6%) and enrolled after their first intracranial event (80.9%). At a median follow-up of 19.6 mos (95% CI 15.6–26.1), intracranial progression occurred in 17 pts (36.2%); median iPFS was not reached (one-sample log-rank p<0.0001 for H0: median iPFS=5 mos). The 6-, 12-, and 18-mo iPFS rates were 91%, 64%, and 59%, respectively. Extracranial progression was rare (2 pts, 4.3%). The safety profile was favorable: grade ≥3 adverse events occurred in 15% (7/47).
  > Conclusions: The addition of tucatinib to HP after radiation met the study’s primary endpoint, with more than half of patients remaining free of intracranial progression at 18 months. These findings support the addition of early tucatinib after local therapy as a well-tolerated strategy to improve intracranial disease control in a HER2+ isolated brain progression population.
  > Clinical trial identification: [[NCT05323955]].

## 🌰 Prostate

PEACE-1 RT arm: SOC+RT median OS 4.6y, same as SOC; no RT-attributable OS HR or volume split in source.

### PEACE-1 · [[NCT01957436]]

> 7.7y f/u: abiraterone OS HR 0.75 (0.65-0.87) in de novo mCSPC; triplet vs ADT+docetaxel HR 0.68, 8y OS 40.3% vs 27.1%.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The four-arm medians are the RT read: SOC+RT 4.6y matched SOC 4.6y, and SOC+RT+abiraterone reached 6.4y vs 5.7y for SOC+abiraterone, but no RT-attributable OS HR or volume split is reported in source. The prostate-RT decision in low-volume de novo disease still rests on earlier PEACE-1 reports, not this update.

- 🔍 Randomized phase III 2x2 factorial, 1:1:1:1, N=1173 de novo mCSPC (57% high-, 43% low-volume)
- 💊 SOC = ADT ± docetaxel; randomized additions: abiraterone/prednisone and/or prostate RT
- 📊 Abiraterone OS HR 0.75 (95% CI 0.65-0.87), p=0.0001 at median f/u 7.7y
- 📊 Pooled median OS 6.1y with abiraterone vs 4.6y without; 8y OS 40.3% vs 29.5%
- 📊 Median OS by randomized arm (y, 95% CI)

  | Arm | Median OS |
  |---|---|
  | SOC | 4.6 y (4.1-5.5) |
  | SOC+RT | 4.6 y (4.0-5.2) |
  | SOC+abiraterone | 5.7 y (4.8-6.6) |
  | SOC+RT+abiraterone | 6.4 y (5.5-7.6) |

- 📊 ADT+docetaxel SOC subgroup (n=710): doublet vs triplet

  | Population | Doublet mOS | Triplet mOS | HR (95% CI) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | All docetaxel pts | 4.4 y (4.0-4.9) | 6.2 y (5.2-7.3) | 0.68 (0.56-0.82) |
  | High-volume | 3.5 y (3.2-4.0) | 4.6 y (3.9-6.1) | 0.68 (0.54-0.85) |
  | Low-volume | 6.2 y (5.4-8.1) | 7.8 y (6.4-NR) | 0.74 (0.52-1.05) |

- 📐 Triplet 8y OS 40.3% (34.3-46.6) vs doublet 27.1% (22.1-32.9)
- 📐 No interaction with metastatic extent, p=0.56
- ⚠️ Low-volume triplet HR CI crosses 1 (0.52-1.05); OncoDaily reports p=0.091
- ⚠️ Docetaxel was SOC choice, not a randomized factor; doublet vs triplet is a subgroup of the abiraterone randomization
- ⚠️ Source discordance: high-volume 8y OS 20% vs 38% (abstract) vs 7y 22.4% vs 40.9% (OncoDaily); low-volume CI lower bound 0.52 vs 0.51
- ⚠️ RT arm: SOC+RT median OS 4.6y, same as SOC; no RT-attributable OS HR or volume split in source
- ⚠️ Abstract-level data only: no safety, subsequent-therapy or rPFS update in source

- 📄 **Long-term overall survival with abiraterone acetate plus prednisone in the PEACE-1 phase 3 trial for de novo metastatic castration-sensitive prostate cancer (mCSPC)** — Karim Fizazi. *ESMO Congress 2026* (2026-10). [article](https://cslide.ctimeetingtech.com/esmo2026/attendee/confcal/presentation/list)
  > ESMO Congress 2026 abstract 2215O · Proffered paper: Genitourinary tumours, prostate, penile and testis · 2026-10-24 · presented by Karim Fizazi (Lille, France) · [[NCT01957436]]
  > Background: Recent trials demonstrated the benefit of systemic treatment intensification in mCSPC. However, long-term analyses are rare, especially for pharma industry trials. PEACE-1 ([[NCT01957436]]) is a 2x2 factorial phase 3 randomized trial that tested the addition of abiraterone acetate/prednisone (abiraterone) and/or prostate radiotherapy (RT) to standard of care (SOC) consisting of androgen deprivation therapy (ADT) with or without docetaxel.
  > Methods: 1173 men (57% high-, 43% low-volume) with de novo mCSPC were randomized (1:1:1:1) to SOC, SOC+abiraterone, SOC+RT or SOC+abiraterone+RT. The initial analysis (median follow-up: 4.4 years [y]) showed abiraterone improved overall survival (OS) (HR: 0·82, 95% CI 0·69–0·98; p=0·030) (Fizazi K, Lancet 2022). Here we report long-term follow-up results.
  > Results: The median follow-up is 7.7 y. Abiraterone significantly improved survival (HR: 0.75 [95%CI: 0.65-0.87], p=0.0001) with median OS of 4.6 y (4.1-5.5) for SOC, 4.6 y (4.0-5.2) for SOC+RT, 5.7 y (4.8-6.6) for SOC+abiraterone, and 6.4 y (5.5-7.6) for SOC+RT+abiraterone, respectively. In the subgroup of 710 patients who received ADT+docetaxel as their SOC, adding abiraterone also improved survival (HR: 0.68 [0.56-0.82]), with a median OS of 4.4 y (4.0-4.9) for ADT+docetaxel (doublet) and 6.2 y (5.2-7.3) for ADT+docetaxel+abiraterone (triplet), resulting in 8-year survival rates of 27.1% (22.1-32.9) and 40.3% (34.3-46.6), respectively. The favorable effect of triplet therapy on OS was independent of metastatic extent (interaction test p= 0.56): median OS was 3.5 y (3.2-4.0) vs 4.6y (3.9-6.1) in patients with high-volume (HR: 0.68 [0,54; 0,85]) and 6.2 y (5.4-8.1) vs 7.8 y (6.4-NR) in patients with low-volume (HR: 0.74 [0.52-1.05]), for doublet versus triplet respectively. In patients with high-volume, 8-year OS rates were 20% (14.6-26.7) with doublet vs 38% (31.3-45.1) with triplet therapy.
  > Conclusions: Very long-term follow-up shows a major OS benefit of adding abiraterone to SOC for patients with mCSPC, irrespective of metastatic extent. Triplet therapy with ADT+docetaxel+abiraterone is associated with almost twice as many long-term survivors in some subgroups.
  > Clinical trial identification: [[NCT01957436]].
