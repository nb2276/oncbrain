# 2026-08-13

**Hypofractionated PBI reirradiation after second lumpectomy: 40 Gy/15 fx, 3yr LR-FS 86%, no grade 3+ late toxicity (N=11).**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Breast carries the only RT-actionable read: a moderately hypofractionated 40 Gy/15 fx partial-breast re-RT schedule with clean second-course dosimetry and no grade 3+ late events, though N=11 with 2 events and no cumulative plan sum cap it as hypothesis-generating against RTOG 1014's BID regimen. Prostate is non-RT: testosterone recovery after ADT stop, 54% relugolix vs 3% leuprolide at 90d.

## 🌰 Prostate

### Testosterone recovery post-ADT discontinuation (podcast review)

> Review of two datasets: 25% of real-world pts recovered T within 12mo; 54% relugolix vs 3% leuprolide at 90d in HERO.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> For short-course ADT with RT, the recovery question is now quantified: only 25% of real-world pts on injectable ADT had documented T >280 ng/dl within 12mo of stopping, and testing happened in only about 40% at all. The authors position relugolix for short-term or intermittent regimens, not continuous long-term ADT.

- 🗞️ Sponsored podcast (Pfizer + Sumitomo) reviewing two published papers on T recovery after ADT stop
- 📊 Real-world Optum cohort (Preston et al, Eur Urol Open Sci): injectable ADT only, no relugolix at data cut
  - 3875 pts initiated + discontinued injectable ADT; 1553 (about 40%) had ≥1 T test within 12mo
  - 390/1553 (25%) recovered T >280 ng/dl within 12mo; 1163 (75%) did not
  - Recoverers younger (67 vs 71y), less diabetes (10% vs 19%), hyperlipidemia (37% vs 46%), HTN (42% vs 50%)
- 📐 Adjusted outcomes, T recovery vs no recovery (real-world)

  | Outcome | HR | 95% CI |
  |---|---|---|
  | New-onset diabetes | 0.47 | 0.27-0.79 |
  | New-onset depression | 0.58 | 0.33-1.02 |
  | Treatment for sexual dysfunction | 1.33 | 0.99-1.78 |

- 📊 No significant differences in hot flashes, MI or CVA between recovery groups
- 📊 HERO subgroup (N=184 completing 48wk ADT), 90-day T recovery: relugolix (n=137) vs leuprolide (n=47)

  | Subgroup | Relugolix | Leuprolide |
  |---|---|---|
  | Overall cumulative incidence | 54% | 3% |
  | Baseline T at/above pretreatment median | 71% | 6% |
  | Age ≤65 | 78% | 13% |
  | Age >65 | 47% | 0% |
  | Biochemical recurrence | 57% | 0% |

- 📐 Overall recovery difference nominal p=.002; median time to recovery 86 vs 112 days
- 💊 Relugolix 360mg load then 120mg PO daily; leuprolide IM q12wk. Recovery defined as T >280 ng/dl or >80% of baseline
- ⚠️ Subgroup analyses beyond the overall rate were post hoc and described as numerically higher, no subgroup p-values in source
- ⚠️ HERO subgroup limits named by the authors: small N, limited diversity, 48wk ADT only, 90d follow-up only
- ⚠️ Real-world cohort captured only the 40% who had any post-ADT T test, and testing was inconsistently scheduled

## 🎀 Breast

Second-lumpectomy salvage RT moves toward daily moderate hypofractionation, on 11 pts.

### Moderately hypofractionated partial breast reirradiation

> 40Gy/15fx PBI re-RT after second lumpectomy: 3yr LR-FS, DR-FS and OS all 86%, no grade 3+ late events (N=11).

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The transferable read is the fractionation, not the outcome: 40 Gy / 2.67 Gy daily PBI met every OAR objective with heart Dmean 1.44 Gy and ipsilateral lung V16Gy 7.74%, so a once-daily 15-fraction re-RT can be planned inside standard constraints. That removes the BID-visit burden that limits RTOG 1014 uptake, though no plan sum with the first WBI course was possible.

- 📊 3yr LR-FS 86% (95% CI 46-62%), DR-FS 86% (95% CI 52-65%), OS 86% (95% CI 47-63%); 9/11 free from LR, 10/11 from DR
- 🔍 Retrospective single-institution series, 13 identified 2017-2021, 2 excluded (off-protocol fractionation, T4 treated with WBI) → N=11
- 🔍 Re-RT dose 40 Gy / 2.67 Gy daily (15 fx) PBI, direct-planning IMRT, supine, no DIBH
- 🔍 Initial course was WBI at 2 Gy/fx in all 11; 2 pts had a 10 Gy/5 fx boost
- 🔍 Eligibility gate: age ≥50, isolated IBTR, tumor <2-3cm, unifocal on US/mammo/MRI, ≥48 months since primary treatment (DEGRO + Portuguese consensus)
- 🔍 Median interval between RT courses 107 months (27-239); median follow-up 41 months (27-62)
- 📐 Re-RT dosimetry, mean (range) vs the stated objective

  | Parameter | Objective | Achieved mean (range) |
  |---|---|---|
  | PTV V95% | > 98% | 98.36 (98-99.45) |
  | PTV V107% | < 2% | 0 (0) |
  | Ipsi lung V16Gy | < 15% | 7.74 (1.41-14.98) |
  | Ipsi lung V8Gy | < 35% | 13.22 (2.78-34.58) |
  | Heart Dmean | < 3.2 Gy | 1.44 (0.48-3.09) |
  | Heart V16Gy | < 5% | 1.53 (0-4.89) |
  | Contra lung V4Gy | < 10% | 1.09 (0-8.74) |

- 📐 Target volumes mean (range): tumor bed 83cc (11.2-220.3), CTV 210cc (93.3-446.9), PTV 474cc (316.2-942.6)
- 📊 No grade 3+ late events; commonest late effects at 1yr were grade 1 fibrosis (9 pts) and grade 1-2 oedema (5 pts)
- 📊 No cardiopulmonary events and no rib fractures observed
- 📊 Cosmesis by the treating radiation oncologist: good 6, fair 2, poor 3
- 📊 Two local recurrences: at 11 months (subsequently distant, died 2 yrs later) and at 15 months post re-RT
- 🔗 vs RTOG 1014 (45 Gy / 1.5 Gy BID, 3D-CRT): 7% late grade 3, no grade 4-5 at 5.5yr median f/u; this series reports none
- 🔗 vs Janssen 2018 (n=83, 45 Gy / 1.8 Gy daily): LR rate 15% at 35 months median f/u
- ⚠️ N=11 with only 2 events, so the 86% estimates carry CIs the paper reports as 46-62% / 52-65% / 47-63%, which do not bracket the point estimate as printed
- ⚠️ TAM-stratified LR-FS rests on 3, 7 and 1 pts per stratum; the single high-risk pt cannot support a stratum-level read
- ⚠️ No plan sum with the first course, so cumulative OAR dose is uncharacterized despite the clean second-course dosimetry
- ⚠️ Cosmesis scored unblinded by the treating radiation oncologist, and 3/11 poor sits awkwardly against 'cosmesis results were good'
