# 2026-06-10

**RTOG 0848: adjuvant CXRT missed OS overall (HR 0.96) but node-negative pts saw 5yr OS 48.1% vs 28.6%, P-interaction=.022.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Upper GI carried the day, and the RT read is the subgroup, not the primary endpoint: adjuvant chemoRT after gemcitabine-based chemo did nothing overall in resected pancreatic cancer but showed a nodal-status interaction, with node-negative pts gaining ~20 absolute points of 5yr OS. Hypothesis-generating on a dated pre-FOLFIRINOX backbone.

## 🍽️ GI Upper

Adjuvant pancreas RT stays unsettled: the trial is negative overall, and the node-negative signal needs prospective confirmation on a modern systemic backbone.

### RTOG 0848 · [[NCT01013649]]

> Adjuvant CXRT after 6 cycles gemcitabine-based chemo missed OS (HR 0.96), but node-negative pts gained: 5yr OS 48.1% vs 28.6%.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The whole RT read sits in 91 node-negative pts: 5yr OS 48.1% vs 28.6%, median OS 3.9 vs 3.0 yr, with interaction P=.022. Delivery was standard and QA'd (50.4 Gy/28 fx, tumor bed plus pancreatojejunostomy plus regional nodes, 81% IMRT), so the technique transfers directly. G4-5 toxicity was unchanged (6% v 5%).

- 📊 1° EP OS negative: HR 0.96 (90% CI 0.79-1.18), 1-sided P=.38, 2-sided P=.77
- 📊 DFS trend only: HR 0.82 (95% CI 0.65-1.03), P=.089
- 📐 Significant treatment × nodal status interaction for OS, P-interaction=.022 (Fig 3)
- 📊 Node-negative subgroup (n=91), the RT-relevant signal

  | Endpoint | Chemo (n=42) | Chemo+CXRT (n=49) |
  |---|---|---|
  | 5yr OS (95% CI) | 28.6 (14.9-42.2) | 48.1 (33.3-62.9) |
  | Median OS, yr (95% CI) | 3.0 (2.2-4.0) | 3.9 (2.5-NR) |
  | Median DFS, yr (95% CI) | 1.5 (0.8-2.7) | 2.3 (1.4-NR) |

- 📊 Node-positive (n=263): 5yr OS 21.2% vs 20.9%, no treatment effect
- 🔍 RT was 50.4 Gy/28 fx to tumor bed, pancreatojejunostomy and regional nodes, 81% IMRT
- 🔍 Real-time central RT QA before start; overall per protocol/acceptable in 92% of treated pts
- ⚠️ 14% randomized to CXRT never received it (18 refusals); as-treated HR 0.98 (90% CI 0.80-1.20)
- ⚠️ G3 AEs 38% v 19% (P<.001), driven by GI and lymphopenia; G4-5 unchanged 6% v 5% (P=.68)
- ⚠️ Step 1 chemo was gemcitabine ± erlotinib; zero pts got FOLFIRINOX, so the systemic backbone is dated
- 🔗 ESPAC-1 found adjuvant CXRT detrimental, but used split-course lower-dose 2D RT accrued 1994-2000 with no QA
