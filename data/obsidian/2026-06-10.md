# 2026-06-10

**RTOG 0848: adjuvant chemoRT misses OS overall (HR 0.96), but node-negative pts hit 48.1% vs 28.6% 5-yr OS (P-interaction .022).**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Upper GI carried the day, and it's an RT-selection question, not an RT-efficacy one. Modern QA'd chemoRT (50.4 Gy/28 fx, 81% IMRT, central review) is flat after gem-based chemo, but the node-negative subgroup separates sharply. Hypothesis-generating: no subgroup HR in source, and the systemic backbone predates FOLFIRINOX.

## 🍽️ GI Upper

One trial, and it reframes adjuvant pancreatic CXRT from whether to whom: IMRT-era delivery with real QA, null overall, node-negative signal only.

### RTOG 0848 · [[NCT01013649]]

> Adjuvant chemoRT did not improve OS overall (HR 0.96), but node-negative pts gained 48.1% vs 28.6% 5-yr OS.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The RT-relevant read is nodal selection: N0 pts (n=91) reached 48.1% vs 28.6% 5-yr OS with CXRT while N1 (n=263) was flat at 20.9% vs 21.2%, P-interaction .022. Dose was 50.4 Gy/28 fx to bed plus regional nodes with real-time central QA, and grade 4-5 AEs were unchanged (6% v 5%).

- 📊 1° EP OS negative: HR 0.96 (90% CI 0.79-1.18), 1-sided P=.38
- 📐 Median OS 2.6 (90% CI 2.1-3.1) vs 2.3 yrs (2.0-2.6) from step-2 randomization
- 📊 DFS trend only: HR 0.82 (95% CI 0.65-1.03), P=.089
- 🔍 Two-step phase IIR/III, N=354 randomized at step 2 after 5 cycles gem-based chemo without progression
- 🔍 RT was 50.4 Gy/28 fx to tumor bed, pancreatojejunostomy and regional nodes, capecitabine or infusional 5-FU sensitized
- 🔍 RT delivery and QA (why this is not ESPAC-1 radiotherapy)
  - 81% IMRT, 19% 3D conformal; centers IROC-credentialed
  - Real-time central plan review before treatment start
  - Overall QA score per protocol/acceptable in 142/155 treated (92%)
  - 88% of the 153 treated got ≥50 Gy; median dose 50.4 Gy, 28 fx, 38 days
  - 25/180 (14%) never received CXRT, mainly refusal (18 pts)
- 📊 Node-negative subgroup (n=91), the only positive signal
  - 5-yr OS 48.1% (33.3-62.9) vs 28.6% (14.9-42.2)
  - Median OS 3.9 yrs (2.5-not reached) vs 3.0 (2.2-4.0)
  - Median DFS 2.3 yrs (1.4-not reached) vs 1.5 (0.8-2.7)
  - N1 (n=263) flat: 5-yr OS 20.9% vs 21.2%
  - P-interaction .022 for nodal status
- ⚠️ Node-negative benefit is a subgroup read, no subgroup HR reported in source text (Fig 3 shows 5-yr rates and P-interaction only)
- ⚠️ Underpowered as reported: 270 OS events vs 316 planned, power 72% for the hypothesized HR 0.76
- ⚠️ Step-1 chemo was gemcitabine ± erlotinib, zero pts got FOLFIRINOX, so systemic backbone is obsolete
- 🔗 vs ESPAC-1 (adjuvant CXRT detrimental): here 2D split-course, no postop imaging, no QA; 0848 used standardized IMRT-era CXRT with central review
- 🔗 Patterns of failure: distant-only first failure 38%, local-only 23%, consistent with the codominant failure argument for locoregional therapy
