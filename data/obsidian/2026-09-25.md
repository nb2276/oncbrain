### ROAM/EORTC-1308

> Adjuvant IMRT 60 Gy/30 fx vs observation after complete resection of WHO grade 2 atypical meningioma: DFS HR 0.51 (0.27–0.97); 5-yr DFS 79.9% vs 64.3%.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The RT effect is probably understated: 12 of 78 assigned pts never started RT, and DFS counts out-of-field and new meningiomas that a 5–10 mm CTV was never aimed at. The planned patterns-of-failure analysis will decide whether dural margins can tighten under modern IGRT, which is the next dosimetric question once adjuvant RT is adopted.

- 🔍 Phase 3 open-label RCT, 1:1, N=157, 58 hospitals in 11 countries
- 🔍 Newly diagnosed atypical meningioma, surgeon-assessed GTR (Simpson I–III), WHO PS ≤2
- 💊 RT arm: fractionated IMRT 60 Gy in 30 fx over 6 weeks, prospective central RTQA
- 🔍 CTV 5–10 mm isotropic from GTV, then 3–5 mm PTV
- 📊 Efficacy at median f/u 64 mo (IQR 50–75)

  | Endpoint | Radiotherapy (n=78) | Observation (n=79) | Effect |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Meningioma recurrence | 11 (14%) | 24 (30%) | n/a |
  | 5-yr DFS | 79.9% (67.6–87.9) | 64.3% (51.9–74.2) | Diff 15.6 pp (0.6–30.5) |
  | DFS, ITT | n/a | n/a | HR 0.51 (0.27–0.97), p=0.0396 |
  | DFS, as-treated | 10/66 (15%) recurred | 25/91 (27%) recurred | HR 0.54 (0.28–1.04), p=0.0642 |
  | Deaths | 7 (9%) | 6 (8%) | 5-yr OS OR 0.91 (0.27–3.05) |
  | Second-line treatment | 8 (10%) | 16 (20%) | n/a |

- ⚠️ Closed short of the 46-event target (41 DFS events) owing to funding constraints
- ⚠️ 12/78 assigned to RT never started it; 2 stopped early, biasing toward the null
- 📊 RT toxicity (as-treated, n=66)
  - Early RT-related AEs: 30 (45%) pts, 65 G1 and 19 G2 events
  - Late RT-related AEs: 17 (26%), mostly hair loss, headache, fatigue
  - G2 or 3 RT-related SAEs: 5 (8%); no treatment-related deaths
  - One G2 radiation necrosis, one G2 optic neuritis with optic dose at constraint limits
- 📊 Post-hoc molecular subset (n=132): DFS HR 0.49 (0.25–0.95); benefit held after methylation-class adjustment
- ⚠️ No central pathology or MRI review; recurrence read locally in an unmasked trial
