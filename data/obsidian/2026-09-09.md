# 2026-09-09

**TORPEdO: IMPT vs IMRT chemoRT in locally advanced OPSCC, 12-mo tube dependence/severe weight loss 18% vs 7% (p=0.079), UW-QoL 78.3 vs 77.1 (p=0.56), no functional gain.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> The one head-neck readout: TORPEdO, a phase 3 RCT of IMPT vs IMRT (N=205, 70 Gy/33 fx + cisplatin). Protons lowered OAR doses but gave no 12-mo functional gain. Tube dependence/severe weight loss was 18% vs 7% (p=0.079) and UW-QoL 78.3 vs 77.1 (p=0.56). Weigh this against missed fractions in 49% of IMPT pts and a two-centre pathway confound.

## 👄 Head & Neck

TORPEdO is the randomised test of whether IMPT's OAR dose sparing buys a functional benefit in chemoRT for OPSCC, and at 12 mo it did not.

### TORPEdO

> IMPT vs IMRT in locally advanced OPSCC: 12-mo tube dependence/severe weight loss 18% vs 7% (p=0.079), UW-QoL 78.3 vs 77.1 (p=0.56).

- 🔍 Phase 3 open-label RCT, 2:1, 20 UK NHS hospitals, N=205 (136 IMPT, 69 IMRT)
- 🔍 Both arms 70 Gy / 56 Gy in 33 fx over 6.5 wk plus cisplatin 100 mg/m² x2
- 📊 Co-primary and exploratory endpoints, IMPT vs IMRT
- 📊 G3 weight loss (≥20%) by timepoint, IMPT vs IMRT

  | Timepoint | IMPT | IMRT |
  |---|---|---|
  | End of CRT | none | none |
  | 6 mo | 20/116 (17%) | 3/56 (5%) |
  | 12 mo | 20/110 (18%) | 3/53 (6%) |
  | 24 mo | 12/95 (13%) | none |

- 📐 UW-QoL physical composite, supporting timepoints (adjusted means, 99% CI)
- ⚠️ IMPT missed fractions in 65 (49%) of 132 pts, cyclotron breakdown in 47 (72%) of 65
- ⚠️ Unmasked; IMPT at 2 national centres vs IMRT at local referring centre, pathway confound
- ⚠️ UW-QoL co-primary evaluable in only 154 (75%) of 205
- 📊 Pre-planned Dutch NTCP-enriched cohort, 78 (51%) of 153
  - UW-QoL 78.2 (95% CI 74.7-81.6) IMPT vs 75.8 (69.3-82.3) IMRT
  - ⚠️ Unbalanced enrichment: 61 (62%) of 99 IMPT vs 17 (31%) of 54 IMRT
- 🔗 IMPT lowered most OAR doses incl highest-priority structures, without a 12-mo functional gain
