# 2026-09-09

**TORPEdO: IMPT not superior to IMRT in locally advanced oropharyngeal SCC, 12-mo G-tube/≥20% weight loss 18% vs 7% (p=0.079), UW-QoL 78.3 vs 77.1 (p=0.56).**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Head-neck carried the day: phase 3 TORPEdO (N=205) showed no co-primary gain for IMPT over IMRT in locally advanced oropharyngeal SCC at 12 mo. The composite numerically favored IMRT (18% vs 7%, adj OR 2.80, p=0.079), driven by weight loss, not tubes. Proton delivery burden: 61% replanning, 49% of IMPT pts missed fractions.

## 👄 Head & Neck

IMPT vs IMRT at 70 Gy/33 fx with cisplatin: both 12-mo co-primaries (G-tube/weight loss composite, UW-QoL) negative, while LRC and OS stay exploratory.

### TORPEdO

> 18% vs 7% 12-mo G-tube dependence/≥20% weight loss (p=0.079), UW-QoL 78.3 vs 77.1 (p=0.56): IMPT not superior to IMRT, locally advanced oropharyngeal SCC.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Lower OAR doses on IMPT did not become function: 12-mo UW-QoL 78.3 vs 77.1, and ≥20% weight loss ran higher on IMPT (20/110 vs 3/53), persisting at 24 mo in 12 of 95. Dutch NTCP-enriched pts also showed no clear gain (78.2 vs 75.8), undercutting model-based IMPT referral in locally advanced oropharyngeal SCC.

- 🔍 Phase 3 open-label RCT, 2:1, 20 UK NHS hospitals, N=205 (136 IMPT, 69 IMRT)
- 🔍 70 Gy/33 fx high-risk, 56 Gy lower-risk, 6.5 wk, bilateral neck; RBE 1.1 for IMPT
- 💊 Cisplatin 100 mg/m² q3w x2 planned; 138 (69%) of 199 received both cycles
- 📊 Co-primary and exploratory endpoints, IMPT vs IMRT

  | Endpoint | IMPT | IMRT | Effect |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | G-tube dependence or ≥20% weight loss, 12 mo (co-primary) | 21/119 (18%) | 4/59 (7%) | adj OR 2.80 (97.5% CI 0.75–10.41), p=0.079 |
  | ≥20% weight loss, 12 mo | 20/110 (18%) | 3/53 (6%) | n/a |
  | G-tube dependence, 12 mo | 2/119 (2%) | 1/59 (2%) | n/a |
  | UW-QoL physical composite, 12 mo (co-primary) | 78.3 | 77.1 | diff 1.3 (97.5% CI –3.7 to 6.2), p=0.56 |
  | UW-QoL physical composite, 3 mo | 70.8 | 66.8 | diff 4.0 (99% CI –1.4 to 9.5) |
  | UW-QoL physical composite, 24 mo | 81.6 | 79.9 | diff 1.7 (99% CI –3.6 to 7.0) |
  | 24-mo freedom from LRR | 94% (99% CI 86–98) | 97% (82–100) | HR 2.6 (99% CI 0.3–20.3), p=0.24 |
  | 24-mo OS | 95% (86–98) | 95% (81–99) | HR 1.6 (99% CI 0.3–8.8), p=0.47 |

- 📊 Nutrition: weight loss, not tubes, drove the composite
  - Grade 3 weight loss at 24 mo: 12 (13%) of 95 IMPT vs none IMRT
  - Feeding-tube use at any time: 61 (45%) of 135 vs 40 (58%) of 69
  - Prophylactic tubes: 35 (26%) of 135 vs 26 (38%) of 69
- 📊 Dutch NTCP-enriched cohort (pre-planned)
  - Enriched: 61 (62%) of 99 IMPT vs 17 (31%) of 54 IMRT
  - 12-mo UW-QoL 78.2 (95% CI 74.7 to 81.6) vs 75.8 (69.3 to 82.3)
- 📊 Treatment-related SAEs 1 IMPT vs 4 IMRT; no treatment-related deaths
- ⚠️ IMPT delivery burden
  - Replanning 81 (61%) of 132 IMPT vs 20 (30%) of 67 IMRT
  - Missed IMPT fractions in 65 (49%) of 132 pts, 47 (72%) from cyclotron breakdown
  - Median RT duration 44 days in both arms
- ⚠️ 2:1 allocation left 69 IMRT pts; composite OR CI 0.75–10.41 excludes neither harm nor benefit
- ⚠️ LRC and OS exploratory at the 1% level; LRR HR 99% CI 0.3 to 20.3
- ⚠️ Open-label with a PRO co-primary; UW-QoL evaluable in 154 (75%) of 205
