# 2026-11-19

**HyTEC prostate SBRT: 5-yr TCP 95% at EQD2 90 Gy (36.1 Gy/5 fx) low-intermediate risk vs 102 Gy (38.7 Gy/5 fx) high risk, 4,821 pts.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Five AAPM HyTEC pooled dose-response models, all study-level, none randomised. Prostate carries the most actionable dose targets: 36.1 Gy/5 fx for 95% TCP in low-intermediate risk, rectal C39Gy ≥40% for late ≥G3 bowel (OR 8.7). Stage I NSCLC maximal LC at PTV-periphery 50±1 Gy/5fx; recurrent previously-irradiated H&N reirradiation suggested at 40 to 50 Gy/5fx.

## 🟡 Hepatobiliary

Liver SBRT GI risk modeled on Rx dose only; pooled series reported no stomach, duodenum or bowel doses.

### HyTEC Liver SBRT Dose-Volume Effects

> Pooled probit NTCP: G≥2 GI D50 48.0 Gy, G≥3 GI D50 87.1 Gy by Rx dose; no significant liver-enzyme dose response vs MLD (P = .10).

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The dose response appeared in GI toxicity, not in liver enzymes. G≥2 GI had a D10 of 18.4 Gy, and a mean Rx of 50 Gy in 3-6 fx kept G≥3 GI under 10%. Because Rx dose stood in for gastric and duodenal dose, central tumors near the hilum (reported to carry higher GI risk) still need organ-specific constraints.

- 🔍 Pooled study-level review of liver SBRT series in PubMed through May 2014
- 🔍 Studies were included only if they reported both toxicity and dose-volume data
- 🔍 Probit NTCP fit by maximum likelihood, using physical dose rather than BED
- 📊 Probit fit parameters by endpoint (95% CI)

  | Endpoint | Events/pts | D50 (Gy) | γ50 | D10 / D20 (Gy) |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | G≥3 liver enzymes (vs MLD) | 17/288 | 40.8 (25.5-∞) | 0.95 (0.58–1.44) | not reported |
  | G≥2 general GI (vs Rx dose) | 99/278 | 48.0 (43.6–65.9) | 0.83 (0.37–1.31) | 18.4 / 28.6 |
  | G≥3 general GI (vs Rx dose) | 25/399 | 87.1 (69.1–194.4) | 1.22 (0.78–1.68) | 50.6 / 63.1 |

- 📊 Liver enzyme fit (5 studies) was not significant (P = .10), so dose independence can't be excluded
- 📊 A mean Rx dose of 50 Gy in 3-6 fx kept the modeled G≥3 GI risk below 10%
- 📊 In series planning with ≥700 cm³ to ≥15 or ≥17 Gy, G≥3 GI was 9.3% (11 of 118), 68% CI 6.6% to 12.8%
- 🔗 QUANTEC MLD limits fall under a 20% probability of G3 enzyme toxicity on this fit
  - Primary liver cancer: 13 Gy (3 fx), 18 Gy (6 fx)
  - Metastases: 15 Gy (3 fx), 20 Gy (6 fx)
- ⚠️ Grade 5 liver failure was reported in 3 series but not modeled
  - Bujold: 3 of 102 HCC
  - Barney: 1 of 9
  - Andolino: 4 of 8 HCC pts with baseline CP ≥8
- ⚠️ GI risk was modeled on Rx dose because stomach, duodenum and bowel doses weren't reported
- ⚠️ Primary and metastatic disease were pooled, although mets were reported to tolerate RT better

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Radiation Dose-Volume Effects for Liver SBRT** — Moyed Miften; Yevgeniy Vinogradskiy; Vitali Moiseenko et al.. *Int J Radiat Oncol Biol Phys*
  - 📎 [[papers/hepatobiliary/miften-radiation-dose-volume-effects-liver-sbrt.pdf|HyTEC Liver SBRT Dose-Volume Effects (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions
> - Toxicity differences between liver mets, HCC and IHC
> - GI organ-specific dose limits beyond prescription dose
> - Validated MLD limit for liver-function decline in HCC

## 🫁 Thoracic / Lung

Stage I T1-T2 NSCLC dose targets from pooled TCP fits; no model fit well by χ2/ndf, regrowth best.

### HyTEC Local Control After SBRT for Stage I NSCLC

> Pooled TCP modeling of 46 SBRT series predicts maximal LC at PTV-periphery 43±1 Gy/3fx, 47±1 Gy/4fx, 50±1 Gy/5fx in T1-T2 stage I NSCLC.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The usable output is Table 2: maximal LC needs physical dose of 43±1, 47±1 and 50±1 Gy at the PTV periphery (80% isodose) in 3, 4 and 5 fx, with T2 needing more (45±1/50±1/53±1 Gy) than T1 (42±1/46±1/48±1 Gy). That argues for stage-adapted prescription, not de-escalation.

- 🔍 Systematic review: 160 SBRT articles identified (published through May 2014), 46 met quality criteria
- 🔍 3268 pts treated at ≥6 Gy/fx, plus 211 pts from 3 studies at 3-4 Gy/fx added to fit the shoulder
- 🔍 Exclusions
  - Series with fewer than 10 pts
  - Tumor stage higher than T2
  - Oligometastatic disease to the lung
- 📊 1, 2, 3 and 5yr TCP for T1, T2 and T1+T2 fit to LQ, USC and regrowth models as a function of BED
- 📐 None of the three models fit well by χ2/ndf; regrowth gave the best fit
- 📐 Regrowth α/β approximately 25 Gy (T1), 19 Gy (T2), 21 Gy (T1+T2 combined)
- 📊 Required physical dose (Gy) for maximal TCP by model and stage (PTV dose = PTV covered by 80% isodose line)

  | Model · stage | Dose point | 3 fx | 4 fx | 5 fx |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Regrowth T1 | PTV | 42±1 | 46±1 | 48±1 |
  | Regrowth T2 | PTV | 45±1 | 50±1 | 53±1 |
  | Regrowth T1+T2 | PTV | 43±1 | 47±1 | 50±1 |
  | LQ T1+T2 | PTV | 44±1 | 47±1 | 50±1 |
  | USC T1+T2 | PTV | 44±1 | 47±1 | 50±1 |
  | Regrowth T1 | Isocenter | 52±1 | 57±1 | 60±1 |
  | Regrowth T2 | Isocenter | 56±1 | 62±1 | 66±1 |
  | Regrowth T1+T2 | Isocenter | 54±1 | 59±1 | 63±1 |
  | LQ T1+T2 | Isocenter | 55±1 | 59±1 | 63±1 |
  | USC T1+T2 | Isocenter | 55±1 | 59±1 | 63±1 |

- 🔗 Historic conventional RT (2-3 Gy/fx to ≈54-60 Gy): long-term LC ≈30-70%, OS ≈15-30%
- ⚠️ Censoring intercurrent deaths may leave a healthier evaluable subset, so 1-3yr actuarial LC may be overestimated
- ⚠️ LC mostly called radiographically; post-SBRT fibrosis and early FDG uptake can mask or mimic recurrence
- ⚠️ Most series reported outcomes only up to 3yr; OS collected but not modeled
- ⚠️ Surgical series count lobar and hilar/mediastinal failure as local failure, so LC vs surgery is not like-for-like

[doi:10.1016/j.ijrobp.2019.03.045](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijrobp.2019.03.045)

> [!question] Open questions

## 🌰 Prostate

Two HyTEC prostate SBRT reviews: TCP dose targets by risk group, and normal-tissue dose-volume thresholds for rectum, bladder, urethra.

### HyTEC Prostate SBRT Normal Tissue Dose-Toxicity (Heterogeneous-dosing Phase 2)

> Rectal C39Gy ≥40% predicted late ≥G3 bowel toxicity (OR 8.7, AUC 0.83); urethra Dmax ≥47 Gy predicted ≥G2 urinary (OR 4.2) in 4-5 fx SBRT.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Model discrimination splits by organ. Rectal models reached AUC 0.83 (C39Gy) and 0.93 (V50Gy), urethral refits reached only 0.60, 0.62 and 0.61, and the 17 Gy bladder cutpoint was picked to maximize its OR. The rectal high-dose limits are the ones backed by evidence. The urethral and bladder thresholds are planning goals.

- ⚠️ Source record mismatch: metadata cites Fuller phase 2 (Eur Urol Oncol 2018); full text is Wang et al HyTEC review
- 🔍 AAPM HyTEC review of prostate SBRT series, [[PMID 2001]]-2018; NTCP refits on four series with dose/volume data
- 📊 Dose thresholds associated with late toxicity, NTCP refits (5-fx physical dose)

  | Organ · endpoint | Series | Threshold | OR (95% CI) | p | Model AUC |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Rectum · late ≥G3 bowel | Kim (multicenter), 91 pts | C39Gy ≥ 40% | 8.7 (1.3-59.4) | 0.02 | 0.83 |
  | Bladder · EPIC incontinence MID | Qi (UCLA), 86 pts, 40 Gy/5 fx | Mean ≥ 17 Gy | 7.7 (1.3-45.0) | 0.008 | 0.63 |
  | Urethra · late ≥G2 urinary | Zhang (UCSF), 78 pts, 38 Gy/4 fx | Dmax ≥ 47 Gy | 4.2 (1.2-15.0) | 0.04 | 0.61 |

- 📊 Rectum V50Gy model: TD50 5.9cc (5.1-7.1), AUC 0.93
- 📊 Urethra 20% G2 risk level: D1cc 47 Gy, D0.1cc 49 Gy, Dmax 50 Gy (5 fx)
- 📊 50 Gy/5 fx escalation: 6 of 61 (10%) CTCAE G3-4 rectal toxicity, 5 needed colostomy
- 📊 Sunnybrook: RTOG ≥G2 late urinary 5% at 35 Gy vs 48% at 40 Gy
- 🔍 Non-dosimetric toxicity modifiers
  - Prior TURP: late G2-3 hematuria 21% vs 2%
  - Prostate > vs ≤ 60 cc: late G2-3 urinary 15% vs 8%
  - Anticoagulants: ≥G2 late hematochezia 47% vs 18%
  - QD vs QOD (Stanford): late G1-2 urinary 56% vs 17%, bowel 44% vs 5%; learning-curve confounded
- ⚠️ Bladder 17 Gy cutpoint chosen to maximize the OR; optimistic until externally validated
- ⚠️ Urethral models discriminate poorly: AUC 0.60, 0.62, 0.61 for D1cc, D0.1cc, Dmax

[doi:10.1016/j.euo.2018.06.013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.euo.2018.06.013)

### HyTEC Prostate SBRT Tumor Control Probability

> 5-yr TCP 95% at EQD2 90 Gy (36.1 Gy/5 fx) for low-intermediate risk vs 102 Gy (38.7 Gy/5 fx) for high risk; 25 cohorts, 4,821 pts.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The low-intermediate 90% TCP point (EQD2 71 Gy, 31.7 Gy/5 fx) sits below the ~80 Gy floor of every reviewed cohort, so it is extrapolation, not a de-escalation floor. The data-supported read is 95% TCP at 36.1 Gy/5 fx; the high-risk 38.7 Gy/5 fx target rests on 85 pts.

- 📊 5-yr FFBR-based TCP dose targets by risk group (α/β=1.5)

  | TCP target | Low-intermediate risk | High risk |
  |---|---|---|
  | 90% | EQD2 71 Gy (31.7 Gy/5 fx) | EQD2 97 Gy (37.6 Gy/5 fx) |
  | 95% | EQD2 90 Gy (36.1 Gy/5 fx) | EQD2 102 Gy (38.7 Gy/5 fx) |

- 🔍 AAPM HyTEC systematic review plus Poisson TCP dose-response model, series published through March 2018
- 🔍 Risk mix across 4,821 pts (when reported)
  - Low risk: N=2,235
  - Intermediate risk: N=1,894
  - High risk: N=446
- 🔍 Model fit drew only on cohorts with ≥5 yr f/u
  - Low-intermediate curve: 9 studies, 1069 pts
  - High-risk curve: 3 studies, 85 pts
- 🔍 Regimens 32-50 Gy in 4-5 fx; most prescriptions 35-40 Gy
- 📐 BED (α/β=1.5) range 183.1-383.3 Gy
- 🔍 All fiducial-guided; 16 cohorts CyberKnife, 7 conventional linac
- ⚠️ No reviewed cohort below ~80 Gy EQD2: the 71 Gy (90% TCP) low-intermediate point is model extrapolation
- ⚠️ Prescription dose only; no dose-volume data, so intraprostatic dose not modeled
- ⚠️ Authors flag the 446 high-risk pts as likely highly selected
- 🔗 HYPO-RT-PC: 7-fx SBRT-like regimen gave similar 5-yr control and toxicity vs conventional fractionation

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Tumor Control Probability Modeling and Systematic Review of the Literature of Stereotactic Body Radiation Therapy for Prostate Cancer** — Royce, Trevor J.; Mavroidis, Panayiotis; Wang, Kyle et al.. *International Journal of Radiation Oncology*Biology*Physics* (2021-05). [doi:10.1016/j.ijrobp.2020.08.014](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijrobp.2020.08.014)
  - 📎 [[papers/prostate/2021-royce-tumor-control-probability-modeling-systematic-review.pdf|HyTEC Prostate SBRT Tumor Control Probability (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions
> - Durability of biochemical control beyond 5 years across risk groups
> - Optimal SBRT dose for high-risk prostate cancer

## 👄 Head & Neck

Recurrent previously-irradiated H&N: 3-yr LC dose-response, against fatal complications (mainly carotid blowout) in up to 10% to 20%.

### HyTEC Head and Neck SBRT for Recurrent Previously-Irradiated Disease

> 3-yr LC 15% at 26.8 Gy vs 40% at 44.4 Gy (5-fx equivalent); 40 to 50 Gy/5fx suggested for SBRT reirradiation of recurrent previously-irradiated H&N.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Dose matters for how long control lasts, not for the 1-yr response: the 1-yr LC dose-response did not reach significance (P=.096), while the 2- and 3-yr models reached P<.01. Volume gates the prescription. Below 25 cm3 the response flattens above 45 Gy/5fx, so escalating to 45 to 50 Gy is a question for larger recurrences, weighed against carotid blowout rates of up to 10% to 20%.

- 🔍 AAPM HyTEC systematic review: 8 publications, >300 cases of SBRT reirradiation for locally recurrent previously-irradiated H&N
- 🔍 293 articles screened, 25 candidates, 16 not fully evaluable; 8 used for the dose-response model
- 📊 Model-estimated outcomes by timepoint (5-fraction equivalent doses)

  | Endpoint | Model estimate | Dose-response test |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1-yr LC | 50% at 25.6 Gy vs 60% at 40.7 Gy | Median split P=.069; model P=.096 |
  | 2-yr LC | 50% at 45 Gy | P<.01 (model and median split) |
  | 3-yr LC | 15% at 26.8 Gy vs 40% at 44.4 Gy; 41% at 45 Gy | P<.01 (model and median split) |
  | 2-yr OS | 47% at 45 Gy | Median split P=.13; model P=.060 |

- 📊 Superior LC, possibly improved OS, at 35 to 45 Gy (5 fx) vs <30 Gy
- 💊 Volume-adapted dose suggestions (5-fraction equivalent)
  - Retreatment overall: 40 to 50 Gy
  - Tumors <25 cm3: maximum 45 Gy, given flat dose-response above this level
  - Larger tumors: 45 to 50 Gy, with novel escalation strategies worth exploring
- 📐 Doses converted to 5-fx equivalent via LQ (α/β 10 Gy); logistic fit, bootstrap CIs (2000 samples)
- ⚠️ Fatal complications, mainly carotid blowout, reported in up to 10% to 20% even at lower doses
- ⚠️ Rwigema 2011 contributes one-third of pts, so it largely drives the 2- and 3-yr fits
- ⚠️ LC and LRC pooled as one endpoint; HPV status, histology, concurrent cetuximab not modeled
- 🔍 Every-other-day fractionation linked to lower carotid toxicity; its effect on tumor control is untested
