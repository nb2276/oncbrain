# 2026-07-19

**RAVES QoL: adjuvant fossa RT drives 16% vs 2% severe urinary leakage at 5yr, with no QOL penalty for delaying to salvage.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> The prostate day is a toxicity read, not an efficacy one: RAVES's QoL substudy prices the cost of treating everyone up front, 16% vs 2% severe urinary leakage at 5yr, while aRT vs sRT-who-actually-got-RT is a wash (16% vs 13%, p=0.7). Time from RP to RT tracked with nothing, so early salvage carries no QOL tax.

## 🌰 Prostate

Post-RP fossa RT: the QOL argument for salvage over adjuvant rests on the half of pts who never need RT, not on any per-patient toxicity advantage.

### TROG 08.03 RAVES (QoL substudy)

> Severe urinary leakage 16% vs 2% at 5yr with aRT vs no RT; sRT timing had no QOL association.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The QOL argument for salvage is about avoidance, not delay: 52% of the sRT arm never needed RT, and among men who did get RT, aRT vs sRT differed by 16% vs 13% severe leakage at 5yr (p=0.7). With 64 Gy/32 fx 3D-CRT, no ADT, no nodal RT, this does not license waiting once PSA rises.

- 📊 1° EP: MCIC (>0.5 SD decline from baseline) per EORTC QLQ-PR25 domain
- 🔍 Planned secondary analysis of the phase 3 non-inferiority RAVES trial; n=166 aRT, n=167 sRT
- 🔍 RT was 64 Gy/32 fx to the prostate fossa in both arms; concurrent ADT and pelvic nodal RT not allowed
- 📐 Only 80/167 (48%) of sRT-arm pts ever received RT; median time to sRT 2.6 yr (IQR 1.5-4.6)
- 📊 Bowel MCIC at 5yr, aRT 37% (40/109) vs no-RT 12% (6/50); vs full sRT arm 37% vs 20%, p=0.009
- 📊 Severe ("quite a bit"/"very much") urinary leakage, aRT vs no RT

  | Time from randomization | Adjuvant RT n/N (%) | No RT n/N (%) | p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 4 yr | 15/124 (12) | 1/59 (1.7) | 0.02 |
  | 5 yr | 18/111 (16) | 1/50 (2) | 0.01 |

- 📊 aRT vs sRT-who-received-RT: severe urinary leakage at 5yr 16% vs 13%, p=0.7
- 📐 Time from RP to RT showed no association with any domain at 3-5 yr (all univariable coefficients ns, Table 2)
- ⚠️ Bowel MCIC threshold was a 2-point change on a 100-point scale, a minor worsening by the authors' own reading
- ⚠️ Most pts treated with 3D-CRT, not IMRT; modern planning would be expected to lower the bowel signal
- ⚠️ Exploratory, no correction for multiple testing; 5yr QOL completion only 64% (214/333)
- ⚠️ sRT-arm pts who received RT had worse sexual activity MCIC at 3yr (58% vs 32%, p=0.004) and 4yr (65% vs 43%, p=0.038), confounded by higher-risk disease and likely ADT
- 🔗 Clinician-rated toxicity in the parent RAVES: G2+ GU 70% aRT vs 54% sRT, with no GI difference, so the patient-reported bowel signal here is the discrepant finding
