# 2026-07-19

**RAVES QOL: post-RP adjuvant RT put severe urinary leakage at 16% vs 2% in men who never needed RT (5 yr, p=0.01).**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Prostate carried the day, and it is a fossa-RT toxicity read, not an efficacy one: RAVES' protocol-planned QOL secondary quantifies what adjuvant RT costs men who would never have needed it, while timing from RP to sRT showed no QOL association at 3-5 yr. Caveats matter: 3D-CRT era, exploratory, 64% completion.

## 🌰 Prostate

Patient-reported toxicity of adjuvant vs salvage fossa RT, from the trial that already showed noninferior biochemical control.

### RAVES

> Post-RP RT drove severe urinary leakage to 16% vs 2% at 5yr; sRT timing showed no QOL association.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The QOL penalty tracks receiving RT, not when you give it: severe leakage was 16% aRT vs 13% in salvage men who were irradiated (p=0.7), and time from RP to RT was flat across every domain at 3-5 yr. So delaying salvage buys nothing functionally; the benefit is the 52% of sRT men never irradiated at all.

- 🔍 Protocol-planned secondary analysis of TROG 08.03 phase 3 noninferiority RCT; aRT n=166 vs sRT n=167
- 📐 RT dose 64 Gy in 32 fx to prostate fossa in both arms; concurrent ADT and pelvic nodal RT not allowed
- 🔍 1° EP for this substudy was MCIC (>0.5 SD decline from baseline) on EORTC QLQ-PR25 domains
- 📐 MCIC thresholds: 7 pts urinary, 2 pts bowel, 14 pts sexual activity, 12 pts sexual function
- 📊 Bowel MCIC at 5 yr, aRT vs the sRT men who never needed RT

  | Comparison | aRT | Comparator | p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | MCIC bowel symptoms, 5 yr (vs no RT) | 37% (40/109) | 12% (6/50) | not reported in source |
  | MCIC bowel symptoms, 5 yr (vs whole sRT arm) | 37% | 20% | 0.009 |
  | Severe urinary leakage, 5 yr (vs no RT) | 16% (18/111) | 2% (1/50) | 0.01 |
  | Severe urinary leakage, 4 yr (vs no RT) | 12% (15/124) | 1.7% (1/59) | 0.02 |

- 📊 aRT vs sRT men who did receive RT: severe urinary leakage 16% vs 13% at 5 yr post-RT, p=0.7
- 📊 Time from RP to RT showed no association with any domain at 3-5 yr; all coefficients ns (Table 2)
- ⚠️ Bowel MCIC threshold was 2.2 pts on a 100-point scale; the instrument's minimum possible change is 8.3 pts
- ⚠️ 82% Gleason 7; most treated with 3D-CRT not IMRT, so bowel signal likely overstates modern fossa RT
- ⚠️ Exploratory, no correction for multiple testing; 5 yr QOL completion only 64% (214/333)
- 🔗 Clinician-rated RAVES data ran the other way on bowel: G2+ GU 70% aRT vs 54% sRT, no GI difference
- 🔗 Prior clinician-rated series put CTCAE G2 urinary incontinence at 10-20%, in line with the 16% here

**Sources:**
