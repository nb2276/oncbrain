# 2026-07-19

**RAVES QOL: severe urinary leakage 16% vs 2% at 5 yr with adjuvant fossa RT vs never irradiated, and timing of salvage RT costs nothing.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Prostate carried the day. RAVES quantifies what deferring fossa RT buys: 48% of the sRT arm never needed RT at all, and among men who did get RT, 5 yr severe urinary leakage was equivalent whether given adjuvant or salvage (16% vs 13%). The patient-reported bowel signal (37% vs 20% MCIC) never showed up on clinician CTCAE grading.

## 🌰 Prostate

Post-RP fossa RT: the QOL cost of adjuvant over salvage, from the trial that already showed no efficacy penalty for waiting.

### TROG 08.03 RAVES QOL Substudy

> Severe urinary leakage 16% vs 2% at 5yr with aRT vs no RT; timing of salvage RT unrelated to QOL.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The QOL benefit of a salvage approach is avoidance, not delay: 52% of the sRT arm never needed RT, and among men who did get RT, severe urinary leakage at 5 yr was the same whether early or late (16% vs 13%, p = 0.7), with no coefficient linking RP-to-RT interval to any domain. Dose was 64 Gy/32 fx fossa-only, no ADT, no nodes.

- 📊 1° EP: proportion with MCIC (>0.5 SD decline from baseline) on EORTC QLQ-PR25 domains
- 🔍 Protocol-planned secondary analysis of the phase 3 noninferiority RAVES trial, n=166 aRT vs 167 sRT
- 🔍 RT was 64 Gy/32 fx to the prostate fossa in both arms; no concurrent ADT, no pelvic nodal treatment allowed
- 📊 Only 80/167 (48%) of the sRT arm ever received RT; 87 never required it
- 📊 aRT vs no RT (sRT arm never irradiated), 5 yr after randomization

  | Endpoint at 5 yr | Adjuvant RT | No RT | p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | MCIC bowel symptoms | 37% (40/109) | 12% (6/50) | not reported in source |
  | Severe urinary leakage | 18/111 (16) | 1/50 (2) | 0.01 |
  | Severe urinary leakage at 4 yr | 15/124 (12) | 1/59 (1.7) | 0.02 |
  | Urinary urgency | 18/110 (16) | 3/50 (6) | 0.072 |

- 📊 aRT vs sRT arm as randomized: MCIC bowel worse with aRT at 5 yr (37% vs 20%; p = 0.009)
- 📊 aRT vs sRT patients who actually received RT: severe urinary leakage at 5 yr equivalent (16% vs 13%; p = 0.7)
- 📐 Time from RP to RT showed no association with any domain: all univariable coefficients ns at 3, 4, 5 yr (e.g. urinary 5 yr 0.14, 95% CI -0.34 to 0.61, p = 0.6)
- ⚠️ Bowel MCIC threshold was a 2-point change on a 100-point scale, and the instrument's minimum possible change is 8.3 points
- ⚠️ Most men treated with 3D-CRT, not IMRT; authors expect modern technique to lower the bowel signal
- ⚠️ Exploratory, no correction for multiple testing; 5-yr QOL completion only 64% (214/333)
- 🔗 RAVES clinician-rated data had G2+ GU toxicity 70% aRT vs 54% sRT, with no GI difference, so the patient-reported bowel signal is not visible on CTCAE
- 🔗 Cross-trial comparison with GETUG-AFU 17 and RADICALS limited by differing urinary symptom reporting and grading
