# 2026-11-16

**ACOSOG Z11 10-yr: OS 86.3% SLNB alone vs 83.6% ALND, LRR 5.3% vs 6.2%; NCCN 2023 talk collates the case for locoregional breast de-escalation.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> No new randomised readouts today, only reviews and practice references. Breast carries the most: an NCCN 2023 talk collating Z11, AMAROS, FAST-Forward and RTOG 1014 behind axillary, PBI and 5-fx de-escalation. ## 🧠 CNS

Two SRS technique references: planning-MRI error sources and three centers' single-isocenter multimet protocols. Neither is a prospective outcome study.

### MRI for brain stereotactic radiotherapy

> 6-mo LC 56% vs 95% with MRI-to-SRS interval ≥14 days; uncorrected gradient distortion would have caused geographic miss in 8 of 28 lesions.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The fixable error is upstream of the plan: outside diagnostic MRI is often only 2D distortion-corrected, and uncorrected scans shifted GTVs up to 3.9 mm with geographic miss in 8 of 28 lesions, against PTV margins of ≤1 mm. Specifying 3D correction, active shimming, higher bandwidth and isotropic 3D T1 is a protocol decision you can make before contouring.

- 🔍 Narrative review from the Erlangen group, Strahlenther Onkol 2020; companion to the DEGRO/DGMP stereotactic QA papers
- 📊 Planning-MRI error sources and the cited magnitudes

  | Factor | Cited finding | Source |
  |---|---|---|
  | MRI-to-SRS interval ≥14 days | 6-mo LC 56% vs 95% | Seymour et al. |
  | MRI-to-SRS interval >7 days | Management change 78% (41% at ≤7 days) | Salkeld et al. |
  | Repeat MRI during 3-5 fx FSRT | PTV coverage loss up to 34.8% intact mets, up to 4.5% cavities | Hessen et al. |
  | No gradient distortion correction | GTV shift median 1.2 mm, max 3.9 mm; miss in 8 of 28 lesions | Seibert et al. |
  | Susceptibility at sinus air-bone interface | Avg 1.6 mm; still 0.8 mm at 12 mm distance | Wang et al. 2013 |

- 📊 Contrast-enhanced T1 sequence comparison (Danieli et al., 16 mets + 38 gliomas)

  | Metric | T1-SPACE | T1-MPRAGE | T1-VIBE |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Median GTV | 1.78 cm3 | 1.36 cm3 | 1.62 cm3 |
  | Best conspicuity rating | All lesions | 27.8% | 44.4% |
  | Brain mets missed | None | 15.8% | Not reported |

- 📐 Consensus tolerance: total MRI distortion <1 mm for brain SRS (2016 MRI-simulation consensus)
- 🔍 Scanner settings the review asks for on a planning MRI
  - Vendor 3D distortion correction; 2D correction leaves through-plane distortion
  - Patient-specific active shimming
  - Higher readout bandwidth, accepting SNR loss
  - Isotropic 3D T1 without slice gaps
- 📊 Contrast timing: mets <5 mm seen in 40.6% at 10 min vs 75.0% at 20 min (Yuh et al.)
- 📐 Volumetric error exceeds 10% when the GTV spans <5 slices, relevant for small mets
- 🔍 Erlangen policy: imaging-to-treatment interval capped at 5 days; repeat MRI at least once in prolonged courses
- ⚠️ Narrative, not systematic: no search strategy, and cited magnitudes come from small single studies

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Magnetic resonance imaging for brain stereotactic radiotherapy** — Putz, Florian; Mengling, Veit; Perrin, Rosalind et al.. *Strahlentherapie und Onkologie* (2020-03). [doi:10.1007/s00066-020-01604-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00066-020-01604-0)
  > Abstract Due to its superior soft tissue contrast, magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) is essential for many radiotherapy treatment indications. This is especially true for treatment planning in intracranial tumors, where MRI has a long-standing history for target delineation in clinical practice. Despite its routine use, care has to be taken when selecting and acquiring MRI studies for the purpose of radiotherapy treatment planning. Requirements on MRI are particularly demanding for intracranial stereotactic radiotherapy, where accurate imaging has a critical role in treatment success. However, MR images acquired for routine radiological assessment are frequently unsuitable for high-precision stereotactic radiotherapy as the requirements for imaging are significantly different for radiotherapy planning and diagnostic radiology. To assure that optimal imaging is used for treatment planning, the radiation oncologist needs proper knowledge of the most important requirements concerning the use of MRI in brain stereotactic radiotherapy. In the present review, we summarize and discuss the most relevant issues when using MR images for target volume delineation in intracranial stereotactic radiotherapy.

### Single isocenter LINAC radiosurgery for multiple brain metastases: practical considerations

> Three centers' working protocols for single-isocenter multimet SRS: 0-1 mm PTV margin, 6DOF correction, fractionate if largest lesion >2-3 cm or V12 >10 cm3.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The portable content is the geometry: PTV margin 0 to 1 mm depends on 6DOF correction, used at all 3 centers, and fractionation starts when the largest lesion exceeds 2-3 cm or V12 >10 cm3. UAB's choice to ignore total V12 across multitarget plans has no necrosis data behind it in this paper.

- 🔍 Practical 'how we do it' reference from 3 centers: UAB, Orlando Health (OHCI), UCLA
- ⚠️ Authors state it is not a consensus guideline; mostly tables, little supporting discussion
- ⚠️ No outcome data: no local control, radionecrosis, or toxicity results reported
- 🔍 Delivery platform by center

  | Parameter | UAB | OHCI | UCLA |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Linac | Varian Edge | Varian Edge | Novalis Tx, Truebeam STx |
  | Delivery | VMAT (RapidArc or HyperArc) | VMAT (RapidArc or HyperArc) | Enhanced Dynamic Conformal Arc |
  | Planning system | Eclipse | Eclipse | Brainlab Elements |
  | Immobilization | QFix Encompass | QFix Encompass | Brainlab frameless mask |
  | IGRT | Orthogonal kV then CBCT | CBCT | Stereoscopic x-ray (ExacTrac) |

- 🔍 PTV margin historically 0 mm; typical frameless multimet margin 0 to 1 mm
- 🔍 6DOF couch used at all 3 centers; can reduce the required PTV margin
- 🔍 Fractionation triggers (body text)
  - Fractionate if largest lesion diameter >2 to 3 cm
  - Fractionate if V12 >10 cm3
  - 3 → 5 fx when OAR constraints can't be met in 3 fx, or by tumor diameter
  - Fractionated schedules listed: 27 Gy in 3 fractions, 30 Gy in 5 fractions
- 🔍 Lesions per session generally up to 15, limited by tumor size and intracranial disease volume
- ⚠️ UAB ignores total V12 in multitarget plans: 10 1-cc targets may be less toxic than 1 10-cc target
- ⚠️ Distant targets carry rotational-error risk → extra isocenters, tighter rotational tolerance, or larger PTV margin
- ⚠️ Several authors disclose Varian and Brainlab grants or honoraria, the two platforms described

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Practical Considerations for Single Isocenter LINAC Radiosurgery of Multiple Brain Metastases** — Meeks, Sanford L.; Mercado, Catherine E.; Popple, Richard A. et al.. *Practical Radiation Oncology* (2022-05). [doi:10.1016/j.prro.2021.09.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.prro.2021.09.007)
  - 📎 [[papers/cns/2022-meeks-practical-considerations-single-isocenter-linac.pdf|Single isocenter LINAC radiosurgery for multiple brain metastases: practical considerations (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions
> - Should total V12 constrain multitarget SRS plans?
> - Safe isocenter-to-target distance for single-isocenter plans

## 🌞 Skin / Melanoma

NMSC hypofractionation: a single-author POV on short-course schedules by age and PS, plus an n=1 HDR surface mould eyelid case.

### HDR surface mould brachytherapy for eyelid carcinoma

> Disease-free at 6 mo with only mild dry eye after 52 Gy/13 fx HDR surface mould to an excised upper-eyelid sebaceous carcinoma, n=1.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> What transfers is the setup, not the outcome: a 5-mm wax-wrapped lead shield under the lid, a dummy wax mould at CT to avoid lead artifact, CTV as scar wire + 1 cm with no PTV, and plan acceptance by D90 and DNR 0.26. The tumor was already excised with no MRI residual, so this informs adjuvant scar treatment, not gross disease.

- 🔍 Single-pt case report: step-by-step technique plus 9 consecutive weekly post-treatment reviews
- 🔍 54F, sebaceous carcinoma R upper eyelid near lateral canthus, 0.8 × 0.5 cm
- 🔍 Excisional biopsy with margin status not reported; MRI showed no residual disease, no nodes
- 🔍 HDR surface mould regimen
  - 52 Gy/13 fx, 4 Gy/fx BID, 6 h apart
  - Ir-192 via Flexitron afterloader
  - EQD2 60.67 Gy, judged equivalent to 60 Gy LDR
- 🔍 Target, shielding and plan acceptance
  - CTV = scar (copper wire on CT) + 1 cm margin, no PTV; CTV 0.71 mL
  - 5-mm lead shield wrapped in wax placed under the lid each fraction
  - Dummy wax mould used at CT to avoid lead artifact
  - IPSA inverse planning, Oncentra Brachy 4.6.0
  - D90 at least 90% of Rx; DNR 0.26 vs limit < 0.36
- 📊 Disease-free at 6 mo post-treatment
- 📊 No acute side effects; mild dry eye the only toxicity
- ⚠️ No residual disease at RT: tumor control here is adjuvant, not definitive
- ⚠️ OAR doses (lens, lacrimal, anterior chamber) given only in Fig 3D, not in source text
- ⚠️ 6-mo f/u too short to capture cataract or late ocular surface effects
- 🔗 Surgery (WLE or Mohs) is the stated standard; GEC-ESTRO favors brachy over EBRT for small skin targets

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Implementing high-dose rate surface mould brachytherapy for carcinoma of eyelid: a practical approach and weekly review** — Patro, Kanhu Charan; Avinash, Ajitesh; Banidutta, Arya et al.. *Radiation Oncology Journal* (2024-06). [doi:10.3857/roj.2023.00682](https://doi.org/10.3857/roj.2023.00682)
  > Surface mould brachytherapy is a conformal radiotherapy technique that can deliver high dose to the target while sparing nearby normal structures, Here, we aim to describe the procedurals details for high-dose rate (HDR) surface mould brachytherapy in sebaceous carcinoma of eyelid in a 54-year old lady. She was hesitant for surgery and any form of invasive intervention like interstitial brachytherapy. So, she was treated with surface mould HDR brachytherapy to a total dose of 52 Gy in 13 fractions at a dose of 4 Gy per fraction delivered twice daily using Iridium-192 isotope with no acute side effects. She was evaluated on a weekly basis for any radiation side effects and now she is disease-free for 6 months post-treatment with only mild dry eye. A detailed step-by-step procedure of surface mould technique, simulation procedure, dose prescription, planning, plan evaluation and treatment has been described in this paper. Surface mould HDR brachytherapy can be safely used as organ preserving modality of treatment for eyelid carcinoma.
  - 📎 [[papers/skin/2024-patro-implementing-high-dose-rate-surface-mould-brachytherapy.pdf|HDR surface mould brachytherapy for eyelid carcinoma (full text)]]

### Hypofractionated RT in non-melanoma skin cancer, post-COVID-19

> Single-author POV argues for ≤15 fx in most NMSC and 1–4 fx as default for ≥80 or ECOG 2/3; hypofractionated LC cited at 90–100%.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The real change is the frailty-stratified grid, not a uniform cut in fraction count: ≥80 or ECOG 2/3 drops to 15–28 Gy in 1–4 fx, and ECOG 3/4 adjuvant cases go to no RT. Longer 45–50 Gy in 15–20 fx is kept only for perioral/orbital sites in pts <70, so the decision this moves is fraction count by frailty, with omission of adjuvant RT in most BCC and low-risk SCC.

- 🔍 Point-of-view article (JMIRO 2020), single author; no new outcome data
- 📊 Cited LC with definitive RT, any technique: >90–95%
- 📊 Cited LC with hypofractionated RT in NMSC: 90–100%
- 🔗 Cited systematic review: 40 publications, over 12 000 NMSC, mean dose 38 Gy, 8 Gy/fx, 3x/week
- 🔗 RCR (UK) suggested trimming: 40 Gy/8 fx instead of 10; 50 Gy/15 fx instead of 55 Gy/20 fx
- 💊 Suggested schedules, localised BCC, by age/PS

## 🎀 Breast

NCCN 2023 locoregional talk: ALND omission for 1-2 positive SLNs, PBI and 5-fx WBRT, and repeat BCT for selected IBTR.

### NCCN 2023 Breast Cancer Congress: locoregional management talk (ACOSOG Z11 10-year update)

> Locoregional de-escalation is evidence-backed in selected early breast cancer: ALND omission for 1-2 positive SLNs, PBI and 5-fraction WBRT, repeat BCT for selected IBTR.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The nodal pCR cohort supporting ALND omission after NAC delivered nodal RT to 78% (SLNB) and 85% (TAD), so it does not license dropping RNI too. On the breast side, NCCN 2023 now names moderately HF PBI, backed by less Grade II/III induration with PBI 40 Gy/15 fx in the Danish trial.

> [!abstract] Presentation
> The speakers argued that locoregional therapy for non-metastatic breast cancer is moving toward personalized de-escalation across surgery and RT. On the surgical side, ACOSOG Z11 and AMAROS anchor omission of completion ALND for 1-2 positive SLNs, while dual tracer mapping, clipped-node retrieval and TAD lower the false-negative rate of axillary staging after NAC. Breast conservation for 2 or 3 ipsilateral foci was presented as acceptable on prospective SABCS 2022 data. On the RT side, moderately hypofractionated PBI, FAST and FAST-Forward schedules, and repeat BCT with PBI for selected IBTR were framed against the corresponding NCCN guideline changes.

> [!warning] Adversarial read
> - Z11 excluded third-field nodal RT, and the talk does not address incidental axillary dose from tangents, so how much breast RT substituted for dissection is left unexamined.
> - The SABCS nodal pCR cohort gave nodal RT to 78% after SLNB and 85% after TAD; it supports omitting dissection, not omitting regional RT, and it is non-randomized.
> - The PBI meta-analysis pooled heterogeneous techniques in mostly T1N0 grade 1 or 2 pts, and the restricted comparison sat at P = .05, so equivalence outside that population remains unproven.
> - Hypofractionated RNI rests on historic plexopathy series, British Columbia PMRT and START, where only 14% received RNI; no randomized nodal data for a 5-fraction schedule are shown.

**Trials cited:** ACOSOG Z11 · AMAROS · SENTINA · RAPID · NSABP B39/RTOG 0413 · UK IMPORT LOW · FAST · FAST-Forward

- 🔍 Two-part talk: surgical de-escalation of axilla and breast, then RT technique and fractionation, mapped to NCCN 2023 changes
- 📊 ACOSOG Z11 at 10 yrs, cT1-2 with 1-2 positive SLNs, lumpectomy + WBRT (median f/u 9.3 years)

- 📊 PBI schedule data behind the NCCN 2023 shift to include moderately HF PBI

- 📊 RTOG 1014: repeat lumpectomy + 3D-EBRT PBI for IBTR after prior BCS + WBRT (58 evaluable)
  - 1.5 Gy BID to 45 Gy (30 fx)
  - Eligibility: <3cm, unifocal, >1 yr to IBTR, negative margins
  - 2nd IBTR 5.2% at median 5.5 yrs; 5-yr OS 94.8%
