# 2026-10-30 — ESMO Congress 2026

**PEACE-1 at 7.7y f/u: abiraterone OS HR 0.75 (95% CI 0.65-0.87), mOS 6.1 vs 4.6y in de novo mCSPC; triplet OS gain independent of volume.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Prostate carried the day. PEACE-1 long-term data hold the triplet OS gain in de novo mCSPC regardless of volume (interaction p=0.56); long-term RT-arm data not in source. CCTG PR21 favored 177Lu-PSMA-617 over docetaxel on FACT-P in post-ARPI mCRPC despite null rPFS. Phase 1 JNJ-6143: PSA50 77% in heavily pretreated mCRPC.

## 🌰 Prostate

mCSPC intensification and post-ARPI mCRPC sequencing dominate; none of today's data isolate an RT effect, and PEACE-1's long-term RT factorial results are not yet in source.

### ESMO 2026 Invited Discussant: Metastatic Prostate Cancer (Vogl)

> PEACE-1 at 7.7-yr median f/u: ~7-yr rPFS 33% vs 11% (high-volume) triplet vs doublet; JNJ-6143 50 mg PSA50 77% in heavily pretreated mCRPC.

- 🔍 Discussant review of two ESMO 2026 abstracts: PEACE-1 long-term OS (Fizazi) and phase 1 JNJ-6143 (Garmezy)
- 🔍 PEACE-1: phase 3, 2x2 factorial, N=1,173 de novo mCSPC, enrolled Nov 2013 to Dec 2018
- 📊 PEACE-1 preplanned long-term analysis: median f/u 7.7 years [7.5; 7.9]
- 📊 PEACE-1 rPFS at ~7 yr, triplet vs doublet, by volume

  | Volume | Triplet | Doublet |
  |---|---|---|
  | High-volume | 33% | 11% |
  | Low-volume | 43% | 21% |

- 📐 No treatment-effect heterogeneity by volume (interaction p = 0.5552)
- ⚠️ PEACE-1 OS HR not reported in source text; only KM separation described
- ⚠️ PEACE-1 RT factorial arms (SOC + RT) part of design; RT results not discussed in source
- 🔗 Long-term context cited by discussant
  - ENZAMET (docetaxel ± enzalutamide): 5-yr OS 60% vs 52%, 8-yr 40% vs 34%, HR 0.81 (95% CI 0.62-1.05)
  - STAMPEDE abiraterone, low-volume: HR 0.58 (95% CI 0.44-0.75)
  - STAMPEDE abiraterone, high-volume: HR 0.66 (95% CI 0.54-0.81)
  - 8-yr OS with ADT + ARPI + docetaxel in de novo mCSPC approximately 40%
- ❓ No prospective RCT of ADT + ARPI vs ADT + ARPI + docetaxel; APCCC: 51% of panelists would use biomarkers (e.g., Decipher)
- 🔍 PSA nadir >0.2 ng/mL → intensification trials (PEACE-6 Poor Responders, TRIPLE-SWITCH, REINFORCE, EvoPARP-PR05); excellent responders → EORTC 2238 DE-ESCALATE

### CCTG PR21 (QoL) · [[NCT04663997]]

> FACT-P total change wk 30 -8.3 Doc vs +4.7 LuP (p<0.001) in post-ARPI mCRPC; earlier analysis found no significant rPFS difference.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The QoL gap is not front-loaded: TOI and total FACT-P separate at wk 24-36, with FACT-P total -8.3 vs +4.7 at wk 30, and high side-effect bother in 40.7% vs 15.4%. For a reader delivering RLT, this is the first randomized PRO data placing LuP against docetaxel rather than an ARPI switch or cabazitaxel, though activity and cycle number are not reported in source.

- 🔍 Randomized phase II, Doc vs 177Lu-PSMA-617 in mCRPC after prior ARPI
- 📊 Primary endpoint rPFS: no significant difference between arms (no effect size reported in source)
- 🔍 189 of 199 randomized pts started treatment (n=94 LuP; n=95 Doc)
- 🔍 QoL analysis required baseline plus ≥1 on-treatment FACT-P questionnaire
- 🔍 FACT-P every 6 wks on therapy, every 12 wks prior to cross-over; TOI pre-specified
- 📊 Mean change from baseline, Doc vs LuP

  | Endpoint · timepoint | Doc | LuP | p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | TOI · wk 24 | -4.1 | +0.5 | p=0.041 |
  | TOI · wk 30 | -7.1 | +2.8 | p<0.001 |
  | Total FACT-P · wk 24 | -4.8 | +1.2 | p=0.024 |
  | Total FACT-P · wk 30 | -8.3 | +4.7 | p<0.001 |
  | Total FACT-P · wk 36 | -8.9 | +1.5 | p=0.027 |
  | High GP5 side-effect bother, any time | 40.7% | 15.4% | not reported |

- 📐 Mixed model: treatment-by-visit interaction for TOI, FACT-P, FACT-G (each p<0.01); Doc worsened, LuP stable
- 📊 FACT-G and physical well-being trajectories also favored LuP (per UroToday)
- 📊 MCID analysis: worse scores for Doc at wk 24 vs wk 12 for FACT-P total and TOI
- ⚠️ Completion 96% at baseline but only >70% at most later time-points; arm separation emerges wk 24-36
- ⚠️ Blinding not stated; self-reported QoL with visibly different treatments invites expectation bias
- ⚠️ Null rPFS in a phase II is not equivalence; trial not described as non-inferiority
- ⚠️ GP5 comparison descriptive only; no test reported

- 📄 **Patient reported quality of life (QoL) for docetaxel (Doc) vs lutetium177-617-PSMA (LuP) for metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC): Results from the Canadian cancer trials group (CCTG) PR21 trial** — Daniel J. Khalaf. *ESMO Congress 2026* (2026-10). [article](https://cslide.ctimeetingtech.com/esmo2026/attendee/confcal/presentation/list)
  > ESMO Congress 2026 abstract 2221RO · Rapid oral: Genitourinary tumours, prostate, penile and testis · 2026-10-23 · presented by Daniel J. Khalaf (Toronto, Canada) · [[NCT04663997]]
  > Background: CCTG PR21 is a phase II randomized clinical trial of Doc vs LuP for patients (pts) with mCRPC who have received prior androgen receptor pathway inhibitor. There was no significant difference in the primary endpoint of radiographic progression-free survival (rFPS). In this analysis, we present the comparison of QOL outcomes between treatment arms.
  > Methods: Inclusion required completion of baseline and minimum of one questionnaire on treatment. FACT-P questionnaire schedule was every 6 weeks (wks) on therapy and every 12 wks prior to cross-over. The FACT-P Trials Outcome Index (TOI) was a pre-specified outcome. For all FACT-P domains, we calculated the mean change from baseline at each timepoint during the 36-wk treatment period. A linear mixed model, with visit (wks from randomization), treatment arm, and treatment-by-visit interaction as the fixed effects, was utilized to compare the change in QOL domains over time. Pts’ scores on the GP5 item (“I am bothered by side effects of treatment”) were described by the distribution of high scores (3 or 4) by arm.
  > Results: 189 of 199 randomized patients started treatment (n=94 LuP; n=95 Doc). Questionnaire completion rates were 96% at baseline and exceeded 70% for most time-points. Baseline scores were similar between arms. Mean change for TOI was worse for Doc vs LuP at wk 24 (-4.1 vs +0.5, p=0.041) and wk 30 (-7.1 vs +2.8, p<0.001) and for total FACT-P at wk 24 (-4.8 vs +1.2, p=0.024), wk 30 (-8.3 vs +4.7, p<0.001) and wk 36 (-8.9 vs +1.5, p=0.027). The mixed model showed a significant interaction between treatment arm and visit, with worsening over time for Doc and stability for LuP for most domains including TOI, FACT-P, and FACT-G (each p<0.01). The proportion of pts reporting high GP5 score at any time was 40.7% vs 15.4% for Doc vs LuP.
  > Conclusions: Doc has a greater detrimental impact on QOL than does LuP as determined by the FACT-P TOI, total FACT-P and most domain scores. Pts also reported more perceived side-effects with Doc, consistent with the global impact of treatment on QOL. Taken together, the PR21 trial findings suggest that LuP is better tolerated without demonstrable difference in rPFS compared to Doc.

### PEACE-1

> At 7.7y median f/u, abiraterone OS HR 0.75 (95% CI 0.65-0.87, p=0.0001), mOS 4.6 vs 6.1y in de novo mCSPC.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The RT finding is indirect. Abiraterone and RT showed no treatment interaction, so the OS gain (HR 0.75) held with or without prostate RT. The source gives no long-term OS or local endpoints for the RT arm. The primary analysis found no OS gain from RT, including low-volume, so RT in a triplet patient still rests on that earlier read.

- 📊 Abiraterone effect by population at long-term f/u

  | Population · endpoint | Without abiraterone | With abiraterone | HR (CI) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | All (SOC ± RT) · OS | 4.6y | 6.1y | 0.75 (95% CI 0.65-0.87), p=0.0001 |
  | All (SOC ± RT) · rPFS | not reported | not reported | 0.55 (95% CI 0.44-0.70) |
  | ADT + docetaxel (n=710) · rPFS | not reported | not reported | 0.50 (99.9% CI 0.38-0.68) |
  | ADT + docetaxel · OS | 4.4y | 6.2y | 0.68 (95.1% CI 0.56-0.82) |
  | High-volume, doublet vs triplet · OS | 3.5y | 4.6y | 0.67 (95% CI 0.54-0.85) |
  | Low-volume, doublet vs triplet · OS | 6.2y | 7.8y | 0.74 (95% CI 0.51-1.05) |

- 🔍 Phase 3, 2x2 factorial, 1:1:1:1, N=1,173 de novo mCSPC (57% high-, 43% low-volume)
- 🔍 Prespecified long-term analysis, median f/u 7.7y (range 7.5-7.9)
- 📊 High-volume 7y OS: 22.4% doublet vs 40.9% triplet
- 📊 Low-volume rPFS, triplet vs doublet: HR 0.54 (95% CI 0.32-0.91), 3.2y absolute benefit
- 📐 Triplet OS benefit independent of volume, interaction p=0.56
- ⚠️ Prostate RT gave no OS gain in the primary analysis, incl low-volume. Long-term RT-arm data not in source
- 🔍 No interaction between abiraterone and RT treatment effects
- 🔍 86% of control arm received an ARPI beyond progression
- 📊 G3-4 AEs of interest, ADT + docetaxel vs + abiraterone
  - Neutropenia: 12% vs 10%
  - Hypertension: 14% vs 21%
  - No long-term safety signals per presenter
- ⚠️ Docetaxel permitted by amendment, so the docetaxel subgroup is not randomized for docetaxel
- ⚠️ Low-volume OS CI crosses 1 (0.51-1.05)
- 🔗 vs STAMPEDE: docetaxel and abiraterone doublets gave similar OS, the rationale for the triplet
