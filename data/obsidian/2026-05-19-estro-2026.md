# 2026-05-19 — ESTRO Congress 2026

**Single-fraction SABR pooled across 3 centres: 2yr LC 90-93%, G3+ 2.9% in 1687 pts (AE denominator n=789, primary NSCLC only).**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Thoracic carried the day: the largest single-fraction SABR series to date holds 2yr LC 90-93% with G3+ 2.9%, though the dose is not reported in source, so the prescription cannot be tied to the outcome. Bladder TMT (CLR 63.7%, salvage cystectomy 9.7%) and the prostate ePLND-vs-PSMA-PET round-up (47.7% of nodal mets outside the ePLND template) both bear on nodal and local-regional coverage decisions.

## 🌰 Prostate

Staging round-up, not new randomised data: the nodal-coverage question is whether a negative PSMA-PET licenses PLND omission when half of nodal disease sits outside the surgical template.

### ePLND vs PSMA-PET staging (AUA2026 round-up)

> AUA2026 round-up: PSMA-PET NPV ~96% may allow PLND omission in intermediate risk; 47.7% of nodal mets sit outside ePLND template.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The RT-relevant number is toxicity sequencing: 19-29% lower limb lymphedema after PLND plus salvage pelvic nodal RT versus 0-9% after pelvic nodal RT alone, with 2-22% genital lymphedema in the combined group. If PSMA-PET is negative and PLND is omitted, later elective or salvage pelvic nodal coverage carries far less lymphedema cost.

- 🔍 Conference round-up of the ePLND-vs-PSMA-PET staging debate, not a new trial result
- 📊 Yaxley BJUI 2019: 1253 consecutive men, primary staging Ga-PSMA PET/CT
- 📊 47.7% of lymph node metastases were outside the boundaries of an extended pelvic lymph node dissection
- 📊 PSMA PET/CT NPV ~96% per the tweet text; the underlying series is not named in source
- 📊 Lymphedema prevalence by treatment (Clinckaert, Cancers 2022 systematic review)
- ⚠️ The PLND + salvage pelvic nodal RT group also had 2-22% genital lymphedema
- ⚠️ Review authors flag no uniform lymphedema definition, so prevalence estimates are unstable
- 📊 Tyritzis J Urol 2015 (3544 pts, 547 PLND): 6-10x increased DVT/PE risk with LND
- ⚠️ Roberts PCAN 2024 SWOT names the core gap: absent level 1 evidence for significant oncological benefit of eLND
- 🔗 Slide conclusions: omit PLND in intermediate risk if PSMA-PET negative for LNI; nomograms add value in GG3; high-risk is individualized
- ⚠️ No effect size reported in source for the therapeutic (BCR) question; the round-up states RCTs have not shown consistent BCR improvement

## 💧 Bladder

A 369-pt multicentre TMT cohort with no cystectomy comparator; RT dose, fractionation and target volume are absent from source, limiting transfer to practice.

### Bladder-preserving TMT multicenter analysis (URONCOR)

> CLR 63.7% in a 369-pt Spanish TMT cohort; salvage cystectomy 9.7%, image-guidance quality and 5-FU-based CRT predicted local response.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The modifiable RT variable here is verification protocol: weekly portal imaging carried OR 0.35 (0.20-0.60) for complete local response, with VMAT trending favorable, across a 2010 to 2022 accrual. Dose, fractionation and target volume are not reported in source, so what transfers is the case for daily volumetric IGRT in a filling, moving organ.

![Bladder-preserving TMT multicenter analysis (URONCOR)](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HIrl5sdWwAAEkhS.jpg)

- 📊 1° EP complete local response (CLR): 63.7%
- 📊 Salvage cystectomy 9.7%
- 📊 Disease progression 28.8%, by pattern
  - Local 10.1%
  - Systemic 10.7%
  - Combined 8.7%
- 🔍 Multicenter retrospective cohort, Spain 2010-2022, cT2-T4aN0M0 treated with definitive TMT (TURBT + concurrent CRT)
- 🔍 Median age 76, 85.1% male
- 🔍 RT dose, fractionation and target volume not reported in source
- ⚠️ Weekly portal imaging tracks treatment era and technique, so its CLR association is confounded
- ⚠️ VMAT trended toward favorable outcomes, not significant
- ⚠️ 5-FU CRT odds ratio CI spans 1.1-22.1, so the magnitude is unstable
- 🔗 No radical cystectomy comparator in cohort; the alternative-to-RC claim rests on external series
- 📊 CR associated with lower local recurrence and better survival per curator source; no effect size reported in source

## 🫁 Thoracic / Lung

Scale, not randomisation: 1687 pts across 3 centres extend the single-fraction SABR experience without a multi-fraction comparator.

### Single-fraction SABR pooled analysis, 1687 pts

> Local control 90-93% at 2yr and G3+ AEs 2.9% across 1687 single-fraction SABR pts at 3 centres.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The oligomet read is the gap between local control and PFS: 90-93% LC at 2yr against median PFS 11 mo, so distant failure, not the treated lesion, drives the course. For primary NSCLC the same LC sits with median PFS 30 mo, which is the split that should decide whether one-visit ablation is offered as definitive treatment or as a break from systemic therapy.
