# 2026-07-22

**ESTRO Delphi locks prostate SBRT at 36.25Gy/5fx and votes 100% against elective pelvic nodal RT alongside it.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Prostate carried the day, both readings pushing toward less treatment. ESTRO's panel fixes a 5fx dose and rules out elective nodal coverage and rectal spacers, while capping SBRT at ISUP 2-3 / cT2c / PSA <20. ProtecT's cribriform re-review argues the 87% cribriform-negative GG2 pts track GG1 for 15yr metastases, with no significant radical-treatment benefit.

## 🌰 Prostate

Two reads on who gets radical local therapy and how much: a consensus framework for delivering SBRT and a biopsy-morphology argument for withholding treatment entirely.

### ProtecT Secondary Analysis (Cribriform Morphology)

> Cribriform-negative grade group 2 carried the same 15yr metastasis risk as GG1, and radical treatment showed no significant benefit in that 87%.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The RT-relevant read is patient selection, not technique: cribriform-negative GG2 behaved like GG1 for 15yr metastasis, and radical treatment (surgery or RT with 3-6mo neoadjuvant ADT) showed no significant metastasis reduction in that 87%. Effect sizes are not in the source text. Cribriform status is already a required biopsy report field, so it is available at consult.

- 🔍 Secondary analysis of ProtecT: centrally re-reviewed biopsy slides from 712 of 1,643 randomized pts
- 🔍 Grades re-scored to 2019 ISUP (original trial used 2005); regrading did not shift group balance
- 📊 Cribriform-positive on biopsy in roughly 13% of the reviewed cohort; 87% cribriform-negative
- 📊 In cribriform-negative pts, early radical treatment gave no statistically significant reduction in 15yr metastases vs active monitoring
- 📊 Consistent across both intention-to-treat and per-protocol analyses (per-protocol accounts for crossover)
- 📊 On multivariable Cox (ITT), cribriform-negative grade group 2 had the same long-term metastasis risk as Gleason 6 / GG1
- 🔗 Frames the ProtecT paradox: 15yr PCa-specific mortality was extremely low (over 3% quoted in source) and equal across arms, while radical treatment cut metastases overall
- 🔗 EAU already advises against active surveillance with any cribriform at diagnostic biopsy; ISUP and GUPS require binary cribriform reporting
- ⚠️ Cribriform status was not a randomization characteristic, so these are non-randomized subgroups within a randomized trial
- ⚠️ Only 43% of the randomized cohort had slides available; source notes a trend to higher cribriform prevalence in the RT + neoadjuvant ADT arm, attributed to chance
- ⚠️ No effect sizes (HR, CI, p) reported in source text for the cribriform-negative or cribriform-positive comparisons: this is a video interview transcript, not the paper

### ESTRO Prostate SBRT Consensus Recommendations

> ESTRO Delphi consensus: 36.25Gy/5fx (40Gy to 95% CTV) standard, 100% against elective pelvic nodal RT with prostate SBRT.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Two votes bind practice hardest: 100% against elective pelvic nodal irradiation with prostate SBRT outside a trial, and 85% against rectal spacers, both areas where department habit often runs ahead of evidence. The dose anchor is dual-level (36.25Gy/5fx to 95% PTV with 40Gy to 95% CTV), so a single-prescription plan is not what PACE-B delivered.

  | Question | Result | Level |
  |---|---|---|
  | Elective pelvic nodal RT with prostate SBRT (Q6) | No: 100% (12 votes) | Strong consensus against |
  | Rectal spacer (Q9) | No: 85% (11 votes); selected pts 15% (2 votes) | Consensus against |
  | Bladder/bowel prep protocol (Q8) | Yes: 100% (14 votes) | Strong consensus for |
  | Concomitant ADT per existing guidelines (Q7) | Yes: 100% (13 votes) | Strong consensus for |

  | Prophylactic α1-blockers/NSAIDs/PDE5i (Q4) | No: 46% (6 votes) | No consensus |
  | Max IPSS cut-off (Q3) | Yes: 85% (11 votes) | Consensus for |

- 📐 Dose: 36.25Gy/5fx of 7.25Gy to 95% PTV, with 40Gy to 95% of prostate CTV
- 📐 Alternative schedule: 42.7Gy in 7 fractions of 6.1Gy (HYPO-RT-PC)
- 🔍 Volume cut-offs quantified where the panel used them: median IPSS cut-off 17 (range 10-20), median prostate volume 90cc (range 70-150cc), median post-BPH-surgery wait 6mo (range 2-12mo)
- 🔍 Margin-dependent motion management: PTV ≥5mm needs pre-treatment CBCT/fiducial matching (79%, 11 votes); at <5mm, 71% (10 votes) wanted added intra-fraction tracking but no consensus was declared
- 🔗 Seminal vesicles: omit in low-risk; PACE trials included proximal 1cm for all, proximal 2cm to 30Gy/5fx for Gleason 4+3 or NCCN high-risk
- 🔗 NRG-GU005 (ASTRO 2025 preliminary) reported lower toxicity with 36.25Gy/5fx but a slightly higher 3-year biochemical relapse rate
- ⚠️ Panel is 15 people (11 rad oncs, 3 physicists, 1 RTT) from 9 European countries; several 100% figures rest on 12-14 votes
- ⚠️ High-risk evidence is thin: HYPO-RT-PC is the only phase III reporting oncologic outcomes in high-risk pts and they were 11% of accrual; PACE-C has toxicity only
- ⚠️ Urethral constraint flagged as possibly too permissive: PACE-B V42 <50% carries an explicit note that hotspots should be avoided; a post-hoc PACE-B analysis found no association between urinary substructure dose and late toxicity
- ⚠️ Tables 1-3 (target volume parameters, OOI dose guidance, IGRT-by-margin grid) arrived with uncertain row-to-value association in source extraction, so per-row constraints are not reproduced here
