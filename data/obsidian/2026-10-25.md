# 2026-10-25

**Guix HDR custom surface molds: 5-yr actuarial LC 98% (99% primary, 87% recurrent) for facial BCC/SCC, N=136, the most mature of today's skin brachy series.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> All five studies cover HDR surface/mould brachy for skin cancer, mostly face and head and neck, with high LC across series: 92% at 1 yr (Gogineni), 84.9% at 2 yr (Casey), 98% at 5 yr (Guix). Follow-up is short outside Guix, and the ABS survey shows no consensus on margins, dose or dosimetric constraints.

## 🌞 Skin / Melanoma

Single-institution HDR surface/mould series plus an ABS practice survey; none has a randomised comparator vs surgery or EBRT.

### Fractionated HDR brachytherapy moulds for carcinoma of the pinna (Allan)

> 13 superficial pinna carcinomas, HDR mould 45 Gy/8 fx over 5 days: complete resolution in all, no recurrence or late toxicity at minimum 18 mo f/u.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The transferable parameter is ultrasound-guided depth prescription: tumour thickness measured to sub-mm (0.84 mm in one case) set the source-to-skin height (5 vs 10 mm) so the 80% isodose covered tumour while sparing cartilage. That steep ~10%/mm gradient is the argument for a mould over electrons when lesions are thinner than a 4 MeV beam's 12 mm 90% depth.

- 🔍 Single-institution series (Christie Hospital), 13 pts treated up to July 1996
- 🔍 Histology
  - BCC: 9 pts
  - SCC: 3 pts
  - Intraepidermal carcinoma: 1 pt
- 🔍 Lesions confined to pinna skin; dermal ultrasound confirmed no cartilage invasion in all but one
- 💊 RT technique: HDR microselectron, single stepping iridium source
  - Single-plane moulds: 45 Gy surface dose in 8 fx over 5 days
  - Twice-daily on 3 middle days, minimum 6 h interfraction interval
  - Ultrasound-measured full tumour thickness placed inside the 80% isodose surface
  - Sandwich mould (full-thickness pinna, 10 mm plane separation): dose reduced to 42.5 Gy
- 📐 Dose falls to 80% of surface at 1.5-3.5 mm depth depending on source height; gradient ~10% per mm
- 📊 Acute reaction: moderate erythema confined to high-dose area, settled by 6-wk first follow-up
- 📊 Complete tumour resolution in all pts; no recurrences, no late complications at minimum 18 mo
- ⚠️ Highly selected small series; no comparator arm, no formal toxicity grading reported in source
- ⚠️ BED comparison vs other regimens via LQ model; Table 2 row association uncertain in source extraction
- ⚠️ Minimum 18 mo f/u is short for late cartilage necrosis and BCC recurrence

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Fractionated high dose rate brachytherapy moulds – a precise treatment for carcinoma of the pinna** — Ernest Allan; Anthony Stanton; David Pye et al.. *Radiotherapy and Oncology* (1998)
  - 📎 [[papers/skin/1998-allan-fractionated-high-dose-rate-brachytherapy-moulds-precise.pdf|Fractionated HDR brachytherapy moulds for carcinoma of the pinna (Allan) (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions
> - Late cartilage toxicity beyond 18 months with higher late-tissue BED
> - Outcomes vs electronic brachytherapy or orthovoltage for pinna lesions
> - Safety of full-thickness sandwich moulds in larger series

### Skin surface brachytherapy practice patterns survey (Likhacheva)

> 16 ABS respondents: margins 3-15mm (median 5mm), doses 30 Gy/5fx to 64 Gy/32fx (EQD2 40-65 Gy), no consensus on dosimetric constraints.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The usable signal is the pretibial outlier: eight respondents recalled severe toxicity on lower-extremity skin, and 63% de-escalated to 2-3 Gy/fx there. Nose lesions tolerated large fractions with little hesitation, so site, not histology, is where fractionation practice converges. The 3-15mm margin spread shows there is no shared CTV standard to benchmark against.

- 🔍 30-question electronic survey of ABS clinician members; 16 complete returns from ~400 invitations
- 🔍 Practices: private, hospital, academic; aggregate experience 8 to 1800 cases
- 📊 60% routinely estimate lesion depth by ultrasound before treatment
- 📊 Median max prescription depth 5 mm (range 1 to 8 mm)
- 📊 Margin on gross disease: range 3-15mm, median 5mm
- 📊 CTV margin adjustment
  - Wider margin by histology: 70% of respondents
  - Squamous histology cited by 41%
  - Diffuse/morpheaform/aggressive BCC cited by 18%
  - Recurrent disease cited by 6%
  - Adjusted by lesion size: 65%
- 📊 Hypofractionation preferred; 30 Gy/5fx to 64 Gy/32fx across scenarios (EQD2 40 Gy-65 Gy)
- 📊 Pretibial skin: 10 respondents (63%) used 2-3 Gy/fx; daily hypofractionated schedules avoided
- 📊 Size cut point for gentler fractionation: median 3 cm, range 2-5 cm
- 📐 Dosimetric parameters endorsed for plan evaluation

  | Parameter | All respondents | >100 career cases |
  |---|---|---|
  | Max dose at skin surface | 13 (81%) | 7 (78%) |
  | Min dose to CTV | 8 (50%) | 3 (33%) |
  | Depth of 90% isodose line | 6 (38%) | 3 (33%) |
  | Min dose to skin surface | 5 (31%) | 4 (44%) |
  | V150 | 4 (25%) | 2 (22%) |
  | D95 | 2 (13%) | 1 (11%) |
  | D100 | 2 (13%) | 2 (22%) |
  | V200 | 2 (13%) | 2 (22%) |
  | V120 | 1 (6%) | 1 (11%) |

- 📐 Max allowable skin dose 125-150% (median 135%) of Rx; CTV coverage 90-100% (median 95%)
- ⚠️ Eight respondents reported severe acute/late toxicity on lower extremity, especially pretibial skin
- ⚠️ Common exclusions: tumor depth >3 mm, perineural invasion, prior RT
- ⚠️ n=16 from ~400 invited; self-selected responders, no outcome data, safety claim rests on recall

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Skin surface brachytherapy: A survey of contemporary practice patterns** — Likhacheva, Anna O.; Devlin, Phillip M.; Shirvani, Shervin M. et al.. *Brachytherapy* (2017-01). [doi:10.1016/j.brachy.2016.10.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brachy.2016.10.006)
  - 📎 [[papers/skin/2017-likhacheva-skin-surface-brachytherapy-survey-contemporary-practice.pdf|Skin surface brachytherapy practice patterns survey (Likhacheva) (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions

### CT-based flap brachytherapy for facial NMSC (Gogineni)

> 1-yr KM local control 92% after 40 Gy/8 fx twice-weekly HDR flap brachy for T1 facial BCC/SCC; 49/53 lesions good/excellent cosmesis.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The transferable detail is the planning: CT-simulated flap in a glued thermoplastic mask, CTV tumor + 5 mm with no PTV margin, and a 5 mm spacer intranasally or under the eyelid. That combination delivered median D90 92% on 1.7 cc targets over nose, eyelid and ear. All 4 recurrences were in area H, 2 of them nasal SCC.

- 🔍 Retrospective review, 50 pts / 53 lesions, treated Jan 2007 to Dec 2018
- 🔍 T1 (AJCC 8th) BCC or SCC, biopsy-confirmed; all NCCN high-risk (area H, or area M ≥10 mm)
- 🔍 Selected case-by-case by multidisciplinary dermatology clinic, mostly surgery intolerance or refusal
- 🔍 Cohort
  - Median age 80 (73-101)
  - BCC 35 (66%), SCC 18 (34%)
  - Area H 40 (75%), nose 17 (32%)
  - Median size 18 mm, median thickness 5 mm
  - 48 (91%) primary, 5 (9%) recurrent
- 💊 Technique
  - 40 Gy/8 fx twice weekly, BED 60 Gy (α/β 10), Ir-192 HDR
  - CTV = visible tumor + 5 mm; PTV = CTV, no added margin
  - Catheter flap: channels 5 mm from surface, 10 mm apart, fixed in thermoplastic mask
  - 5 mm spacer intranasal, under eyelid, or gingivolabial sulcus
  - Median PTV 1.7 cc, D90 92%, V100 85%
- 📊 1-yr KM LC 92%; 4 local recurrences (2 nasal SCC, 1 eyelid BCC, 1 lip BCC)
- 📊 Toxicity
  - Acute G1 60%, G2 35%, G3 5%; all after the 5th fraction
  - All 8 eyelid/periorbital pts: G1 conjunctivitis, resolved with antibiotic/steroid drops
  - Chronic G1 10% at 6 mo; G1 4% and G2 4% at 12 mo
  - No chronic G≥3, no treatment-related hospitalization
- ⚠️ Median f/u 15 mo (6-27), too short to read out late BCC recurrence or late toxicity
- ⚠️ Toxicity is reported inconsistently: abstract calls the 5% acute events G2 (Results says G3); Discussion reports no chronic G≥2, though 4% had chronic G2
- ⚠️ Toxicity percentages track the 53 lesions, but the text labels them as pts
- 🔗 Other HDR surface-mold series in the paper report LC 87-100%
- 🔗 The one cited face-BCC RCT (surgery vs RT, mostly interstitial BT) showed 4-yr failure 7.5% vs 0.7% favoring surgery

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Computed tomography-based flap brachytherapy for non-melanoma skin cancers of the face** — Gogineni, Emile; Cai, Haocheng; Carillo, Dawn et al.. *Journal of Contemporary Brachytherapy* (2021). [doi:10.5114/jcb.2021.103587](https://doi.org/10.5114/jcb.2021.103587)
  - 📎 [[papers/skin/2021-gogineni-computed-tomography-based-flap-brachytherapy-non.pdf|CT-based flap brachytherapy for facial NMSC (Gogineni) (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions
> - Local control and late toxicity beyond 2 years of follow-up
> - HDR flap brachytherapy vs Mohs for area-H NMSC
> - Histology-specific dose or margin for nasal SCC

### HDR brachytherapy with custom surface molds for facial skin carcinomas (Guix)

> 5-yr actuarial LC 98% overall (99% primary, 87% recurrent) with HDR Ir-192 custom PMMA surface molds for facial BCC/SCC, N=136.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> What transfers is the physics: optimized dwell times on a face-conformal PMMA mold held surface and 5 mm dose within 5% of prescription on curved sites, with a lead insert to spare the eye. That favors mold HDR over electrons on nose, eyelid and ear. The protracted 180 cGy/fx schedule is the part that dates.

- 📊 Local control by presentation

  | Cohort | N | Local recurrences | 5-yr actuarial LC |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | All pts | 136 | 3 | 98% |
  | Primary tumors | 73 | 1 | 99% |
  | Recurrent after prior surgery | 63 | 2 | 87% |

- 🔍 Prospective single-institution series, 136 consecutive facial BCC/SCC, treated 1992-1997
- 🔍 Gross tumor left in place in most pts; margins positive in 128
- 🔍 Dose: 6000-6500 cGy in 33-36 fx of 180 cGy, prescribed at 5 mm depth
- 🔍 Lesions ≥4 cm: boost 1800 cGy/10 fx after 3-wk rest, total 7500-8000 cGy
- 🔍 Ir-192 HDR via 117 custom PMMA molds and 19 Brock applicators
- 📊 Mold dose at surface and 5 mm depth within 5% of prescription; TLD agreed within 5%
- 📊 Cosmesis good/excellent in 98% at 6 mo; no severe late complications reported
- 🔗 vs Lovett EBRT series: superficial X-ray LC 95% (180/189), electrons 77% (44/57)
- ⚠️ Min f/u 12 mo; only 32 primary and 29 recurrent pts at risk at 5 yr
- ⚠️ Internal inconsistencies in the source
  - Boost 1800 cGy in Methods vs 1500 cGy in Fig 5 legend
  - Recurrent-tumor LC 87% given at 5 yr in Results, at 4 yr in Discussion
  - Diameter reported as average 3.8 cm, ranging 3.8 to 8.0 cm

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Treatment of skin carcinomas of the face by high-dose-rate brachytherapy and custom-made surface molds** — Guix, Benjamin; Finestres, Fernando; Tello, José-Ignacio et al.. *International Journal of Radiation Oncology*Biology*Physics* (2000-04). [doi:10.1016/s0360-3016(99)00547-7](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0360-3016(99%2900547-7)
  - 📎 [[papers/skin/2000-guix-treatment-skin-carcinomas-face-high-dose-rate.pdf|HDR brachytherapy with custom surface molds for facial skin carcinomas (Guix) (full text)]]

> [!question] Open questions
> - Hypofractionated mold HDR vs conventional 180 cGy fractions for late cosmesis
> - Mold HDR brachytherapy vs Mohs surgery for facial BCC/SCC
> - Predictors of local failure in tumors recurrent after surgery

### Surface mould brachytherapy for skin cancers, BC Cancer experience (Casey)

> 2-yr LC 84.9%, CR 96.8%, no G3+ toxicity with HDR Ir-192 surface mould in elderly, mostly head and neck skin cancer pts; median f/u 8 mo.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The coverage detail is the RT read: wired target with no PTV, prescribed at 0.5 cm, median V100 only 73.7%, and 2-yr LC 76.9% when V90 <95% vs 88.9% above (NS). Three of five recurrences were nasal BCC, so target-margin and coverage choices on the nose are the parameter to tighten.

- 🔍 Retrospective single-institution review, 2010-2017: 59 pts, 65 lesions
- 🔍 BCC 54% (n=35), SCC 31% (n=20); also melanoma, LM, Merkel, cutaneous NHL, 1 keloid
- 🔍 Median age 82 (range 45-97); 7 lesions treated with palliative intent, included in analysis
- 🔍 Technique: Ir-192 HDR, CT-planned individualized Aquaplast + superflab mould
  - Catheters typically spaced 1 cm apart
  - Target wired clinically, CTV contoured on CT; no PTV expansion
  - Prescribed at 0.5 cm depth, intent 90% dose covering wired target
  - Most common 40 Gy/10 fx (48.2%, n=28); range 15 Gy/3 to 60 Gy/30
- 📐 Dosimetry (medians)
  - Target volume 12.85 cc (range 0.63-130.1 cc)
  - Skin surface dose 126.5%
  - V90 92.3%, V95 84.6%, V100 73.7%
  - Median dose to target volume 108.9%
- 📊 CR 96.8% (61 lesions); PR in 2, 2 not assessable at 2-4 mo
- 📊 2-yr OS 77.6%, 2-yr PFS 71.5%; 2 of 20 deaths from the skin primary
- 📊 Local recurrence in 5 pts: 3 BCC of the nose, 1 forehead SCC, 1 palliative lesion
- 📊 2-yr LC by subgroup (univariate, all NS on log-rank)

  | Variable | Group | 2-yr LC (%) |
  |---|---|---|
  | V90 | <95% | 76.9 |
  | V90 | >95% | 88.9 |
  | Median dose to TV | <100% | 75 |
  | Median dose to TV | >100% | 88.2 |
  | Histology | BCC | 70 |
  | Histology | SCC | 88.2 |

- 📊 Toxicity (CTCAE v4.03): no G3-5 acute or late
  - Acute G2: dermatitis 32% (n=21), ulceration 8% (n=5)
  - Late G2: one necrosis (2%), appeared ~10 mo post-treatment
  - Late G1: hypopigmentation 15.4% (n=10), fibrosis 8% (n=5)
- 🔗 vs Gauden et al. (in source): 236 lesions, 36 Gy/12 fx, LC 98% at median f/u 7 mo
- ⚠️ 2-yr actuarial LC and OS rest on median f/u of 8 mo (range 4.1-11.9)
- ⚠️ Internal inconsistencies: median age 83 vs 82; f/u eight vs nine mo; sex counts sum to 65
- ⚠️ Toxicity graded retrospectively from records and photos; no cosmesis outcome reported in source

**Sources:**
- 📄 **Surface Mould Brachytherapy for Skin Cancers: The British Columbia Cancer Experience** — Casey, Stephanie; Awotwi-Pratt, Joseph; Bahl, Gaurav. *Cureus* (2019-12). [doi:10.7759/cureus.6412](https://doi.org/10.7759/cureus.6412)
  - 📎 [[papers/skin/2019-casey-surface-mould-brachytherapy-skin-cancers-british.pdf|Surface mould brachytherapy for skin cancers, BC Cancer experience (Casey) (full text)]]
