# 2026-10-25

**HDR mould/flap skin brachy for BCC/cSCC: 5yr actuarial LC 98% (n=136), 2yr LC 84.9%, no G3+ late toxicity across four single-institution series.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Skin was the day's only site. HDR surface mould and catheter-flap series report LC 84.9-98% with no G3+ late toxicity, but all four are single-institution cohorts with no comparator vs surgery or EBRT. Short follow-up in the modern series (median 8 and 15 mo) limits the late-toxicity read.

## 🌞 Skin / Melanoma

Dose and fractionation for HDR skin brachy vary widely (30 Gy/5 fx to 64 Gy/32 fx), and pretibial skin carries most of the reported severe toxicity.

### HDR skin brachytherapy: surface mould and flap series

> Mould/flap HDR for BCC/cSCC: 5yr actuarial LC 98% (n=136), 1yr LC 92% (flap), 2yr LC 84.9%; no G3+ late toxicity reported in any series.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Depth, not fractionation, is the gating parameter: series using 180 cGy daily, 40 Gy/10 fx and 40 Gy/8 fx twice weekly all report LC in the 85-98% band when prescribed at 5 mm, while survey respondents avoid brachy beyond 3 mm depth. Pretibial skin is the one site where respondents converge on gentler 2-3 Gy fractions.

- 📊 HDR mould/flap series: dose, local control, toxicity

  | Series | Population | Dose/fx | Local control | Follow-up | Toxicity |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Guix 2000 (IJROBP) | 136 pts, face BCC/SCC, 63 recurrent | 6000-6500 cGy/33-36 fx at 180 cGy; boost to 7500-8000 cGy if ≥4 cm | 5yr actuarial 98%; primary 99%, recurrent 87% | Min 12 mo | No severe early or late complications |
  | Allan 1998 (Radiother Oncol) | 13 pts, pinna, cartilage not invaded | 45 Gy/8 fx over 5 days; sandwich mould 42.5 Gy | No recurrences | Min 18 mo | Moderate erythema; no late complications |
  | Casey 2019 (Cureus) | 59 pts/65 lesions, median age 82, mixed histology | 40 Gy/10 fx most common (48.2%) | 2yr LC 84.9%; CR 96.8% | Median 8 mo | No G3-5; one G2 necrosis |
  | Gogineni 2021 (J Contemp Brachy) | 50 pts/53 lesions, T1 face, median age 80 | 40 Gy/8 fx twice weekly, catheter flap | 1yr LC 92% | Median 15 mo | Acute G3 5%; no chronic G3+ |

- 🔍 All four outcome papers are single-institution series; no randomised comparator vs surgery or EBRT
- 🔍 Prescription depth clusters at 5 mm across Guix, Casey and Gogineni
- 🔍 ABS practice survey (Likhacheva 2017, 16 respondents)
  - Doses ranged 30 Gy/5 fx to 64 Gy/32 fx (EQD2 40-65 Gy)
  - Margin on gross disease range 3-15mm, median 5mm
  - Sixty percent routinely estimated lesion depth via ultrasound
  - Avoided brachy for depth over 3 mm, perineural invasion, prior RT
  - Pretibial skin: 10 respondents (63%) used 2-3 Gy per fraction
- ⚠️ Severe toxicity reported mainly on pretibial skin; three respondents referred non-healing ulcers for hyperbaric oxygen
- ⚠️ Casey cohort includes melanoma, lymphoma, Merkel and 7 palliative lesions; LC not BCC/cSCC-specific
- ⚠️ Short follow-up in the modern series (median 8 and 15 mo) underestimates late toxicity and late recurrence
- ⚠️ Guix reports average diameter 3.8 cm with range 3.8 to 8.0 cm, inconsistent with 29 pts ≤2 cm
- 🔗 Guix cites Lovett superficial X-ray LC 95% (180/189) as the EBRT benchmark; primary-tumor LC 99% here
