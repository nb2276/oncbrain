# 2026-11-08

**ASTRO-ASCO-SSO PMRT guideline: PMRT strongly recommended for pN1, any pT4, ypN1 and cT4/cN2-3 after NST; conditional for pT3N0; moderate hypofractionation preferred.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Breast carried the day: one joint guideline sets PMRT indications by pathologic and post-NST stage; another says RT should not be withheld in BRCA1/2, ATM or moderate-penetrance carriers, but intact-breast RT is contraindicated in germline TP53. HyTEC models put VS SRS 3-5y TCP at 91.2% at 12 Gy and adrenal SBRT 1-yr LC >95% at BED10 116.4 Gy.

## 🎀 Breast

Two joint ASTRO/ASCO/SSO guidelines: PMRT indications after upfront mastectomy or NST, and RT decisions in germline carriers.

### ASCO-ASTRO-SSO Hereditary Breast Cancer Guideline

> Joint consensus guideline: RT should not be withheld in BRCA1/2, ATM or other moderate-penetrance carriers; intact-breast RT is contraindicated in germline TP53 carriers.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The ATM recommendation carries only low evidence: RT should be offered when indicated because toxicity data are limited and inconsistent, and the one CBC signal involved rare predicted-deleterious variants of uncertain pathogenicity. TP53 is the only genotype where the guideline moves the RT decision, toward mastectomy with PMRT reserved for significant LRR risk.

- 🔍 Joint ASCO/ASTRO/SSO guideline built on a systematic review plus a formal consensus process
- 🔍 58 articles informed the local therapy recs; 28 of them addressed RT in germline carriers
- 🔍 6 RCTs informed the systemic therapy recs
- 📊 Radiation and local therapy recommendations by genotype

  | Rec | Population | Recommendation | Evidence / strength |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1.1 | BRCA1/2, BCT-eligible | BRCA status should not preclude BCT | Intermediate / moderate |
  | 6.1 | BRCA1/2 or moderate-penetrance gene | RT not withheld for mutation status, after BCT or when PMRT is considered | Intermediate / strong |
  | 6.2 | ATM carriers | Offer RT when clinically indicated; discuss data with ATM carriers considering BCT | Low / moderate |
  | 6.3 | Germline TP53 | Intact-breast RT contraindicated; mastectomy recommended; PMRT only with significant LRR risk | Low / moderate |
  | 4.1 | BRCA1/2, unilateral mastectomy | Offer CRRM; lowers CBC risk, survival benefit unproven | Intermediate / moderate |
  | 4.2 | Moderate-penetrance gene | CRRM decision not driven mainly by mutation status | Low / moderate |

- 💊 Systemic therapy recommendations by genotype

  | Rec | Setting | Recommendation | Evidence / strength |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 7 | gBRCA, metastatic, platinum-naive | Platinum preferred over taxane | Intermediate / moderate |
  | 8 | gBRCA, (neo)adjuvant | No routine platinum added to anthracycline + taxane | Intermediate / moderate |
  | 9.1 | BRCA1/2, metastatic HER2-negative | Olaparib or talazoparib as alternative to chemo, lines 1-3 | High / strong |
  | 9.2 | Moderate-penetrance genes | No robust data supporting PARP inhibitors | Insufficient / moderate |
  | 10 | gBRCA, nonmetastatic | Insufficient data to recommend a PARP inhibitor | Intermediate / moderate |

- 🔗 No evidence of a significant increase in toxicity or CBC from RT in BRCA1/2 or moderate-penetrance carriers
- ⚠️ ATM signal: one reviewed study reported higher CBC with RT in carriers of rare predicted-deleterious ATM variants; not consistent across studies
- ⚠️ ATM variant pathogenicity is unsettled; most ATM variants are missense, which confounds RT-risk studies
- ⚠️ CHEK2 1100delC is the only moderate-penetrance gene with CBC data: ~3-fold risk, 10% to 12% absolute at 10 years
- 🔍 Round 1 agreement ranged 72% to 98% (mean 90%); 1 of 17 recs missed the 75% threshold, passed at 90% on revote

[doi:10.1200/jco.20.00299](https://doi.org/10.1200/jco.20.00299)
  > PURPOSE To develop recommendations for management of patients with breast cancer (BC) with germline mutations in BC susceptibility genes. METHODS The American Society of Clinical Oncology, American Society for Radiation Oncology, and Society of Surgical Oncology convened an Expert Panel to develop recommendations based on a systematic review of the literature and a formal consensus process. RESULTS Fifty-eight articles met eligibility criteria and formed the evidentiary basis for the local therapy recommendations; six randomized controlled trials of systemic therapy met eligibility criteria. RECOMMENDATIONS Patients with newly diagnosed BC and BRCA1/ 2 mutations may be considered for breast-conserving therapy (BCT), with local control of the index cancer similar to that of noncarriers. The significant risk of a contralateral BC (CBC), especially in young women, and the higher risk of new cancers in the ipsilateral breast warrant discussion of bilateral mastectomy. Patients with mutations in moderate-risk genes should be offered BCT. For women with mutations in BRCA1/ 2 or moderate-penetrance genes who are eligible for mastectomy, nipple-sparing mastectomy is a reasonable approach. There is no evidence of increased toxicity or CBC events from radiation exposure in BRCA1/ 2 carriers. Radiation therapy should not be withheld in ATM carriers. For patients with germline TP53 mutations, mastectomy is advised; radiation therapy is contraindicated except in those with significant risk of locoregional recurrence. Platinum agents are recommended versus taxanes to treat advanced BC in BRCA carriers. In the adjuvant/neoadjuvant setting, data do not support the routine addition of platinum to anthracycline- and taxane-based chemotherapy. Poly (ADP-ribose) polymerase (PARP) inhibitors (olaparib and talazoparib) are preferable to nonplatinum single-agent chemotherapy for treatment of advanced BC in BRCA1/ 2 carriers. Data are insufficient to recommend PARP inhibitor use in the early setting or in moderate-penetrance carriers. Additional information available at www.asco.org/breast-cancer-guidelines .

### ASTRO-ASCO-SSO PMRT Guideline

> PMRT is recommended for pN1, any pT4, ypN1 and cT4/cN2-3 after NST, and conditional for pT3N0 and cT1-3N1/cT3N0 ypN0. Moderate hypofractionation is preferred.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The ypN0 split is the new operating rule: cT1-3N1 or cT3N0 converting to ypN0 is conditional (omission reasonable at ypT0N0), while cT1-2N0 ypN0 is a strong no. Moderate hypofractionation is now strongly recommended even with reconstruction, and routine bolus is out, restricting it to skin involvement, superficial margins or LVI.

- 🔍 Joint ASTRO-ASCO-SSO task force, 4 key questions: upfront mastectomy, post-NST, volumes/fractionation, technique
- 🔍 Systematic review Jan 2005 to Oct 15, 2024; 110 studies included (104 initial + 6 updated)
- 🔍 Retrospective studies excluded for KQ1; inclusion required ≥50 pts for RCTs and ≥100 pts for nonrandomized studies
- 📊 KQ1: PMRT after mastectomy as initial treatment

  | Population | Recommendation | Strength | QoE |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | pN1 | PMRT recommended | Strong | High |
  | Any pT4 | PMRT recommended, even without other risk factors | Strong | High |
  | pT3N0 | PMRT conditionally recommended | Conditional | High |
  | pT1-2N0 | PMRT not recommended | Strong | Low |
  | Positive margins, no other indication | Chest wall/reconstructed breast alone | Conditional | Expert opinion |

- 📊 KQ2: PMRT after neoadjuvant systemic therapy

  | Population | Recommendation | Strength | QoE |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | cT4 or cN2-3 | PMRT regardless of pathologic response | Strong | Moderate |
  | ypN1 | PMRT recommended | Strong | Moderate |
  | cT1-3N1 or cT3N0 → ypN0 | PMRT conditionally recommended | Conditional | Moderate |
  | cT1-2N0 → ypN0 | PMRT not recommended | Strong | Moderate |
  | Positive margins after NST | PMRT recommended | Strong | Expert opinion |

- 📊 KQ3: volumes and fractionation

  | Scenario | Recommendation | Strength |
  |---|---|---|
  | Any PMRT | Chest wall/reconstructed breast + RNI (axilla, SCV/ICV, IMN) | Strong |
  | No reconstruction | Moderate hypofractionation | Strong |
  | Reconstruction | Moderate hypofx (preferred) or conventional | Strong |
  | T4 or close/positive margins | Chest wall/scar boost | Conditional |
  | Nodal disease not surgically addressed | Nodal boost | Strong |

- 📊 Technique: CT-based 3D-CRT; IMRT only when 3D-CRT can't meet goals; DIBH for normal tissue sparing
- 📊 Bolus for skin involvement, positive superficial margins and/or LVI; routine tissue-equivalent bolus not recommended
- 🔍 De-escalation carve-outs (implementation remarks)
  - pN1mic or low-burden pN1a after ALND with favorable features: omission may be appropriate
  - Favorable = pT1-2, low-intermediate grade, HR+/HER2-, postmenopausal, no LVI, low 21-gene RS
  - pT3N0 favorable: omit or chest wall alone
  - ypT0N0 after cT1-3N1/cT3N0: PMRT may be omitted
  - IMN coverage individualized by tumor location, size, nodal extent
- ⚠️ Dose/fraction schedules not specified in source excerpt; 'moderate hypofractionation' left undefined here
- ⚠️ pN1 benefit rests on EBCTCG trials initiated by 1995, before modern HER2-directed therapy and IO
- ⚠️ Positive-margin recommendations rest on expert opinion only

## 🧠 CNS

HyTEC dose-response modeling for sporadic VS SRS, plus a LINAC replanning study where VMAT gains conformity at ~41% more MU.

### HyTEC Vestibular Schwannoma SRS TCP

> Pooled LQ model of 35 series (5162 pts) predicts 3-5y TCP 91.2% at 12 Gy and 93.5% at 13 Gy single-fraction SRS.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The usable read is the 1, 3 and 5 fx dose equivalence table: 95% TCP sits at 13.8-14.1 Gy x1, 18.8-19.2 Gy/3 and 21.5-23.0 Gy/5 across both models. Current 12-13 Gy, 18 Gy/3 and 25 Gy/5 prescriptions all land on the plateau. That supports picking fractionation on nerve and hearing goals rather than on tumor control.

- 🔍 HyTEC literature pooling: 35 publications, 5162 pts, sporadic VS only
- 🔍 TCP taken at 5y when reported, else 3y (21 articles 3y, 14 articles 5y)
- 📊 3-5y tumor control >90% to 95% in 89% of reviewed studies; 4 smaller studies at 80% to 90%
- 📊 Model-predicted 3-5y TCP by regimen

  | Regimen | LQ (α/β 12.4) | LQ-L (α/β 2.97) |
  |---|---|---|
  | 10 Gy x1 | 85.0% | 89.7% |
  | 11 Gy x1 | 88.4% | 93.1% |
  | 12 Gy x1 | 91.2% | 95.6% |
  | 13 Gy x1 | 93.5% | 97.33% |
  | 18 Gy/3 fx | 93.6% (EQD2 23.0 Gy) | 94.0% (EQD2 32.5 Gy) |
  | 25 Gy/5 fx | 97.2% (EQD2 30.2 Gy) | 96.9% (EQD2 40.1 Gy) |

- 📐 Physical dose for 95% TCP (Table 3)

  | Model | 1 fx | 3 fx | 5 fx |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | LQ | 13.8 Gy | 19.2 Gy | 21.5 Gy |
  | LQ-L | 14.1 Gy | 18.8 Gy | 23.0 Gy |

- 📐 α/β 12.4 Gy (95% CI 9.0-19.3) by LQ vs 2.97 Gy (95% CI 1.72-4.27) by LQ-L
- ⚠️ No outcome data below the equivalent of 11 Gy x1; lower-dose predictions are extrapolation
- ⚠️ Only 46% of articles gave Kaplan-Meier estimates; the rest were crude control at median f/u
- ⚠️ Untreated TCP fixed at an assumed 30%; prescription dose treated as GTV minimum dose
- 🔍 NF2 excluded (TCP approximately 75% at 5y); pediatric and post-resection progression not modeled

### LINAC SRS: DCA vs VMAT Dosimetric Study

> Paddick CI 0.78 VMAT vs 0.75 DCA+SSO+VDR (p=0.03); GI and V12Gy not different between the two; VMAT ~41 ± 17% more MU.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Against fully optimized DCA (SSO + VDR), coplanar VMAT bought only a CI gain of 0.75 to 0.78, with GI (p=0.29) and brain V12Gy (p=0.38) not different, at 41 ± 17% more MU. Because DCA was noncoplanar and VMAT coplanar, this compares beam geometry as much as optimization, which limits any class-solution claim.

- 🔍 Retrospective replanning study, 11 brain cases, 4 plans each on Monaco TPS (v.5.11.03)
- 🔍 Case mix and planning parameters
  - 7 solitary mets, 2 meningioma, 1 acoustic schwannoma, 1 pituitary adenoma
  - 9 SRS + 2 SRT cases; prescription 18 to 30Gy
  - PTV volume 0.93 to 22.5 cc (median 4.99cc); PTV = GTV + 2 mm
  - 6 FFF, Agility MLC with 5 mm leaves, Monte Carlo, 0.3 cm grid
- ⚠️ Geometry confounded: all DCA plans noncoplanar, VMAT single-arc coplanar only
- 📊 Mean plan metrics by technique (paired t-test, sequential comparisons)

  | Metric | DCA | DCA+SSO | DCA+SSO+VDR | VMAT | p (DCA vs SSO, SSO vs VDR, VDR vs VMAT) |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Paddick CI | 0.6 | 0.69 | 0.75 | 0.78 | 0.0004, 0.015, 0.03 |
  | Paddick GI | 1.35 | 1.21 | 1.20 | 1.17 | 0.03, 0.33, 0.29 |
  | RTOG HI | 1.17 | 1.22 | 1.26 | 1.28 | 0.008, 0.04, 0.06 |
  | MU | 2507 | 2724 | 2818 | 4040 | 0.001, 0.20, 0.0007 |
  | Brain V12Gy (cc) | 16.75 | 14.13 | 11.75 | 11.14 | 0.10, 0.01, 0.38 |

- 📊 VMAT V12Gy lower than every DCA variant in 9 of 11 pts
- ⚠️ No delivery time measured; authors' claim of shorter VMAT treatment time is unquantified
- ⚠️ Multiple paired t-tests, no correction; only adjacent techniques compared, never DCA vs VMAT directly
- ⚠️ V12Gy reported across SRS and SRT cases alike, though it is a single-fraction metric
- 🔗 Authors cite Chang et al: VMAT needed higher MU than DCA by a factor of 2.37

**Sources:**
- 📄 **The Optimal Choice of Technique for Stereotactic Radiosurgery—A LINAC-Based Dosimetric Study between DCA, DCA-SSO, DCA-SSO-VDR, and VMAT** — Venugopal, Ramya; S., Sowmya Narayanan; Tiwari, Richa et al.. *Asian Journal of Oncology* (2022-07). [doi:10.1055/s-0042-1751117](https://doi.org/10.1055/s-0042-1751117)
  > Abstract Introduction Advanced radiation therapy delivery techniques require greater understanding of various planning sequences and methods. The aim of this study is to determine a class solution that finds the best possible technique to deliver for stereotactic radiosurgery between dynamic conformal arc (DCA) techniques using various options such as DCA, DCA + SSO (segment shape optimization), and DCA + SSO + VDR (variable dose rate) using noncoplanar beam arrangement and volumetric modulated arc therapy (VMAT) using coplanar beams. Materials and Methods In this dosimetric study, 11 brain cases were retrospectively planned for various techniques and analyzed for the Paddick conformity index (CI), Radiation Therapy Oncology Group homogeneity index (HI), Paddick gradient index (GI), treatment time in terms of monitor units (MU) and normal brain dose (V12Gy). The paired t-test was performed to know the statistical significance between the techniques. Results In terms of CI, GI, and control of the normal brain dose, the VMAT plan was superior to other techniques. But, HI was found to be better with DCA. Above all, VMAT delivered higher MU than any other technique. The p-values between DCA + SSO and DCA, DCA + SSO + VDR and DCA + SSO, and VMAT and DCA + SSO + VDR are as follows: CI: 0.0004, 0.015, and 0.03; GI: 0.03, 0.33, and 0.29; HI: 0.008, 0.04, and 0.06; V12 Gy of normal brain: 0.1, 0.01, 0.38. If VMAT is not available in the facility, DCA + SSO + VDR technique using non coplanar beams can be used to deliver SRS treatment.

## 🎯 Oligometastatic / Mets

HyTEC adrenal SBRT fit built on small retrospective series; the 1-yr LC endpoint may understate underdosing.

### HyTEC Adrenal SBRT TCP

> 1-yr LC >95% predicted at BED10 116.4 Gy (EQD2 ~97 Gy) in a Poisson TCP fit to published adrenal metastasis SBRT series.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The 116.4 Gy figure is a GTV-coverage estimate: study medians were adjusted for the prescription IDL, so it describes how well the plan covers gross disease, not the nominal Rx line. The series at BED10 85.5 and 72 Gy held 1-yr LC but lost it at 2-3 yr, so the argument for escalating rests on durability.

- 📊 Model: 1-yr LC >95% at BED10 116.4 Gy (EQD2 ~97 Gy)
- 📐 LC fit χ2 57.5, P<.0001; D50 34.6 Gy EQD2 (95% CI 27.1-45.4)
- 🔍 Literature review 2007-2017: 250 hits, small retrospective series of 6-49 lesions each
- 🔍 Dose per study = median Rx adjusted for IDL when known; no DVH data
- 💊 Across series: median 40 Gy (mean 37.2 Gy), median 5 fx (range 1-27)
- 📊 Fractionations the authors give as examples near the target
  - 45 Gy / 3 fx: BED10 112.5 Gy
  - 55 Gy / 5 fx: BED10 115.5 Gy
  - Casamassima: 36 Gy / 3 fx to 70% IDL, BED10 just over 139 Gy at isocenter, LC 90% at 1 and 2 yr
- 🔗 Predicted 1-yr LC by BED10, in-press meta-analysis (22 studies), within this model's CI

  | BED10 | Predicted 1-yr LC |
  |---|---|
  | 60 Gy | 70.5% |
  | 80 Gy | 84.8% |
  | 100 Gy | 92.9% |

- 🔗 Later series at BED10 112.5 Gy: 96.5% 1-yr LC (31 pts, 33 lesions), on the curve
- ⚠️ Series at BED10 85.5 and 72 Gy lost LC at 2-3 yr; a 1-yr endpoint understates underdosing
- ⚠️ Crude 1-yr LC ranged 44% to 100% across series; competing death from other mets lowers its reliability
- ⚠️ OS fit (χ2 44.7, P<.001) has wide CIs; dose likely tracks selection, not causation
- 🔍 No PTV margin or IDL (70-100%) correlation with LC; margins ranged 0 to 10 mm
