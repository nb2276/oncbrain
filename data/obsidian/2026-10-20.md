# 2026-10-20

**Nine-trial NACT pool (N=10,075): 5-yr LRR 4.1% with pCR vs 9.5% without (HR 3.33), TNBC non-pCR 18.5%, the risk group to target for post-NACT RT.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> All-breast, all-retrospective day on locoregional risk for RT selection. After NACT, residual disease and TNBC drive LRR (non-pCR HR 3.33; CPS+EG 5-yr LRR-free 71% to 99%), and RNI's adjusted LRR HR 0.497 rests on a null univariate split. With upfront mastectomy and no RT, RS ≥18 (10-yr LRR 16.5% vs 9.7%) and age <45 with >25% nodes positive flag LRR risk.

## 🎀 Breast

Five retrospective or pooled cohorts on who needs PMRT or RNI. None randomised RT, so every RT signal here is confounded by indication.

### 21-gene Recurrence Score and locoregional recurrence in node-positive breast cancer (SWOG 8814 correlative)

> 10-yr LRR 16.5% vs 9.7% for RS ≥18 vs <18 (P=.02) in ER/PR+ node-positive breast; widest split at 1-3 nodes after mastectomy without RT.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> In mastectomy-only pts with 1-3 nodes, 10-yr LRR was 1.5% at RS <18 vs 11.1% at RS ≥18 (P=.051, n=165). That gives the PMRT de-escalation hypothesis TailorRT is testing a biological basis. At ≥4 nodes low RS offered no protection (25.9% vs 27.0%), so a low score is no reason to drop PMRT in pN2a.

- 🔍 Retrospective translational cohort from phase 3 SWOG S8814 (tamoxifen alone vs CAF-T), N=316 postmenopausal ER/PR+ node-positive women
- 🔍 Excluded mastectomy + RT (n=37), BCS without RT (n=9), unknown surgery (n=5)
- 📊 Prespecified primary analysis (continuous RS, multivariable) null: HR 1.14 per 10-point increase (95% CI 0.97-1.35, P=.11)
- 📊 10-yr actuarial LRR by RS category (secondary analysis, cut at 18)

  | Cohort | n | RS 0-17 | RS ≥18 | P |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | All pts | 316 | 9.7% | 16.5% | .02 |
  | Mastectomy, no RT | 252 | 7.7% | 16.8% | .03 |
  | Mastectomy, no RT, 1-3 nodes | 165 | 1.5% | 11.1% | .051 |
  | Mastectomy, no RT, ≥4 nodes | not stated | 25.9% | 27.0% | .27 |

- 📐 LRR multivariable model

  | Variable | HR (95% CI) | P |
  |---|---|---|
  | Intermediate or high RS | 2.36 (1.02-5.45) | .04 |
  | ≥4 nodes | 3.37 (1.69-6.72) | .001 |
  | Mastectomy | 1.11 (0.45-2.71) | .82 |
  | Chemotherapy | 0.58 (0.29-1.15) | .12 |

- 📊 Nodal burden: 10-yr LRR 9.0% with 1-3 nodes vs 24.4% with ≥4 (P=.002)
- ⚠️ ≥4 nodes after mastectomy: low RS did not lower LRR (25.9% vs 27.0%), so no PMRT omission signal in pN2a
- 🔗 vs NSABP B-28 (all pts got chemo, no PMRT allowed), 10-yr LRR
  - 1-3 nodes: 2.4% low / 4.1% intermediate / 6.0% high RS
  - ≥4 nodes, mastectomy: 5.5% / 9.6% / 23.5%
  - S8814 1-3 node mastectomy pts with RS ≥18 ran higher at 11.1%
- ⚠️ Cut point of 18 confirmed as optimal only post hoc; subsets exploratory, not adjusted for multiplicity
- ⚠️ BCS arm uninformative: 6 LRR events total (2/29 low vs 4/35 intermediate/high RS)
- 🔍 Mean LRR follow-up 8.2 y; median 8.7 y (IQR 7.0-10.2) for pts without LRR

### Locoregional recurrence after neoadjuvant chemotherapy: pooled analysis of nine trials

> 5-yr LRR 4.1% with pCR vs 9.5% without (multivariate HR 3.33); TNBC non-pCR 18.5%. Surgery type not independent after NACT.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Residual disease interacts with subtype: TNBC non-pCR LRR was 18.5% at 5y versus 4.2% after pCR, and HR-/HER2+ 14.8% vs 3.1%. With RT use unrecorded, these rates show where post-neoadjuvant RNI/PMRT escalation trials should focus. Within pCR, only cN+ trended toward risk (HR 1.61, p=0.054), relevant to node-based de-escalation.

- 🔍 Exploratory IPD pooled analysis of 9 prospective GBG/AGO-B neoadjuvant trials (1998-2013), N=10,075
- 🔍 Anthracycline/taxane NACT throughout; median f/u 67 mo
- 🔍 LRR as first event; distant recurrence, second malignancy and death treated as competing events (Gray / Fine-Gray)
- 📊 Overall 5-yr LRR: 7.8% after BCS vs 11.3% after mastectomy
- 📊 5-yr LRR by subtype: HR+/HER2- 6.0%, HR+/HER2+ 7.6%, HR-/HER2+ 10.5%, TNBC 14.4%
- 📐 Multivariate: non-pCR HR 3.33 (95% CI 2.60-4.28); TNBC vs HR+/HER2- HR 2.72 (2.23-3.31); both p<0.001
- 📊 5-yr cumulative LRR, pCR vs non-pCR

  | Stratum | pCR | Non-pCR |
  |---|---|---|
  | HR+/HER2- | 3.9% | 5.9% |
  | HR+/HER2+ | 4.8% | 8.1% |
  | HR-/HER2+ | 3.1% | 14.8% |
  | TNBC | 4.2% | 18.5% |
  | BCS | 4.0% | 8.8% |
  | Mastectomy | 3.9% | 11.3% |

- 📐 Independent LRR predictors within non-pCR pts
  - Age <50: HR 1.32 (1.12-1.55)
  - cN+: HR 1.49 (1.27-1.74)
  - G3: HR 1.39 (1.16-1.65)
  - TNBC: HR 2.77 (2.27-3.39)
- 📊 After pCR, only cN+ trended toward higher LRR: HR 1.61 (0.99-2.61), p=0.054
- 📊 Mastectomy subgroup: non-pCR HR 4.00 (2.22-7.21); TNBC HR 2.48; cN+ HR 1.86
- ⚠️ No patient-level RT data: PMRT and RNI use unknown, so RT's contribution to these rates cannot be separated
- ⚠️ Mastectomy LRR excess likely selection bias (higher-risk pts sent to mastectomy), per authors
- ⚠️ Pre-modern post-neoadjuvant therapy: older trials gave no anti-HER2 therapy to HER2+ pts; no T-DM1 or capecitabine escalation
- 🔗 vs EBCTCG NACT vs adjuvant meta-analysis: higher local recurrence with NACT, especially with suboptimal local therapy

[doi:10.1016/j.ejca.2020.02.015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejca.2020.02.015)
  - 📎 [[papers/breast/2020-werutsky-locoregional-recurrence-risk-neoadjuvant-chemotherapy.pdf|Locoregional recurrence after neoadjuvant chemotherapy: pooled analysis of nine trials (full text)]]

### PMRT selection in T1-T2, 1-3 node-positive breast cancer

> 10y KM LRR±SDR 15.9% without PMRT in pT1-T2 1-3 N+; age <45 with >25% nodes positive reached 58.0%.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Clavicular nodes carried 19 of 57 regional recurrences (33%) versus 2 for internal mammary, so the residual risk this model flags sits mostly in the axillary-supraclavicular field. In women ≥45 with ≤25% nodes positive, 10y LRR±SDR was 10.8%, near the authors' <10% omission threshold, while medial or ER-negative tumors pushed that subset to approximately 20%.

- 🔍 Retrospective population-based BCCA cohort, N=821, diagnosed 1989-1997, mastectomy without adjuvant RT
- 🔍 All had modified radical mastectomy + ALND; median 10 nodes removed (range 1-39)
- ⚠️ 296 pts who received PMRT were excluded, so the no-RT cohort may be selected toward lower risk
- 💊 Systemic therapy in 94%: AC 202, CMF 91, FAC 4 of 302 chemo pts; tamoxifen in 581
- 📊 10y KM ILRR 12.7% (SE 1.3), LRR±SDR 15.9% (SE 1.5); median f/u 7.7y
- 📊 10y KM BCSS 71.3%, OS 58.2%; distant recurrence 31.2% (n=256)
- 📊 Regional recurrence sites (n=57)
  - Axillary: 36 (63%)
  - Clavicular: 19 (33%)
  - Internal mammary: 2 (3%)
- 📐 Multivariable Cox: independent predictors of LRR

  | Factor | ILRR HR (95% CI), p | LRR±SDR HR (95% CI), p |
  |---|---|---|
  | Age <45 vs ≥45 | 3.44 (1.68-7.07), p=0.001 | 3.22 (1.54-6.75), p=0.002 |
  | >25% vs ≤25% nodes positive | 2.00 (0.99-4.05), p=0.05 | 2.61 (1.31-5.22), p=0.006 |
  | Medial vs lateral | 2.46 (1.15-5.26), p=0.002 | 3.69 (2.17-6.29), p=0.0001 |
  | ER-negative vs positive | 2.02 (1.16-3.54), p=0.01 | 1.82 (1.05-3.17), p=0.03 |

- 📊 Risk classification model, 10y KM LRR (Fig 1)

  | Subgroup | N | ILRR | LRR±SDR |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Age <45, >25% N+ | 19 | 47.6% | 58.0% |
  | Age <45, ≤25% N+ | 104 | 18.5% | 23.8% |
  | Age ≥45, >25% N+ | 122 | 20.6% | 26.7% |
  | Age ≥45, ≤25% N+ | 570 | 8.7% | 10.8% |

- ⚠️ >25% N+ ILRR CI 0.99-4.05 crosses 1 despite p=0.05
- ⚠️ Model breakpoints chosen by significance + subgroup size; no external validation; top-risk cell N=19
- 🔗 DBCG 82b/82c no-PMRT LRR ~30% at 10y vs this cohort's 13-16%, attributed to axillary staging + selection differences

### CPS+EG staging and local-regional recurrence after neoadjuvant chemotherapy

> 5-yr freedom from LRR by CPS+EG score spanned 71% to 99%, vs 86% to 97% by clinical or pathologic stage, in 1697 pts after NACT.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The PMRT read is in the CPS+EG ≤2 slice of the PMRT-indicated cohort (cStage III or ypN+): 5-yr LRR-free survival did not differ by local therapy (P=0.73), but only 72 pts had mastectomy alone. ER-positive or low-grade biology drives those low scores, so the proposed de-escalation gate is biology on top of residual nodal stage.

- 🔍 Retrospective MD Anderson cohort, N=1697, treated 1997 to 2005, prospectively maintained database
- 🔍 CPS+EG = clinical stage + post-NACT pathologic stage + ER status + nuclear grade (AJCC 7th)
- ⚠️ Excluded pts given neoadjuvant trastuzumab and pts given preoperative RT
- 🔍 Median f/u 51 mo (range 4 to 113)
- 📊 Crude LRR by local therapy (overall 6.5%, N=110)

  | Local therapy | N | LRR events | Crude LRR |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Breast conserving therapy | 656 | 36 | 5.5% |
  | Mastectomy alone | 297 | 24 | 8.1% |
  | Mastectomy + PMRT | 744 | 50 | 6.7% |

- 📊 5-yr LRR-free survival by CPS+EG score

  | CPS+EG score | 5-yr LRR-free (%) | 95% CI (%) |
  |---|---|---|
  | 0 | 97.8 | 91.3–99.5 |
  | 1 | 98.6 | 95.8–99.5 |
  | 2 | 95.3 | 92.4–97.1 |
  | 3 | 93.5 | 90.2–95.7 |
  | 4 | 85.4 | 79.8–89.6 |
  | 5 | 79.0 | 67.5–86.8 |
  | 6 | 70.8 | 49.8–84.3 |

- 📊 PMRT-indicated cohort (cStage III or ypN+, N=1103): 5-yr LRR-free survival by local therapy

  | Subgroup | BCT N | Mastectomy alone N | Mastectomy + PMRT N | P |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | CPS+EG 0-2 (N=358) | 114 | 72 | 172 | 0.73 |
  | CPS+EG 3-6 (N=745) | 202 | 51 | 492 | 0.0006 |

- 📊 MVA: CPS+EG ≥3, ≥4 and ≥6 tied to higher LRR risk; ≥5 nonsignificant trend
- 📊 MVA: mastectomy alone significantly higher LRR risk vs BCT; PMRT marginally lower
- ⚠️ Table 5 row-level HRs not reliably attributable in source extraction; omitted here
- ⚠️ Omission signal rests on 72 mastectomy-alone pts in the CPS+EG ≤2 subset
- ⚠️ Confounding by indication: local therapy chosen by pt, surgeon and radonc, not randomized
- ⚠️ Text says mastectomy alone had HIGHER LRR-free survival in CPS+EG ≥3, contradicting the MVA; per-arm rates not in source text
- ⚠️ Overall LRR given as 6.5% in Results and 6.1% in Discussion
- ⚠️ RT dose, fractionation and target volumes not reported in source
- 🔗 vs NSABP B-18/B-27 (cited): 10-yr LRR 10.3% BCT, 12.3% mastectomy, but no PMRT given

### Long-term impact of regional nodal irradiation after neoadjuvant systemic therapy

> Adjusted LRR HR 0.497 and DR HR 0.731 with RNI after NAT in cN+ stage II-III breast; unadjusted 10-yr LRR 9.4% vs 8.1%, P=.62.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The unadjusted 10-yr LRR curves barely separate (9.4% vs 8.1%), yet RNI held up in residual-nodal disease on MVA (HR 0.476), which is where most RNI is already given. The axillary pCR arm stays uninformative: 5.5% vs 4.6% with too few events to model, so it does not answer the omission question.

- 🔍 Single-institution retrospective (MD Anderson), N=1289, treated 1989-2007
- 🔍 Stage II-III, cytologically confirmed axillary mets, NAT then BCS or mastectomy with ALND
- 🔍 RNI given to 1080/1289 (83.8%), at treating radiation oncologist's discretion
- 📊 Median f/u 10.2 yrs after definitive surgery; 162/1289 (12.6%) had LRR
- 📊 Axillary pCR in 368/1289 (28.5%); 10-yr LRR 9.7% no pCR vs 4.8% pCR, P=.006
- 📊 Univariate 10-yr cumulative incidence, no RNI vs RNI

  | Endpoint | No RNI | RNI | P |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | LRR, all pts | 9.4% | 8.1% | .62 |
  | LRR, axillary pCR | 5.5% | 4.6% | .98 |
  | LRR, no axillary pCR | 12.5% | 9.3% | .24 |
  | DR, all pts | 31.3% | 36.5% | .16 |
  | DR, axillary pCR | 20.3% | 15.9% | .25 |
  | DR, no axillary pCR | 40.4% | 43.4% | .68 |

- 📐 Multicovariate Cox, RNI vs no RNI (HER2+ without trastuzumab excluded)
- 📊 RNI did not affect DSS or OS on univariate or multicovariate analysis
- ⚠️ Univariate null flips to benefit only after adjustment: confounding by indication (RNI pts younger, LVSI+, higher cT/cN)
- ⚠️ LRR MVA in axillary pCR pts not attempted: too few events (n=19)
- ⚠️ Tangent details unavailable; high-tangent coverage in 'no RNI' pts unknown
- ⚠️ 51/162 LRRs excluded from LRR analysis because DM preceded LRR (competing risk)
- 🔗 Authors frame result as consistent with EORTC 22922 and MA.20 RNI benefit after adjuvant chemo
