# 2026-10-20

**MDACC RNI after NAT in cN+ breast (N=1289): adjusted 10-yr LRR HR 0.497 (0.279-0.884), DR HR 0.731, but univariate LRR 9.4% vs 8.1%, P=.62.**

> [!summary] TL;DR
> All five studies are breast, all retrospective or pooled, all asking who needs PMRT or RNI. After NAC, risk follows residual disease and biology: 5y LRR 4.1% with pCR vs 9.5% without, TNBC non-pCR 18.5%, CPS+EG spread 71% to 99%. Without NAC, age <45 with >25% N+ reached 58.0% 10-yr LRR±SDR without PMRT.

## 🎀 Breast

Five non-randomized datasets on selecting PMRT and RNI by risk. None randomized RT, so every RT effect estimate here carries confounding by indication.

### CPS+EG staging and local-regional recurrence after neoadjuvant chemotherapy

> 5-yr LRR-free survival spanned 71% to 99% across CPS+EG scores vs 86% to 97% by clinical or pathologic stage alone, in 1697 pts after NAC.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> In the PMRT-indicated subgroup (cStage III or ypN+), pts with CPS+EG 0-2 had no LRR-free difference across BCT, mastectomy alone and PMRT (P=0.73, N=358), while scores 3-6 separated (P=0.0006). ER status and grade add biology to the post-NAC PMRT decision beyond ypN status, but cell sizes are small and HER2+ pts given trastuzumab are absent.

- 🔍 Retrospective, single-institution (MD Anderson), N=1697 treated 1997-2005; median f/u 51 mo
- 🔍 CPS+EG = clinical stage + post-NAC pathologic stage + ER-negative (1 pt) + nuclear grade 3 (1 pt); score 0-6
- 💊 NAC anthracycline- and/or taxane-based; pts given neoadjuvant trastuzumab excluded
- 🔍 Local therapy after NAC (non-randomized, chosen by pt/surgeon/rad onc)
  - BCT: 656 (39%)
  - Mastectomy alone: 297 (17%)
  - Mastectomy + PMRT: 744 (44%)
- 📊 5-yr LRR-free survival by CPS+EG score (Table 4)

  | CPS+EG score | 5-yr LRR-free (%) | 95% CI (%) |
  |---|---|---|
  | 0 | 97.8 | 91.3-99.5 |
  | 1 | 98.6 | 95.8-99.5 |
  | 2 | 95.3 | 92.4-97.1 |
  | 3 | 93.5 | 90.2-95.7 |
  | 4 | 85.4 | 79.8-89.6 |
  | 5 | 79.0 | 67.5-86.8 |
  | 6 | 70.8 | 49.8-84.3 |

- 📊 Crude LRR by local therapy (overall 6.5%, N=110)
  - BCT: 36 events, 5.5%
  - Mastectomy alone: 24 events, 8.1%
  - Mastectomy + PMRT: 50 events, 6.7%
- 📊 PMRT-indicated subgroup (cStage III or ypN+, N=1103): LRR-free by local therapy (Fig 2)

  | CPS+EG stratum | BCT N | Mastectomy N | PMRT N | P |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 0-2 (N=358) | 114 | 72 | 172 | 0.73 |
  | 3-6 (N=745) | 202 | 51 | 492 | 0.0006 |

- 📐 MVA: CPS+EG ≥3, ≥4 and ≥6 linked to higher LRR risk; mastectomy alone higher, PMRT marginally lower
- ⚠️ Table 5 HRs can't be reliably tied to rows in source; per-score HRs not quoted here
- ⚠️ Text says LRR-free was higher with mastectomy alone in CPS+EG ≥3, contradicting the MVA; direction unclear from source
- ⚠️ Overall LRR given as 6.5% in Results but 6.1% in Discussion
- ⚠️ PMRT-omission inference rests on non-randomized comparisons with small cells (mastectomy alone N=72 in CPS+EG 0-2)
- 🔗 vs NSABP B-18/B-27: 10-yr LRR 10.3% BCT, 12.3% mastectomy; those trials gave no PMRT

### Pooled analysis of nine neoadjuvant breast cancer trials: locoregional recurrence after NACT

> 5y LRR 4.1% with pCR vs 9.5% without (MV HR 3.33); TNBC non-pCR 18.5%. Surgery type not an independent predictor in 10,075 pts.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> The pCR split is where the RT read lives: LRR after pCR was 3.1-4.8% across all subtypes, while non-pCR HR-/HER2+ and TNBC reached 14.8% and 18.5%. Because RT delivery was not captured, these are LRR rates under guideline-directed RT, framing ypN0 de-escalation versus non-pCR TNBC as the highest-yield target for intensified locoregional therapy.

- 🔍 Exploratory pooled IPD analysis, N=10,075, nine GBG/AGO-B neoadjuvant trials, 1998-2013
- 🔍 Anthracycline/taxane-based NACT in all trials; cT4a-c and cT4d eligible in all but GeparDuo
- 📊 Median f/u 67 mo; LRR as first event, competing risks (Gray / Fine-Gray)
- 📊 Overall 5y LRR: BCS 7.8%, mastectomy 11.3%; mastectomy not significant on multivariate analysis
- 📐 Non-pCR vs pCR: MV HR 3.33 (95% CI 2.60-4.28), p<0.001
- 📐 TNBC vs HR+/HER2-: MV HR 2.72 (95% CI 2.23-3.31), p<0.001
- 📊 5y cumulative LRR by subtype, pCR vs non-pCR

  | Subtype | Overall | pCR | Non-pCR |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | HR+/HER2- | 6.0% | 3.9% | 5.9% |
  | HR+/HER2+ | 7.6% | 4.8% | 8.1% |
  | HR-/HER2+ | 10.5% | 3.1% | 14.8% |
  | TNBC | 14.4% | 4.2% | 18.5% |

- 📊 5y cumulative LRR by surgery type

  | Surgery | pCR | Non-pCR | TNBC | HR+/HER2- |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | BCS | 4.0% | 8.8% | 12.5% | 4.9% |
  | Mastectomy | 3.9% | 11.3% | 20.4% | 8.1% |

- 📐 Independent LRR predictors within non-pCR pts
  - Age <50: HR 1.32 (95% CI 1.12-1.55), p=0.001
  - cN+: HR 1.49 (95% CI 1.27-1.74), p<0.001
  - G3: HR 1.39 (95% CI 1.16-1.65), p<0.001
  - TNBC: HR 2.77 (95% CI 2.27-3.39), p<0.001
- 📊 After pCR, only cN+ trended toward LRR: HR 1.61 (95% CI 0.99-2.61), p=0.054
- ⚠️ No patient-level adjuvant RT data; protocols only recommended RT per guidelines
- ⚠️ Surgery type likely confounded by selection: higher-risk pts steered to mastectomy
- ⚠️ Older trials gave HER2+ pts no anti-HER2 therapy; no post-neoadjuvant T-DM1 or capecitabine

[doi:10.1016/j.ejca.2020.02.015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejca.2020.02.015)
  - 📎 [[papers/breast/2020-werutsky-locoregional-recurrence-risk-neoadjuvant-chemotherapy.pdf|Pooled analysis of nine neoadjuvant breast cancer trials: locoregional recurrence after NACT (full text)]]

### Postmastectomy RT selection in T1-T2 N1-3 breast cancer (BC Cancer cohort)

> 10-yr LRR±SDR 15.9% without PMRT overall; 58.0% if age <45 with >25% nodes positive vs 10.8% if ≥45 with ≤25% positive.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Pattern of failure is the target-volume read: of 57 regional recurrences, 33% were clavicular and 3% internal mammary. That supports SCV coverage in a selected N1 patient over routine IMN coverage. Staging was ALND with a median of 10 nodes removed, so the >25% nodal-ratio cutpoint does not carry over to SLNB-only axillae.

- 🔍 Retrospective population-based BCCA cohort, N=821 pT1-T2 N1-3, mastectomy + ALND, no PMRT, diagnosed 1989-1997
- 🔍 296 pts who received RT excluded from the 1117 eligible, so the cohort is the RT-unselected remainder
- 💊 Systemic therapy in 94%: HT alone 57.1%, CHT alone 21.7%, both 15.1%; CHT mostly AC or CMF
- 📊 10-yr KM ILRR 12.7%, LRR±SDR 15.9%; median f/u 7.7 yr
- 📐 Multivariate Cox: independent predictors of LRR

  | Factor | ILRR HR (95% CI), p | LRR±SDR HR (95% CI), p |
  |---|---|---|
  | Age <45 vs ≥45 | 3.44 (1.68-7.07), p=0.001 | 3.22 (1.54-6.75), p=0.002 |
  | >25% vs ≤25% nodes positive | 2.00 (0.99-4.05), p=0.05 | 2.61 (1.31-5.22), p=0.006 |
  | Medial vs lateral | 2.46 (1.15-5.26), p=0.002 | 3.69 (2.17-6.29), p=0.0001 |
  | ER-negative vs positive | 2.02 (1.16-3.54), p=0.01 | 1.82 (1.05-3.17), p=0.03 |

- 📊 Risk classification model, 10-yr KM LRR

  | Subgroup | n | ILRR | LRR±SDR |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Age <45 | 123 | 22.8% | 29.3% |
  | Age <45, >25% N+ | 19 | 47.6% | 58.0% |
  | Age <45, ≤25% N+ | 104 | 18.5% | 23.8% |
  | Age ≥45 | 698 | 10.8% | 13.7% |
  | Age ≥45, >25% N+ | 122 | 20.6% | 26.7% |
  | Age ≥45, ≤25% N+ | 570 | 8.7% | 10.8% |

- 📊 Pattern of regional failure (57 regional recurrences)
  - Axilla: 36 (63%)
  - Clavicular: 19 (33%)
  - Internal mammary: 2 (3%)
- 📊 Grade 3, T2, and >1 positive node raised univariate risk but dropped out on multivariate analysis
- 🔗 Baseline LRR well below the ~30% at 10 yr in DBCG 82b/82c no-PMRT arms
- ⚠️ Pre-taxane, pre-trastuzumab era; HER2 status not analyzed
- ⚠️ RT benefit inferred from an assumed two-thirds relative reduction, not measured in this cohort
- ⚠️ Small cells drive the top tier: the >50% LRR group is n=19

### 21-Gene Recurrence Score and Locoregional Recurrence in Node-Positive Breast Cancer (SWOG 8814 analysis)

> 10-yr LRR 9.7% vs 16.5% for RS <18 vs ≥18 (P=.02) in postmenopausal ER/PR+ node-positive pts; signal confined to N1 after mastectomy alone.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> In N1 disease treated by mastectomy alone, 10-yr LRR was 1.5% with RS <18 vs 11.1% with RS ≥18 (P=.051), while ≥4 nodes ran 25.9% vs 27.0% regardless of RS. That splits the PMRT question: RS may help select N1 pts for omission (the TailorRT question), but gives no route to omit PMRT in N2+.

- 🔍 Retrospective translational cohort from phase 3 SWOG S8814 (tamoxifen or CAF-T arms), N=316 with RS
- 🔍 Postmenopausal, ER/PR+, node-positive; excluded mastectomy + RT (n=37) and BCS without RT (n=9)
- 📊 1° prespecified model (continuous RS) not significant: HR 1.14 per 10-point RS (95% CI 0.97-1.35), P=.11
- 📊 10-yr actuarial LRR by RS category (low <18 vs intermediate/high ≥18)

  | Subset | RS <18 | RS ≥18 | P |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | All pts (n=316) | 9.7% | 16.5% | .02 |
  | Mastectomy, no RT (n=252) | 7.7% | 16.8% | .03 |
  | Mastectomy, no RT, 1-3 nodes (n=165) | 1.5% | 11.1% | .051 |
  | Mastectomy, no RT, ≥4 nodes | 25.9% | 27.0% | .27 |

- 📐 MVA: RS ≥18 HR 2.36 (95% CI 1.02-5.45), P=.04
- 📐 MVA: ≥4 nodes HR 3.37 (95% CI 1.69-6.72), P=.001
- 📊 By nodal burden, whole cohort: 10-yr LRR 9.0% (1-3 nodes) vs 24.4% (≥4), P=.002
- ⚠️ RS 18 cut point confirmed only in post hoc exploration; subset analyses not adjusted for multiplicity
- ⚠️ Mastectomy-alone cohort is physician-selected: higher-risk pts received PMRT and were excluded
- ⚠️ BCS + RT subgroup: only 6 events (2/29 vs 4/35), underpowered for RS comparison
- 🔗 vs NSABP B-28 (1-3 nodes, all chemo): 10-yr LRR 2.4% / 4.1% / 6.0% for low / intermediate / high RS
- 🔍 Median f/u 8.7 yr (IQR 7.0-10.2) in pts without LRR

[doi:10.1001/jamaoncol.2019.5559](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaoncol.2019.5559)

### Long-term impact of regional nodal irradiation after neoadjuvant systemic therapy

> Adjusted 10-yr LRR HR 0.497 (0.279-0.884) and DR HR 0.731 with RNI after NAT in cN+ breast; univariate LRR 9.4% vs 8.1%, P=.62.

> [!info] Why it matters · Radiation oncology
> Residual nodal disease is where the adjusted LRR benefit holds (HR 0.476 without axillary pCR). In the ypN0 group, too few events allowed no model, so this supports RNI for ypN+ and says nothing on omission after nodal pCR. Two-thirds of the no-RNI group had no RT at all, so the HRs likely overstate RNI's increment over breast/chest wall RT.

- 🔍 Single-institution retrospective (MDACC), N=1289, cytologically confirmed node-positive stage II-III, NAT 1989-2007
- 🔍 RNI given in 1080/1289 (83.8%), at the discretion of the treating radiation oncologist
- 🔍 Median f/u 10.2 yr after definitive surgery
- 📊 RNI effect: univariate 10-yr cumulative incidence vs multicovariate Cox

  | Endpoint | Univariate 10-yr (no RNI vs RNI) | Multicovariate HR (95% CI) |
  |---|---|---|
  | LRR | 9.4% vs 8.1%, P=.62 | 0.497 (0.279-0.884), P=.02 |
  | Any DR | 31.3% vs 36.5%, P=.16 | 0.731 (0.541-0.988), P=.04 |
  | DSS / OS | Not significant | Not significant |

- 📊 Without axillary pCR: adjusted LRR HR 0.476 (0.253-0.898), P=.02
- ⚠️ No multicovariate analysis for axillary pCR: too few LRR events. The B-51 question goes untested here
- 📊 HER2+ on trastuzumab: adjusted DR HR 0.237 (0.109-0.517), P=.0003
- 📊 Axillary pCR 368/1289 (28.5%), prognostic on univariate analysis
  - 10-yr LRR 9.7% no pCR vs 4.8% pCR, P=.006
  - 10-yr DR 43.0% vs 17.0%, P<.001
- 🔍 RT: median breast/CW 50 Gy/25 fx, then median boost 10 Gy/5 fx; multi-isocentric technique
- ⚠️ Control arm is mostly no RT: 142/209 (67.9%) of no-RNI pts got no RT at all
- ⚠️ Tangent field details unavailable, so high-tangent incidental nodal dose in the 'no RNI' group is unknown
- ⚠️ Univariate signal null or reversed for DR. The benefit appears only after adjustment, so confounding by indication is likely
- ⚠️ HER2+ pts without trastuzumab were excluded from all multicovariate models
